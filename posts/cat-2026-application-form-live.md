Candidates aiming for the prestigious IIMs and other top B-schools in India can now register online at the official website, [iimcat.ac.in](https://iimcat.ac.in).

## CAT 2026 Application Fee Structure

The application fee has been updated for the 2026 exam cycle. Payment must be made online via debit/credit card, net banking, or UPI during the registration process. **The fee is strictly non-refundable.**

*   **General / EWS / NC-OBC Candidates:** ₹2,700
*   **SC / ST / PwD Candidates:** ₹1,350

## How to Apply for CAT 2026: Step-by-Step Guide

Follow these steps to successfully register for CAT 2026:

1.  **Visit the Official Website:** Go to [iimcat.ac.in](https://iimcat.ac.in).
2.  **Register as a New User:** Click on "New Candidate Registration" and generate your unique User ID and Password using your mobile number and email address.
3.  **Fill the Application Form:** Log in with your credentials. Enter your personal details, academic history, and work experience (if any).
4.  **Select Programs & Interview Cities:** Choose the IIMs, specific MBA/PGP programs, and preferred cities for the interview process.
5.  **Choose Exam Center Cities:** You can select up to six preferred cities for taking the CAT exam.
6.  **Upload Documents:** Upload scanned copies of your passport-size photograph, signature, and category/PwD certificates (if applicable) as per the specified dimensions.
7.  **Pay the Application Fee:** Complete the final step by paying the application fee online.
8.  **Print Confirmation:** Download and print the confirmation page for your records.

## Essential Documents Needed for Registration

Before you start filling out the form, keep these documents handy:

*   Class 10th and 12th Marksheets
*   Graduation Marksheet/Degree Certificate (or previous semester marksheets if in final year)
*   Work Experience Certificates (if applicable)
*   Scanned Passport Size Photo and Signature
*   Valid Category or PwD Certificate (if applicable)
*   Debit/Credit Card, UPI, or Net Banking details for payment

The **Rajasthan Engineering Admission Process (REAP) 2026** is currently in full swing. With the final state merit list declared on July 2, 2026, and the Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS) round completed, students are eagerly waiting for the final counselling rounds, regular seat allotments, and the ultimate cutoffs. 

Conducted by the **Centre for Electronic Governance (CEG), Rajasthan**, REAP is the single-window gateway to secure admission into government and private B.Tech programs in Rajasthan. Whether you are aiming for prestigious institutions like MBM University Jodhpur, CTAE Udaipur, and RTU Kota, or looking for top private institutions like SKIT and JECRC in Jaipur, understanding the final cutoff trends and college list is crucial to making the right choice.

Read on for a comprehensive guide to the REAP 2026 final expected cutoffs, the complete college list, and strategic choice-filling advice.

## **Understanding REAP 2026 Counselling Rounds**

The REAP admission lifecycle follows a structured sequence. To maximize your chances of getting a premium seat, you need to understand how the final rounds progress:

1. **Round 1 (TFWS Allotment):** Dedicated to students qualifying under the Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS). This round offers significant fee concessions for high-merit students.
2. **Round 2 (State & All India Merit Allotment):** The regular seat allotment round for Rajasthan Domicile (85% seats) and Out of State (15% seats) candidates.
3. **Round 3 (Upward Movement):** If you are not satisfied with your allotted seat in Round 2, you must physically report to the allotted college, pay the admission fee, and opt for **Upward Movement**. If a vacant seat matching a higher preference on your list opens up, you will be upgraded automatically.
4. **Spot Round (Direct Admission):** Conducted at the end of the regular counselling rounds to fill remaining vacant seats across colleges. It is held at the institute level.
5. **Internal Sliding:** For candidates who have already reported and settled in a college. This process allows sliding to a better branch (e.g., from ECE to CSE) within the same college based on merit.

For a general overview of eligibility and dates, check our detailed [REAP Counselling 2026 Guide](/blog/reap-counselling-2026-rajasthan-btech-admission).

## **Strategic Choice Filling & Admission Tips**

Locking your preferences correctly in REAP final counselling can drastically alter your college outcome. Follow this strategy:

*   **Rank Government Colleges First:** Always keep MBM, RTU Kota, and CTAE Udaipur at the top of your list. Even if your JEE Main percentile is slightly below the expected cutoff, vacant seats from early dropouts might clear up for you in the **Upward Movement** round.
*   **Don't Ignore TFWS Seats:** If your family income is under ₹8 Lakhs per annum, apply for TFWS seats. It waives the entire tuition fee. Ensure you place TFWS options above non-TFWS options for the same branch.
*   **Physical Reporting is Mandatory:** If you get allotted a seat in Round 2, you *must* report physically to that college, submit your documents, and pay the first-semester fee to keep your candidature alive for Round 3 (Upward Movement). If you do not report, you are kicked out of the regular counselling process.
*   **Keep Spot Rounds as a Backup:** If your merit rank is very low and you missed out on top private colleges during regular rounds, keep an eye on college websites for Spot Round announcements. Many colleges fill vacant seats directly on a first-come, first-served basis during spot rounds.

### **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**

**1. When will the REAP 2026 final seat allotment be declared?**
The final rounds of seat allotment, including the upward movement rounds, are typically declared between mid-July and early August 2026.

**2. Which is the best government college under REAP?**
MBM University (Jodhpur) is the most sought-after government college under REAP, followed by CTAE Udaipur and RTU Kota (University Department).

**3. Can outside Rajasthan candidates apply for REAP?**
Yes, candidates from other states can apply under the All India Quota, which accounts for 15% of the total seats in participating colleges.

**4. What is the REAP upward movement process?**
Upward movement is a seat upgrade option. You must first report to the allotted college, pay the fee, and then apply online for upward movement to get a higher-preference seat.

**5. Does REAP accept Class 12 board marks?**
Yes, while JEE Main ranks are given Priority 1, candidates who did not appear for JEE can apply based on their Class 12th PCM marks under Priority 2.

**6. Is LNMIIT Jaipur part of REAP 2026?**
No, LNMIIT Jaipur conducts its own independent counselling process based on JEE Main scores and does not participate in REAP.

### 1. [Jaipuria Institute of Management](/colleges/jaipuria-institute-of-management-lucknow), Noida (Sector 62)
- **NIRF Ranking & Accreditation**: Consistently ranked among the top 50 B-schools nationally by NIRF with NBA accreditation and AIU MBA-equivalence.
- **Academic Ecosystem**: Offers a centralized pool placement system across 4 campuses (Noida, Lucknow, Jaipur, Indore), opening up top tier MNCs to all students.
- **Top Recruiters**: Deloitte, Amazon, EY, HCL Technologies, Dabur, Genpact, ICICI Bank, Protiviti.

### 2. Amity Business School, Noida (Sector 125)
- **Infrastructure & Scale**: Massive 60-acre university campus featuring world-class sporting, library, and incubation infrastructure.
- **Industry Interface**: Global corporate summits, international exchange pathways, and a massive alumni base exceeding 100,000+ professionals worldwide.
- **Best Suited For**: Candidates looking for high-energy campus life, specialized dual-specialization electives, and strong marketing/media networking.

### 3. Asian Business School (ABS Noida, Sector 125)
- **Unique Selling Proposition**: Includes a complimentary 1-week residential educational trip to Oxford Business College (UK) and integrated executive diplomas.
- **Placement Track**: Strong placement drive in domestic retail, digital marketing agencies, fin-tech sales, and financial advisory roles.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the CAT cutoff for [Jaipuria Institute of Management](/colleges/jaipuria-institute-of-management-noida) Noida?
For the PGDM Core and specialized programs, the CAT cutoff is typically **70 to 75 percentile**. Jaipuria also considers MAT (80+ percentile), CMAT (75+ percentile), and XAT scores.

### Is Asian Business School (ABS) Noida AICTE approved?
Yes, Asian Business School is approved by AICTE, Ministry of Education, Government of India, and its PGDM program is recognized by AIU as equivalent to an MBA.

### What are the best low fee MBA options in Noida?
For candidates seeking low-cost MBA programs, AKTU-affiliated institutions like **JSS Academy of Technical Education (Sector 62)** offer MBA degrees for around ₹3 Lakhs total fees.

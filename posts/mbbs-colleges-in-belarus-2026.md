**Belarus** is an Eastern European nation known for government-run medical universities offering high academic standards at low costs. With 100% English medium programs, dedicated clinical rotations in large city hospitals, and safe student environments, Belarus is an excellent option for Indian medical aspirants.

---

## 🌟 Key Benefits of MBBS in Belarus

1. **Affordable European Education:** Complete your degree in **₹20 Lakhs to ₹28 Lakhs**.
2. **State-Owned Medical Universities:** All medical universities are government institutions.
3. **NMC FMGL 2021 Compliant:** 6-year duration includes 54 months theory and 12 months internship.
4. **No University Entrance Exam:** Direct admission based on NEET score and 12th PCB marks.

## 📋 Eligibility Criteria 2026

* Minimum 50% in PCB in 12th Board Exam (40% for SC/ST/OBC).
* NEET UG 2026 / 2025 / 2024 Qualified.
* Minimum age of 17 years by Dec 31, 2026.

### What is the pattern of REET Level 1?
REET Level 1 (for Primary Teachers) consists of 150 questions carrying 150 marks with a duration of 2.5 hours. It covers Child Development, Language 1, Language 2, Mathematics, and EVS.

### Is there negative marking in REET?
No, there is no negative marking in the REET written examination. Candidates can safely attempt all 150 questions.

### What are the qualifying marks for REET?
General category candidates need to score at least 60% (90/150) to qualify, while non-creamy layer OBC, SC, and ST candidates need 55% (82/150).

The **Rajasthan Eligibility Examination for Teacher (REET)** is the state-level teacher qualification exam conducted by the **Board of Secondary Education Rajasthan (BSER)**. Passing REET is mandatory for any candidate who wishes to be eligible for teaching positions at **Level 1 (Classes 1–5)** or **Level 2 (Classes 6–8)** in Rajasthan government schools.

With thousands of teaching vacancies in Rajasthan's vast government school system and intense competition among aspirants, a qualified REET score opens the door to a stable, respected teaching career.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the pattern of REET Level 1?
REET Level 1 (for Primary Teachers) consists of 150 questions carrying 150 marks with a duration of 2.5 hours. It covers Child Development, Language 1, Language 2, Mathematics, and EVS.

### Is there negative marking in REET?
No, there is no negative marking in the REET written examination. Candidates can safely attempt all 150 questions.

### What are the qualifying marks for REET?
General category candidates need to score at least 60% (90/150) to qualify, while non-creamy layer OBC, SC, and ST candidates need 55% (82/150).

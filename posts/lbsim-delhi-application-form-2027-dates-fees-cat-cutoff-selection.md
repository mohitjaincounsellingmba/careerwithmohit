Accredited by **NBA**, recognized by the **Association of Indian Universities (AIU)**, and an active member of **AACSB**, LBSIM combines value-based management education with cutting-edge financial labs, including a live **Bloomberg Terminal Lab** directly on campus.

## 6. Selection Process: Extempore & Personal Interview

1. **Online Registration:** Submit your profile at `forms.lbsim.ac.in` and select your program preferences.
2. **Shortlisting:** Candidates are called for selection rounds based on CAT 2026 / XAT 2027 performance.
3. **Extempore Speech:** Candidates speak for 1 to 2 minutes on an unannounced contemporary socio-economic or business topic to test spontaneous logic and articulation.
4. **Group Discussion (GD):** A structured discussion testing teamwork and reasoning.
5. **Personal Interview (PI):** A 15-minute panel interaction evaluating finance fundamentals, academic clarity, general awareness, and work profile.
6. **Final Selection Weightage:** CAT/XAT Score (60%) + GD/Extempore & PI (25%) + Academics & Work Experience (15%).

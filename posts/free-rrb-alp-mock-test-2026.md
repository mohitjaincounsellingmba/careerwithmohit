## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the pattern of RRB ALP CBT 1?
RRB ALP CBT 1 is a 60-minute test consisting of 75 questions carrying 75 marks. The sections include Mathematics (20 Qs), Mental Ability (25 Qs), General Science (20 Qs), and General Awareness (10 Qs).

### Is there negative marking in RRB ALP CBT 1?
Yes, there is a negative marking of 1/3rd (-0.33) of the marks assigned to each question for every wrong response.

### Is CBT 1 score counted in the final RRB ALP merit list?
No, CBT 1 is only qualifying in nature. It is used to screen candidates for CBT 2. The final merit is prepared based on CBT 2 Part A and Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) scores.

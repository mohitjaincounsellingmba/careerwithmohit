Poland is Europe's **most affordable study destination** for Indian students — offering Schengen-zone access, English-taught degrees, and tuition fees as low as ₹3.5 Lakhs per year. As a rapidly growing European economy with a booming IT sector, Poland is the smart choice for Indian students who want a European degree without the European price tag.

---

## 🎓 Why Study in Poland in 2026?

- **Extremely Low Fees:** ₹3.5–₹9 Lakhs/year (among the cheapest in Europe)
- **Schengen Zone:** Travel freely to 27 European countries on your student visa
- **English-Taught Programs:** Growing rapidly, especially in business, IT, and engineering
- **Fast-Growing Economy:** Warsaw is a major European financial and tech hub
- **Safe Environment:** Poland is ranked as one of the safest countries in Europe
- **Indian Diaspora:** Growing Indian student community, especially in Warsaw, Wrocław

## 🛂 Schengen Zone Benefit for Polish Students

As a Schengen Member State, a **Polish Student Visa (Type D)** allows:
- Travel to any of the **27 Schengen countries** without additional visa
- 90-day stays within 180-day rolling window across Europe
- Access to EU internships and short-term work placements during studies

For students wanting a UK degree in Poland:
- **University:** Coventry University (UK – ranked in top 401-500 globally)

## 🎯 Admission Requirements for Poland

- **English Proficiency:** IELTS 5.5–6.5 / TOEFL 65–90 / B1 Cambridge
- **Academic Transcripts** (may require Apostille authentication)
- **SOP / Personal Statement**
- **Financial Proof:** PLN 776/month (approx. ₹15,000/month)
- **Health Insurance:** Required for student visa
- **Temporary Residence Permit (TRP):** Applied for within 30 days of arrival

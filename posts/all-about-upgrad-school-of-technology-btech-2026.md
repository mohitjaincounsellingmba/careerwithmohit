## Key Highlights & USPs: What Makes uGSOT Different?

Instead of adopting standard lecture-heavy models, upGrad School of Technology focuses on active engineering preparation:

*   🤖 **AI-First Curriculum:** The syllabus is designed to prepare students for the era of Generative AI. It covers high-demand fields like **Generative AI & Machine Learning, Quantum Computing, and Full Stack AI Development & Cloud Innovation**.
*   💻 **Silicon Valley-Style Learning:** Instruction is led by active industry professionals, including masterclasses and mentorship sessions from engineers at MAANG companies and global tech firms.
*   📈 **Integrated "Mini-MBA" Module:** Recognizing that modern engineers need business acumen, the curriculum features product management, entrepreneurship, and leadership courses to prepare graduates for startup and management roles.
*   🏢 **Rigorous "Hacker" Schedule:** Preparing students for actual corporate lives, the learning environment adopts a highly intensive 9 AM to 9 PM, 6-days-a-week culture designed to maximize hands-on coding and project building.
*   🤝 **Global Ecosystem Access:** Students gain access to upGrad’s vast online portal, career support mechanisms, and global alumni network.

Because upGrad School of Technology is an educational provider rather than a standalone university, it operates within the physical, residential campuses of its partner institutions. The final degree is awarded by the respective partner university.

## Admission Process & uGNET 2026

Admissions to upGrad School of Technology are holistic and aptitude-based.

1.  **Eligibility:**
    *   Completed Class 12 with a minimum of **50% marks in PCM** (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics).
    *   Must have completed Class 12 in 2024, 2025, or be appearing in 2026.
    *   Age must be **below 20 years** as of July 2026.
2.  **uGNET Entrance Exam:**
    *   A 120-minute, online-proctored aptitude exam consisting of 90 questions.
    *   Evaluates **Mathematical Ability, Logical Reasoning, and Verbal Aptitude** (prior coding experience is not required).
3.  **Personal Interview:**
    *   A comprehensive interaction checking analytical capabilities, passion for technology, and communication skills.
4.  **Scholarships:**
    *   uGSOT offers generous scholarships (Merit-based, Girls in Tech, Need-based) ranging from 10% to 100% tuition fee waivers based on uGNET scores.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the admission test for upGrad School of Technology?
Admissions are conducted through the uGNET (upGrad School of Technology National Entrance Test), which is a 120-minute online aptitude-based exam testing mathematical, logical, and verbal skills.

### Which universities award the B.Tech degree for uGSOT?
The B.Tech degree is awarded by upGrad's partner universities, which include Sri Siddhartha Academy of Higher Education (SSAHE) and Ajeenkya DY Patil University (ADYPU).

### What is the age limit for uGSOT B.Tech admission?
Candidates must be under 20 years of age as of July 2026 to be eligible for admission.

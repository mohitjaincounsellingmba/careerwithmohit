Located in New Delhi, it is renowned for its intellectual environment, highly subsidized education, and vibrant student politics. If you're aiming for JNU in 2026, here is a detailed overview of what to expect.

## **Admission Process & Cutoffs 2026**

JNU is highly selective, utilizing national-level exams for its intake:
*   **Undergraduate (BA Hons):** Admissions to its popular foreign language programs are strictly via **CUET UG**.
*   **B.Tech:** Admissions are channeled through **JEE Main** and JoSAA counseling.
*   **Postgraduate & PhD:** CUET PG is used for most MA/MSc courses. MBA requires CAT, M.Tech requires GATE, and PhD admissions rely on the university's JNUEE.
*   **Cutoffs:** JNU cutoffs are notoriously high due to the limited number of seats and high demand. For BA Hons in languages, general category cutoffs often touch the upper echelons of CUET percentiles.

## **Fees Structure: The Most Affordable in India**

JNU is globally recognized for its accessibility.
*   **Humanities & Sciences:** The tuition fees for BA, MA, and research programs are almost negligible, often ranging between a mere **₹600 to ₹1,000 annually**.
*   **Professional Courses:** While B.Tech fees total around ₹5.51 lakhs, JNU offers a progressive waiver system where students from low-income families (under ₹1 lakh) get full tuition waivers.

## **Placements vs. Higher Education**

Unlike traditional engineering or management colleges, JNU is primarily a **research-oriented institution**.
*   The majority of students in arts and sciences pursue PhDs, civil services, or careers in academia, journalism, and NGOs.
*   **Professional Schools:** The School of Engineering and the School of Management have active placement cells. These students see median salaries of ₹6.5 to ₹9.5 LPA, with highest packages reaching up to **₹33 LPA** from recruiters like Amazon, Google, and KPMG.

## **Hostel Admission and Living**

JNU is inherently residential, but getting a hostel is competitive.
*   **Allotment:** Hostels are allocated based on a strict priority system focusing on merit and outstation status.
*   **Cost:** The living cost is incredibly low. Room rent is around **₹1,200 per year**, and monthly mess bills range from ₹2,000 to ₹3,500, making it extremely affordable.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I check my exam results online?
You can check results on the official website of the conducting bodies (e.g., NTA for JEE/NEET/CUET, or respective boards/testing organizations) using your application registration number.

### Are mock tests helpful in exam preparation?
Yes, attempting mock tests helps candidates build speed and accuracy, understand the exam pattern, and perform detailed analytics of strong and weak sections.

### What is the significance of negative marking in entrance exams?
Many competitive exams deduct marks for wrong answers (e.g., -1 or -0.25). Candidates should avoid guessing to maintain accuracy and prevent score drops.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is IIM Shillong part of the IIM CAP process?
No. IIM Shillong conducts its own independent admission process. It does not participate in the centralized IIM Common Admission Process (CAP). Candidates are shortlisted directly based on CAT scores and IIM Shillong's institutional criteria.

### 2. What makes IIM Shillong's placement performance stand out?
IIM Shillong records an audited average CTC of **₹26.10 LPA** with a median of **₹25.00 LPA**, placing it directly alongside older IIMs (BLACKI). Top consulting firms like McKinsey, BCG, Bain, and Deloitte, alongside Goldman Sachs and JP Morgan, are regular campus recruiters.

### 3. Does IIM Shillong have a permanent campus?
Yes. IIM Shillong operates fully from its sprawling 120-acre permanent green campus located at Umsawli, New Shillong, Meghalaya.

### 4. What are the summer internship stipend figures at IIM Shillong?
For the recent summer placement season, the average two-month summer internship stipend stood at **₹1.90 Lakhs**, with the highest stipend offered reaching **₹4.75 Lakhs** for two months.

### 5. Can I get direct admission in IIM Shillong?
No. There is no management quota, NRI quota, or direct admission route at IIM Shillong. Admission is strictly based on CAT performance, personal interview evaluation, and composite merit criteria.

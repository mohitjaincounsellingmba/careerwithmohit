Running a career upgrade requires choosing the right management program. For working professionals in Bangalore, the Executive MBA / Executive PGDM offered by [Ramaiah Institute of Management, Bangalore](/colleges/ms-ramaiah-institute-of-management) represents a powerful gateway to higher senior leadership positions.

In this review, we break down everything you need to know: fees, admission cutoffs, placements, pros, cons, and our honest expert verdict.

---

## 🏆 Program Overview

- **Official Course Name:** PGDM Executive
- **Approximate Fees:** ₹3.50 Lakhs (Total Fees)
- **Entrance Exams Accepted:** CAT/XAT/MAT/CMAT + Personal Interview
- **Course Duration:** 15 Months to 2 Years (Format varies by program)

---

## 📈 Eligibility & Admission Details

- **Academic Requirements:** Graduation + minimum 2 years of work experience (preferably 5 years).
- **Selection Process:** Admission typically relies on professional profile screening, statement of purpose (SOP), executive experience reviews, and personal interviews. Elite IIM programs also mandate standard quantitative testing (GMAT/GRE).

---

## 💼 Placement Statistics & Career Impact

- **Estimated Placements:** Decent career enhancement, average CTC is ₹6-8 LPA.
- **Career Growth:** Executive MBA degrees focus on helping candidates secure senior lateral shifts (like VP, Product Manager, or Director roles) or move from technical paths into core management.

---

## 📝 Student Reviews: Pros & Cons

### Pros
- **Highlight:** Highly reasonable fees for an Executive program.
- **Highlight:** Strong academic foundations and excellent corporate connections.
- **Highlight:** Convenient schedule for professionals working in Bangalore.

### Cons
- **Limitation:** Average placement salary is moderate.
- **Limitation:** Smaller campus compared to major universities.

---

## 🔍 Our Expert Verdict

The Executive MBA program at [Ramaiah Institute of Management, Bangalore](/colleges/ms-ramaiah-institute-of-management) is highly recommended for professionals based in Bangalore who want to scale their careers without disrupting their current geographic setup. 

If you are looking for top-tier consulting placements and have 5+ years of experience, full-time residential paths are stellar. However, if you are looking to continue your full-time job, their weekend/evening classes offer outstanding return on investment.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is the Ramaiah Executive PGDM AICTE approved?
Yes, the program is fully approved by AICTE.

### What is the minimum work experience required?
A minimum of 2 years of post-qualification corporate or entrepreneurial experience is required.

### What are the class timings?
Classes are conducted primarily on weekends and in evening sessions on weekdays.

Denmark — the land of **design, sustainability, and innovation** — is one of Scandinavia's most exciting study destinations for Indian students. With a strong focus on project-based and problem-oriented learning, world-class universities, and a thriving green tech economy, Denmark offers Indian students a transformative academic experience. Plus, as an EU/Schengen member, a Danish education opens doors across 27 European countries.

---

## 🎓 Why Study in Denmark in 2026?

- **World-Class Education:** Technical University of Denmark (DTU), University of Copenhagen are globally ranked
- **Innovation & Sustainability Focus:** Denmark leads in wind energy, circular economy, and clean tech
- **English-Taught Programs:** 700+ English-medium programs available
- **Schengen Access:** Travel across 27 European countries on student visa
- **High Quality of Life:** Denmark consistently ranks as one of the world's happiest countries
- **Work While Studying:** International students can work 20 hours/week

## 🛂 Denmark Student Visa & Post-Study Work

- **Study Permit:** Required for programs over 3 months
- **Work Rights:** 20 hours/week during studies (full-time in June–August)
- **Post-Study Work:** Job-seeker visa for 6 months after graduation
- **Schengen:** Travel freely across Europe during your studies

---

## 🎯 Admission Requirements for Denmark

- **English Proficiency:** IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 88 / Duolingo 110+
- **Academic Transcripts** (officially translated)
- **SOP / Motivation Letter**
- **Financial Proof:** DKK 6,397/month (approx. ₹70,000/month)
- **Health Insurance:** Danish student health card issued after arrival
- **Application Platform:** Direct to university portal

### How can I choose the right college for higher studies?
Consider critical factors such as UGC/AICTE accreditations, historical placement reports, fee structure vs ROI, faculty quality, and location.

### What is the role of a career counsellor?
A career counsellor helps students evaluate their strengths, interests, and career options, providing personalized guidance to secure admissions and achieve long-term professional goals.

### How important is NAAC accreditation for a university?
NAAC accreditation grades (like A++, A+, A) evaluate the overall quality of education, infrastructure, and research at a university, serving as a reliable benchmark for students.

## Official CAT 2026 Registration Dates & Schedule

The official notification for CAT 2026 has been released on July 26, 2026, by IIM Indore. The official registration cycle follows this schedule:

| Event | Official Date / Timeline |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Official CAT 2026 Notification Release** | July 26, 2026 |
| **CAT 2026 Application Form Open Date** | **August 3, 2026 (10:00 AM)** |
| **Registration Ends** | **September 15, 2026 (5:00 PM)** |
| **Correction Window Opens** | Last week of September 2026 |
| **Admit Card Release** | November 4, 2026 |
| **CAT 2026 Exam Date** | **November 29, 2026 (Sunday)** |

Candidates will have a window of approximately 44 days to register. To avoid server errors or last-minute rush, it is recommended to register well ahead of the deadline.

## CAT 2026 Application Fee

The application fee must be paid online during registration using credit card, debit card, UPI, or net banking:

* **General / NC-OBC / EWS Candidates:** ₹2,700
* **SC / ST / PwD Candidates:** ₹1,350

## Eligibility Criteria for CAT 2026 Registration

Before filling out the CAT 2026 application form, ensure you meet the minimum eligibility requirements:

1. **Bachelor's Degree:** Candidates must hold a bachelor's degree or equivalent from a recognized university with a minimum of **50% marks** (or equivalent CGPA) for General/NC-OBC/EWS candidates, and **45% marks** for SC/ST/PwD candidates.
2. **Final Year Students:** Candidates appearing for the final year of their bachelor's degree or awaiting results can also apply, provided they submit certificate proof of university graduation later.
3. **No Age Bar:** There is no age limit for taking the CAT exam.
4. **Professional Degrees:** Candidates with CA/CS/ICWA degrees are also eligible if they meet the percentage requirements.

## Documents Needed for Online Registration

Having these documents ready in digital format beforehand will make the registration process seamless:

* **Personal Information:** Name, date of birth, parent's names, mobile number, and email address.
* **Academic Details:** Marks/CGPA obtained in Class 10, Class 12, Bachelor's degree, and any post-graduation or professional certifications.
* **Work Experience:** Dates of employment, company names, and certificates if applying with work history.
* **Scanned Images:** 
  * A passport-size photograph (recent, white background, size up to 80 KB).
  * A scanned signature (black ink, white background, size up to 80 KB).
* **Category Certificate:** EWS, NC-OBC, SC, ST, or PwD certificate (if applicable, in PDF format).

## Step-by-Step Guide to Apply for CAT 2026

Once the **CAT 2026 application form open date** arrives, follow these simple steps on the official website **[iimcat.ac.in](https://iimcat.ac.in)**:

1. **Register for User ID & Password:** Click on the "New Candidate Registration" tab. Enter your active email ID and mobile number to receive verification OTPs. You will receive login credentials via email/SMS.
2. **Fill the Application Form:** Log in using the credentials. Fill out the six required sections:
   * **Personal Details:** Category, gender, and contact details.
   * **Academics:** Detailed marks from high school to graduation.
   * **Work Experience:** Total months of relevant work experience (if any).
   * **Program Selection:** Select the specific IIMs and courses (e.g., PGP, PGDM) you want to apply to. Select interview city preferences for each.
   * **Test City Preference:** Choose up to six preferred exam cities.
   * **Upload Documents:** Upload your signature, photo, and certificates.
3. **Payment & Submission:** Preview the form carefully. Pay the application fee online. Once the payment is successful, download and print the confirmation page and copy of the application.

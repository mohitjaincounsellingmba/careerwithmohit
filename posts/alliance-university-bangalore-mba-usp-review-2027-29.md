## What is the Admission Selection Process for Alliance University MBA 2027–29?

**Admission to the Alliance University MBA program is based on a composite evaluation of national entrance exam percentiles (CAT, XAT, NMAT, MAT, CMAT) or AMAT, followed by an Oral Extempore Presentation and Personal Interview (PI).**

### Step-by-Step Admission Roadmap

1. **Online Application Form**: Fill out the application form on the official Alliance University portal or register through authorized counseling partner portals.
2. **Score Submission / AMAT Booking**: Submit your valid CAT/XAT/NMAT/MAT/CMAT scorecard. Candidates without an entrance score can take the online/offline **AMAT (Alliance Management Aptitude Test)**.
3. **Oral Extempore Presentation (OEP)**: Shortlisted candidates present a 3-minute oral extempore on a contemporary business, technology, or socioeconomic topic to evaluate communication clarity and analytical reasoning.
4. **Personal Interview (PI)**: An interview panel assesses your domain knowledge, career aspirations, leadership potential, and cultural fit.
5. **Offer Letter & Seat Acceptance**: Selected candidates receive an official offer letter and confirm their seat by submitting the initial registration fee installment.

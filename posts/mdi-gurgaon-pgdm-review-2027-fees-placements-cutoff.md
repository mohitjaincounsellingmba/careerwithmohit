## 7. Step-by-Step Admission Process & Key Timelines

To secure a seat in the 2027–2029 PGDM batch at MDI Gurgaon, follow this structured roadmap:

1. **Step 1 - Register for CAT (August – September)**: Apply for the Common Admission Test.
2. **Step 2 - Apply on MDI Portal (September – November)**: Complete the independent MDI Gurgaon application on `mdi.ac.in` before the deadline.
3. **Step 3 - Appear for CAT Exam (November)**: Score above sectional (80+) and overall (95+) percentiles.
4. **Step 4 - Personal Interview Round (February – March)**: Attend Personal Interview rounds conducted in Gurgaon, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bangalore, and Chennai.
5. **Step 5 - Final Offer Merit List (April)**: Receive the final admission offer and pay the seat acceptance fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Does MDI Gurgaon accept XAT or GMAT scores for Indian candidates?
No. Indian candidates can apply only through valid CAT scores. GMAT scores are accepted exclusively for NRI/PIO/Foreign national applicants.

### 2. What is unique about the PGDM-IB program at MDI Gurgaon?
PGDM-IB (International Business) is a collaborative dual-degree program with ESCP Europe. Students spend a full academic term studying in Europe and graduate with both an MDI PGDM and an ESCP Master in Management degree.

### 3. What is the average placement package at MDI Gurgaon?
The audited average placement package at MDI Gurgaon stands between **₹25.50 LPA and ₹26.70 LPA**, with the median CTC at **₹24.20 LPA** and the top 10% average reaching **₹39.50 LPA**.

### 4. What are the summer internship stipend figures at MDI Gurgaon?
The average two-month summer internship stipend at MDI Gurgaon stands at **₹1.75 Lakhs**, with the highest stipend offered reaching **₹4.40 Lakhs** for the two-month period.

### 5. Can I get direct admission in MDI Gurgaon through management quota?
No. Direct admission, management quota, or NRI donation seats do not exist at MDI Gurgaon. Admission is strictly based on CAT merit, personal interview evaluation, and profile composite scores.

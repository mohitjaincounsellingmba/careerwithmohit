## What is the MAT IBT Mode?

The **Internet-Based Test (IBT)** is AIMA's most flexible MAT format — you can take it **from anywhere** using your own computer or laptop with a reliable internet connection. No test centre visit required.

Key facts about MAT IBT:
- **Exam Venue:** Home or any location with stable internet
- **Device Required:** Computer or laptop with webcam (for proctoring)
- **Duration:** 2.5 hours (150 minutes)
- **Total Questions:** 150 MCQs
- **Sections:** Language Comprehension (30), Intelligence & Critical Reasoning (30), Mathematical Skills (30), Data Analysis & Sufficiency (30), Indian & Global Environment (30)
- **Marking Scheme:** +1 for correct, −0.25 for incorrect
- **Composite Score:** Out of 800 (IGE excluded from composite)
- **Proctoring:** AI + human proctoring — webcam and microphone must be enabled

## How to Check MAT May 2026 IBT Score: Step-by-Step

Once AIMA releases your IBT result, here's exactly how to access it:

### Step 1: Open the AIMA MAT Official Portal
Visit **[mat.aima.in](https://mat.aima.in)**. This is the **only official portal** for MAT scorecards. Any other website claiming to show your IBT result is unreliable.

### Step 2: Log In to Candidate Portal
Click **"Candidate Login"** on the homepage. Enter:
- **Registered Email ID** (used at time of MAT registration)
- **Password**

> Lost your password? Use **"Forgot Password"** → enter your registered email → reset via the link sent to your inbox. Do this before result day to avoid delays.

### Step 3: Access Your Candidate Dashboard
Your dashboard shows all your active MAT registrations. Once your IBT result is published, a **Result / Scorecard** option will appear for that exam slot.

### Step 4: View Your IBT Scorecard
Click on the **Scorecard** section to view:
- Section-wise raw scores (LC, ICR, MS, DA, IGE)
- **Composite Score (out of 800)**
- **Percentile**
- Exam date and slot details

### Step 5: Download and Save the PDF
- Click **Download Scorecard** to save the PDF
- **Print 2–3 copies** — one for records, one for each college you apply to
- Store it in a safe digital folder — you'll need it multiple times during the admission process

| Field | Details |

| **Percentile** | Your relative rank across all MAT May 2026 test-takers |
| **Score Validity** | 12 months from declaration date |

> **Key Detail:** MAT IBT percentile is calculated by comparing your score against all candidates who appeared in the **same May session** (PBT + CBT + IBT combined). Your mode does not affect how percentile is calculated.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1. When will MAT May 2026 IBT result be declared?**  
For early IBT slots (May 1–15), results are expected by **June 10–15, 2026**. For late slots (May 16–31), by **June 20–25, 2026**.

**Q2. Where do I check my MAT IBT scorecard?**  
At **[mat.aima.in](https://mat.aima.in)** → Candidate Login → Dashboard → Scorecard. AIMA does not send results by email or SMS.

**Q3. Is the MAT IBT score valid at all MBA colleges?**  
Yes. Every college accepting MAT treats IBT, PBT, and CBT scores as completely equivalent.

**Q4. Can I appear in IBT + CBT in the same May session?**  
Yes. AIMA allows you to appear in multiple modes at a combined fee. Your best score from any mode is typically considered by colleges.

**Q5. What if my IBT session had technical issues?**  
If your session was flagged due to disconnection, check your dashboard for any alert. Contact AIMA at **mat@aima.in** or through the official grievance portal if your scorecard is not visible.

**Q6. How soon can I start applying to colleges after my IBT result?**  
Immediately on the day of result declaration. Download the scorecard and start applying the same day — IBT candidates have a strategic window before PBT/CBT results come out.

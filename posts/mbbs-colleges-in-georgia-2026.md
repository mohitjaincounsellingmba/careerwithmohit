**Georgia** is renowned for offering European-standard medical education. Situated at the intersection of Eastern Europe and Western Asia, Georgia has become a prime destination for students aiming for USMLE (USA), PLAB (UK), or NEXT (India).

---

## 🌟 Why Study MBBS in Georgia in 2026?

1. **European Quality & ECTS Credits:** Georgia follows European Union education standards (360 ECTS credits).
2. **100% English Medium:** All 6 years are taught completely in English.
3. **High Licensing Exam Pass Rates:** Georgian university graduates achieve impressive success in USMLE and FMGE/NEXT.
4. **Safety & High Quality of Life:** Georgia is ranked among the safest countries in the world.

## 📋 Admission Eligibility & NMC Compliance

* **NMC Guideline:** 360 ECTS credits over 6 years (54 months study + 12 months internship).
* **Eligibility:** 50% in 12th PCB + NEET UG Qualified.
* **Admission Process:** Online interview / document verification by university.

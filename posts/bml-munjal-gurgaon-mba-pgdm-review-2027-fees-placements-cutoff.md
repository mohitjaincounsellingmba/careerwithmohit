## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the total fee structure for the MBA program at BML Munjal University Gurgaon?
The total 2-year tuition and academic fee for the flagship MBA program at BML Munjal University (School of Management) is approximately ₹14.50 Lakhs to ₹15.10 Lakhs, payable in semester-wise installments. Fully residential on-campus hostel and dining amenities range between ₹1.40 and ₹1.80 Lakhs per annum.

### What is the average and highest placement package at BML Munjal University MBA?
During recent recruitment seasons, BML Munjal University recorded an average domestic CTC of ₹8.50 to ₹9.80 LPA, with top 25% performers securing an average package of ₹14.50 LPA and peak domestic offers touching ₹33.60 LPA.

### What is the academic mentorship tie-up with Imperial College London?
BML Munjal University was founded under the academic mentorship of Imperial College London, offering joint curriculum design, student exchange immersions, faculty development, and global innovation projects via the Institute of Innovation and Entrepreneurship (I2E).

### Which entrance exams are accepted for BML Munjal University MBA 2027 admission?
BMU accepts scores from CAT, NMAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT, and GMAT, alongside its proprietary BMU-MAT aptitude test, followed by an online case discussion / extempore and Personal Interview (PI).

### Does BML Munjal University offer merit scholarships for MBA students?
Yes, BMU awards Dean’s Scholarships and Hero Group Merit Scholarships offering up to 50% tuition fee waivers for high scorers in CAT/XAT (80%+ percentile), NMAT (210+ score), or GMAT (650+ score).

## 1. Institutional Background: The Hero Group Legacy & Imperial College Pedagogy

BML Munjal University (BMU), named after the visionary founder of the Hero Group, Brijmohan Lall Munjal, was established in 2014 under the Haryana State Private Universities Act. Situated on a 50-acre residential campus on NH-48 in Sidhrawali, Gurugram, BMU was conceptualized to fundamentally transform higher education in India through an experiential, hands-on learning paradigm.

Mentored by **Imperial College London** during its formative years, BMU's School of Management blends rigorous analytical thinking with corporate execution. The university integrates engineering, technology, and management into a unified interdisciplinary ecosystem. Students participate in live enterprise consulting, operational supply chain audits, and global immersion programs with international academic partners including UC Berkeley and London School of Economics (LSE).

For candidates seeking an ethically grounded, corporate-driven business school with strong automotive, industrial manufacturing, and corporate consulting placement networks, BML Munjal University provides a modern educational launchpad in Delhi NCR.

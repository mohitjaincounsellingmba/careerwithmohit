The Central University of Rajasthan (CURaj) is an emerging central university known for its robust infrastructure, research facilities, and quality education. With highly competitive admissions through the CUET, CURaj has become a top choice for students aiming for undergraduate and postgraduate degrees.

### 📝 Admission Process 2026

Admissions at CURaj are primarily managed through the **Common University Entrance Test (CUET)**:
*   **Entrance Exams:** Admission to most UG programs requires **CUET UG** scores. PG admissions require **CUET PG** scores. For M.Tech, admissions are processed via **CCMT** (based on GATE scores).
*   **Registration:** Candidates must appear for the relevant entrance exam and then register on the official CURaj Admission Portal once it opens.
*   **Counselling:** Participate in the university's online counselling process based on merit lists released by the university.
*   **Final Step:** Complete document verification and pay the admission fee to secure your seat.

### 📊 CUET Cutoff Trends

The cutoffs at CURaj are subject to change annually based on applicant volume and seat availability.
*   **UG Programs:** Competitive scores for popular programs often range from **450 to 600+** for the General category, though this varies significantly by subject.
*   **Merit Lists:** Candidates should check the university’s published merit lists and cutoff notifications on their official website following CUET results.

### 💰 Fee Structure (2025-26)

CURaj offers affordable education, though the fee structure differs across programs.

| Program | Estimated Semester Fees |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Typical UG and PG Programs** | ₹15,000 to ₹28,500 |
| **B.Tech Programs** | ₹77,000 to ₹85,000+ |

*Note: B.Tech fees include tuition, lab, and other necessary components. A lump sum of ₹9,000 is typically required as a mess deposit.*

### 🚀 Placements at CURaj

The placement cell at CURaj is growing and consistently working to connect students with top recruiters.
*   **Average Salary:** The average package offered is around **₹5 LPA**.
*   **Highest Salary:** Peak packages have reached approximately **₹6.5 LPA** in recent placement drives.
*   **Top Recruiters:** TCS, Wipro, Infosys, Genpact, and Deloitte frequently participate in recruitment drives at CURaj.

### 🏢 Hostel Facilities

CURaj provides excellent residential facilities, making it highly accommodating for students from all over India.
*   **Overview:** The university offers separate, fully furnished hostels for boys and girls.
*   **Amenities:** Rooms are well-equipped, and hostels provide Wi-Fi, 24/7 security, common entertainment rooms, and gymnasium facilities.
*   **Fees:** Hostel room rent is affordable at roughly **₹6,000 per semester**, plus electricity and water charges (approx. ₹2,500). Mess services are managed by student committees.

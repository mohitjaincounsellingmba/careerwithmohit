## 7. Step-by-Step Admission Process & Key Timelines

To secure a seat in the 2027–2029 MBA batch at IIM Udaipur, follow the standard CAP roadmap:

1. **Step 1 - Register for CAT (August – September)**: Apply for the Common Admission Test and select IIM Udaipur.
2. **Step 2 - Appear for CAT (November)**: Secure qualifying sectional (73+) and overall (94+) percentiles.
3. **Step 3 - CAP Shortlist Call (January)**: Receive IIM CAP interview shortlist based on CAT scores and profile screening.
4. **Step 4 - Centralized Personal Interview (February – March)**: Attend the common online CAP Personal Interview conducted by the coordinating IIM.
5. **Step 5 - Final Merit Offer (May)**: Check composite score merit lists and deposit the seat acceptance fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What makes IIM Udaipur unique among Second-Generation IIMs?
IIM Udaipur is the youngest business school in India to achieve AACSB accreditation, ranks in the global Financial Times MiM ranking, follows strict IPRS-audited placement reporting, and holds a top-tier research publication record.

### 2. What is the fee structure for the MBA program at IIM Udaipur?
The total course fee for the two-year full-time MBA at IIM Udaipur is approximately **₹21.00 Lakhs**, covering academic tuition, single-occupancy hostel accommodations, digital library access, and course case studies.

### 3. What is the average placement package at IIM Udaipur?
The verified average placement package at IIM Udaipur stands at **₹20.02 LPA**, with the median CTC at **₹18.53 LPA** and the top 10% average reaching **₹33.50 LPA**.

### 4. What are the summer internship stipend figures at IIM Udaipur?
The average two-month summer internship stipend at IIM Udaipur stands at **₹1.40 Lakhs**, with the highest stipend offered reaching **₹3.50 Lakhs** for the two-month period.

### 5. Can I get direct admission in IIM Udaipur without CAT?
No. Direct admission, management quota, or NRI quota seats do not exist at IIM Udaipur. Admission is strictly based on CAT merit, IIM CAP interview evaluation, and profile composite scores.

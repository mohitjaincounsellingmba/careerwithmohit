## How to Check Maharashtra MBA CET 2026 Result (Step-by-Step)

Follow these steps to download your MAH MBA CET 2026 scorecard:

1. **Visit the Official Website:** Go to [cetcell.mahacet.org](https://cetcell.mahacet.org).
2. **Click on the Result Link:** Look for **"MAH MBA/MMS-CET 2026 Scorecard"** or **"Maharashtra CET Result 2026"** link on the homepage.
3. **Login:** Enter your registered **Email ID** and **Password** (or Application Number and Date of Birth, if prompted).
4. **Access Your Dashboard:** Navigate to the **"Scorecard"** or **"View Result"** tab.
5. **Download & Save:** Download the scorecard PDF and save it — you will need it for the **CAP Counselling process**.

> 💡 **Tip:** Keep multiple copies of your scorecard. You will need it at E-Scrutiny centres and during CAP registration.

## Maharashtra MBA CET 2026 CAP Counselling: What's Next?

Following the Maharashtra CET result declaration, the CET Cell will initiate the **Centralized Admission Process (CAP)** for 300+ MBA/MMS colleges in Maharashtra. The CAP process involves:

1. **CAP Registration & Application** (expected: June–July 2026)
2. **Document Verification at E-Scrutiny Centres**
3. **Publishing of Merit List** (Provisional → Final)
4. **Option Form Filling** (College Preference Submission)
5. **CAP Round 1, 2, and 3 Seat Allotment**
6. **Freeze/Float/Cancel & Reporting to College**

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. When was the Maharashtra MBA CET 2026 Result declared?
The MAH MBA CET 2026 result was officially declared on **May 30, 2026** by the State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra.

### 2. Where can I download the Maharashtra CET result 2026?
Visit **[cetcell.mahacet.org](https://cetcell.mahacet.org)** and log in with your registered email and password to download the scorecard.

### 3. What is a good score in Maharashtra MBA CET 2026?
Any score above **130 out of 200** (approximately 99.5 percentile) is considered good for top-tier colleges. For JBIMS, you need **160+ marks** (99.99 percentile).

### 4. Can I apply to colleges with a low Maharashtra CET score?
Yes. Candidates with lower percentiles can apply to private MBA colleges via **CMAT, ATMA, or MAT scores**, or through direct/management quota admission.

### 5. When will Maharashtra MBA CET CAP Counselling 2026 start?
CAP Counselling is expected to begin in **June–July 2026** after the result is processed and merit lists are finalized.

### 6. What is the difference between Maharashtra State and OMS (All India) cutoff?
Maharashtra State (MS) candidates have access to **85% of seats**, while OMS (Outside Maharashtra State) candidates compete for the remaining **15% seats**, which requires significantly higher percentiles.

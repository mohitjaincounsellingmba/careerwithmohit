Running a career upgrade requires choosing the right management program. For working professionals in Greater Noida, the Executive MBA / Executive PGDM offered by [Shiv Nadar University (SNU), Greater Noida](/colleges/snu-greater-noida) represents a powerful gateway to higher senior leadership positions.

In this review, we break down everything you need to know: fees, admission cutoffs, placements, pros, cons, and our honest expert verdict.

---

## 🏆 Program Overview

- **Official Course Name:** Executive MBA
- **Approximate Fees:** ₹7.50 Lakhs (Total Fees)
- **Entrance Exams Accepted:** SNU Management Admission Test (SMAT) or CAT/GMAT + Personal Interview
- **Course Duration:** 15 Months to 2 Years (Format varies by program)

---

## 📈 Eligibility & Admission Details

- **Academic Requirements:** Graduation + minimum 2-3 years of work experience.
- **Selection Process:** Admission typically relies on professional profile screening, statement of purpose (SOP), executive experience reviews, and personal interviews. Elite IIM programs also mandate standard quantitative testing (GMAT/GRE).

---

## 💼 Placement Statistics & Career Impact

- **Estimated Placements:** Great career transition support, average lateral CTC ₹12-14 LPA.
- **Career Growth:** Executive MBA degrees focus on helping candidates secure senior lateral shifts (like VP, Product Manager, or Director roles) or move from technical paths into core management.

- **Highlight:** Modern infrastructure, including cutting-edge digital labs.
- **Highlight:** Emphasis on entrepreneurship and venture building.

### Cons

- **Limitation:** Strict residential or weekend attendance requirements.

## 🔍 Our Expert Verdict

The Executive MBA program at [Shiv Nadar University (SNU), Greater Noida](/colleges/snu-greater-noida) is highly recommended for professionals based in Greater Noida who want to scale their careers without disrupting their current geographic setup. 

If you are looking for top-tier consulting placements and have 5+ years of experience, full-time residential paths are stellar. However, if you are looking to continue your full-time job, their weekend/evening classes offer outstanding return on investment.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the core focus of the SNU Executive MBA?
The program focuses heavily on Digital Transformation, Business Analytics, Venture Creation, and Strategic Management.

### What is SMAT?
SMAT is the Shiv Nadar University Management Admission Test, an internal entrance exam designed to test candidates on quantitative and analytical skills.

### Is the SNU campus residential?
SNU has a fully residential 286-acre campus, but the Executive MBA is structured to accommodate commuter and weekend candidates.

Due to heavy traffic, the official portals may experience slowdowns. You can use any of the following direct links to access your result:

### 📊 CBSE 12th Result 2026: Key Highlights & Statistics
This year's results show a significant trend in student performance. Here are the key highlights:

*   **Overall Pass Percentage:** **85.20%**
*   **Top Performing Region:** **Trivandrum** (95.62%)
*   **Girls vs. Boys:** Continuing the trend, girls have outperformed boys in the 2026 examinations.
*   **Topper List:** Following the board's policy to avoid unhealthy competition, CBSE has **not released an official topper list** or merit list this year.

### 🛠️ How to Check Your Result (Step-by-Step)
To view your scorecard, keep your **CBSE 12th Admit Card** handy and follow these steps:

1.  Visit **[results.cbse.nic.in](https://results.cbse.nic.in)**.
2.  Click on the link **"Class XII Result 2026"**.
3.  Enter your **Roll Number**, **School Number**, and **Admit Card ID**.
4.  Submit the details and view your marksheet.
5.  **Download and Print**: Ensure you take a printout for immediate use in admission processes.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I check my exam results online?
You can check results on the official website of the conducting bodies (e.g., NTA for JEE/NEET/CUET, or respective boards/testing organizations) using your application registration number.

### Are mock tests helpful in exam preparation?
Yes, attempting mock tests helps candidates build speed and accuracy, understand the exam pattern, and perform detailed analytics of strong and weak sections.

### What is the significance of negative marking in entrance exams?
Many competitive exams deduct marks for wrong answers (e.g., -1 or -0.25). Candidates should avoid guessing to maintain accuracy and prevent score drops.

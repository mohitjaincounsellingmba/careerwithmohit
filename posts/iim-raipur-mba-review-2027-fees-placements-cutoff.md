## 7. Step-by-Step Admission Process & Key Timelines

Securing a seat in the 2027–2029 MBA batch at IIM Raipur follows the centralized CAP timeline:

1. **Step 1 - CAT Registration (August – September)**: Apply for the Common Admission Test (CAT) and select IIM Raipur among your preferred choices.
2. **Step 2 - Appear for CAT (November)**: Secure sectional percentiles above 73 and an overall percentile above 94.
3. **Step 3 - CAP Shortlist Call (January)**: Receive interview shortlist based on CAT scores and initial profile screening.
4. **Step 4 - Centralized Personal Interview (February – March)**: Attend the common online CAP Personal Interview conducted by the coordinating IIM.
5. **Step 5 - Final Merit Offer (May)**: Review final composite score offers and secure your seat with the acceptance fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What makes IIM Raipur unique among Second-Generation IIMs?
IIM Raipur is distinguished by its NIRF #14 ranking, audited average placement package of ₹21.04 LPA, 200-acre world-class campus in Atal Nagar, and exceptional corporate placement records across BFSI and consulting sectors.

### 2. What is the batch profile at IIM Raipur?
The batch comprises approximately 60% engineering graduates and 40% students from commerce, economics, science, and humanities backgrounds. Nearly 65% of the students bring 12 to 36 months of corporate experience.

### 3. Does IIM Raipur provide single-room accommodation?
Yes. All enrolled full-time MBA students are provided with single-occupancy air-conditioned hostel rooms equipped with individual study spaces, balconies, and high-speed internet access.

### 4. What are the summer internship stipend figures at IIM Raipur?
The average two-month summer internship stipend at IIM Raipur stands at **₹1.39 Lakhs**, with the highest stipend offered reaching **₹3.50 Lakhs** for the two-month duration.

### 5. Can I get direct admission in IIM Raipur through management quota?
No. There is no management quota, NRI quota, or direct admission route at IIM Raipur. Admission is strictly based on CAT scores, IIM CAP personal interviews, and composite merit criteria.

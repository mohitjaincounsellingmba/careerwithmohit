Running a career upgrade requires choosing the right management program. For working professionals in Mumbai, the Executive MBA / Executive PGDM offered by [SP Jain Institute of Management and Research (SPJIMR), Mumbai](/colleges/spjimr-mumbai) represents a powerful gateway to higher senior leadership positions.

In this review, we break down everything you need to know: fees, admission cutoffs, placements, pros, cons, and our honest expert verdict.

---

## 🏆 Program Overview

- **Official Course Name:** PGEMP (Post Graduate Executive Management Programme - 21 Months)
- **Approximate Fees:** ₹11.00 Lakhs (Total Fees)
- **Entrance Exams Accepted:** Internal Test + Personal Interview (Corporate sponsorship preferred)
- **Course Duration:** 15 Months to 2 Years (Format varies by program)

---

## 📈 Eligibility & Admission Details

- **Academic Requirements:** Graduation + minimum 5 years of executive experience.
- **Selection Process:** Admission typically relies on professional profile screening, statement of purpose (SOP), executive experience reviews, and personal interviews. Elite IIM programs also mandate standard quantitative testing (GMAT/GRE).

---

## 💼 Placement Statistics & Career Impact

- **Estimated Placements:** Stellar industry transitions, average post-graduation CTC ₹24-28 LPA.
- **Career Growth:** Executive MBA degrees focus on helping candidates secure senior lateral shifts (like VP, Product Manager, or Director roles) or move from technical paths into core management.

## 📝 Student Reviews: Pros & Cons

### Pros
- **Highlight:** AACSB accredited top-tier business school.

- **Highlight:** Strong focus on ethics, value-based leadership, and mental health.

## 🔍 Our Expert Verdict

The Executive MBA program at [SP Jain Institute of Management and Research (SPJIMR), Mumbai](/colleges/spjimr-mumbai) is highly recommended for professionals based in Mumbai who want to scale their careers without disrupting their current geographic setup. 

If you are looking for top-tier consulting placements and have 5+ years of experience, full-time residential paths are stellar. However, if you are looking to continue your full-time job, their weekend/evening classes offer outstanding return on investment.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the modular structure of the PGEMP at SPJIMR?
The program is divided into 5 contact phases. Students visit the Mumbai campus for 9 consecutive days (residential) every quarter over a 21-month period.

### Do you need a CAT score to apply for SPJIMR PGEMP?
No, CAT is not mandatory. Selection is based on the applicant's academic history, executive achievements, and an internal entrance process.

### Is company sponsorship necessary?
While company-sponsored candidates are preferred and secure direct entry paths, self-sponsored candidates with strong profiles are also admitted.

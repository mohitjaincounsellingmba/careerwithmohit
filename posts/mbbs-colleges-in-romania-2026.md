**Romania** offers one of the most cost-effective entry points for earning an **EU-recognized medical degree**. With annual tuition fees starting from €6,000, English-taught 6-year MD programs, and European Union medical licensing, Romania is gaining popularity among Indian students.

---

## 🌟 Why Study MBBS in Romania in 2026?

1. **Cheapest EU Destination:** Low tuition (€6,000 - €8,500/yr) and affordable living costs (€400 - €600/month).
2. **EU Medical Registration:** Graduates can register and practice anywhere in Europe.
3. **No Difficult Entrance Test:** Most universities admit based on high school mark sheet evaluation and NEET qualification.
4. **360 ECTS Credits:** Meets 54 months academic study + 12 months internship required by NMC.

## 📋 Admission & NMC Eligibility 2026

* 10+2 with minimum 50-60% in Physics, Chemistry, Biology.
* NEET UG Qualified.
* English proficiency verification (IELTS/TOEFL or university interview).

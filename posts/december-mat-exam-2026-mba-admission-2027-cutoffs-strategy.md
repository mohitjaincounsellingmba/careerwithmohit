## How to Prepare for December MAT CBT & PBT

**The key to cracking December MAT is maximizing speed and accuracy to solve 150 questions in 120 minutes (approx. 48 seconds per question).**

- **Language Comprehension (30 Qs)**: Focus on RC passages, vocabulary, sentence correction, and critical cloze tests.
- **Intelligence & Reasoning (30 Qs)**: Practice blood relations, direction sense, linear/circular seating arrangements, and syllogisms.
- **Data Analysis & Sufficiency (30 Qs)**: Master table interpretations, bar graphs, pie charts, and data sufficiency logic.
- **Mathematical Skills (30 Qs)**: Drill arithmetic formulas (percentages, profit & loss, simple & compound interest, time & work).

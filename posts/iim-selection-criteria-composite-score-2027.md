## How is the IIM Composite Score Calculated for 2027 Admissions?

**The IIM composite score is calculated using a multi-factor formula combining scaled CAT percentile (40–55%), Class 10/12 board marks (15–20%), graduation percentage (10–15%), work experience (5–10%), and diversity points (up to 5%).** Shortlisting for the Personal Interview (PI) and Analytical Writing Test (AWT) depends strictly on this aggregate score rather than exam percentile alone.

### Standard IIM Composite Weightage Breakdown:
- **CAT Exam Score**: 40% – 55%
- **Class 10th Board Marks**: 10% – 15%
- **Class 12th Board Marks**: 10% – 15%
- **Graduation Academic Profile**: 5% – 10%
- **Work Experience (24–36 Months)**: 5% – 10%
- **Gender & Academic Diversity**: 3% – 5%

## Safe CAT Percentiles: Engineers vs Non-Engineers (General Category)

**General Engineering Males (GEM) require a minimum 99.4+ CAT percentile to guarantee interview calls from top IIMs, whereas Non-Engineering candidates with consistent 85%+ academic profiles can secure calls at 96.5–98.0+ percentile.**

- **General Engineering Male (GEM)**: 99.40+ Percentile
- **General Engineering Female (GEF)**: 98.20+ Percentile
- **Non-Engineering Male (Commerce/Arts/Science)**: 97.50+ Percentile
- **Non-Engineering Female**: 96.00+ Percentile

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the duration of the TOEFL iBT exam?
The TOEFL iBT exam is approximately 2 hours long. It includes 4 sections: Reading, Listening, Speaking, and Writing.

### What is the scoring scale for TOEFL iBT?
Each of the 4 sections is scored out of 30, making the total score out of 120. A score of 100+ is generally considered good for top global university admission.

### Is a calculator or scratch paper allowed in TOEFL?
Scratch paper and a pencil are provided by the test center for taking notes during all sections of the TOEFL exam.

Hungary is one of Central Europe's **most affordable and strategically located study destinations** for Indian students. With tuition fees as low as ₹4.2 Lakhs/year, membership in the Schengen Zone, and the prestigious **Stipendium Hungaricum Scholarship** offering fully-funded places for international students, Hungary provides exceptional value for Indian students seeking a European education in 2026.

---

## 🎓 Why Study in Hungary in 2026?

- **Extremely Affordable:** Fees from ₹4.2–₹7 Lakhs/year (some of the lowest in Europe)
- **Schengen Zone Member:** Travel freely across 27 European countries
- **Stipendium Hungaricum Scholarship:** Fully-funded scholarships for international students (covers tuition, housing, health insurance + monthly stipend)
- **English-Taught Programs:** Wide range of English-medium degrees
- **Central Europe's Rising Hub:** Budapest is a major European financial and startup center
- **High Cultural Value:** Budapest — the "Paris of the East" — offers an incredible student lifestyle

## 🏅 Stipendium Hungaricum Scholarship 2026

The Hungarian Government's flagship scholarship for international students:

| Component | Benefit |
|:---|:---|
| Tuition Fee | 100% waiver |
| Monthly Stipend | HUF 40,000 (approx. ₹9,000) for UG; HUF 140,000 (approx. ₹32,000) for PG |
| Accommodation | University dormitory provided |
| Health Insurance | Covered |
| Eligible Programs | Bachelor's, Master's, PhD, Non-Degree |

**Application Process for Indian Students:**
1. Apply at Tempus Public Foundation portal (stipendiumhungaricum.hu)
2. Nominate via Indian Embassy/High Commission
3. Indian students are among the **highest applicants** — very competitive

## 🛂 Hungary Student Visa & Residence

- **Application:** Hungarian Embassy / Consulate in India (Mumbai, New Delhi)
- **Documents:** Acceptance letter, financial proof, health insurance, accommodation proof
- **Financial Proof:** EUR 285/month (approx. ₹25,000/month)
- **Processing Time:** 4–8 weeks
- **Work Rights:** 20 hours/week during semester, full-time during holidays

Running a career upgrade requires choosing the right management program. For working professionals in Delhi, the Executive MBA / Executive PGDM offered by [Department of Management Studies (DMS), IIT Delhi](/colleges/iit-delhi) represents a powerful gateway to higher senior leadership positions.

In this review, we break down everything you need to know: fees, admission cutoffs, placements, pros, cons, and our honest expert verdict.

---

## 🏆 Program Overview

- **Official Course Name:** Executive MBA (Technology Management)
- **Approximate Fees:** ₹12.00 Lakhs (Total Program Fees)
- **Entrance Exams Accepted:** Written Test + Personal Interview (conducted on-campus)
- **Course Duration:** 15 Months to 2 Years (Format varies by program)

---

## 📈 Eligibility & Admission Details

- **Academic Requirements:** Graduation in Engineering/Technology/Physical Sciences with min 60% + minimum 2 years of work experience.
- **Selection Process:** Admission typically relies on professional profile screening, statement of purpose (SOP), executive experience reviews, and personal interviews. Elite IIM programs also mandate standard quantitative testing (GMAT/GRE).

---

## 💼 Placement Statistics & Career Impact

- **Estimated Placements:** Stellar industry networking, lateral salary packages average ₹22-25 LPA.
- **Career Growth:** Executive MBA degrees focus on helping candidates secure senior lateral shifts (like VP, Product Manager, or Director roles) or move from technical paths into core management.

---

## 📝 Student Reviews: Pros & Cons

### Pros
- **Highlight:** World-class tech-management curriculum integrating AI, analytics, and strategy.
- **Highlight:** Access to IIT Delhi’s sprawling campus, laboratories, and incubator ecosystem.
- **Highlight:** Strong brand reputation globally in technical and management circles.

### Cons
- **Limitation:** Eligibility is restricted primarily to science, engineering, and tech graduates.
- **Limitation:** The academic rigor is highly demanding, requiring extensive quantitative project work.

---

## 🔍 Our Expert Verdict

The Executive MBA program at [Department of Management Studies (DMS), IIT Delhi](/colleges/iit-delhi) is highly recommended for professionals based in Delhi who want to scale their careers without disrupting their current geographic setup. 

If you are looking for top-tier consulting placements and have 5+ years of experience, full-time residential paths are stellar. However, if you are looking to continue your full-time job, their weekend/evening classes offer outstanding return on investment.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can non-engineers apply for the DMS IIT Delhi Executive MBA?
The program accepts candidates with a bachelor's degree in Engineering, Technology, Physical Sciences, or other allied sciences. Pure humanities or commerce graduates without a quantitative background may not meet eligibility requirements.

### What is the duration of the DMS IIT Delhi Executive MBA?
The program is structured over 3 years, comprising 9 terms, allowing working managers to balance academics with professional duties.

### Are there placements for IIT Delhi Executive MBA?
While primarily a sponsored/part-time program, the corporate relations team provides career support and networking opportunities, leading to high-profile lateral shifts.

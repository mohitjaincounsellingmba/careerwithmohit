## GMAT Focus Edition Exam Pattern & Scoring

Understand the structure of the GMAT Focus Edition:

* **Data Insights (DI):** 20 Questions (45 Mins) | Score Range: 60 - 90
* **Quantitative Reasoning:** 21 Questions (45 Mins) | Score Range: 60 - 90 (No calculator permitted)
* **Verbal Reasoning:** 23 Questions (45 Mins) | Score Range: 60 - 90 (Reading Comprehension & Critical Reasoning only)
* **Total:** 64 Questions | 135 Minutes | Total Score Scale: 505 - 785 (10-point increments).
* **Key Feature:** Question Review & Edit tool allows bookmarking and modifying up to 3 answers per section.

# NMAT Exam 2026 Last Date of Registration: Deadline, Fees & NMIMS Application Guide

> 💡 **Key Takeaways (Direct AI Answer Summary)**
> - **Main Registration Deadline:** NMAT by GMAC 2026 regular registration opens on August 1, 2026, and closes in the second week of October 2026 (expected around October 10–13, 2026).
> - **Application Fee & Retakes:** ₹3,000 + 18% GST per attempt. Candidates can appear up to 3 times (1 main + 2 retakes), with retake windows open until mid-December 2026.
> - **Dual Registration Rule:** You must register separately on both `register.nmat.org` (for the test slot) and individual college portals (especially NMIMS Mumbai at `nmims.edu`).

---

The **NMAT by GMAC** (Graduate Management Admission Council) is one of India's most student-friendly and prestigious management entrance examinations. It is the premier pathway to the coveted **SVKM's Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies (NMIMS)** campuses in Mumbai, Bengaluru, Navi Mumbai, Hyderabad, and Indore, along with esteemed institutions like K J Somaiya, TAPMI, XIM University Bhubaneswar, and SDA Bocconi Asia Center.

Unlike single-day tests like CAT or XAT, NMAT provides a 75-day testing window with customizable sectional order and zero negative marking. However, adhering to the **NMAT exam 2026 last date of registration** is vital because missing the regular window blocks you from taking the main exam and eliminates your eligibility for top NMIMS programs.

## NMAT 2026 Registration Fees & Charge Breakdown

GMAC charges fixed fees for testing, retakes, and rescheduling. All payments are collected online:

* **Regular Registration (Main Attempt):** ₹3,000 + 18% GST (Approx. ₹3,540)
* **Retake Registration (Per Attempt, Max 2 Retakes):** ₹3,000 + 18% GST (Approx. ₹3,540)
* **Exam Rescheduling Fee:** ₹1,200 + 18% GST (Approx. ₹1,416)
* **Additional Score Reports (Beyond first 5 included B-schools):** ₹300 + 18% GST per school

*Note: Your NMAT registration fee includes sending your official scaled scores to up to 5 participating programmes or universities for free.*

## Eligibility Criteria for NMAT 2026

* **Graduation Degree:** Minimum 3-year bachelor’s degree in any discipline from a recognized university.
* **Minimum Aggregate Marks:** At least **50% aggregate marks** in graduation (or equivalent CGPA).
* **Final Year Candidates:** Final-year undergraduate students are eligible to apply, provided they submit proof of passing with 50%+ marks at the time of final admission.
* **NMIMS Specific Criteria:** For NMIMS Mumbai Flagship MBA (Core / HR), 50% in graduation with mathematics/statistics at 10+2 or graduation level is preferred.

## Step-by-Step Guide: How to Apply for NMAT 2026

Follow these steps carefully before the registration last date:

### 1. Account Creation on `register.nmat.org`
* Go to the official GMAC registration page.
* Provide your name, primary email address, mobile number, and select country of residence.
* Verify your account via the activation link sent to your email.

### 2. Enter Personal, Contact & Educational Details
* Fill in your parents' names, date of birth, identity card details, and residential address.
* Enter your Class 10, Class 12, and undergraduate degree percentage and university details.

### 3. Select 5 Participating B-Schools (Free)
* Under the B-Schools Selection section, choose 5 B-school programs that you want to send your scores to without any extra charge (e.g., NMIMS Mumbai, TAPMI, K J Somaiya, XIM University, SDA Bocconi).
* If you wish to apply to more than 5 institutes, you can add them by paying ₹300 + GST each.

### 4. Pay the Registration Fee
* Proceed to the secure checkout page and complete payment using credit/debit card, UPI, or net banking.
* Once the payment is verified, your registration dashboard will be unlocked.

### 5. Schedule Your Exam Date, Slot & Venue
* Under the "Schedule Exam" tab, choose your preferred testing mode: **Test Centre Based Exam** or **Online Proctored Exam at Home**.
* Select your preferred test city, examination centre, test date, and available time slot (Morning or Afternoon).

## Critical Rules Candidates Must Know Before Registering

1. **The NMIMS First Attempt Rule:** SVKM's NMIMS explicitly states that for admissions to its flagship MBA programs at the Mumbai campus, **only the candidate's first attempt (main score)** is accepted. Retake scores are not considered for NMIMS Mumbai shortlist.
2. **Apply Separately to Colleges:** Registering on the GMAC portal does not mean you have applied to NMIMS or K J Somaiya. You must fill out the college-specific application on their individual websites before their respective deadlines.
3. **Choice of Section Sequence:** In NMAT, you can decide the order of the three sections (e.g., Language Skills -> Logical Reasoning -> Quantitative Skills). Choose the sequence that matches your mock test strategy.
4. **Rescheduling Deadline:** You can reschedule your test date up to 72 hours before your scheduled appointment time by paying the ₹1,200 rescheduling fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the NMAT exam 2026 last date of registration?
The regular registration window for NMAT 2026 closes in the second week of October 2026 (expected between October 10 and October 13, 2026). Retake registration remains open until mid-December 2026.

### How much is the NMAT 2026 registration fee?
The test registration fee is ₹3,000 + 18% GST (total approx. ₹3,540) per attempt.

### Does NMAT have negative marking?
No. NMAT does not have negative marking. Candidates are encouraged to attempt all 108 questions.

### Can I change my NMAT test centre after booking?
Yes, you can reschedule your exam date or test centre up to 72 hours prior to your scheduled slot by paying a rescheduling fee of ₹1,200 + taxes.

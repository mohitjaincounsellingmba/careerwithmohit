## 4. What is the MU-BAAT Exam and Selection Criteria?

**The MU-BAAT (Masters' Union Business Aptitude and Admissions Test) is a 90-minute online proctored entrance exam evaluating Data Interpretation & Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, Verbal Ability, and a mandatory Caselet Assessment assessing strategic business thinking.**

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
|                     MU-BAAT EXAM STRUCTURE                  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Section                                         | Focus     |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Section 1: Quantitative Aptitude                | Logic, Math|
| Section 2: Data Interpretation & Reasoning      | Charts, Data|
| Section 3: Verbal Skills & Reading              | Comprehension|
| Section 4: Caselet Analysis (Subjective Prompt) | Business Strategy|
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Profile Shortlisting Factors:
- **Aptitude Score:** Performance in MU-BAAT (or 88+ percentile in CAT / 680+ in GMAT).
- **Video Essay / Creative Pitch:** Applicants submit a 1-minute video answering why they want to join Masters' Union and what unique perspective they bring.
- **Personal Interview:** Conducted by a panel of startup founders, venture capitalists, and CXOs focusing on problem-solving ability, leadership drive, and cultural agility.

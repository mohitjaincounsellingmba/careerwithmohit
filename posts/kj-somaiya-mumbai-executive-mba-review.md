Running a career upgrade requires choosing the right management program. For working professionals in Mumbai, the Executive MBA / Executive PGDM offered by [K. J. Somaiya Institute of Management, Mumbai](/colleges/k-j-somaiya-institute-of-management) represents a powerful gateway to higher senior leadership positions.

In this review, we break down everything you need to know: fees, admission cutoffs, placements, pros, cons, and our honest expert verdict.

---

## 🏆 Program Overview

- **Official Course Name:** MBA Executive (15-Month Program)
- **Approximate Fees:** ₹11.80 Lakhs (Total Fees)
- **Entrance Exams Accepted:** CAT/XAT/GMAT/NMAT/CMAT + Personal Interview
- **Course Duration:** 15 Months to 2 Years (Format varies by program)

---

## 📈 Eligibility & Admission Details

- **Academic Requirements:** Bachelor's degree with min 50% + minimum 5 years of full-time professional experience.
- **Selection Process:** Admission typically relies on professional profile screening, statement of purpose (SOP), executive experience reviews, and personal interviews. Elite IIM programs also mandate standard quantitative testing (GMAT/GRE).

---

## 💼 Placement Statistics & Career Impact

- **Estimated Placements:** Active placement support, average package is ₹12.50 LPA.
- **Career Growth:** Executive MBA degrees focus on helping candidates secure senior lateral shifts (like VP, Product Manager, or Director roles) or move from technical paths into core management.

---

## 📝 Student Reviews: Pros & Cons

### Pros
- **Highlight:** Lush 50-acre campus in the middle of Mumbai.
- **Highlight:** AMBA accredited program structure.
- **Highlight:** Strong focus on digital transformation, entrepreneurship, and ethics.

### Cons
- **Limitation:** 15-month program requires candidates to leave their job temporarily.
- **Limitation:** Relatively high cost compared to JBIMS.

---

## 🔍 Our Expert Verdict

The Executive MBA program at [K. J. Somaiya Institute of Management, Mumbai](/colleges/k-j-somaiya-institute-of-management) is highly recommended for professionals based in Mumbai who want to scale their careers without disrupting their current geographic setup. 

If you are looking for top-tier consulting placements and have 5+ years of experience, full-time residential paths are stellar. However, if you are looking to continue your full-time job, their weekend/evening classes offer outstanding return on investment.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is the KJ Somaiya Executive MBA a residential course?
Yes, it is a full-time 15-month program, and students are encouraged to reside in the campus hostels.

### What is the minimum GMAT score accepted?
There is no fixed minimum score, but candidates scoring above 600 in GMAT have high selection odds.

### Does the program include a global immersion module?
Yes, the program integrates a mandatory/optional international immersion trip to expose students to global supply chains.

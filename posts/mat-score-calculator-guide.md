## 📊 MAT 2026 Exam Pattern & Scoring Scheme

The MAT exam comprises **200 questions** distributed equally across 5 sections. However, the overall **Composite Score** is based on only **4 sections (160 questions)**. 

| Section Name | Questions | Marks | Contribution to Composite Score |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Language Comprehension (LC)** | 40 | 40 | **Yes** (Scaled 0-200) |
| **Intelligence & Critical Reasoning (ICR)** | 40 | 40 | **Yes** (Scaled 0-200) |
| **Mathematical Skills (MS)** | 40 | 40 | **Yes** (Scaled 0-200) |
| **Data Analysis & Sufficiency (DA)** | 40 | 40 | **Yes** (Scaled 0-200) |
| **Indian & Global Environment (IGE)** | 40 | 40 | **No** (Reported Separately) |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** | **4 Sections contribute to Composite (Out of 800)** |

### **Scoring Rules:**
*   **Correct Answer:** +1.00 Mark
*   **Incorrect Answer:** -0.25 Mark
*   **Unattempted Question:** 0.00 Mark

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q: How is the MAT composite score calculated?**  
**A:** AIMA takes the raw score from Language Comprehension, Mathematical Skills, Data Analysis, and Intelligence & Critical Reasoning, scales each to a range of 0 to 100, and converts the total scaled score into a composite score out of 800.

**Q: Does a high score in Indian & Global Environment (IGE) help in shortlisting?**  
**A:** The IGE score is printed on the scorecard but is not factored into the composite score out of 800. Ninety-nine percent of colleges do not consider it, though a few B-schools might look at it as a tie-breaker.

**Q: When will the MAT May 2026 results be declared?**  
**A:** Results for all MAT modes (PBT, CBT, IBT) are generally declared in the **last week of June 2026** on AIMA's portal: [mat.aima.in](https://mat.aima.in).

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I check my exam results online?
You can check results on the official website of the conducting bodies (e.g., NTA for JEE/NEET/CUET, or respective boards/testing organizations) using your application registration number.

### Are mock tests helpful in exam preparation?
Yes, attempting mock tests helps candidates build speed and accuracy, understand the exam pattern, and perform detailed analytics of strong and weak sections.

### What is the significance of negative marking in entrance exams?
Many competitive exams deduct marks for wrong answers (e.g., -1 or -0.25). Candidates should avoid guessing to maintain accuracy and prevent score drops.

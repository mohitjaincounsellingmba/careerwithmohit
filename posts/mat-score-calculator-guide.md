# How to Use the MAT May 2026 Score Calculator

The **MAT May 2026 Score Calculator** helps you instantly compute your composite score (out of 800) and estimate the percentile you can expect for MBA admissions. Follow these simple steps:

1. **Select a Section** – Choose one of the four scoring sections (LC, MS, DA, ICR). The IGE section is informational only.
2. **Enter Correct Answers** – Input the number of questions you answered correctly for the section.
3. **Enter Wrong Answers** – Input the number of questions you answered incorrectly (‑0.25 marks each).
4. **Repeat for All Sections** – Fill in LC, MS, DA, and ICR. IGE is optional.
5. **Unlock Full Results** – Provide your name/email to view the detailed composite score, percentile, and a list of top MBA colleges that accept your MAT score.

---

## Understanding the Scoring Formula

- **Correct Answer:** +1 point
- **Wrong Answer:** ‑0.25 points
- **Unattempted:** 0 points
- Each section’s raw score is scaled to a maximum of **200**, giving a total composite maximum of **800**.

> **Tip:** A composite score of **600+** (≈97th percentile) is considered strong for top MBA colleges like **TAPMI, MDI, and JBIMS**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q:** *What is a good MAT score for top colleges?*  
**A:** Aim for **600+** (≈97 %ile) for tier‑1 institutes. Scores **530‑600** (90‑97 %ile) are solid for mid‑tier schools.

**Q:** *Does the IGE section affect my score?*  
**A:** No, IGE is not included in the composite score. Some colleges may still consider it, so check their criteria.

**Q:** *When are the results announced?*  
**A:** Results are typically released **10‑15 days** after the exam, around the **last week of June 2026**.

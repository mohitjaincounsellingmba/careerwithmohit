Running a career upgrade requires choosing the right management program. For working professionals in Ghaziabad, the Executive MBA / Executive PGDM offered by [Institute of Management Technology (IMT), Ghaziabad](/colleges/imt-ghaziabad) represents a powerful gateway to higher senior leadership positions.

In this review, we break down everything you need to know: fees, admission cutoffs, placements, pros, cons, and our honest expert verdict.

---

## 🏆 Program Overview

- **Official Course Name:** PGDM Executive (15-Month Accelerated)
- **Approximate Fees:** ₹17.50 Lakhs (Total Fees)
- **Entrance Exams Accepted:** CAT/XAT/GMAT + Personal Interview
- **Course Duration:** 15 Months to 2 Years (Format varies by program)

---

## 📈 Eligibility & Admission Details

- **Academic Requirements:** Bachelor's degree with min 50% + minimum 5 years of executive experience.
- **Selection Process:** Admission typically relies on professional profile screening, statement of purpose (SOP), executive experience reviews, and personal interviews. Elite IIM programs also mandate standard quantitative testing (GMAT/GRE).

---

## 💼 Placement Statistics & Career Impact

- **Estimated Placements:** Outstanding executive placement drives, average CTC is ₹21.00 LPA.
- **Career Growth:** Executive MBA degrees focus on helping candidates secure senior lateral shifts (like VP, Product Manager, or Director roles) or move from technical paths into core management.

---

## 📝 Student Reviews: Pros & Cons

### Pros
- **Highlight:** AMBA accredited program with premium brand reputation.
- **Highlight:** Top-notch campus infrastructure and highly qualified faculty.
- **Highlight:** Stellar placements with top consulting and FMCG firms.

### Cons
- **Limitation:** High program cost.
- **Limitation:** 15-month full-time residency requires a complete break from work.

---

## 🔍 Our Expert Verdict

The Executive MBA program at [Institute of Management Technology (IMT), Ghaziabad](/colleges/imt-ghaziabad) is highly recommended for professionals based in Ghaziabad who want to scale their careers without disrupting their current geographic setup. 

If you are looking for top-tier consulting placements and have 5+ years of experience, full-time residential paths are stellar. However, if you are looking to continue your full-time job, their weekend/evening classes offer outstanding return on investment.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Does IMT Ghaziabad offer placement for Executive PGDM?
Yes, IMT has a highly active dedicated placement cell that secures top corporate placements for the executive batch, yielding an average CTC of around ₹20-22 LPA.

### Is the IMT Ghaziabad Executive program residential?
Yes, it is a full-time residential program for 12 months, followed by 3 months of a live field project.

### What are the accepted entrance test scores?
IMT accepts valid scores from CAT, XAT, and GMAT.

## 1. Who Will Conduct the CAT 2026 Exam?

The **Indian Institute of Management (IIM) Indore** is the official convening and conducting body for **CAT 2026**.

Each year, the responsibility of hosting and administering the Common Admission Test rotates among the **six older IIMs** (known as the *BLACKI + Indore* core group: [IIM Ahmedabad](/colleges/iim-ahmedabad), [IIM Bangalore](/colleges/iim-bangalore), [IIM Calcutta](/colleges/iim-calcutta), IIM Lucknow, IIM Kozhikode, and IIM Indore). 

Following the established rotation cycle—where [IIM Calcutta](/colleges/iim-calcutta) convened CAT 2024 and IIM Kozhikode convened CAT 2025—**IIM Indore** takes charge of the examination for the 2026–27 admission cycle.

### Key Highlights of CAT 2026 Convening Body
* **Conducting Institute:** Indian Institute of Management (IIM) Indore
* **Expected Convening Convenor:** Appointed Senior Faculty / Admissions Chair, IIM Indore
* **Official CAT Portal:** [iimcat.ac.in](https://iimcat.ac.in)
* **Exam Mode:** Computer-Based Test (CBT) across 3 slots
* **Expected Total Aspirants:** Over 3.2 Lakh candidates

## 3. What Are the Roles & Responsibilities of the Convening IIM?

While CAT is a collective entrance test accepted across all 21 IIMs, the **convening IIM** holds exclusive responsibility for managing the end-to-end examination lifecycle:

1. **Official Notification Release:** Publishing the official CAT 2026 advertisement, eligibility criteria, reservation guidelines, and media announcements by **late July 2026**.
2. **Registration & Helpdesk Management:** Operating the registration portal (`iimcat.ac.in`), handling student grievances, application corrections, and test center allocations.
3. **Question Paper & Pattern Design:** Curating the question bank for all three exam slots, ensuring equivalence in testing standards across Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension (VARC), Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning (DILR), and Quantitative Aptitude (QA).
4. **Exam Administration:** Coordinating with national testing partners (such as TCS iON) to conduct seamless, tamper-proof Computer-Based Testing across 170+ cities.
5. **Normalization & Result Declaration:** Executing the statistically rigorous equating and normalization process across slots and releasing official percentiles and scorecards by **December 2026 / January 2027**.

## 5. What to Expect from an IIM Indore Convened CAT Paper?

Historically, when **IIM Indore** convened CAT in **2020** and **2014**, the papers exhibited distinct characteristics that candidates should keep in mind while preparing their mock test strategies:

### A. Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension (VARC)
* **Passage Diversity:** IIM Indore tends to include well-structured Reading Comprehension passages covering literature, social sciences, humanities, and economics.
* **Inference-Driven Questions:** Expect questions testing author tone, underlying assumptions, and critical inference rather than mere fact-retrieval.
* **Verbal Logic Focus:** Para-jumbles and para-summaries emphasize logical connectivity and syntax flow.

### B. Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning (DILR)
* **Hybrid LR-DI Sets:** Sets often combine numerical calculations with logical constraints (e.g., matrix-based arrangements, reasoning with percentages or averages).
* **Selection Strategy:** Identifying the two most solvable sets in the first 5 minutes remains crucial for scoring above the 98th percentile.

### C. Quantitative Aptitude (QA)
* **Arithmetic & Algebra Dominance:** Arithmetic (Profit & Loss, Ratios, Time & Work) and Algebra typically constitute nearly **60–65%** of the quant section.
* **Accuracy Over Speed:** While calculations are generally manageable, conceptual clarity is rewarded over rote formula substitution.

1. **Start Sectional Timed Practice Early:** Since you cannot switch sections during the 120-minute test, build focus for 40-minute individual bursts.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Who is conducting the CAT 2026 exam?
The **Indian Institute of Management (IIM) Indore** is the official convening and conducting body for the **CAT 2026 exam**, following the rotational policy among the top six older IIMs.

### What is the expected exam date for CAT 2026?
CAT 2026 is scheduled to be conducted on **Sunday, November 29, 2026** (the last Sunday of November), across over 170 exam cities in India in three separate slots.

### How are convening IIMs selected for conducting the CAT exam?
The responsibility of convening CAT rotates annually among the **six older IIMs**: [IIM Ahmedabad](/colleges/iim-ahmedabad), [IIM Bangalore](/colleges/iim-bangalore), IIM Calcutta, IIM Lucknow, IIM Kozhikode, and IIM Indore.

### What is the official website for CAT 2026 registration?
The official website for CAT 2026 registration, admit card download, official notifications, and scorecard download is **[iimcat.ac.in](https://iimcat.ac.in)**.

### Will the syllabus or pattern change for CAT 2026 under IIM Indore?
No major structural deviation is expected. CAT 2026 is expected to retain the 66-question, 120-minute CBT pattern across three sections: VARC (24 questions), DILR (20 questions), and QA (22 questions).

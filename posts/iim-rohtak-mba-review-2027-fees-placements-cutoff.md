## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Does IIM Rohtak accept IIM CAP interview scores?
No. IIM Rohtak conducts its own independent admission process and does not participate in the centralized IIM Common Admission Process (CAP). Candidates must register separately on the official IIM Rohtak website.

### 2. What is the batch diversity like at IIM Rohtak?
IIM Rohtak leads all premier business schools in India with over 70% female student representation. The cohort features an equitable mix of engineering, commerce, arts, and science graduates.

### 3. What is the average summer internship stipend at IIM Rohtak?
The average two-month summer internship stipend at IIM Rohtak stands at **₹1.05 Lakhs**, with the highest domestic stipend reaching **₹2.50 Lakhs** for the two-month internship.

### 4. What are the major recruiting sectors at IIM Rohtak?
The dominant recruiting sector at IIM Rohtak is Strategy & Management Consulting (45% of offers), followed by BFSI & FinTech (25%), IT/ITES & Product Management (15%), and Manufacturing & Operations (9%).

### 5. Can I get direct admission in IIM Rohtak without CAT?
No. Direct admission, management quota, or sponsored seats do not exist at IIM Rohtak. Admission is strictly based on CAT merit, online personal interview performance, and academic diversity score.

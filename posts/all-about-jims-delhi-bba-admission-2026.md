[Jagan Institute of Management Studies](/colleges/jagan-institute-of-management-studies) (JIMS) is a top-tier group of institutions in Delhi, primarily known for its flagship campuses in Rohini and Kalkaji. Affiliated with Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU), JIMS has earned a reputation for its academic rigor and excellent placement support for BBA aspirants in 2026.

## 🏛️ Why Choose JIMS Delhi for BBA in 2026?
JIMS offers a practical approach to management education, focusing on skill development and industry readiness.
- **Top Rankings:** Consistently ranked among the top B-Schools in India by NIRF and other major ranking bodies.
- **Industry Exposure:** Regular industrial visits, guest lectures from corporate leaders, and live projects.
- **Modern Infrastructure:** High-tech classrooms, well-stocked libraries, and vibrant campus life.

## 💰 Latest Fee Structure (2026)
As a self-financing institute under IP University, JIMS offers a competitive fee structure. For 2026:
- **Annual Tuition Fee:** ₹1,00,000 – ₹1,10,000 per year.
- **Note:** This is inclusive of university charges and student activity fees.

## 🚀 Placement Process & Statistics 2026
JIMS has a robust placement cell that actively connects students with top MNCs and emerging startups.
- **Average Package:** ₹4.5 LPA
- **Highest Package:** ₹10 LPA (for BBA).
- **Top Recruiters:** Amazon, Deloitte, EY, KPMG, HCL, ICICI Bank, Lenskart.
- **Placement Process:** Includes resume building workshops, mock interviews, and final recruitment drives on campus.

## 📝 Admission Process 2026 (IPU CET)
Admission to JIMS (Rohini & Kalkaji) is through the **IPU CET (Indraprastha University Common Entrance Test)**.
- **Exam Pattern:** General English, Logical Reasoning, Management Aptitude, and General Awareness.
- **Selection:** Based strictly on the rank obtained in the IPU CET centralized counseling.
- **Note:** Higher ranks (typically within the top 2000) are required for the Rohini Sector-5 campus.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Q1. Which JIMS campus is better for BBA — Rohini or Kalkaji?**
Both campuses are highly reputed. Rohini (Sector-5) is the flagship campus with slightly higher cutoffs, while Kalkaji is excellent for its South Delhi location and corporate connections.

**Q2. Does JIMS offer BBA in shifts?**
Yes, both Rohini and Kalkaji campuses usually operate in two shifts: First Shift (Morning) and Second Shift (Afternoon).

**Q3. Is there a hostel facility at JIMS?**
JIMS provides assistance and tie-ups for hostel and PG accommodations for outstation students near its campuses.

## 7. Step-by-Step Admission Process & Key Timelines

Securing admission into the 2027–2029 MBA batch at IIM Sirmaur follows the standard CAP roadmap:

1. **Step 1 - Register for CAT (August – September)**: Apply for the Common Admission Test and select IIM Sirmaur and your preferred programs (MBA, MBA-T&HM).
2. **Step 2 - Appear for CAT (November)**: Secure qualifying sectional and overall percentiles.
3. **Step 3 - CAP Shortlist Call (January)**: Receive IIM CAP interview shortlist based on CAT scores and profile parameters.
4. **Step 4 - Centralized Personal Interview (February – March)**: Attend the common online CAP Personal Interview conducted by the coordinating IIM.
5. **Step 5 - Final Merit Offer (May)**: Check composite score merit lists and pay the seat confirmation fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the fee structure for the MBA program at IIM Sirmaur?
The total course fee for the two-year full-time MBA at IIM Sirmaur is approximately **₹16.00 Lakhs**, covering tuition, single-occupancy hostel accommodations, digital library access, and course case studies.

### 2. What is unique about IIM Sirmaur's MBA-T&HM program?
IIM Sirmaur is the first Indian Institute of Management to offer a specialized Master of Business Administration in Tourism & Hospitality Management (MBA-T&HM), preparing graduates for leadership roles in travel technology, luxury hospitality, and tourism consulting.

### 3. Does IIM Sirmaur have its own permanent campus?
Yes. IIM Sirmaur operates from its permanent 210-acre green campus located in Dhaula Kuan, Paonta Sahib, Sirmaur district, Himachal Pradesh.

### 4. What are the summer internship stipend figures at IIM Sirmaur?
The average two-month summer internship stipend at IIM Sirmaur stands at **₹70,000**, with the highest stipend offered exceeding **₹2.00 Lakhs** for the two-month period.

### 5. Can I get direct admission in IIM Sirmaur without CAT?
No. Direct admission, management quota, or NRI quota seats do not exist at IIM Sirmaur. Admission is strictly based on CAT merit, IIM CAP interview evaluation, and profile composite scores.

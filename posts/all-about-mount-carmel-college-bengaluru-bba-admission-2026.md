Mount Carmel College (MCC), Bengaluru, is one of the most prestigious autonomous colleges for women in India. Known for its strong academic foundation and holistic development, MCC is a top destination for BBA aspirants in 2026.

## 🏛️ Why Choose Mount Carmel College in 2026?
MCC offers a BBA program that is highly industry-oriented, with various specializations like Finance, Marketing, and Human Resources.
- **Women Empowerment:** A supportive environment focused on developing female leaders in the corporate world.
- **Academic Excellence:** Consistently ranked among the top colleges in India by major ranking bodies.
- **Vibrant Campus:** Known for its diverse student body and active clubs/societies.

## 💰 Latest Fee Structure (2026)
As an autonomous college affiliated with Bengaluru City University, the fees are relatively moderate. For 2026:
- **Annual Tuition Fee:** ₹1,50,000 – ₹1,80,000 per year.
- **Note:** Fees for outstation students may vary slightly from those for students from Karnataka.

## 🚀 Placement Process & Statistics 2026
MCC has a dedicated Placement Cell that works with top global and national firms to provide internships and final placements.
- **Average Package:** ₹5.2 LPA
- **Highest Package:** ₹11 LPA
- **Top Recruiters:** Goldman Sachs, Deloitte, EY, KPMG, Amazon, Target, Cisco.
- **Placement Process:** Includes skill development workshops, pre-placement talks, and final recruitment drives.

## 📝 Admission Process 2026 (Entrance & Interview)
Admission to the BBA program at MCC is typically through an entrance process.
- **Stage 1: Entrance Test:** Tests basic aptitude, logical reasoning, and English.
- **Stage 2: Personal Interview:** Shortlisted candidates are called for an interview with the faculty.
- **Selection Criteria:** Merit is based on Entrance Score + Interview Performance + Class 12 Marks.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Q1. Is Mount Carmel College only for girls?**
Yes, Mount Carmel College is a women-only institution for its undergraduate programs.

**Q2. Does MCC have a hostel facility?**
Yes, MCC provides hostel facilities for outstation students, but seats are limited and usually allotted on a merit-cum-first-come-first-served basis.

**Q3. Can I get direct admission in MCC for BBA?**
No, all students must qualify for the entrance test and interview to secure a seat in the BBA program.

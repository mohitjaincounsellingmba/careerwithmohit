## How is the MAT Composite Score Calculated out of 800?

**The MAT composite score is calculated using the raw scores from the first four sections (120 questions), which are statistically scaled onto a standardized composite scale of 199 to 801 (commonly referenced as out of 800).** 

- **Core Raw Score (120 Marks)** = (Correct in 4 Sections × 1.00) − (Incorrect in 4 Sections × 0.25)
- **Scaled Composite Score** = Calibrated on historical normalization models benchmarked by AIMA.
- **Economic & Business Environment**: Handled as an independent score card metric, giving students the freedom to attempt GK without risking their core 800 score.

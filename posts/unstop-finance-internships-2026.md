### **Key Finance Roles Available on Unstop**

When browsing finance internships on Unstop, you will encounter several specialized job profiles. Understanding these roles can help you tailor your resume effectively:

1. **Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) Intern**:
   - Focuses on budgeting, forecasting, variance analysis, and management reporting.
   - Highly suitable for MBA Finance students and analytical BBA/B.Com graduates.

2. **Investment Banking & Valuation Intern**:
   - Involves building financial models, preparing pitch books, company valuations (DCF, Relative Valuation), and market research.
   - Ideal for CFA candidates and top-tier business school students.

3. **Internal Audit & Statutory Compliance Intern**:
   - Focuses on examining internal controls, financial records, taxation compliance, and risk management.
   - Primarily targeted at CA Inter / CA Articleship students and M.Com graduates.

4. **Accounting & Bookkeeping Intern**:
   - Covers day-to-day accounting operations, general ledger reconciliation, GST/TDS filing, and ERP software management (Tally, SAP, Zoho Books).
   - Perfect for B.Com and commerce undergraduates looking for practical corporate exposure.

5. **FinTech & Venture Capital Analyst Intern**:
   - Blends financial analysis with technology, assessing fintech business models, credit risk algorithms, and startup deal-flow evaluation.

### **Eligibility Criteria & Essential Skills**

To stand out in competitive finance internship drives on Unstop, candidates should build a balanced mix of academic foundation, technical tools, and professional soft skills.

#### **1. Educational Qualification**
- **Undergraduate**: Students pursuing B.Com (Hons/Prog), BBA (Finance), BMS, BA Economics, or B.Tech with a strong interest in financial analytics.
- **Postgraduate**: MBA / PGDM in Finance, M.Com, or Master’s in Economics.
- **Professional Courses**: Aspirants preparing for CA (Foundation/Inter), CFA (Level 1/2), CS, or CMA.

#### **2. Must-Have Technical Skills**
- **Advanced Excel**: Mastery over formulas (XLOOKUP, VLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH), Pivot Tables, conditional formatting, and financial modeling shortcuts.
- **Financial Accounting & Statements**: Ability to read, interpret, and analyze Balance Sheets, Income Statements, and Cash Flow Statements.
- **Valuation & Ratio Analysis**: Understanding of liquidity, profitability, and leverage ratios, along with basic DCF valuation concepts.
- **Data & BI Tools**: Basic knowledge of Power BI, Tableau, or SQL is increasingly preferred for financial analyst roles.

#### **3. Core Soft Skills**
- Strong numerical aptitude and attention to detail.
- Effective written and verbal communication for executive summaries and presentations.
- Problem-solving mindset and adaptability to fast-paced financial environments.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Who is eligible to apply for finance internships on Unstop?
Undergraduate students (B.Com, BBA, BMS, Economics Hons), postgraduate students (MBA/PGDM Finance, M.Com), as well as professional aspirants pursuing CA, CS, CFA, or CMA are eligible to apply for various finance roles on Unstop.

### What is the average stipend for finance internships on Unstop?
Stipends typically range from ₹10,000 to ₹25,000 per month for startups and mid-size firms, while top MNCs, investment banks, and consulting firms offer between ₹25,000 and ₹50,000+ per month along with potential Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs).

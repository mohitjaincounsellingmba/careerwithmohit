## 7. Selection Process: Extempore & Personal Interview (PI)

1. **Online Form Submission:** Submit credentials at `fsm.ac.in` and select your program preferences by December 15, 2026.
2. **Profile-Based Shortlisting:** Candidates are called for selection rounds based on CAT 2026 / XAT 2027 performance.
3. **Extempore Evaluation:** Candidates speak for 1 to 2 minutes on a random business, economic, or social prompt to test clarity, presence of mind, and composure.
4. **Personal Interview (PI):** A 15–20 minute structured interaction with senior faculty assessing business fundamentals, general awareness, career aspirations, and cultural fit.
5. **Final Composite Merit:** CAT/XAT Score (40%) + Extempore & PI (35%) + Academic Performance (15%) + Work Experience & Diversity (10%).

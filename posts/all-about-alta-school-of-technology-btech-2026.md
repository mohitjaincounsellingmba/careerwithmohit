## Key Highlights & USPs: Why Choose Alta?

Alta School of Technology has restructured the engineering journey to prepare students for modern software engineering roles. Its core USPs include:

*   💻 **Apple MacBook from Day 1:** Upon enrolling, every student receives a high-spec MacBook. This ensures that every student works on an industry-standard development environment from their first day of class.
*   🤖 **AI-First Curriculum:** The pedagogy actively integrates advanced generative AI tools (such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini) and code assistants (like GitHub Copilot). Students learn how to leverage AI to speed up development, debug code, and build smarter software.
*   💻 **70% Practical Hands-On Coding:** Say goodbye to endless lectures on paper. The curriculum shifts focus from rote theory to active coding, requiring students to build live web applications, mobile apps, and databases from the start.
*   👨‍🏫 **MAANG & Tech Industry Mentors:** Students receive direct portfolio reviews, coding guidance, and mock interviews from senior developers and engineering managers at top-tier firms, including MAANG.
*   📂 **Portfolio-Driven Placement:** Instead of just drafting a plain paper resume, students build a comprehensive GitHub portfolio with live projects and public repositories. Recruiters hire based on what students have actually built.
*   💼 **6-Month Corporate Apprenticeship:** The final year includes a mandatory, high-intensity corporate apprenticeship, providing real-world professional work experience before graduation.

## Admission & Eligibility Process 2026

Securing admission to the B.Tech program at Alta School of Technology involves a streamlined profile-evaluation process:

1.  **Eligibility:** Candidates must have completed Class 12 (or equivalent) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) from a recognized board, securing at least **50% aggregate marks**.
2.  **Entrance Exam:** Applicants must appear for the **ASAT (Alta Scholastic Aptitude Test)**, which tests logical reasoning, quantitative aptitude, and basic programming interest.
3.  **Interview Round:** A personal counseling and interview round is conducted to evaluate the student's motivation and tech alignment.
4.  **Enrollment:** Shortlisted students receive their offer letters and can choose their preferred partner campus.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I get direct admission in B.Tech without JEE Main?
Yes, several state-level entrance exams and private colleges offer direct admission based on Class 12th board marks or management quota seats.

### Which specialization has the highest placement package in B.Tech?
Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), along with emerging fields like Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Machine Learning (ML), and Data Science, consistently offer the highest placements.

### What is the difference between B.Tech and B.E.?
B.Tech (Bachelor of Technology) is generally more practical and application-oriented, whereas B.E. (Bachelor of Engineering) tends to focus more on theoretical concepts and foundation sciences.

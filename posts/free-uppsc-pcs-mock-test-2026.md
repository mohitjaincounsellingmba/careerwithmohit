## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the pattern of UPPSC PCS Prelims?
UPPSC Prelims consists of two papers on the same day: Paper 1 (General Studies I - 150 questions, 200 marks) and Paper 2 (CSAT - 100 questions, 200 marks).

### Is the CSAT paper counted in the UPPSC Prelims cutoff ranking?
No, Paper 2 (CSAT) is qualifying in nature. Candidates only need to secure a minimum of 33% marks (66 marks out of 200) in CSAT. The cutoff is decided solely on Paper 1 (GS I) marks.

### Is there negative marking in UPPSC PCS Prelims?
Yes, there is a negative marking of 1/3rd (-0.33) of the marks assigned to each question for every wrong response.

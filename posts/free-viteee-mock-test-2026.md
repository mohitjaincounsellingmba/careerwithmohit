The **VIT Engineering Entrance Examination (VITEEE)** is the official entrance gateway to secure your seat at Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) campuses across Vellore, Chennai, AP, and Bhopal. VIT is consistently ranked among the top private engineering universities in India.

The key to cracking VITEEE is speed and navigating its unique syllabus, which includes an Aptitude and English section. With our **[Free VITEEE 2026 Mock Test](/tools/mock-test/viteee)**, you can assess exactly where you stand.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the syllabus and section distribution of VITEEE 2026?
VITEEE is a 2.5-hour exam with 125 questions: Mathematics/Biology (40 Qs), Physics (35 Qs), Chemistry (35 Qs), Aptitude (10 Qs), and English (5 Qs).

### Is there negative marking in VITEEE?
No, there is no negative marking in the VITEEE exam. Candidates should answer all 125 questions to maximize their score.

### What rank is required to get CSE in Category 1 at [VIT Vellore](/colleges/vit-vellore)?
To secure Computer Science Engineering (CSE) in Category 1 (lowest fee category) at [VIT Vellore](/colleges/vit-vellore), candidates usually need a rank under 1,000.

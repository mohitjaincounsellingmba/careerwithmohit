## 7. Selection Process: Video Assessment & Personal Interview

1. **Application Submission:** Submit credentials online at `admission.imi.edu` by December 19, 2026.
2. **Profile-Based Screening:** Shortlisting takes place after entrance exam score evaluation.
3. **Asynchronous Video Assessment:** Shortlisted students record timed video responses to behavioral and situational management prompts.
4. **Personal Interview (PI):** A comprehensive 20-minute interaction with IMI faculty and corporate leaders assessing business acumen, current affairs, and domain knowledge.
5. **Final Composite Offer:** Final offers are computed based on CAT/XAT scores (40%), Interview (35%), Video Assessment (10%), and Academics/Work Experience (15%).

## 🎯 What is the Admission Selection Process for Alliance University MBA 2027?

**Admission to the Alliance University MBA program is based on a composite evaluation of national entrance exam percentiles (CAT, XAT, NMAT, MAT, CMAT) or AMAT, followed by an Oral Extempore Presentation and Personal Interview (PI).**

```
Alliance University Admission Evaluation Pipeline
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Application Submission & Entrance Score / AMAT           │
│ 2. Oral Extempore Presentation (OEP) (3-Minute Speech): 20% │
│ 3. Personal Interview (PI) with Faculty Panel: 25%          │
│ 4. Entrance Exam Percentile (CAT/XAT/NMAT/MAT/AMAT): 40%    │
│ 5. Academic Record (10th/12th/UG) & Work Experience: 15%    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Selection Steps:
1. **Online Application**: Register online on the official Alliance portal.
2. **Submit Test Score / Take AMAT**: Submit valid CAT/XAT/NMAT/MAT scores or take the online **Alliance Management Aptitude Test (AMAT)**.
3. **Oral Extempore Presentation (OEP)**: Candidates deliver a 3-minute oral extempore on contemporary business, technology, or economic topics.
4. **Personal Interview (PI)**: Evaluates leadership potential, domain knowledge, and career objectives.
5. **Final Offer & Fee Confirmation**: Selected candidates receive provisional offer letters with scholarship details.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the total fee structure for the 2-year MBA at Alliance University Bangalore?
The total 2-year tuition and academic fee for the flagship MBA program at Alliance School of Business is ₹16.00 to ₹18.00 Lakhs, payable in 4 installment semesters. Residential hostel amenities range between ₹1.60 and ₹2.00 Lakhs per annum.

### What is the average and highest placement package at Alliance University Bangalore?
Alliance University recorded an audited average domestic package of ₹8.50 to ₹10.50 LPA, with the top 25% securing an average CTC of ₹14.80 LPA and top international/niche offers touching ₹38.00 to ₹40.00 LPA.

### Is Alliance University MBA a recognized university degree or an autonomous PGDM?
Alliance University is a full UGC-recognized State Private University established under the Karnataka State Legislature Act. Graduating students receive a formal Master of Business Administration (MBA) degree rather than a postgraduate diploma.

### Which entrance exams are accepted for Alliance University MBA 2027 admission?
Alliance School of Business accepts scores from CAT, XAT, NMAT, MAT, CMAT, GMAT, and KMAT, as well as its proprietary Alliance Management Aptitude Test (AMAT), followed by an Oral Extempore Presentation (OEP) and Personal Interview.

### Does Alliance University offer international student exchange and dual degrees?
Yes, Alliance University maintains formal academic partnerships with over 50 renowned international universities across the USA, UK, France, Germany, and Switzerland, offering semester exchange programs, summer immersion schools, and dual-degree pathways.

### What are the merit scholarship criteria at Alliance University Bangalore?
Merit-based scholarships offering up to 50% tuition fee waivers are awarded to candidates scoring 90+ percentile in CAT/XAT/NMAT and applicants with outstanding national-level academic or athletic distinctions.

## 1. Institutional Background: The Global Business School Identity in Bangalore

Alliance University, established under Karnataka State Act No. 34 of 2010, is one of South India's premier private universities. Its flagship business school, **Alliance School of Business**, has provided postgraduate management education for over two decades.

Situated across a 55-acre green campus in Chandapura-Anekal, south of Bangalore, Alliance operates in close proximity to Electronic City—India’s foremost IT hub housing corporate headquarters including Infosys, Wipro, HP, and Siemens. Accredited internationally by the **International Accreditation Council for Business Education (IACBE, USA)** and holding membership in AACSB, Alliance integrates international business pedagogy with case studies from Harvard and Ivey.

For aspirants targeting corporate careers in Bangalore’s thriving financial, technology, and analytics ecosystems, Alliance University provides an established alumni network, modern infrastructure, and steady recruitment ties.

## 2. Program Architecture & Distinct Postgraduate Verticals

The full-time Master of Business Administration (MBA) at Alliance School of Business is an intensive 24-month program spread across six terms:

### A. Marketing Management
- **Key Focus**: Strategic Brand Positioning, Digital Marketing Architecture, Omni-Channel Retailing, B2B Relationship Selling, and MarTech Systems.
- **Career Pathways**: Product Marketing Manager, Category Lead, Digital Marketing Strategist, and Enterprise Account Executive.

### B. Financial Management & FinTech
- **Key Focus**: Corporate Valuation, Equity Research, Financial Econometrics, FinTech Ecosystems, Mergers & Acquisitions, and Wealth Management.
- **Career Pathways**: Financial Analyst, Credit Underwriter, Wealth Relationship Manager, and Investment Operations Associate.

### C. Operations & Supply Chain Management
- **Key Focus**: Global Logistics, Warehousing Systems Automation, Strategic Sourcing, Lean Six Sigma, and Supply Chain Analytics.
- **Career Pathways**: Operations Coordinator, Procurement Executive, and Logistics Analyst.

### D. Human Resource Management & People Analytics
- **Key Focus**: Strategic Talent Acquisition, Organizational Development, HR Metrics & Analytics, Industrial Labor Laws, and Performance Systems.
- **Career Pathways**: HR Business Partner (HRBP), Talent Acquisition Lead, and People Analytics Associate.

### E. Business Analytics & Intelligence
- **Key Focus**: Python for Decision Making, Advanced SQL, Predictive Forecasting, PowerBI Data Visualization, and Machine Learning for Managers.
- **Career Pathways**: Business Intelligence Analyst, Data Consultant, and Analytics Strategist.

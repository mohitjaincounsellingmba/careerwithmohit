## 6. Selection Process: Case Analysis & Personal Interview

1. **Online Form Submission:** Fill basic details at `jaipuria.ac.in` and pay ₹1,000.
2. **Profile Shortlisting:** Applications are reviewed based on graduation scores, 10th/12th percentages, and entrance percentiles.
3. **Case Analysis (CA):** Candidates are given a written real-world business case study to assess problem identification, critical thinking, and structured recommendation skills.
4. **Personal Interview (PI):** A 15-minute conversation with senior faculty evaluating domain fundamentals, general awareness, career clarity, and attitude.
5. **Final Composite Merit:** Computed on Entrance Score (35%), Personal Interview (30%), Case Analysis (15%), and Academics/Work Experience (20%).

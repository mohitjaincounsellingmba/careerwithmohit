## 1. Institutional Background: The British Dual-Degree Pedagogy in Pune

International School of Management Studies (ISMS Pune), established in 2009, was founded to bring globally accredited British qualifications within reach of Indian management students. Operating from modern campuses in Wadgaon and Hinjawadi, Pune, ISMS was developed around an international business curriculum.

Accredited by **Pearson Edexcel (UK)**—the UK's largest awarding organization regulated by Ofqual—ISMS offers dual qualifications: an Indian management curriculum combined with an internationally recognized Pearson BTEC Level 7 Extended Diploma in Strategic Management and Leadership. Students have optional progression pathways to complete their final master's dissertation at partner universities in the United Kingdom, including **Leeds Beckett University** and **University of Central Lancashire (UCLan)**.

For students seeking an affordable, internationalized business school in Pune with fees under ₹7.50 Lakhs and reliable corporate placement support, ISMS represents a practical, high-value choice.

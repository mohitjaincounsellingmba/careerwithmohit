**Echelon Institute of Technology (EIT), Faridabad**, established in 2007, is a premier private engineering and management institute located in the Delhi NCR region. Approved by the **All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)**, EIT has built a reputation for delivering high-quality technical education, modern infrastructure, and student-focused career development.

---

### **The Big Update: Shift to GGSIPU Affiliation**
For many years, EIT Faridabad was affiliated with J.C. Bose University of Science and Technology (YMCA), Faridabad. However, the institute has officially shifted its affiliation to **Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU), New Delhi**. 

This transition is a major milestone for the campus:
* **IP University Degree:** Students will now graduate with a highly valued degree from GGSIPU, one of the most respected state universities in India.
* **Centralized Counselling:** Admissions are conducted through the GGSIPU online counselling portal based on national/state exams (like JEE Main for B.Tech).
* **Improved Opportunities:** The affiliation shift is expected to attract a more diverse student body and bring in a broader network of corporate recruiters.

### **Placement Review: Excellent ROI and Global Brands**
The Training and Placement cell at EIT Faridabad is highly active, starting placement preparation and aptitude training as early as the second year of undergraduate courses.
* **Highest Package:** **₹52 LPA** (offered to outstanding circuit branch graduates).
* **Average Package:** Ranges between **₹5.3 LPA and ₹8.5 LPA**, with Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) and specializations securing the highest averages.
* **Placement Success:** Over **90%** of eligible graduates secure placement opportunities during the campus drives.
* **Top Recruiters:** Major global and domestic companies like Adobe, Oracle, Paytm, Ford, Godrej, Pfizer, TCS, and Wipro regularly participate.

### **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**

**1. Is Echelon Institute of Technology affiliated with YMCA or GGSIPU?**
Echelon Institute of Technology (EIT), Faridabad was previously affiliated with J.C. Bose UST (YMCA) but has officially shifted its affiliation to Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU), New Delhi. Admissions are now conducted through the GGSIPU centralized counselling process.

**2. What is the highest and average placement package at EIT Faridabad?**
The highest placement package offered at Echelon Institute of Technology is ₹52 LPA, while the average package ranges between ₹5.3 LPA and ₹8.5 LPA (especially for CSE and circuit branches).

**3. What are the B.Tech fees at EIT Faridabad for the 2026-27 session?**
The total first-year fee for the B.Tech program at EIT Faridabad is ₹1,87,200. Subsequent years cost approximately ₹1,75,200, making the total tuition fee around ₹7.12 Lakhs for the 4-year course.

**4. Are BBA and BCA courses offered at EIT Faridabad?**
Yes, EIT Faridabad offers both BBA and BCA undergraduate courses. The first-year fee is ₹1,49,700 for BBA and ₹1,47,600 for BCA.

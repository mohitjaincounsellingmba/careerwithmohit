The Consortium of National Law Universities (NLUs) has officially opened the registration for the **Common Law Admission Test (CLAT) 2027**. Law aspirants looking to secure admission into the prestigious 5-year integrated BA LLB (UG) or LLM (PG) programs across 24 participating NLUs can now fill out the application form.

If you are aiming for top NLUs like NLSIU Bangalore, NALSAR Hyderabad, or WBNUJS Kolkata, it is essential to complete your CLAT 2027 registration well before the deadline to avoid last-minute technical glitches.

Here is a comprehensive guide covering the important dates, eligibility criteria, application fee, and a step-by-step process to apply for CLAT 2027.

## 📅 CLAT 2027 Important Dates

It is crucial to keep track of the exam timeline so you don't miss any deadlines. While the exact dates should be verified on the official website, the typical schedule for the CLAT 2027 cycle is as follows:

| Event | Tentative Dates for CLAT 2027 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Release of Official Notification** | July 2026 |
| **Start of Online Registration** | First Week of August 2026 |
| **Last Date to Apply Online** | First Week of November 2026 |
| **Release of Admit Card** | Last Week of November 2026 |
| **CLAT 2027 Exam Date** | First Week of December 2026 |
| **Declaration of Results** | December 2026 / January 2027 |

## 🎓 Eligibility Criteria for CLAT 2027

Before filling out the CLAT 2027 application form, candidates must ensure they meet the eligibility criteria prescribed by the Consortium of NLUs.

### For Undergraduate (UG) - 5-Year Integrated LLB
*   **Educational Qualification:** Candidates must have cleared their Class 12 (10+2) or an equivalent examination.
*   **Appearing Candidates:** Students appearing for their Class 12 board exams in March/April 2027 are also eligible to apply.
*   **Minimum Marks:**
    *   **45% aggregate** for General / OBC / PWD / NRI / PIO / OCI categories.
    *   **40% aggregate** for SC / ST categories.
*   **Age Limit:** There is **no upper age limit** for appearing in CLAT UG.

### For Postgraduate (PG) - LLM
*   **Educational Qualification:** Candidates must hold an LLB Degree or an equivalent examination.
*   **Appearing Candidates:** Those appearing in their final year of the LLB degree in 2027 are eligible.
*   **Minimum Marks:**
    *   **50% aggregate** for General / OBC / PWD / NRI / PIO / OCI categories.
    *   **45% aggregate** for SC / ST categories.
*   **Age Limit:** There is **no upper age limit** for appearing in CLAT PG.

## 📝 How to Fill the CLAT 2027 Registration Form?

The CLAT 2027 application process is completely online. Follow these simple steps to complete your registration smoothly:

### Step 1: Online Registration
1.  Visit the official website of the Consortium of NLUs: **consortiumofnlus.ac.in**.
2.  Click on the "CLAT 2027" tab and then select "Register".
3.  Enter your Name, Mobile Number, Email ID, and create a strong Password.
4.  Verify your mobile number using the OTP received to activate your account.

### Step 2: Fill out the Application Form
1.  Log in using your registered mobile number and password.
2.  Fill in your personal details, academic qualifications, and communication address accurately.
3.  Select your preferred **Test Center Cities** (you usually get to choose three options in order of preference).
4.  Choose your preferred **NLU Reservations** (if applicable based on your domicile category).

### Step 3: Upload Documents
Candidates must upload scanned copies of the required documents in the prescribed format and size.
*   Front-facing Passport Size Recent Photograph (plain background).
*   Scanned Signature.
*   Category/Caste Certificate (if applicable).
*   PWD/BPL Certificate (if applicable).

### Step 4: Pay the Application Fee
Once you have verified all the details, proceed to pay the application fee online using a Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, or UPI.

| Category | Application Fee | Previous Years' Question Papers (Optional) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **General/OBC/PWD/NRI/PIO/OCI** | ₹ 4,000 | ₹ 500 |
| **SC/ST/BPL** | ₹ 3,500 | ₹ 500 |

### Step 5: Final Submission and Print
After successful payment, your CLAT 2027 registration is complete. Download the application confirmation page and take a printout for your future reference. 

## 💡 Important Tips for Aspirants

*   **Avoid the Last-Minute Rush:** The server tends to slow down in the final days before the deadline. Register and submit your application well in advance.
*   **Double-Check Details:** Ensure that your name matches your Class 10/12 mark sheets exactly. Mistakes in category or domicile details are difficult to change later.
*   **Start Practicing:** Now that you've applied, intensify your preparation. Solve previous years' question papers and take regular mock tests to improve your speed and accuracy.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### When is the last date to apply for CLAT 2027?
The last date to fill out the CLAT 2027 registration form is typically in the first week of November. Check the official Consortium of NLUs website for the exact deadline.

### Who is eligible to apply for CLAT 2027 (UG)?
Candidates who have passed or are appearing for their Class 12 board examinations with at least 45% aggregate marks (40% for SC/ST categories) are eligible for the CLAT UG 2027 exam. There is no upper age limit.

### What is the application fee for CLAT 2027?
The application fee for General/OBC/PWD/NRI/PIO/OCI candidates is usually ₹4,000, while it is ₹3,500 for SC/ST/BPL candidates. An additional ₹500 is required if you wish to purchase previous years' question papers.

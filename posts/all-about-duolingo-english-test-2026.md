## 1. Why Choose the Duolingo English Test?

The DET is gaining massive popularity among Indian students for three main reasons:
*   **Convenience:** No need to travel to a test center. You can take the test from your own room at any time of the day.
*   **Affordability:** At roughly one-fourth the price of IELTS or TOEFL, it is the most budget-friendly international proficiency test.
*   **Speed:** While other tests take up to two weeks for results, DET delivers your certified score in just 2 days.

---

## 2. Adaptive Exam Pattern 2026

The DET is **computer-adaptive**, meaning the difficulty of the questions changes based on your performance. If you answer correctly, the next question gets harder.

### Key Question Types:
*   **Literacy:** Read and Complete, Read and Select.
*   **Comprehension:** Listen and Select, Listen and Type.
*   **Conversation:** Listen and Speak.
*   **Production:** Write About the Photo, Speak About the Photo, Writing Sample.

Unlike IELTS, there are no long reading passages or extensive listening monologues. The tasks are short, fast-paced, and test your real-world language application.

## 3. University Acceptance in 2026

Is the DET accepted? **Yes!** Over 5,000 institutions worldwide now accept Duolingo English Test scores. This includes:
*   **USA:** Yale, Columbia, NYU, Georgia Tech, and hundreds of others.
*   **UK:** University of Southampton, University of Warwick, and many more.
*   **Canada:** Major universities like McGill, Concordia, and various colleges.
*   **Europe:** Increasing acceptance in Germany, Ireland, and France.

**Important Note:** Always check the specific program page of your target university, as some departments might still prefer IELTS/TOEFL for certain research-intensive courses.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. What is a good score in DET for top universities?
Most competitive universities look for a score of **120 or higher**. Ivy League schools and top-tier programs often require **130+**.

### 2. Can I use a regular laptop for the test?
Yes, but you must have a working webcam, microphone, speakers, and a stable internet connection. You cannot use headphones or earphones during the test for security reasons.

### 3. How many times can I take the DET?
You can take the test as many times as you like, but you can only purchase three tests in a 30-day period.

Running a career upgrade requires choosing the right management program. For working professionals in Ghaziabad, the Executive MBA / Executive PGDM offered by [ITS School of Management, Ghaziabad](/colleges/its-ghaziabad) represents a powerful gateway to higher senior leadership positions.

In this review, we break down everything you need to know: fees, admission cutoffs, placements, pros, cons, and our honest expert verdict.

---

## 🏆 Program Overview

- **Official Course Name:** Executive PGDM
- **Approximate Fees:** ₹3.00 Lakhs (Total Fees)
- **Entrance Exams Accepted:** MAT/CMAT/Merit + Personal Interview
- **Course Duration:** 15 Months to 2 Years (Format varies by program)

---

## 📈 Eligibility & Admission Details

- **Academic Requirements:** Bachelor's degree + minimum 2 years of work experience.
- **Selection Process:** Admission typically relies on professional profile screening, statement of purpose (SOP), executive experience reviews, and personal interviews. Elite IIM programs also mandate standard quantitative testing (GMAT/GRE).

---

## 💼 Placement Statistics & Career Impact

- **Estimated Placements:** Corporate lateral placements, average CTC ~5-6 LPA.
- **Career Growth:** Executive MBA degrees focus on helping candidates secure senior lateral shifts (like VP, Product Manager, or Director roles) or move from technical paths into core management.

## 📝 Student Reviews: Pros & Cons

### Pros
- **Highlight:** Highly economical fee structure.
- **Highlight:** AICTE-approved curriculum focusing on practical management practices.
- **Highlight:** Easy accessibility for candidates living in Ghaziabad and East Delhi.

### Cons
- **Limitation:** Limited high-paying consulting or product management roles.
- **Limitation:** A smaller campus compared to premium universities.

## 🔍 Our Expert Verdict

The Executive MBA program at [ITS School of Management, Ghaziabad](/colleges/its-ghaziabad) is highly recommended for professionals based in Ghaziabad who want to scale their careers without disrupting their current geographic setup. 

If you are looking for top-tier consulting placements and have 5+ years of experience, full-time residential paths are stellar. However, if you are looking to continue your full-time job, their weekend/evening classes offer outstanding return on investment.

## 3 Core Strategies for High GMAT Focus Scores

1. **Master the New Data Insights Section:** Data Insights carries exactly one-third (33.3%) of your total GMAT Focus score. Practice reading graphical scatter plots, table trend correlations, and 2-statement Data Sufficiency questions daily.
2. **Prioritize Critical Reasoning in Verbal:** Without Sentence Correction, Verbal Reasoning is 100% focused on RC and Critical Reasoning (Assumptions, Boldface roles, Strengthen, Weaken, Evaluate). Map the logical argument premise $\to$ conclusion before viewing the 5 options.
3. **Keep an Eye on the 45-Minute Clock:** You have approximately 2 minutes per question in Quant and DI. If a problem solving derivation takes more than 2.5 minutes, use approximation to eliminate extreme outliers and pick your best answer.

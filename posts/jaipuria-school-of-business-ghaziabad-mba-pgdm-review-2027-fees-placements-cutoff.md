## 1. Institutional Background: Seven Decades of Educational Excellence in Indirapuram

Jaipuria School of Business (JSB Ghaziabad) operates under the umbrella of the Seth Anandram Jaipuria Education Society, an educational group with over 75 years of history in India. Established in 2008 in Indirapuram, Ghaziabad, JSB was created to provide practical, corporate-oriented management education in the National Capital Region.

Approved by the **All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)**, JSB's flagship Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) integrates classroom study with corporate mentorship, live projects, and executive certifications.

Its campus in Indirapuram places students within 10 minutes of Noida Sector 62 (a major IT, banking, and media hub), with easy connectivity to Central Delhi via NH-24 / Delhi-Meerut Expressway.

For students seeking an accredited business school in Delhi-NCR with fees under ₹10 Lakhs, modern infrastructure, and consistent placement performance, JSB Indirapuram represents a balanced choice.

## 2. Program Architecture & Modern PGDM Specialization Verticals

The 2-year full-time PGDM program at JSB is structured into six academic trimesters offering dual specializations:

### A. Marketing & Customer Experience Management
- **Curriculum Focus**: Omnichannel retail, digital brand management, marketing analytics, sales force automation, and customer experience design.
- **Career Pathways**: Product Manager, Sales Lead, Brand Strategist, Key Account Manager.

### B. Financial Management & FinTech Analytics
- **Curriculum Focus**: Corporate finance, security analysis, financial modeling with Excel, commercial banking operations, and fintech ecosystems.
- **Career Pathways**: Financial Analyst, Credit Associate, Wealth Manager, Investment Banking Associate.

### C. Human Resource Management & People Technology
- **Curriculum Focus**: Strategic human capital, HR analytics, performance appraisal systems, labor law compliances, and organization development.
- **Career Pathways**: HR Business Partner, Talent Acquisition Specialist, Training & Development Manager.

### D. Operations & Global Supply Chain Management
- **Curriculum Focus**: Supply chain logistics, procurement strategies, warehouse operations, inventory control, and Lean concepts.
- **Career Pathways**: Supply Chain Analyst, Logistics Coordinator, Operations Supervisor.

### E. Business Analytics & Intelligence
- **Curriculum Focus**: Predictive modeling with Python, SQL database queries, data visualization with Tableau and PowerBI, and data-driven business decisions.
- **Career Pathways**: Business Analyst, Data Visualization Specialist, Analytics Consultant.

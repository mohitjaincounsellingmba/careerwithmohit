## 4. Academic Rigor, Curriculum Design & Interdisciplinary Minors

What makes Quantum University distinct is its **Passion Programs** and major-minor curriculum architecture:

### 1. Interdisciplinary Major-Minor Architecture
Students pursuing an MBA can pair their core business major with a complementary minor from an entirely different discipline. For example, a student majoring in Marketing can minor in Digital Media, Web Technologies, or Graphic Design, acquiring hard creative skills that set them apart in contemporary marketing agencies.

### 2. Semester Curriculum Breakdown
* **Semester I (Management Fundamentals)**: Principles & Practice of Management, Managerial Economics, Accounting for Managers, Quantitative Analysis, Marketing Management, and Executive Communication.
* **Semester II (Functional Capabilities)**: Financial Management, Human Resource Management, Operations Research, Management Information Systems (MIS), and Business Research Methods.
* **Summer Corporate Internship**: Mandatory 8-week corporate residency between first and second year evaluated through viva voce and project reports.
* **Semesters III & IV (Dual Specialization Electives)**:
  - **Marketing Management**: Consumer Behaviour Analytics, Strategic Brand Management, B2B Marketing, Services Marketing, and Omnichannel Retail.
  - **Financial Management**: Investment Analysis & Portfolio Management, Financial Derivatives, International Finance, and Corporate Valuation.
  - **Human Resource Management**: Talent Acquisition & Retention, Industrial Relations & Labor Law, Strategic HRM, and Performance Appraisal.
  - **Supply Chain & E-Commerce**: Global Logistics Management, Warehouse Automation, and E-Commerce Platform Architecture.

### Skill Enhancement Bootcamps
* **Advanced Excel & Financial Modeling**: Hands-on masterclasses covering VLOOKUP, pivot reporting, dynamic cash flow modeling, and scenario sensitivity analysis.
* **Digital Marketing Masterclasses**: Practical training on Google Analytics 4, search engine optimization basics, content marketing funnels, and social media ad optimization.
* **Corporate Communication & Soft Skills**: Personality grooming, mock group discussions, business presentation etiquette, and simulated technical interview drills.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is Quantum University recognized by UGC and approved by AICTE?
Yes. Quantum University is a state private university recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) under Section 2(f) and approved by the AICTE for its management and technical programs.

### 2. What is the total fee structure for the MBA program at Quantum University?
The total course fee for the 2-year full-time MBA program at Quantum University is approximately **₹4.20 Lakhs to ₹4.35 Lakhs**, payable in semester installments.

### 3. What is the average salary package for MBA graduates at Quantum University?
The audited average placement package for MBA graduates at Quantum University stands at **₹5.80 LPA to ₹6.50 LPA**, with top performers securing domestic packages reaching up to **₹18.00 LPA**.

### 4. Does Quantum University provide hostel facilities on campus?
Yes. Quantum University provides comprehensive on-campus hostel accommodations for both male and female students with high-speed Wi-Fi, 24/7 security, sports facilities, and nutritious dining services on its 30-acre campus.

### 5. Can I get direct admission in Quantum University without CAT?
Yes. Quantum University accepts scores from MAT, CMAT, ATMA, and CUET-PG. Candidates can also take the university's internal entrance test (Q-CARE) or apply directly based on graduation merit (50%+).

## 1. Institutional Background: The Hinjawadi IT Corridor Advantage

Akemi Business School (Akemi Group of Institutes), located in Marunji, Hinjawadi, Pune, was established to address the growing demand for corporate-ready management graduates in western India’s primary industrial and technology hub. 

Operating under the academic supervision of **Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU)**—one of India's oldest and most prestigious state universities—Akemi offers a curriculum reinforced by hands-on corporate certifications. Situated minutes from Hinjawadi Phase-1 and Phase-2, home to hundreds of multinational technology enterprises and financial service global capability centers (GCCs), Akemi leverages its geographic location to facilitate corporate guest lectures, live industrial internships, and localized recruitment drives.

For students who want an accredited SPPU university degree in Pune without taking on ₹12 Lakh to ₹18 Lakh private university loan debts, Akemi Business School represents an accessible, high-ROI alternative.

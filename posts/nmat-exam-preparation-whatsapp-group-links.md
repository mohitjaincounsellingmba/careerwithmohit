## NMAT 2026 Exam Pattern & Structure

A quick look at NMAT's structure for effective daily group practice:

* **Language Skills:** 36 Questions (28 Mins) | Score Range: 12 - 120
* **Quantitative Skills:** 36 Questions (52 Mins) | Score Range: 12 - 120
* **Logical Reasoning:** 36 Questions (40 Mins) | Score Range: 12 - 120
* **Total:** 108 Questions | 120 Minutes | Total Scaled Score Range: 36 - 360
* **Key Feature:** No negative marking, but candidates cannot skip questions or go back to previous questions.

Set against a beautiful backdrop, Hemvati Nandan Bahuguna Garhwal University (HNBGU) is a Central University offering a robust academic environment. For 2026, it remains a heavily sought-after institution for students looking for quality and affordable education through the CUET.

### 📊 HNBGU 2026 Key Highlights

| Category | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Admission Exams** | CUET UG & CUET PG |
| **Course Fees Range** | ₹3,000 - ₹2.1 Lakhs (Total) |
| **B.Tech Total Fees** | ₹1.0 Lakh - ₹1.4 Lakhs |
| **Median Package** | ₹3.0 LPA - ₹6.0 LPA |
| **Top Recruiters** | TCS, Wipro, Tech Mahindra, Infosys |
| **Hostel Fees** | ₹3,000 - ₹5,000/year (Plus Mess) |

### **Admission & CUET Cutoff**
*   **Process:** Entirely merit-based through CUET. Candidates must register on the university’s counseling portal.
*   **Cutoff Trends:** Highly variable. B.Tech (CSE) often requires scores upwards of 320–370. Programs like B.Sc Forestry also witness high competition (e.g., cutoffs up to 459 in recent years). Standard BA/B.Sc programs may have much more relaxed closing scores.

### **Fees Structure**
As a Central University, HNBGU maintains extremely low fees:
*   **General Range:** Most non-technical UG and PG courses cost between ₹3,000 to a few tens of thousands per year.
*   **B.Tech:** The entire 4-year B.Tech program is capped at roughly ₹1 Lakh to ₹1.4 Lakhs, making it a very economical engineering option.

### **Placements Overview**
*   **Scenario:** The university’s placement cell actively arranges campus drives. However, a significant portion of successful candidates secure roles via off-campus drives.
*   **Statistics:** The median package hovers between ₹3 LPA and ₹6 LPA.
*   **Recruiting Companies:** Regular recruiters include major IT players like TCS, Wipro, HCL, Infosys, and Tech Mahindra.

### **Hostel Facilities**
*   **Allocation:** Hostels are allotted on a merit basis, factoring in 12th-grade marks, distance from the campus, and category reservations.
*   **Affordability:** Annual accommodation costs are minimal (₹3,000 – ₹5,000), while monthly mess charges are approximately ₹3,200 – ₹3,500.
*   **Amenities:** Double/triple occupancy rooms, 24/7 security, Wi-Fi, and basic medical support.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I check my exam results online?
You can check results on the official website of the conducting bodies (e.g., NTA for JEE/NEET/CUET, or respective boards/testing organizations) using your application registration number.

### Are mock tests helpful in exam preparation?
Yes, attempting mock tests helps candidates build speed and accuracy, understand the exam pattern, and perform detailed analytics of strong and weak sections.

### What is the significance of negative marking in entrance exams?
Many competitive exams deduct marks for wrong answers (e.g., -1 or -0.25). Candidates should avoid guessing to maintain accuracy and prevent score drops.

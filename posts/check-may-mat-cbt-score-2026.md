## What is the MAT CBT Mode?

The **Computer-Based Test (CBT)** is a centre-based, computer-administered version of the MAT exam. Unlike IBT (Internet-Based Test taken from home), the CBT requires you to visit an **AIMA-designated test centre**, but you respond on a computer screen rather than a paper sheet.

Key details about MAT CBT:
- **Exam Centre:** Designated AIMA CBT centres across India
- **Duration:** 2.5 hours (150 minutes)
- **Total Questions:** 150 MCQs
- **Sections:** Language Comprehension (30), Intelligence & Critical Reasoning (30), Mathematical Skills (30), Data Analysis & Sufficiency (30), Indian & Global Environment (30)
- **Marking Scheme:** +1 for correct, −0.25 for incorrect
- **Composite Score:** Out of 800 (IGE section NOT included in composite)

## How to Check MAT May 2026 CBT Score: Step-by-Step

Once AIMA publishes the CBT result, here is exactly how to access your scorecard:

### Step 1: Open the Official AIMA MAT Portal
Go directly to **[mat.aima.in](https://mat.aima.in)**. This is the **one and only official website** for MAT results. Avoid any other website or app claiming to show results early.

### Step 2: Click "Candidate Login"
On the homepage, find the **Candidate Login** button. Click it to proceed to the login page.

### Step 3: Log In with Your Registered Credentials
Enter:
- **Email ID** — the one used when you registered for MAT May 2026
- **Password** — your account password

> Forgot your password? Use the **"Forgot Password"** link and reset it via your registered email. Do this now if you're unsure — don't scramble on result day.

### Step 4: Navigate to Your Dashboard
After login, your candidate dashboard will display your profile and active exam records. Once the CBT result is released, you will see a **Scorecard / Result** section appear.

### Step 5: View and Download Your Scorecard
- Click **Download Scorecard**
- Your PDF will contain your section-wise scores, composite score, and percentile
- **Save and print at least 2–3 copies** for college applications and personal records

## MAT CBT vs PBT vs IBT: Key Differences

Wondering how your CBT result compares to other modes? Here's a quick overview:

| Feature | PBT | CBT | IBT |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Exam Venue** | Test Centre | Test Centre | Home/Any device |
| **Answer Method** | OMR Sheet | Computer Screen | Computer/Laptop |
| **Result Speed** | 14–20 days | 14–20 days | 10–14 days |
| **Score Acceptance** | All MAT colleges | All MAT colleges | All MAT colleges |
| **Validity** | 12 months | 12 months | 12 months |

> **Important:** All three modes carry **equal weightage** with MBA colleges. A score from CBT mode has the same value as PBT or IBT at every participating B-School.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1. When will MAT May 2026 CBT result be declared?**  
The CBT (June 14, 2026) result is expected between **June 28 – July 5, 2026**.

**Q2. How to check MAT CBT scorecard online?**  
Log in at **[mat.aima.in](https://mat.aima.in)** → Candidate Dashboard → Scorecard section.

**Q3. Is the CBT score different from the PBT score?**  
No. Scores from all MAT modes (PBT, CBT, IBT) are scaled to the same 800-point composite and are treated equally by all colleges.

**Q4. How many colleges can I send my MAT CBT score to?**  
During registration, you can select up to **5 colleges** for score sharing. After the result, you can send your scorecard to additional colleges directly.

**Q5. Can I appear in both CBT and IBT in the same session?**  
Yes. AIMA allows candidates to appear in multiple modes (e.g., CBT + IBT) in the same May session at a combined fee. Your best score is typically considered.

**Q6. Is a CBT score accepted for all MBA colleges accepting MAT?**  
Yes. Every MBA/PGDM institute accepting MAT treats CBT, PBT, and IBT scores interchangeably.

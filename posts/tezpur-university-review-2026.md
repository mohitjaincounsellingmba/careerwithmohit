## Admission Process & Cutoff

*   **B.Tech Admissions**: Conducted via JEE Main ranks through JoSAA/CSAB. CSE closing ranks often fall in the 56k-57k range for certain categories.
*   **UG/PG Admissions**: Driven by **CUET** scores. Candidates must apply through the university's portal post-results.
*   **MBA Admissions**: Based on national exams like CAT, MAT, or XAT followed by GD and PI.

## Fee Structure & Hostel Facilities

Tezpur University offers a highly subsidized and affordable education model.
*   **Fees**: B.Tech costs around ₹15,000/semester, while M.Sc. is about ₹6,000/semester.
*   **Hostels**: The fully residential campus offers excellent hostel facilities. Seat rent is incredibly low (~₹675/semester), and mess charges are around ₹1,500 - ₹2,000 monthly.

## Placement Statistics

With a placement rate of around 80%, the Training and Placement Cell is highly active.
*   **Salary Packages**: The highest package has touched ₹37 LPA, with an average of ₹6 LPA.
*   **Top Companies**: Major recruiters include Infosys, Accenture, TCS, HDFC Bank, and core sector companies like Oil India.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I get direct admission in B.Tech without JEE Main?
Yes, several state-level entrance exams and private colleges offer direct admission based on Class 12th board marks or management quota seats.

### Which specialization has the highest placement package in B.Tech?
Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), along with emerging fields like Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Machine Learning (ML), and Data Science, consistently offer the highest placements.

### What is the difference between B.Tech and B.E.?
B.Tech (Bachelor of Technology) is generally more practical and application-oriented, whereas B.E. (Bachelor of Engineering) tends to focus more on theoretical concepts and foundation sciences.

The **Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC)** conducts the Combined Competitive Examination for recruitment to **Group B and Group C** state government services in Bihar. Commonly referred to as **BPSC PCS**, the exam recruits candidates for prestigious posts including **Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP), Block Development Officer (BDO), Revenue Officer, District Minorities Welfare Officer, Planning Officer**, and many more across Bihar's vast government machinery.

With Bihar's growing administrative depth and increasing number of vacancies per cycle, BPSC has become one of the most sought-after state PSC exams in Eastern India.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the pattern of BPSC Prelims?
BPSC Prelims is a qualifying offline test consisting of 150 objective questions on General Studies (GS) carrying 150 marks, with a duration of 2 hours.

### Is there negative marking in the BPSC Prelims exam?
Yes, BPSC has a negative marking of -0.33 marks (1/3rd) for every incorrect answer. Candidates must attempt questions carefully.

### What are the key areas to focus on for BPSC GS?
Candidates should focus heavily on Bihar Special GK, General Science, Indian History & Culture, and National/International Current Affairs, which carry the maximum weightage in BPSC Prelims.

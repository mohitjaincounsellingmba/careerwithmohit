The **UPSC EPFO (Employees' Provident Fund Organisation) Recruitment Test** is conducted by the **Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)** for recruitment to the posts of **Enforcement Officer (EO)** and **Accounts Officer (AO)** in the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation under the Ministry of Labour & Employment.

EPFO officers are responsible for ensuring compliance with the **Employees' Provident Fund Act, 1952**, inspecting establishments, and managing provident fund accounts of millions of Indian workers. It's one of the most impactful administrative roles in the Indian government.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the pattern of the UPSC EPFO EO/AO exam?
The UPSC EPFO written exam (Recruitment Test) consists of 120 multiple-choice questions carrying 300 marks, with a duration of 2 hours.

### Is there negative marking in the UPSC EPFO exam?
Yes, there is a negative marking of 1/3rd (-0.33) of the marks allotted to each question for every wrong response.

### What are the key subjects tested in UPSC EPFO?
The syllabus includes General English, Indian Freedom Struggle, Current Events, Indian Economy & Polity, General Accounting Principles, Industrial Relations & Labor Laws, General Science, and Quantitative Aptitude.

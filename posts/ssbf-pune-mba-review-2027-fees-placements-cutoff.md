## 1. Institutional Legacy & Niche Identity: Why SSBF Pune Is Unique

Symbiosis School of Banking and Finance (SSBF Pune) was established in 2010 under Symbiosis International (Deemed University) to bridge a critical talent deficit in India’s banking, financial services, and insurance (BFSI) industry. 

While conventional MBA programs offer generalized finance electives covering theoretical corporate finance, the financial sector requires specialized operational expertise: credit risk underwriting, Basel III/IV regulatory capital adequacy, treasury derivatives, algorithmic liquidity management, retail wealth structuring, and core banking technology integration.

Operating from SIU's flagship hilltop campus at Lavale, Pune, SSBF has cultivated a reputation as an industry-focused finance incubator. The curriculum is mapped against international financial certifications such as CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst), FRM (Financial Risk Manager), and NISM certifications. For graduates who know early on that they want to build an enduring career in banking and capital markets, SSBF offers a tailored, domain-intensive curriculum.

## 2. Program Structure & Specialized Financial Curriculum

SSBF offers a specialized, full-time, two-year **Master of Business Administration in Banking and Finance [MBA (B&F)]**. 

The program is structured across four rigorous academic semesters, ensuring that students develop strong analytical foundations before transitioning into advanced corporate financial modeling and banking simulations.

### Key Foundational Modules (Year 1)
- Financial Accounting & Managerial Reporting
- Principles of Macroeconomics & Monetary Policy
- Quantitative Methods for Financial Decision Making
- Retail Banking Products & Core Banking Operations
- Corporate Finance & Capital Structure Optimization
- Financial Regulations, Legal Frameworks & Banking Compliance

### Advanced Specialized Electives (Year 2)
1. **Investment Banking & Capital Markets**: Advanced Corporate Valuation, Mergers & Acquisitions, Fixed Income Securities, Equity Analysis, and Initial Public Offerings (IPO) Advisory.
2. **Commercial & Corporate Banking**: Credit Appraisal & Risk Management, Working Capital Financing, Syndicated Loans, Trade Finance, and Letters of Credit (LC) Operations.
3. **Treasury & Risk Management**: Foreign Exchange Hedging, Interest Rate Derivatives, Market Risk, Operational Risk, Liquidity Coverage Ratios (LCR), and Asset-Liability Management (ALM).
4. **Wealth Management & Private Banking**: Portfolio Construction, Estate Planning, Alternate Investment Funds (AIFs), Mutual Fund Distribution, and Behavioral Finance.
5. **FinTech & Digital Banking**: Blockchain in Financial Transactions, UPI Architecture, AI in Credit Scoring, RegTech Solutions, and Digital Lending Ecosystems.

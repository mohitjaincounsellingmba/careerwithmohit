### **Eligibility Criteria for SBI PO 2026**

Candidates must meet the following eligibility requirements to apply for the Probationary Officer posts:

#### **1. Educational Qualification (As of Application Close)**
* Candidates must hold a **Bachelor’s degree (Graduation)** in any discipline from a recognized university or possess an equivalent qualification recognized by the Central Government.
* **Final Year Candidates:** Those in their final year or semester of graduation can apply provisionally. However, if called for the interview, they must produce proof of having passed the graduation examination on or before the specified cutoff date.

#### **2. Age Limit (As of April 1, 2026)**
* **Minimum Age:** 21 Years
* **Maximum Age:** 30 Years
* Candidates must have been born not earlier than **April 2, 1996**, and not later than **April 1, 2005** (both dates inclusive).

#### **3. Upper Age Relaxation**
Relaxation in the upper age limit is provided to various reserved categories as follows:

| Category | Age Relaxation |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Scheduled Castes (SC) / Scheduled Tribes (ST)** | 5 Years |
| **Other Backward Classes (OBC - Non-Creamy Layer)** | 3 Years |
| **Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD - SC/ST)** | 15 Years |
| **Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD - OBC)** | 13 Years |
| **Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD - Gen/EWS)** | 10 Years |
| **Ex-Servicemen (Commissioned Officers)** | 5 Years |

### **SBI PO Selection Process & Exam Pattern**

The SBI PO selection process is a multi-layered filter designed to test speed, conceptual clarity, communication skills, and personality. It is divided into three distinct phases:

#### **Phase I: Preliminary Examination (Online)**
The Prelims exam is qualifying in nature and does not count toward the final merit list. It consists of an objective test of **100 marks** conducted online for a duration of **1 hour**. 

There are no sectional cutoffs in the prelims exam; candidates are shortlisted for the Mains based on their aggregate marks.

| Section | Number of Questions | Maximum Marks | Sectional Duration |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 Minutes |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 35 | 35 | 20 Minutes |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 Minutes |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 Minutes** |

* **Negative Marking:** 1/4th (0.25) of the marks assigned to a question will be deducted for every wrong answer.

#### **Phase II: Mains Examination (Online)**
Candidates numbering approximately 10 times the number of vacancies in each category are shortlisted for the Mains. The Mains exam consists of an **Objective Test (200 marks)** and a **Descriptive Test (30 marks)**. Both are conducted online on the same day.

##### **1. Mains Objective Test (3 Hours, 200 Marks)**
The objective test has 4 sections with separate sectional timings:

| Section | Questions | Max Marks | Duration |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Reasoning & Computer Aptitude | 40 | 60 | 50 Minutes |
| Data Analysis & Interpretation | 30 | 60 | 45 Minutes |
| General/Economy/Banking Awareness | 60 | 60 | 45 Minutes |
| English Language | 40 | 20 | 40 Minutes |
| **Total** | **170** | **200** | **3 Hours** |

##### **2. Mains Descriptive Test (30 Minutes, 30 Marks) - *REVISED PATTERN***
The Descriptive Test evaluates written English communication skills and is conducted online. For the 2026 recruitment cycle, the test structure has been revised to focus on more practical corporate and analytical writing formats:

* **Total Marks:** 30 Marks
* **Number of Questions:** 3 (Candidates get choices for each)
  1. **Email Writing** (10 Marks)
  2. **Situation Analysis** (10 Marks)
  3. **Report or Precis Writing** (10 Marks)

#### **Phase III: Psychometric Test, Group Exercise & Interview**
Candidates qualifying in the Mains exam are shortlisted for Phase III.

1. **Psychometric Test:** This is a personality assessment conducted to profile candidate traits. It is qualifying in nature and the results are presented to the interview panel to aid evaluation.
2. **Group Exercise (GE):** Carries **20 marks**. Candidates participate in group discussions or group task exercises.
3. **Interview:** Carries **30 marks**. Panel members evaluate the candidate's suitability for banking leadership roles.

---

### **Final Merit List Calculation**

The final selection is made on the basis of candidate scores in the Mains Exam (Phase II) and the Group Exercise/Interview (Phase III). Preliminary Exam marks are **not** added.

* **Mains Examination (Phase II):** Normalized to **75 Marks** (Out of 230 actual marks)
* **Group Exercise & Interview (Phase III):** Normalized to **25 Marks** (Out of 50 actual marks)
* **Total Combined Score:** **100 Marks**

### **How to Apply for SBI PO 2026**

Interested candidates can apply online by visiting the Careers section on the official SBI portals:
1. Visit the official website: **[sbi.bank/careers](https://sbi.bank/careers)** or **[bank.sbi/careers](https://bank.sbi/careers)**.
2. Under the "Latest Announcements" section, click on the application link for **Recruitment of Probationary Officers**.
3. Register using your email ID and mobile number to obtain your provisional registration number and password.
4. Fill in the online form with your personal details, educational qualifications, and preferences.
5. Upload scanned copies of your photograph, signature, left-thumb impression, and hand-written declaration.
6. Pay the application fee (General/EWS/OBC: ₹750; SC/ST/PwBD: Exempted).
7. Submit the application and print the confirmation page for future reference.

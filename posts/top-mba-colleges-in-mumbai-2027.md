## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### How can I get into JBIMS Mumbai through MAH-CET?
Admission to JBIMS MMS program is conducted strictly via the Maharashtra State Common Entrance Test (MAH-MBA CET) through Centralized Admission Process (CAP) rounds. General category Maharashtra domicile cutoffs are typically **99.90 to 99.95+ percentile**, while All-India quota cutoffs require **99.99 percentile**.

### What is the difference between SPJIMR profile-based and score-based calls?
SPJIMR shortlists a majority of its applicants in Phase 1 based on exceptional academic records, diversity, and relevant work experience at a lower CAT/GMAT cutoff (85+ percentile). Phase 2 shortlists are purely test-score driven (96+ percentile in CAT/GMAT).

### Does Welingkar Mumbai accept CMAT and ATMA?
Yes, Welingkar Mumbai accepts CAT, XAT, CMAT, ATMA, and GMAT scores for its PGDM programs, followed by a comprehensive GD-PI assessment and psychometric evaluation.

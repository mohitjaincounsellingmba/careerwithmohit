Germany is a **dream destination** for Indian STEM and business students — combining world-class education with minimal tuition fees at public universities. Even private universities in Germany are significantly more affordable than the UK or USA. Plus, Germany's booming job market and **EU Blue Card** make it one of the best long-term investment choices for Indian students in 2026.

---

## 🎓 Why Study in Germany in 2026?

- **Low/No Tuition:** Most public universities charge only a semester fee (₹30,000–₹80,000/sem)
- **EU Blue Card:** Fast-track work permit for qualified professionals
- **18-Month Job Seeker Visa:** After graduation, stay in Germany to look for work
- **Strong Industry Links:** Mercedes, BMW, SAP, Siemens — German giants recruit graduates
- **English-Taught Programs:** 1,900+ English-medium programs at German universities
- **Ranked Excellence Universities:** TU Munich, LMU Munich, Heidelberg are world-ranked

## 🎯 Admission Requirements for Germany

- **English Proficiency:** IELTS 6.5–7.0 / TOEFL 90+ / Duolingo 110+
- **Academic Transcripts** (evaluated by uni-assist or directly)
- **SOP (Motivationsschreiben)**
- **Letters of Recommendation** (for research programs)
- **APS Certificate** — **Mandatory for Indian students** applying to German universities
- **Blocked Account** — €11,208/year frozen as proof of funds

### APS Certificate (Academic Evaluation Centre)
- Required for all Indian students applying to Germany
- Documents verified at APS India office (New Delhi, Chennai, Mumbai)
- Processing time: 4–8 weeks
- Fee: Approx. ₹7,000

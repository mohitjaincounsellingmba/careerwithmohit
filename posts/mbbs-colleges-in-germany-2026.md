**Germany** offers world-class medical education at **zero tuition fees** in its top-tier public universities. With state-of-the-art healthcare infrastructure and high demand for licensed doctors in Europe, Germany is a dream destination for focused students.

## 🌟 Why Study Medicine in Germany in 2026?

1. **Zero Tuition Fees:** Public universities do not charge tuition fees.
2. **High Standard of Living:** Affordable cost of living supported by part-time work rights.
3. **Approbation Medical License:** Allows unrestricted practice across the European Union (EU).
4. **World-Renowned Hospitals:** Train at famous university medical centers like Charité Berlin and Heidelberg.

## 📋 Admission Steps for Indian Students 2026

1. **Learn German:** Complete A1 to B2/C1 levels in India.
2. **Studienkolleg (M-Course):** Attend 1-year foundation college in Germany (covers Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and German).
3. **Feststellungsprüfung (FSP) Exam:** Pass the university entrance qualification exam in Germany.
4. **NEET Qualification:** Mandatory for Indian students to comply with NMC rules.
5. **Blocked Account:** Deposit €11,208 in a German blocked account to cover 1st year living costs.

Running a career upgrade requires choosing the right management program. For working professionals in Pune, the Executive MBA / Executive PGDM offered by [Symbiosis [Institute of Management Studies](/colleges/institute-of-management-studies) (SIMS), Pune](/colleges/symbiosis-institute-of-management-studies) represents a powerful gateway to higher senior leadership positions.

In this review, we break down everything you need to know: fees, admission cutoffs, placements, pros, cons, and our honest expert verdict.

---

## 🏆 Program Overview

- **Official Course Name:** MBA Executive (Weekend)
- **Approximate Fees:** ₹4.50 Lakhs (Total Fees)
- **Entrance Exams Accepted:** SIMS Entrance Assessment + Personal Interview
- **Course Duration:** 15 Months to 2 Years (Format varies by program)

---

## 📈 Eligibility & Admission Details

- **Academic Requirements:** Graduation + minimum 2 years of work experience (with special reservation for Defense personnel).
- **Selection Process:** Admission typically relies on professional profile screening, statement of purpose (SOP), executive experience reviews, and personal interviews. Elite IIM programs also mandate standard quantitative testing (GMAT/GRE).

---

## 💼 Placement Statistics & Career Impact

- **Estimated Placements:** Decent industry connect, average package range ₹8-10 LPA.
- **Career Growth:** Executive MBA degrees focus on helping candidates secure senior lateral shifts (like VP, Product Manager, or Director roles) or move from technical paths into core management.

---

## 📝 Student Reviews: Pros & Cons

### Pros
- **Highlight:** Highly reasonable fees for a Symbiosis program.
- **Highlight:** Specialized focus on corporate leadership and risk management.
- **Highlight:** Vibrant campus culture in Kirkee, Pune.

### Cons
- **Limitation:** Limited slots for general category candidates due to defense quotas.
- **Limitation:** Fewer high-tier consulting recruiter listings compared to SIBM.

---

## 🔍 Our Expert Verdict

The Executive MBA program at [Symbiosis [Institute of Management Studies](/colleges/institute-of-management-studies) (SIMS), Pune](/colleges/symbiosis-institute-of-management-studies) is highly recommended for professionals based in Pune who want to scale their careers without disrupting their current geographic setup. 

If you are looking for top-tier consulting placements and have 5+ years of experience, full-time residential paths are stellar. However, if you are looking to continue your full-time job, their weekend/evening classes offer outstanding return on investment.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is the SIMS Executive MBA open to civilians?
Yes, while SIMS has reservations for defense personnel and their dependents, a percentage of seats is open to general corporate civilian candidates.

### What is the fee structure for SIMS Executive MBA?
The total fee for the entire program is approximately ₹4.50 Lakhs, making it highly competitive.

### Are classes online or offline?
The program is primarily offline, with classes held on weekends at the Kirkee campus in Pune.

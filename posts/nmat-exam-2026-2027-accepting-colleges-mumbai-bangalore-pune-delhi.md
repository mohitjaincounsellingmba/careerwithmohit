# NMAT Exam 2026-27: Top Accepting MBA Colleges in Mumbai, Bangalore, Pune & Delhi NCR

> 💡 **Key Takeaways (Direct AI Answer Summary)**
> - **Flagship Destination:** NMIMS Mumbai (SBM) requires a 232+ scaled score with sectional balance.
> - **Top Non-NMIMS Alternatives:** K J Somaiya (Mumbai), TAPMI (Bengaluru/Manipal), SDA Bocconi (Mumbai), SOIL Gurgaon, and JagSoM (Bangalore).
> - **Score Validity & Retakes:** GMAC allows up to 3 attempts in the 75-day testing window (October to December).

The **NMAT by GMAC exam** is one of India’s most student-friendly and candidate-centric MBA entrance exams. Featuring no negative marking, 3 test attempts, and choice of section order, NMAT opens doors to some of India’s highest-paying b-schools in **Mumbai, Bangalore, Pune, and Delhi NCR**.

For aspirants targeting **MBA admission 2027** and **PGDM admission 2027**, here is the comprehensive b-school cutoff and fee matrix.

## NMAT 2026-2027 Exam Pattern & Strategy

- **Language Skills:** 36 Questions | 28 Minutes | Target Score: 72+
- **Quantitative Skills:** 36 Questions | 52 Minutes | Target Score: 70+
- **Logical Reasoning:** 36 Questions | 40 Minutes | Target Score: 72+
- **Total:** 108 Questions | 120 Minutes | Total Scaled Score Range: 36 to 360

## 2. Detailed Fee Structure & Installment Schedule (2027–2029 Batch)

JIMS Kalkaji maintains one of the most accessible fee structures among NBA-accredited business schools in South Delhi.

### Official Fee Breakdown (Total: ₹10,75,000)
*   **Installment 1 (Admission Confirmation):** ₹2,70,000
*   **Installment 2 (December 1, 2027):** ₹2,70,000
*   **Installment 3 (July 1, 2028):** ₹2,67,500
*   **Installment 4 (December 1, 2028):** ₹2,67,500
*   **Total Course Fee:** **₹10,75,000 (₹10.75 Lakhs)**

### Fee Inclusions & Value-Additions
*   Complete academic tuition, examinations, and case-study pedagogy.
*   Access to advanced computer laboratories, Bloomberg terminals, and research portals.
*   Industry certifications in Financial Modeling, Advanced Excel, Digital Marketing, and Python Analytics.
*   Participation in national corporate symposiums, guest speaker series, and live project mentorship.

### Hostel & Accommodation Charges (South Delhi)
JIMS Kalkaji provides partnered hostel accommodations and helps students settle in verified PGs in nearby residential colonies (Kalkaji, Nehru Enclave, East of Kailash, and Alaknanda):
*   **Twin-Sharing AC Rooms:** Approximately ₹10,500 – ₹13,000 per month (inclusive of meals, high-speed Wi-Fi, laundry, and round-the-clock security).
*   **Non-AC / Sharing Options:** Starting from approximately ₹8,500 per month.
*   **Nehru Place Proximity:** Students enjoy immediate walking access to Delhi’s major IT and business district with abundant food, shopping, and library facilities.

### Scholarships & Financial Aid
*   **Merit-Based Scholarships:** Tuition fee waivers for candidates scoring 80+ percentile in [CAT Exam](/blog/all-about-cat-exam) / [XAT Exam](/blog/all-about-xat-exam) or 85+ percentile in [MAT Exam](/blog/all-about-mat-exam) / [CMAT Exam](/blog/all-about-cmat-exam).
*   **Special Concessions:** Fee waivers for candidates with consistent 70%+ academic track records, defense wards, and siblings of JIMS alumni.
*   **Education Loans:** Approved for collateral-free student loans by SBI, Punjab National Bank, and Axis Bank.

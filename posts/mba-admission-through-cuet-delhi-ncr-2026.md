## Why Choose CUET for MBA?
- **Standardised Evaluation:** CUET provides a uniform benchmark across multiple universities.
- **Multiple Seats:** Both public and private institutions in Delhi NCR allocate a sizable quota for CUET‑score holders.
- **Flexibility:** Allows you to apply to a variety of specialisations (Finance, Marketing, HR, etc.) after the exam.

## Eligibility Criteria (2026)
| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Undergraduate Degree** | Minimum 50 % (45 % for SC/ST) in any recognised bachelor's program. |
| **Work Experience** | Not mandatory, but 1‑2 years adds a point in merit.
| **Age Limit** | Typically 21‑30 years, with relaxations for reserved categories. |

## Admission Process Overview
1. **Register for CUET** – Visit the official CUET portal, fill the application, and pay the fee (≈ ₹1,500).  
2. **Take the Exam** – Conducted in June 2026, covering Quantitative Aptitude, Verbal Ability, Logical Reasoning, and General Awareness.
3. **Obtain Score & Rank** – Scores are released in August; check the percentile to gauge your chances.
4. **Apply to Colleges** – Use the CUET score to fill application forms for preferred institutes (deadlines usually Sep‑Oct).
5. **Counselling & Seat Allocation** – Participate in the centralized counselling process; secure your seat based on rank and preferences.

## Application Tips for 2026
- **Start Early:** Begin the registration process by March to avoid last‑minute glitches.
- **Mock Tests:** Use CUET mock exams to fine‑tune time management and accuracy.
- **Document Checklist:** Keep marksheets, identity proof, and photographs ready for seamless uploads.
- **Leverage Work Experience:** Even a short internship can boost your profile during counselling.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Q:** *Can I apply to both CUET and CAT in the same year?*\
**A:** Yes. Many candidates use CUET as a backup while preparing for CAT.

**Q:** *What if my CUET percentile is below the cut‑off?*\
**A:** Consider applying to colleges with lower cut‑offs or explore alternative exams like XAT or MAT.

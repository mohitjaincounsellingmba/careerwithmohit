Malaysia is the **most affordable English-speaking study destination** in Asia for Indian students — offering world-class **international branch campuses** of UK and Australian universities at a fraction of the original cost. With a warm climate, multicultural environment, and a rapidly growing economy, Malaysia has become a strategic study destination for Indian students in 2026.

---

## 🎓 Why Study in Malaysia in 2026?

- **International Branch Campuses:** Monash, Nottingham, Southampton, Heriot-Watt — all at 30–40% lower tuition than UK/Australia
- **English Medium:** All programs taught in English
- **Affordable Living Costs:** ₹30,000–₹50,000/month (much cheaper than UK/Australia)
- **MQA Accreditation:** Globally recognized Malaysian Qualifications Agency
- **Strategic Location:** Gateway to ASEAN job markets
- **Warm Indian Community:** Large Tamil, Telugu, and Hindi-speaking diaspora

## 🛂 Malaysia Student Visa Process

- **Apply:** Through university after receiving acceptance letter
- **Processing Time:** 4–8 weeks
- **Documents:** Passport, acceptance letter, health certificate, financial proof
- **Work Rights:** Part-time 20 hours/week during semester
- **Post-Study:** Apply for employment pass to stay and work

---

## 🎯 Admission Requirements for Malaysia

- **English Proficiency:** IELTS 5.5–6.5 / TOEFL 61–80 / Duolingo 90+
- **Academic Transcripts:** Min. 60% in 12th standard
- **SOP / Personal Statement**
- **Financial Proof:** USD 1,000/month approx.
- **Medical Examination** — Required for Malaysia Student Pass

### What is the role of a career counsellor?

### How important is NAAC accreditation for a university?

## **Admission Process & CUET Cutoffs 2026**

Admissions to DU's undergraduate programs are strictly conducted through the **Common University Entrance Test (CUET)**. Following the exam, students must register on the Common Seat Allocation System (CSAS) portal to select their course and college preferences.

*   **Top Tier Competition:** For elite colleges like SRCC, Hindu, St. Stephen's, and Miranda House, the competition is intense. Courses like B.Com (Hons), Economics (Hons), and Political Science (Hons) typically require scores in the **98th–99th percentile** (often 750+ marks out of 800).
*   **Off-Campus Colleges:** Cutoffs are relatively more accessible, providing excellent opportunities for students with slightly lower scores to still earn a coveted DU degree.

## **Fees and Financials**

DU is celebrated for its highly affordable education. Regular Arts, Commerce, and Science programs usually range between **₹15,000 and ₹50,000 annually**. However, self-financed or professional courses like B.Tech and BMS may have higher fee structures. The exceptional return on investment (ROI) makes DU unparalleled among Indian universities.

## **Placements and Career Opportunities**

DU boasts robust placement cells in top colleges, alongside a Central Placement Cell (CPC). Students are heavily recruited for roles in consulting, finance, analytics, and IT. Top-tier colleges frequently see average packages in double digits, offering a massive ROI given the low tuition fees. 

## **Hostel Admission**

While DU provides hostel facilities, availability is quite limited compared to the massive student intake. 
*   **Process:** Hostel admission is distinct from course admission. Once admitted to a college, outstation students must apply separately for college-specific hostels or university halls.
*   **Merit-Based:** Allotment is highly merit-based, factoring in CUET scores and sometimes personal interviews.

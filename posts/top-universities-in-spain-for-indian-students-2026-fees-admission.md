Spain is rapidly emerging as a **top European study destination** for Indian students — offering vibrant culture, sunny weather, English-taught programs, and some of Europe's most innovative business schools. From the globally ranked **IESE Business School** to affordable private universities in Barcelona and Madrid, Spain has something for every budget and academic aspiration in 2026.

---

## 🎓 Why Study in Spain in 2026?

- **Affordable Tuition:** Many programs cost under ₹15 Lakhs/year
- **English-Taught Programs:** Growing rapidly, especially in Barcelona and Madrid
- **Rich Cultural Experience:** Art, architecture, food, and lifestyle
- **Gateway to EU:** Work in any EU country after graduating
- **Business & Sports Management Hub:** Unique programs like Real Madrid Graduate School
- **Non-Lucrative Residence Visa** — Students can apply for long-stay visas

## 🛂 Spain Student Visa & Post-Study Options

- **Student Visa (Type D):** Long-stay visa for programs over 6 months
- **Required Documents:** University acceptance, financial proof (€564.90/month), health insurance
- **After Graduation:** Apply for **job search visa** or transition to work permit
- **Schengen Zone:** Travel freely across 27 European countries as a student

---

## 🎯 Admission Requirements for Spain

- **English Proficiency:** IELTS 6.0–6.5 / TOEFL 80+ / Duolingo 100+
- **Academic Transcripts** (Apostilled if required)
- **SOP / Motivation Letter**
- **Proof of Financial Means:** Min. €564.90/month
- **Health Insurance:** Valid for Spain
- **Housing Proof:** Required for visa application

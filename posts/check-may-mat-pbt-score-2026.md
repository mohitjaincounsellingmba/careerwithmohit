## What is the MAT PBT Mode?

The **Paper-Based Test (PBT)** is the traditional pen-and-paper format of the Management Aptitude Test conducted by the **All India Management Association (AIMA)**. Unlike the CBT (Computer-Based Test) or IBT (Internet-Based Test), the PBT is conducted at **designated test centres** across India.

Key facts about the PBT mode:
- **Duration:** 2.5 hours (150 minutes)
- **Total Questions:** 150 MCQs
- **Sections:** Language Comprehension (30), Intelligence & Critical Reasoning (30), Mathematical Skills (30), Data Analysis & Sufficiency (30), Indian & Global Environment (30)
- **Marking:** +1 for correct, −0.25 for wrong
- **Composite Score:** Out of 800 (IGE section excluded from composite)

## How to Check MAT May 2026 PBT Score: Step-by-Step

Once AIMA officially releases the PBT result, follow these steps to access your scorecard:

### Step 1: Go to the Official AIMA MAT Portal
Visit **[mat.aima.in](https://mat.aima.in)** — this is the **only authentic source** for your MAT scorecard. Avoid any third-party websites claiming to show results early.

### Step 2: Click on "Candidate Login"
On the homepage, locate the **Candidate Login** section. Do not use social login or third-party shortcuts.

### Step 3: Enter Your Credentials
Log in using:
- **Registered Email ID** (the one used during MAT registration)
- **Password**

Forgot your password? Click **"Forgot Password"** and reset it using your registered email.

### Step 4: Access Your Dashboard
After logging in, your candidate dashboard will load. Once the result is published, you will see:
- **Scorecard/Result** option in the left or top navigation menu
- Your **Section-wise scores**
- Your **Composite Score (out of 800)**
- Your **Percentile**

### Step 5: Download Your Scorecard PDF
- Click **"Download Scorecard"**
- Save the PDF on your device
- **Print at least 2–3 copies** — one for your records and one to attach with college applications

## Understanding Your MAT PBT Scorecard

Your MAT PBT scorecard contains several important fields:

| Component | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Candidate Name & Roll No.** | Your identity details |
| **Test Mode** | Paper-Based Test (PBT) |
| **Section-wise Raw Scores** | Individual scores for LC, ICR, MS, DA, IGE |
| **Composite Score** | Total out of 800 (LC + ICR + MS + DA scaled) |
| **Percentile** | Your relative performance vs. all test-takers |

> **Note:** The **Indian & Global Environment (IGE)** score appears on your scorecard but is **excluded from the composite score** for merit purposes. Always check individual college criteria, as a few institutes may factor in IGE for shortlisting.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1. When will MAT May 2026 PBT result be declared?**  
The MAT May 2026 PBT result (exam date: May 31) is expected between **June 14–20, 2026**.

**Q2. Where can I check my MAT PBT scorecard?**  
Only at the official AIMA portal: **[mat.aima.in](https://mat.aima.in)** → Candidate Login → Dashboard → Scorecard.

**Q3. Is the scorecard sent by email?**  
No. AIMA does not email or post scorecards. You must log in and download it yourself.

**Q4. Can I use my MAT PBT score for CBT-mode colleges?**  
Yes. MAT score is mode-agnostic — colleges accept scores regardless of whether you took PBT, CBT, or IBT.

**Q5. How long is the MAT PBT score valid?**  
Your score is valid for **12 months** from the date of declaration.

**Q6. What is the maximum composite score in MAT?**  
The maximum composite score is **800** (200 per section × 4 sections: LC, ICR, MS, DA).

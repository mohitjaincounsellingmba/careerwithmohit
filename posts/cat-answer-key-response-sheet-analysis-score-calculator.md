## 📥 Step-by-Step: How to Download CAT Response Sheet

1.  Visit the official IIM CAT portal: **`iimcat.ac.in`**.
2.  Click on the **'Registered Candidate Login'** button under CAT 2026.
3.  Enter your **User ID** and **Password** (sent via SMS/Email during registration).
4.  Navigate to the tab labeled **'Candidate Response'**.
5.  Click on the link stating: *"Click here to generate your response sheet for CAT 2026"*.
6.  Your complete question paper will display on screen showing:
    *   **Question ID & Text**
    *   **Status** (Answered / Not Answered / Marked for Review)
    *   **Chosen Option** by the candidate
    *   **Correct Option** verified by the conducting IIM

## 🧮 How to Accurately Calculate Your CAT Raw Score

The CAT scoring algorithm distinguishes between Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) and Type In The Answer (TITA) questions:

$$\text{Raw Score} = (\text{Total Correct Questions} \times 3) - (\text{Incorrect MCQs} \times 1)$$

### Sectional Calculation Template

| Metric | VARC (Max 72) | DILR (Max 60) | QA (Max 66) | Total (Max 198) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Correct Attempts (+3)** | $\text{Count} \times 3$ | $\text{Count} \times 3$ | $\text{Count} \times 3$ | Sum (+Marks) |
| **Incorrect MCQs (-1)** | $\text{Count} \times (-1)$ | $\text{Count} \times (-1)$ | $\text{Count} \times (-1)$ | Sum (-Marks) |
| **Incorrect TITA (0)** | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ |
| **Unattempted (0)** | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ |
| **Net Sectional Raw Score** | **VARC Score** | **DILR Score** | **QA Score** | **Total Raw Score** |

## ⚖️ Objection Raising: Process, Fees & Refund Rules

If you identify an error in the provisional answer key (e.g., an incorrect official answer key option, ambiguous question phrasing, or multiple correct choices), you can submit an official challenge.

```
       ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
       │              CAT Objection Filing Workflow              │
       └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                   │
         ┌─────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────┐
         │                         │                         │
         ▼                         ▼                         ▼
   1. Select Question       2. Upload Supporting      3. Pay ₹1,200 Fee
      & Section ID             Proof / Calculation       (Refundable if valid)
```

### Key Objection Rules:
*   **Objection Fee:** **INR 1,200 per question** plus transaction charges.
*   **Document Proof:** You must upload a clear reference (standard textbook proof, derivation, or standard grammatical authority).
*   **Refund Policy:** If the expert committee accepts your objection:
    *   The answer key is officially revised for all candidates.
    *   Your full fee of INR 1,200 per accepted question is refunded to the source payment method.
*   **No Objections Allowed on TITA Numeric Formatting:** Ensure you followed the exact decimal and integer conventions specified in the exam instructions.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### When is the CAT answer key and response sheet released?
The CAT provisional answer key and individual candidate response sheets are typically released by IIMs within 4 to 7 days after the exam (usually the first week of December).

### How can I calculate my raw score using the CAT response sheet?
For each correct MCQ or non-MCQ (TITA), add +3 marks. For each incorrect MCQ, deduct -1 mark. Incorrect TITA questions carry 0 negative marks. Total raw score is (Correct Attempts * 3) - (Incorrect MCQs * 1).

### What is the fee to challenge an answer key question in CAT?
Candidates must pay an objection fee of INR 1,200 per challenged question. If your objection is reviewed and upheld by the CAT committee, the full fee is refunded.

### Will my percentile be the same as my raw score percentage?
No. Your raw score is first normalized across all three slots into a scaled score, and your percentile is calculated based on your relative performance against all candidates.

### What should I do immediately after checking my CAT response sheet?
Calculate your sectional and overall raw scores, check college application deadlines for non-IIMs (like MDI, SPJIMR, FMS, IMT, FORE, GIM) which close before CAT results, and start preparing for WAT-GD-PI.

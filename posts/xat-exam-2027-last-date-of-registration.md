## XAT 2027 Application Fee Structure

XLRI collects application fees directly through its integrated payment portal. Candidates can pay via Net Banking, Credit Card, Debit Card, or UPI:

* **XAT 2027 Base Registration Fee (All Candidates):** ₹2,200
* **Fee for XLRI Programmes (Optional):** ₹200 per programme
  * *Example:* XAT Base Fee + XLRI Business Management (BM) = ₹2,400
  * *Example:* XAT Base Fee + XLRI BM + XLRI Human Resource Management (HRM) = ₹2,600
* **Indian Candidates Applying through GMAT / GRE (for XLRI):** ₹2,500
* **NRI / Foreign Candidates Applying through GMAT:** ₹5,000

## Eligibility Criteria for XAT 2027

1. **Academic Qualification:** Candidates must have completed a Bachelor’s degree of minimum three years' duration in any discipline from a recognized university in India or abroad.
2. **Final Year Students:** Students in the final year of their bachelor's degree who expect to complete their final examination by June 10, 2027, are fully eligible to apply.
3. **No Minimum Aggregate Requirement:** Unlike CAT (which mandates 50%), XAT does not specify a strict minimum percentage in graduation to register, though participating institutes (like XLRI) maintain internal graduation cutoff policies.
4. **No Age Limit:** There is no age restriction or attempt limit for taking the XAT examination.

## How to Apply for XAT 2027: Step-by-Step Instructions

Follow these five steps to complete your registration without errors:

### Step 1: Register on `xatonline.in`
* Visit `xatonline.in` and click on **"Register 2027"**.
* Fill in your Name, Email ID, Mobile Number, State, and City, and set a strong password.
* Verify your email to receive your unique **XAT ID** and login credentials.

### Step 2: Fill Personal, Contact & Address Details
* Log in using your XAT ID and password.
* Complete personal details: Date of Birth, Gender, Blood Group, Father's/Mother's name, and complete permanent and correspondence addresses.

### Step 3: Fill Academic Details & Work Experience
* Enter your Class 10, Class 12, and Graduation degrees, subjects, universities, passing years, and aggregate marks.
* Enter completed full-time work experience (in months) as of the cutoff date.

### Step 4: Choose XLRI Programmes & Test Cities
* Select whether you wish to apply to XLRI programmes (PGDM-BM, PGDM-HRM, or Innovation, Entrepreneurship & Venture Creation).
* Select up to **two preferred test cities** from the list of 100+ cities nationwide.

### Step 5: Upload Scanned Documents & Pay Registration Fee
* Upload your photograph and signature in the prescribed dimensions.
* Review the full application preview to ensure zero typographical errors.
* Pay the application fee through the online payment gateway.
* Download the **XAT 2027 Application Confirmation PDF** and print a copy for your records.

## Unique Features of the XAT Exam Pattern

Unlike CAT, XAT tests candidates across unique parameters:

1. **Decision Making (DM) Section:** A 21-question section testing ethical, administrative, and corporate problem-solving logic.
2. **Negative Marking on Unattempted Questions:** In Part 1, after **8 consecutive unattempted questions**, a penalty of **-0.10 marks per unattempted question** applies.
3. **General Knowledge & Analytical Essay:** Evaluated only during XLRI's personal interview stage, meaning GK percentiles do not affect the primary Part 1 shortlist cutoff.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the XAT exam 2027 last date of registration?
The standard registration deadline for XAT 2027 is expected to fall between **November 30 and December 5, 2026** (by 11:59 PM). XLRI frequently provides an extended window up to the second week of December.

### Can I apply to XLRI after the XAT registration closes?
No. You must select and pay for XLRI programmes during your XAT registration. Once the registration portal closes, candidates cannot add XLRI applications.

### What is the fee for XAT 2027?
The base fee is ₹2,200. Each additional XLRI programme selected requires an extra fee of ₹200.

### What is the XAT 2027 exam date?
XAT 2027 is scheduled for **Sunday, January 3, 2027**, conducted in a single afternoon session from 2:00 PM to 5:30 PM.

## 1. Institutional Background: The Global Scale Advantage of Amity Noida

Amity University Noida, situated on a 60-acre campus in Sector 125, Noida (Delhi NCR), is one of India's largest private multidisciplinary research universities. Its flagship business school, **Amity Business School (ABS)**, established in 1995, was among the first private management institutions in Delhi NCR to incorporate international corporate curriculum frameworks.

Accredited with an **'A+' Grade by NAAC** and recognized internationally by the **WASC Senior College and University Commission (USA)** and **IACBE (USA)**, Amity provides global scale. Its location directly on the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway places it adjacent to corporate tech parks housing multinational technology firms, consulting majors, and financial institutions.

For candidates who value world-class campus facilities, diverse global student cohorts, extensive student clubs, and direct access to hundreds of visiting campus hiring teams, Amity Business School represents an expansive management launchpad.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the total fee structure for the MBA program at Amity University Noida?
The total 2-year tuition fee for the flagship MBA program at Amity University Noida (Sector 125 campus) ranges between ₹15.50 Lakhs and ₹16.80 Lakhs, payable across 4 academic semesters. Specialized tracks such as 3-Continent MBA or International Business carry additional overseas immersion charges.

### What is the average and highest placement package at Amity University Noida?
Amity University recorded an overall average domestic CTC of ₹6.50 to ₹8.20 LPA during recent placement drives, with top performers and tech analytics specialists securing domestic packages up to ₹25.00 LPA and peak international offers touching ₹61.75 LPA.

### Which entrance exams are accepted for MBA admission at Amity University Noida?
Amity accepts scores from CAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT, NMAT, and GMAT. Candidates meeting high percentile criteria (e.g., 80%+ in CAT/MAT) are exempted from the written test and proceed directly to the video/personal interview round.

### Does Amity University Noida offer merit-based scholarships for MBA students?
Yes, Amity awards 100% tuition fee scholarships to candidates scoring 90+ percentile in CAT/MAT or 650+ in GMAT, 50% scholarship for 85+ percentile in CAT/MAT, and 25% scholarship for 75+ percentile in CAT/MAT.

### Is direct admission or management quota available at Amity University Noida?
Yes, Amity conducts profile-based direct evaluation admissions for candidates with consistent 60%+ undergraduate academic records, valid entrance test scores, and strong personal interview performance.

The **Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) RAS/RTS (Rajasthan Administrative Service / Rajasthan Taxation Service)** exam is the most coveted state civil services examination in Rajasthan. Conducted by RPSC, it recruits candidates into elite Rajasthan administrative roles including **District Collectors, Sub-Divisional Magistrates, District Administrative Officers, Block Development Officers**, and other Group A & B state service roles.

The RAS Prelims is the first and largest competitive filter — with lakhs of aspirants appearing for a few hundred posts. Take our **[Free RPSC RAS Prelims 2026 Mock Test](/tools/mock-test/rpsc-ras)** to benchmark yourself.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the pattern of RPSC RAS Prelims?
RPSC RAS Prelims features a single paper on General Knowledge and General Science consisting of 150 questions carrying 200 marks, with a duration of 3 hours.

### Is there negative marking in the RAS Prelims exam?
Yes, there is a negative marking of 1/3rd (-0.33) of the mark of the question for every incorrect answer.

### How many marks are required to clear RAS Prelims?
Historically, clearing RAS Prelims requires a score around 80-85 marks out of 200 for general category candidates.

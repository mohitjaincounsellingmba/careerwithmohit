## 2. Management Programs Offered at SOIL Gurgaon

SOIL offers tailored programs structured around diverse career stages:

### A. 2-Year Full-Time PGDM (Business Design)
*   **Target Audience:** Fresh college graduates and early-career professionals (0 to 36 months of work experience).
*   **Core Focus:** AICTE-approved flagship management curriculum integrating **Design Thinking, Liberal Arts, Data Science, and Core Business Disciplines** (Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations, Business Analytics).
*   **Campus:** Modern Manesar & Gurgaon campuses with experiential learning studios.

### B. 1-Year Full-Time PGPM (Business Leadership)
*   **Target Audience:** Working professionals with a minimum of **2+ years** of full-time professional work experience.
*   **Core Focus:** Accelerated, high-intensity general management program designed for fast-track career pivots into senior managerial and consulting roles.

### C. 1-Year Full-Time PGPM-HR (Human Resources Leadership)
*   **Target Audience:** Candidates with prior experience or a deep interest in HR strategy, talent transformation, and organizational psychology.
*   **Core Focus:** Co-designed with senior Chief Human Resources Officers (CHROs) from Fortune 500 corporations.

## 5. Eligibility Criteria for 2027–29 Admissions

Before submitting the application form, verify that you satisfy the following conditions:

1.  **Academic Qualification:** A recognized Bachelor’s degree in any discipline (Arts, Science, Commerce, Engineering, etc.) with a minimum aggregate score of **50% marks** (or equivalent CGPA).
2.  **Final Year Students:** Final-year undergraduate students are eligible to apply, provided they complete all graduation requirements and submit proof of 50% aggregate by the designated institute deadline.
3.  **Qualifying Entrance Exam:** A valid test score in any one of the following national exams:
    *   **CAT (2026 / 2025)**
    *   **XAT (2027 / 2026)**
    *   **GMAT / GRE**
    *   **NMAT by GMAC**
    *   **CMAT / MAT / ATMA**
4.  **Work Experience:**
    *   **For 2-Year PGDM:** Open to freshers as well as applicants with up to 3 years of work experience.
    *   **For 1-Year PGPM:** Minimum of **24 months** of full-time post-qualification work experience required.

## 6. Selection Process & Weightage Criteria

SOIL does not rely exclusively on entrance test percentiles; it adopts a **holistic profile-evaluation model**:

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------+
|             SOIL GURGAON ADMISSION WEIGHTAGE BREAKDOWN        |
+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| Evaluation Component                               | Weightage |
+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| Entrance Exam Scores (CAT/XAT/GMAT/NMAT/CMAT)       |    35%    |
| Design Thinking Workshop (Group Exercise) & PI     |    40%    |
| Academic Consistency (10th, 12th & Graduation)     |    15%    |
| Work Experience & Extracurricular Leadership       |    10%    |
+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| Total                                              |   100%    |
+---------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Key Stages in the Selection Journey:

1.  **Stage 1: Online Application Submission**
    *   Fill out academic credentials, entrance test IDs, statement of purpose, and extracurricular details.
    *   Pay the application fee of **₹2,000**.
2.  **Stage 2: Profile Shortlisting**
    *   The admissions board screens applications based on academic trajectory, clarity of purpose, and entrance test performance.
3.  **Stage 3: Design Thinking Workshop (Group Exercise)**
    *   Unlike traditional stressful GDs, SOIL organizes a **Design Thinking Group Workshop** where candidates collaborate in teams to ideate and prototype solutions for real-world business and social problems.
4.  **Stage 4: Personal Interview (PI)**
    *   A 25–30 minute conversational interview with faculty and senior industry leaders assessing self-awareness, communication agility, leadership potential, and cultural fit.
5.  **Stage 5: Final Offer Letter & Admission Confirmation**
    *   Selected candidates receive provisional admission offers and can reserve their seats by paying the initial commitment deposit.

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. Is the SOIL Gurgaon application form open for the 2027–29 batch?
Yes, admissions are open for the 2-Year PGDM (2027–2029) and 1-Year PGPM programs. Candidates can register and apply online through the official portal.

### Q2. What is the total fee for the PGDM program at SOIL Gurgaon?
The total 2-year program fee is approximately **₹16.35 Lakhs to ₹17.20 Lakhs**, which includes tuition, academic materials, student activities, and a ₹30,000 refundable security deposit.

### Q3. Does SOIL Gurgaon accept CMAT, MAT, or NMAT scores?
Yes, SOIL accepts scores from **CAT, XAT, GMAT, NMAT, CMAT, MAT, and ATMA**. Candidates without a CAT score can apply using any of these national test scores.

### Q4. What is the average salary package at SOIL Gurgaon?
The average CTC stands at **₹11.17 LPA** for the 2-Year PGDM and **₹12.30 LPA** for the 1-Year PGPM, with the highest CTC reaching **₹20.70 LPA to ₹21.80 LPA**.

### Q5. Is work experience mandatory for the 2-year PGDM at SOIL?
No, the 2-Year PGDM is open to both **fresh graduates** and candidates with up to **36 months of work experience**. For the 1-Year PGPM, a minimum of 2 years of work experience is required.

### Q6. What is the Design Thinking Workshop in the SOIL admission process?
Instead of a conventional debate-oriented Group Discussion, SOIL conducts a hands-on **Design Thinking Workshop** where candidates work collaboratively on a real-world problem statement, demonstrating creative problem solving, teamwork, and innovation.

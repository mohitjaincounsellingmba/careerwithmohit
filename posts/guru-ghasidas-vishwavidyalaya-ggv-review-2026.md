Guru Ghasidas Vishwavidyalaya (GGV) in Bilaspur is a reputed Central University offering extremely affordable education without compromising on academic quality. With a sprawling campus and growing placement records, it is a top target for CUET 2026 aspirants.

### 📊 GGV 2026 Key Highlights

| Category | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Admission Exams** | CUET UG, CUET PG, JEE Main (B.Tech) |
| **UG/PG Annual Fees** | ₹500 - ₹40,000 |
| **B.Tech Total Fees** | ~₹1.1 Lakh - ₹1.4 Lakh |
| **Average Package** | ₹4.0 - ₹7.0 LPA |
| **Highest Package** | Up to ₹18 LPA (Special cases up to ₹64 LPA) |
| **Placement Rate** | 55% - 80% |

### **Admission & CUET Cutoff**
*   **Process:** UG admissions use CUET UG scores, while PG relies on CUET PG. B.Tech is through JEE Main, and Ph.D. via VRET or UGC-NET/JRF.
*   **CUET Cutoff Expected:** Varies widely by course. Competitive programs like BA (Hons) Hindi often require 280–342 marks. B.Sc (Hons) in Chemistry/CS usually see cutoffs in the 70–160 marks range for the General category.

### **Fees Structure**
GGV boasts one of the most budget-friendly fee structures in India:
*   **UG & PG Programs:** Generally cost between ₹500 to ₹12,000 per year, though some PG courses might touch up to ₹40,000/year.
*   **B.Tech:** Highly affordable at around ₹1.1 lakh to ₹1.4 lakh for the entire 4-year degree.

### **Placements Overview**
*   **Success Rate:** Between 55% to 80% of students in professional and technical courses get placed.
*   **Salary Stats:** The average package is generally between ₹4 LPA and ₹7 LPA. The highest packages typically range from ₹12 LPA to ₹18 LPA, with rare exceptional cases reaching an impressive ₹64 LPA for B.Tech CSE.
*   **Top Recruiters:** Amazon, TCS, Wipro, Tech Mahindra, Infosys, and ICICI Bank.

### **Hostel Facilities**
*   **Availability:** Hostel seats are allocated strictly on a merit basis due to limited availability.
*   **Fees:** Excellent affordability with annual fees ranging from ₹7,000 to ₹12,000 (excluding mess charges).
*   **Amenities:** Standard facilities including beds, water coolers, recreation rooms, and Wi-Fi access.

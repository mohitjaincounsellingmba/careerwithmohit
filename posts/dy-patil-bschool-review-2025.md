DY Patil B-School is one of Pune's most modern and rapidly growing management institutes. Located strategically near the Hinjewadi IT hub, it offers a high-tech learning environment and a strong focus on industry readiness.

### **Quick Highlights (2025 Batch):**
*   **Avg Placement**: ₹7.0 - ₹8.0 LPA
*   **Highest Package**: ₹18.00 LPA+
*   **Placement Rate**: ~90%+
*   **Entrance Exam**: CAT / MAT / CMAT / MAH CET / ATMA

### **Why Choose DY Patil B-School?**
*   **Global Immersion**: Offers international study tours and global immersion programs to broaden students' perspectives.
*   **Infrastructure**: State-of-the-art labs, digital libraries, and smart classrooms that rival top global B-schools.
*   **Corporate Connect**: Benefit from the massive DY Patil legacy, which attracts hundreds of companies every year for placements and internships.

### **Fee Structure:**
The total fee for the two-year program ranges from **₹8.5 Lakhs to ₹11.0 Lakhs**, depending on the chosen specialization. It is a solid investment for students looking for a premium experience in one of Pune's educational powerhouses.

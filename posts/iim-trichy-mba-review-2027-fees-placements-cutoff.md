## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the fee structure for the PGPM program at IIM Trichy?
The total course fee for the two-year full-time PGPM / PGPM-HR program at IIM Trichy is approximately **₹19.50 Lakhs**, covering academic tuition, single-occupancy hostel accommodations, digital library access, and course case studies.

### 2. What is the average placement package at IIM Trichy?
The audited average placement package at IIM Trichy stands between **₹19.40 LPA and ₹20.55 LPA**, with the median CTC benchmarked at **₹19.50 LPA** and the top 10% average reaching **₹31.80 LPA**.

### 3. Does IIM Trichy offer a specialized program in Human Resources?
Yes. IIM Trichy offers the Post Graduate Programme in Human Resources (PGPM-HR), designed to develop strategic HR leaders with specialized coursework in talent analytics, labor relations, and organizational design.

### 4. What are the summer internship stipend figures at IIM Trichy?
The average two-month summer internship stipend at IIM Trichy stands at **₹1.30 Lakhs**, with the highest stipend offered reaching **₹3.20 Lakhs** for the two-month period.

### 5. Can I get direct admission in IIM Trichy without CAT?
No. Direct admission, management quota, or NRI quota seats do not exist at IIM Trichy. Admission is strictly based on CAT merit, IIM CAP interview evaluation, and profile composite scores.

Alliance University, Bengaluru, is a top-ranked private university in India, known for its flagship Alliance School of Business. With its beautiful 50-acre green campus and international focus, it is a premier choice for BBA aspirants in 2026 who want a global management education experience.

## 🏛️ Why Choose Alliance University Bengaluru in 2026?
Alliance School of Business is one of the few in India with international accreditations like IACBE, ensuring a high-quality global curriculum.
- **International Accreditation:** Global recognition of its management programs.
- **Green Campus:** One of the most beautiful and sustainable university campuses in India.
- **Industry Immersion:** Strong focus on internships, live projects, and interactions with global business leaders.

## 💰 Latest Fee Structure (2026)
As a premium private university, the fees reflect its high-end infrastructure and faculty. For 2026:
- **Annual Tuition Fee:** ₹2,50,000 – ₹3,00,000 per year.
- **Note:** Fees may vary depending on the specialization (General, International Business, Marketing, Finance).
- **Hostel Fees:** ₹1.2 Lakhs – ₹2.0 Lakhs per year depending on the type of accommodation.

## 🚀 Placement Process & Statistics 2026
The Alliance Career Management Center (ACMC) works with over 600 companies to provide placement opportunities to its students.
- **Average Package:** ₹5.8 LPA
- **Highest Package:** ₹14 LPA (for BBA).
- **Top Recruiters:** Amazon, Deloitte, EY, KPMG, Oracle, Tata Motors, HUL.
- **Placement Process:** Includes skill development workshops, resume shortlisting, and final interview rounds conducted on campus.

## 📝 Admission Process 2026 (AUSAT)
Admission to the BBA program at Alliance University is through the **AUSAT (Alliance University Scholastic Aptitude Test)**.
- **Exam Pattern:** General Knowledge, Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, and English Proficiency.
- **Selection Process:** AUSAT Score + Personal Interview (PI).
- **Note:** Candidates with high scores in national exams like CUET may be exempted from AUSAT.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Q1. Is AUSAT mandatory for BBA admission?**
While AUSAT is the primary exam, students with high scores in CUET or other recognized national entrance tests might be eligible for direct interviews.

**Q2. Does Alliance University have a dress code?**
Yes, Alliance University typically has a professional dress code for its management students to prepare them for the corporate world.

**Q3. Is the BBA degree from Alliance University recognized globally?**
Yes, due to its international accreditations and UGC recognition, the BBA degree from Alliance is widely accepted for higher studies and jobs globally.

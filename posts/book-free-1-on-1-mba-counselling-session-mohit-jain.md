## Common Myths Busted During Mohit's Counselling Sessions

### Myth 1: "I scored 65 percentile in CAT, my MBA career is over."
**The Reality:** Over 40% of premier AICTE-approved and university business schools accept scores from CMAT, MAT, ATMA, or internal entrance tests. Additionally, numerous top private institutions evaluate the overall candidate profile rather than relying solely on raw CAT scores.

### Myth 2: "All colleges charging ₹20 Lakhs offer ₹18 LPA placements."
**The Reality:** Many tier-2 and tier-3 colleges artificially inflate their average packages by combining off-campus figures or quoting CTC packages bloated with retention bonuses and non-cash ESOPs. Mohit breaks down the real in-hand monthly payout you can expect upon graduation.

### Myth 3: "Management Quota admission is illegal or dangerous."
**The Reality:** Legitimate institutional quota seats are approved by regulatory bodies (such as state CET cells, UGC, and AICTE) with clear merit and eligibility guidelines. The danger lies in unauthorized agents who claim to alter entrance exam scores. Mohit guides students exclusively through official, transparent institutional admission routes.

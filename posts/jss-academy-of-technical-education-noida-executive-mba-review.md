Running a career upgrade requires choosing the right management program. For working professionals in Noida, the Executive MBA / Executive PGDM offered by [JSS Academy of Technical Education, Noida](/colleges/jssate-noida) represents a powerful gateway to higher senior leadership positions.

In this review, we break down everything you need to know: fees, admission cutoffs, placements, pros, cons, and our honest expert verdict.

---

## 🏆 Program Overview

- **Official Course Name:** MBA for Working Professionals
- **Approximate Fees:** ₹2.50 Lakhs (Total Fees)
- **Entrance Exams Accepted:** CUET PG / AKTU Entrance + Personal Interview
- **Course Duration:** 15 Months to 2 Years (Format varies by program)

---

## 📈 Eligibility & Admission Details

- **Academic Requirements:** Graduation + minimum 2 years of work experience.
- **Selection Process:** Admission typically relies on professional profile screening, statement of purpose (SOP), executive experience reviews, and personal interviews. Elite IIM programs also mandate standard quantitative testing (GMAT/GRE).

---

## 💼 Placement Statistics & Career Impact

- **Estimated Placements:** Decent local placements, average CTC ~5-6 LPA.
- **Career Growth:** Executive MBA degrees focus on helping candidates secure senior lateral shifts (like VP, Product Manager, or Director roles) or move from technical paths into core management.

---

## 📝 Student Reviews: Pros & Cons

### Pros
- **Highlight:** Extremely affordable central university (AKTU) aligned fees.
- **Highlight:** Located in Sector 62, Noida, right in the middle of the IT park area.
- **Highlight:** Good academic discipline and strong corporate network in NCR.

### Cons
- **Limitation:** Infrastructure is primary and typical of engineering colleges.
- **Limitation:** Placement packages are relatively lower than business-only schools.

---

## 🔍 Our Expert Verdict

The Executive MBA program at [JSS Academy of Technical Education, Noida](/colleges/jssate-noida) is highly recommended for professionals based in Noida who want to scale their careers without disrupting their current geographic setup. 

If you are looking for top-tier consulting placements and have 5+ years of experience, full-time residential paths are stellar. However, if you are looking to continue your full-time job, their weekend/evening classes offer outstanding return on investment.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is JSS Noida MBA degree recognized?
Yes, JSS offers an MBA degree affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU) and approved by AICTE.

### Are classes held daily for the Working Professionals batch?
No, classes are scheduled primarily on weekends and in evening blocks to accommodate working schedules.

### What is the entrance exam accepted?
Admissions are made based on CUET PG or the university's counselling guidelines for working candidates.

Aligarh Muslim University (AMU) is a historic central university recognized for its excellent academic standards, grand architecture, and distinct cultural ethos. Offering a vast array of programs from engineering to medicine and humanities, AMU is a highly preferred destination for students across India. Here is a detailed review for the 2026 session.

## **Admission Process & Cutoffs 2026**

AMU follows a dual admission approach:
*   **AMU Entrance Tests:** For major professional courses like B.Tech (AMUEEE), MBA (AMU-CAT), and MBBS, the university conducts its own stringent entrance examinations.
*   **CUET UG Integration:** Several undergraduate programs, such as B.A. (Hons), B.Sc. (Hons), and B.Voc, now accept **CUET UG** scores.
*   **Selection:** Instead of traditional cutoffs, AMU releases merit lists (Firm Lists) based on entrance performance. A minimum aggregate in Class 12 (usually 45-50%) is mandatory. Students must register on the `amucontrollerexams.com` portal for counseling.

## **Fees Structure**

AMU is renowned for providing quality education at highly subsidized rates.
*   **Regular Academic Fees:** Typical UG and PG fees range from **₹13,000 to ₹25,000 annually**.
*   **Self-Financing Scheme (SFS):** Certain courses offered under the SFS have higher fees (up to a few lakhs) but remain economical compared to private universities.

## **Placements & Career Opportunities**

AMU’s Training and Placement Cell facilitates campus recruitment across various departments.
*   **Engineering & Management:** B.Tech and MBA students receive the best placement opportunities, with placement rates often touching 100% in core branches. Median packages typically range from **₹6 LPA to ₹10 LPA**.
*   **Top Companies:** Renowned organizations like Adobe, IBM, TCS, and prominent banking sector firms regularly participate in campus drives.

## **Hostel Admission and Campus Life**

AMU's residential life is a core part of its identity, featuring historic halls of residence.
*   **Availability:** While extensive, hostel accommodation is not guaranteed for all due to high demand. Regular students are given priority, whereas those in evening or part-time courses might not get accommodation.
*   **Process:** After admission, students undergo document verification and a physical fitness check to secure a hostel seat.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I check my exam results online?
You can check results on the official website of the conducting bodies (e.g., NTA for JEE/NEET/CUET, or respective boards/testing organizations) using your application registration number.

### Are mock tests helpful in exam preparation?
Yes, attempting mock tests helps candidates build speed and accuracy, understand the exam pattern, and perform detailed analytics of strong and weak sections.

### What is the significance of negative marking in entrance exams?
Many competitive exams deduct marks for wrong answers (e.g., -1 or -0.25). Candidates should avoid guessing to maintain accuracy and prevent score drops.

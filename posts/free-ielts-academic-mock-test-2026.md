## Understanding the Sections

### Listening Section (40 Questions)
The Listening section tests your ability to understand everyday conversations, monologues, and academic discussions. It is crucial to practice multi-tasking—listening, reading the questions, and writing the answers simultaneously.

### Reading Section (40 Questions)
The Reading section comprises three long texts ranging from the descriptive and factual to the discursive and analytical. These are taken from books, journals, magazines, and newspapers. Time management is key here, as you have only 60 minutes to read and answer.

## Tips for Scoring Band 7.0+
1. **Familiarize yourself with question types:** Make sure you know how to tackle *Matching Headings*, *Summary Completion*, and *Multiple Choice* questions.
2. **Practice under timed conditions:** Time pressure is the biggest challenge in the Reading section. Use our mock test to simulate exam conditions.
3. **Enhance your vocabulary:** A strong vocabulary is essential for both understanding the texts and paraphrasing in your answers.

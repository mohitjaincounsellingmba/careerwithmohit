The wait is finally over! **IIT Roorkee** has officially declared the **JEE Advanced 2026 results** on **May 31, 2026** — a day ahead of the originally scheduled date of June 1. Over 1.8 lakh students who appeared for the exam on **May 17, 2026** can now access their scorecards, All India Rank (AIR), and category-wise ranks on the official portal.

A total of **56,880 candidates** have qualified this year, including **10,107 female candidates** — a significant milestone in gender representation at the IIT level.

---

## How to Check JEE Advanced 2026 Result?

Follow these simple steps to download your JEE Advanced 2026 scorecard:

1. **Visit the Official Portal**: Go to [jeeadv.ac.in](https://jeeadv.ac.in)
2. **Click on the Result Link**: Find the "JEE Advanced 2026 Result / Rank Card" link on the homepage.
3. **Enter Login Details**: Use your **Roll Number**, **Date of Birth**, and **Registered Mobile Number**.
4. **View Your Scorecard**: Your subject-wise marks, aggregate score, AIR, and category rank will be displayed.
5. **Download & Save**: Download the PDF scorecard for use during JoSAA counselling and document verification.

> **Important**: Keep your scorecard safe — it is a mandatory document for all IIT/NIT/IIIT admission processes.

## JEE Advanced 2026 Qualifying Cutoff (Official)

To be included in the rank list, candidates must have met both the **aggregate minimum score** and **subject-wise minimum marks** in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. The official category-wise qualifying marks are:

*Note: Candidates must also secure the subject-wise minimum qualifying marks in each paper to be eligible for rank consideration.*

## What to Do After JEE Advanced 2026 Result?

### ✅ If You Have Qualified for IIT Admission

1. **Register on JoSAA Portal**: The **JoSAA 2026 counselling** process started from **June 2, 2026** at [josaa.nic.in](https://josaa.nic.in). Register immediately to avoid missing deadlines.
2. **Fill Your Choices Carefully**: List your preferred IIT branch and campus combinations in order of priority. Fill as many choices as possible.
3. **Check Mock Allotment**: Use the JoSAA mock rounds to predict your seat allotment and re-order your preferences if needed.
4. **Apply for AAT (if interested in B.Arch)**: If you are targeting IIT B.Arch programs, register for the **Architecture Aptitude Test (AAT) 2026** via the JEE Advanced candidate portal.

### ❌ If You Did Not Qualify

Don't be disheartened — this is not the end of the road. Here are your options:

- **JEE Main AIR**: Use your JEE Main 2026 rank for admissions to **NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs** via JoSAA/CSAB counselling.
- **BITSAT / VITEEE / SRMJEEE**: Many top private universities run their own entrance exams and offer excellent placements.
- **State Engineering Entrances**: MHT-CET, COMEDK, REAP, and other state-level exams have great college options.
- **Direct Admission Private BTech Colleges**: Several reputed institutions offer direct admissions with good academic environments.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I get direct admission in B.Tech without JEE Main?
Yes, several state-level entrance exams and private colleges offer direct admission based on Class 12th board marks or management quota seats.

### Which specialization has the highest placement package in B.Tech?
Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), along with emerging fields like Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Machine Learning (ML), and Data Science, consistently offer the highest placements.

### What is the difference between B.Tech and B.E.?
B.Tech (Bachelor of Technology) is generally more practical and application-oriented, whereas B.E. (Bachelor of Engineering) tends to focus more on theoretical concepts and foundation sciences.

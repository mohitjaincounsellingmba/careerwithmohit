The Management Aptitude Test (MAT) is one of the most widely accepted MBA entrance exams in India. Conducted by the All India Management Association (AIMA) in multiple cycles (February, May, September, and December), MAT is a popular choice for candidates who want to bypass the high difficulty of CAT/XAT while still targetting premium B-schools.

Pune, the 'Oxford of the East,' is one of India's most beloved student cities. It is a hub for the automobile, manufacturing, and IT industries, providing massive placement support to local B-schools. Several highly reputed institutes in Pune accept MAT scores.

---

## 🏛️ Top MBA/PGDM Colleges Accepting MAT in Pune

Here is the detailed overview of the top management institutes in Pune accepting MAT scores for the 2026 admission cycle:

### 1. PUMBA (Department of Management Sciences)
The official MBA department of Pune University, offering exceptional ROI and low fees.
- **MAT Cutoff Percentile:** 75+ Percentile
- **Approx Tuition Fees (2 Years):** ₹2.40 Lakhs
- **Average Placement Package:** ₹8.80 LPA

### 2. [PIBM Pune](/colleges/pibm-pune) (Pune Institute of Business Management)
Known for rigorous corporate training, industry-relevant profiles, and practical education.
- **MAT Cutoff Percentile:** 70+ Percentile
- **Approx Tuition Fees (2 Years):** ₹8.75 Lakhs
- **Average Placement Package:** ₹7.50 LPA

### 3. [RIIM Pune](/colleges/riim-pune) (Ramachandran International Institute)
Offers excellent PGDM + MBA packages with dual certifications and high placement focus.
- **MAT Cutoff Percentile:** 65+ Percentile
- **Approx Tuition Fees (2 Years):** ₹6.50 Lakhs
- **Average Placement Package:** ₹6.80 LPA

### 4. IIEBM - Indus Business School
Known for military-style discipline, professional grooming, and strong placement tie-ups.
- **MAT Cutoff Percentile:** 65+ Percentile
- **Approx Tuition Fees (2 Years):** ₹7.90 Lakhs
- **Average Placement Package:** ₹7.20 LPA

### 5. [ISMS Pune](/colleges/isms-pune)
Provides global business exposure and corporate training programs for all candidates.
- **MAT Cutoff Percentile:** 60+ Percentile
- **Approx Tuition Fees (2 Years):** ₹6.00 Lakhs
- **Average Placement Package:** ₹6.00 LPA

### 6. Lexicon MILE - Management Institute
An innovative, state-of-the-art campus featuring digital marketing labs and corporate integrations.
- **MAT Cutoff Percentile:** 65+ Percentile
- **Approx Tuition Fees (2 Years):** ₹9.00 Lakhs
- **Average Placement Package:** ₹7.30 LPA

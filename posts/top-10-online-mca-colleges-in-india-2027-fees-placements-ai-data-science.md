## Online MCA Eligibility & Admission Process (2027)

1. **Academic Eligibility**: Bachelor’s degree in BCA, B.Sc (Computer Science/IT), or any Bachelor's degree (B.Com/B.Sc/B.A/B.Tech) with Mathematics at the 10+2 or graduation level with minimum 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories).
2. **No Entrance Exam**: Most online universities offer direct merit-based admission based on graduation marks.
3. **Flexible Semester EMIs**: Split tuition fees into zero-cost monthly installments starting from ₹3,200/month.

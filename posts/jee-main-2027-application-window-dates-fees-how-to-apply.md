## When Does the JEE Main 2027 Application Window Open and Close?

**The official JEE Main 2027 Session 1 application window opens in late October or early November 2026 and closes in late November or early December 2026 on jeemain.nta.nic.in.** Candidates are granted approximately 30 days to complete registration, upload verified credentials, and submit application fees before the official correction window goes live.

NTA conducts JEE Main in **two independent sessions** to offer students multiple opportunities to improve their NTA percentiles. The best of the two scores is utilized to compute the final All India Rank (AIR) for JoSAA and CSAB counselling.

| Milestone / Event | Session 1 (January 2027) | Session 2 (April 2027) |

## What is the Category-Wise JEE Main 2027 Application Fee Structure?

**The JEE Main 2027 application fee for Indian exam centres is ₹1,000 for General and OBC-NCL Male candidates applying for Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech), ₹800 for Female candidates across all categories, and ₹500 for SC, ST, PwD, and Transgender applicants.** If applying for dual papers (e.g., B.Tech + B.Arch), the fee is exactly doubled.

The fee must be settled strictly through online payment gateways (State Bank of India, Canara Bank, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank) using UPI, Net Banking, Credit Cards, or Debit Cards. Cash deposits, demand drafts, or postal orders are not accepted.

### Comprehensive Fee Matrix (Indian vs. Foreign Test Centres)

| Category & Gender | Single Paper (Paper 1 or Paper 2A or 2B) | Dual Papers (Paper 1 + Paper 2A or 2B) | Foreign Exam Centre (Single Paper) | Foreign Exam Centre (Dual Papers) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **General / Gen-EWS / OBC-NCL (Male)** | ₹1,000 | ₹2,000 | ₹4,500 | ₹9,000 |
| **General / Gen-EWS / OBC-NCL (Female)** | ₹800 | ₹1,600 | ₹4,000 | ₹8,000 |
| **SC / ST / PwD / Transgender (Male/Female)** | ₹500 | ₹1,000 | ₹2,500 | ₹5,000 |

> ⚠️ **Important Payment Protocol**: Always ensure that you receive a **"Transaction Successful"** prompt and that your confirmation page status shifts to "Payment Received". If your account is debited but the confirmation page is not generated, do not initiate duplicate payments immediately; wait up to 24 hours for bank reconciliation or use the alternative payment gateway provided on the NTA dashboard.

## What Documents Are Required for the JEE Main 2027 Application Form?

**To register successfully during the JEE Main 2027 application window, candidates must upload a recent passport-sized photograph (10 KB–200 KB), scanned signature (4 KB–30 KB), and valid Category/PwD certificates (50 KB–300 KB in PDF format).** Having these scanned documents pre-formatted according to NTA's exact file standards prevents technical upload failures.

### Mandatory Document Specification Checklist

| Required Document | Permissible Format | File Size Limit | Key NTA Verification Guidelines |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Passport Size Photograph** | JPG / JPEG | 10 KB to 200 KB | Must be recent (taken on or after Sept 1, 2026), white background, 80% face visible without mask, ears clearly visible. Glasses allowed only if worn regularly; tinted glasses or caps strictly prohibited. |
| **Candidate's Signature** | JPG / JPEG | 4 KB to 30 KB | Must be signed in running handwriting using a black or dark blue ballpoint pen on unruled white paper. **Signatures in capital/block letters will lead to application rejection.** |
| **Category Certificate (Gen-EWS / OBC-NCL)** | PDF | 50 KB to 300 KB | Must be issued **on or after April 1, 2026**, as per Central Government format. State OBC certificates not listed in the Central OBC list are not eligible for central quota seats. |
| **SC / ST Certificate** | PDF | 50 KB to 300 KB | Central or State government issued competent authority caste certificate (no expiry restriction, but must be legible). |
| **PwD / UDID Certificate** | PDF | 50 KB to 300 KB | Disability certificate issued by a recognized government medical board or Chief Medical Officer confirming 40% or more disability. |
| **Government Identity Proof** | Verification Mode | Direct Digital Validation | Aadhaar Card, APAAR/ABC ID, Passport, PAN Card, or Digilocker account credentials for instant biometric matching. |

## Step-by-Step Guide: How to Fill the JEE Main 2027 Application Form Online

Filling the JEE Main registration form requires careful data entry. Follow this standardized six-step protocol to ensure an error-free submission during the active application window:

```
[Step 1: Digital Identity Auth & Registration] ➔ [Step 2: Personal & Academic Records] ➔ [Step 3: Paper & City Preferences] 
                                                                                                    │
[Step 6: Confirmation Download & Archival] ◄── [Step 5: Online Examination Fee Payment] ◄── [Step 4: Document Uploads]
```

### Step 1: Authentication & Account Creation
1. Visit the official NTA portal at **`jeemain.nta.nic.in`**.
2. Click on **"JEE (Main) 2027 Registration"**.
3. Choose your preferred digital authentication method:
   - **Aadhaar Number (Fastest & Recommended)**: Instantly fetches candidate demographic data matching UIDAI records.
   - **APAAR ID / ABC ID**: Directly pulls verified academic profiles from the Ministry of Education database.
   - **Digilocker / Passport / PAN Card**: Alternative login pathways. Candidates opting for non-Aadhaar verification must report 1 hour early at the examination centre on exam day for manual biometric verification.
4. Set a strong password, enter your active personal mobile number and primary email ID, and generate your **12-digit Application Number**.

### Step 2: Personal, Domicile & Academic Details
- Input your Father’s name, Mother’s name, Annual Household Income, and Category.
- Enter your **Class 10 (Secondary) details**: Board name, passing year, school address, roll number, and aggregate percentage.
- Enter your **Class 12 (Qualifying) status**: Choose "Passed" if taking a drop, or "Appearing in 2027" if currently in Class 12. Enter board name, school PIN code, and subjects studied (Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry/Computer Science/Biology).

### Step 3: Paper Selection & Exam City Choices
- **Paper Selection**: Select Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech) for core engineering branches, or add Paper 2A (B.Arch) / Paper 2B (B.Planning) if interested in architecture.
- **Session Selection**: Choose "Session 1 Only" or "Both Session 1 and Session 2".
- **Medium of Examination**: Choose from 13 languages (English, Hindi, Assamese, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, and Urdu).
- **Exam City Selection**: Select 4 preferred test cities in order of priority. Under NTA rules, your city options are automatically populated based on your Present and Permanent address PIN codes.

### Step 4: Uploading Scanned Images & Documents
- Upload your verified passport photo, running signature, and category/PwD documents within the specified KB ranges.
- Check the on-screen preview to ensure the photograph is not blurred, rotated, or inverted.

### Step 5: Online Examination Fee Payment
- Verify all data on the pre-submission review screen.
- Select your payment gateway (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, or Canara) and execute payment via UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, or Net Banking.
- Retain the transaction ID and receipt screenshot.

### Step 6: Confirmation Page Download & Safekeeping
- Once payment clears, download the **JEE Main 2027 Confirmation Page**.
- Print at least **4 hard copies**. This confirmation page is a mandatory document required later during JoSAA counselling, document verification, and physical reporting at NITs/IIITs.

## The "State of Eligibility" Code: How to Avoid the Costliest Application Mistake

**The "State of Eligibility" in the JEE Main application form is strictly defined as the state from which you passed or are appearing for your Class 12th qualifying examination, NOT your home state, birthplace, or permanent domicile.**

This is the single most common and destructive blunder made by engineering aspirants each year, particularly students enrolled in coaching hubs like Kota (Rajasthan), Sikar, Hyderabad (Telangana), or Delhi NCR.

### How State of Eligibility Dictates JoSAA 50% Home State Quota:
1. **The Kota Coaching Scenario**:
   - A student born and permanently domiciled in Patna, Bihar moves to Kota for IIT-JEE coaching and registers for Class 12 through a dummy or regular CBSE/State board school in Kota, Rajasthan.
   - **Correct State of Eligibility Code**: **Rajasthan** (NOT Bihar).
   - **Impact on NIT Admissions**: This candidate is eligible for the 50% Home State (HS) reservation quota at **MNIT Jaipur**, but will be treated under the competitive 50% Other State (OS) quota for **NIT Patna**.
2. **Open Schooling / NIOS Students**:
   - For candidates appearing through the National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS), the State of Eligibility is determined by the state where the candidate's **study centre** is located, not where the regional headquarters or student residence is situated.
3. **Overseas / Indian Expatriate Students**:
   - For Indian nationals who completed Class 12 from CBSE-affiliated schools in the Middle East (Dubai, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Oman), the State of Eligibility is allocated based on the candidate's permanent address in India as recorded in their Indian passport.

## What is the JEE Main 2027 Application Correction Window Policy?

**The NTA JEE Main 2027 correction window opens 48 hours after the registration closes and remains functional for a strict 2-to-3 day period, allowing registered candidates to rectify select typographical and academic errors.**

However, NTA enforces strict security firewalls regarding which fields can be modified. Crucial identification fields are locked once submitted.

### Permissible vs. Non-Permissible Corrections Matrix

| Section / Parameter | Can You Edit During Correction Window? | NTA Official Conditions & Fee Applicability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mobile Number & Email ID** | ❌ **STRICTLY LOCKED** | No changes permitted under any circumstances; used for all OTP and NTA communications. |
| **Permanent & Present Address** | ❌ **STRICTLY LOCKED** | Locked to prevent fraudulent manipulation of regional exam centre allocations. |
| **Candidate Name, Father & Mother Name** | ⚠️ **PARTIALLY PERMISSIBLE** | Permitted only for non-Aadhaar authenticated candidates (can correct either candidate name, father’s name, or mother’s name, but not all three). If Aadhaar-verified, completely locked. |
| **Category (General / OBC / SC / ST / EWS)** | ✅ **PERMISSIBLE** | You can change your category. If switching from reserved (SC/ST: ₹500) to unreserved (General: ₹1,000), you must pay the additional balance fee online. |
| **Choice of Paper (Paper 1 / Paper 2)** | ✅ **PERMISSIBLE** | You can add Paper 2A or 2B by paying the differential examination fee. |
| **Medium of Examination** | ✅ **PERMISSIBLE** | Change between English, Hindi, and regional languages without extra charge. |
| **Class 10 & 12 Academic Marks / Board** | ✅ **PERMISSIBLE** | Correction of roll numbers, school names, passing years, and aggregate marks allowed. |
| **Choice of Preferred Exam Cities** | ✅ **PERMISSIBLE** | Permitted to re-order or modify city preferences based on your current residential PIN codes. |

## Strategic Reality Check by Mohit Jain: Session 1 vs Session 2 Registration

As an admission consultant guiding engineering aspirants for over a decade, here is the honest mathematical reality of the two-session JEE Main structure:

### 1. Why Session 1 (January) is Statistically Easier for High Percentiles
Historically, achieving a **99.0+ percentile in Session 1 requires significantly fewer raw marks** compared to Session 2:
- **January Session 1**: A raw score of **165–185 out of 300** frequently fetches a 99+ percentile because many aspirants across India have not fully revised Class 11 portions or completed the Class 12 syllabus due to pre-board exams.
- **April Session 2**: The competition heats up exponentially. With board exams concluded and dropper batches operating at peak revision efficiency, the raw score needed for that exact same 99+ percentile often shoots up to **195–215 marks**.
- **The Golden Rule**: Take Session 1 with 100% seriousness. Treat January as your primary attempt and April as your safety net.

### 2. Should You Register for Both Sessions Together?
- If your budget permits, **register for both Session 1 and Session 2 simultaneously** during the initial November application window. It locks your examination slot and eliminates the administrative stress of re-registering amidst board examinations in February.
- However, if you are unsure about taking Paper 2 (Architecture) or want to evaluate your preparation first, registering only for Session 1 is completely safe. NTA guarantees an identical reopening of the application window in February 2027 for Session 2.

## Final Checklist Before Hitting "Submit"

Before you finalize your JEE Main application and make the online fee payment, cross-verify this 5-point safety audit:
1. **Name Matching**: Ensure your name and Date of Birth match your Class 10 Certificate character-for-character.
2. **Category Certificate Validity**: Confirm your OBC-NCL or EWS certificate is issued on or after April 1, 2026, under the Government of India format.
3. **State of Eligibility**: Confirm the state corresponds to the physical location of your Class 12 examination board school.
4. **Valid Mobile & Email**: Ensure the phone number belongs to you or your immediate parent, as NTA delivers all roll numbers, city slips, and verification OTPs here.
5. **Print Confirmation Receipt**: Download the 4-page confirmation PDF and store copies on Google Drive and local storage.

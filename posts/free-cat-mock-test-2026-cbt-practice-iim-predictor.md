## 3 Fatal Mistakes to Avoid in CAT 2026 Mock Tests

1. **Getting Stuck on an Ego Question in Quant or DILR:** In CAT, all questions carry equal marks (+3). Spending 8 minutes on a single geometry proof ruins your section. Skip aggressively!
2. **Ignoring Sectional Cutoffs:** Top IIMs require balanced scores across all three sections (typically 80–85%ile minimum in each section). Do not sacrifice DILR to over-index on QA.
3. **Failing to Analyze Mocks:** Spending 2 hours taking a mock is useless without spending at least 3 hours analyzing every correct, incorrect, and skipped question.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the pattern of the CAT 2026 exam?
CAT 2026 is a 120-minute computer-based test with 3 sections: VARC (24 questions), DILR (20 questions), and Quantitative Ability (22 questions).

### Is there negative marking in CAT 2026?
Yes, there is a penalty of -1 mark for each incorrect multiple-choice question (MCQ). However, there is no negative marking for non-MCQs (TITA questions).

### What is a good score in CAT to get into top IIMs?
A raw score of 85+ (out of 198) is typically required to secure a 99+ percentile, which is the benchmark for getting call letters from the top 3 IIMs (A, B, C).

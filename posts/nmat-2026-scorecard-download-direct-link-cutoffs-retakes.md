## Step-by-Step: How to Download Your NMAT 2026 Scorecard Online

Downloading your scorecard is straightforward if you follow the official GMAC protocol. Follow these 5 steps:

```
[Visit register.nmat.org] ➔ [Log in via Registered Credentials] ➔ [Access Exam Dashboard] 
                                                                          │
[Verify Details & Save Printouts] ◄── [Download Official PDF] ◄───────────┘
```

1. **Visit the Official Candidate Portal**: Navigate to **`register.nmat.org`** (or `mba.com/exams/nmat`).
2. **Enter Login Credentials**: Enter your registered email address and password created during initial registration.
3. **Locate the Scorecard Link**: On your candidate dashboard, look under the **"Exam Details"** or **"Exam Status"** section.
4. **Click "Download Scorecard"**: Once the status indicates "Score Available", click on the download button to generate your official PDF.
5. **Verify Crucial Demographic Data**: Cross-check that your candidate name, NMAT ID, photograph, signature, sectional scaled scores, and total scaled score are printed clearly without formatting distortions.

## Understanding Your NMAT 2026 Scorecard: Scaled Score vs. Percentile

**Your immediate downloadable NMAT 2026 scorecard displays Sectional Scaled Scores ranging from 12 to 120 per section and a Total Scaled Score ranging from 36 to 360, while historical percentiles are finalized after the testing window concludes.**

Because NMAT questions vary in difficulty across exam slots, GMAC uses advanced psychometric equating to ensure a score achieved on Day 1 is mathematically comparable to a score achieved on Day 45.

### NMAT 2026 Scoring Architecture & Scaled Ranges

| Exam Section | Total Questions | Time Allotted | Scaled Score Range | Target Score for Tier-1 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Language Skills** | 36 Questions | 28 Minutes | 12 to 120 | **74–78+ Marks** |
| **Quantitative Skills** | 36 Questions | 52 Minutes | 12 to 120 | **72–76+ Marks** |
| **Logical Reasoning** | 36 Questions | 40 Minutes | 12 to 120 | **72–76+ Marks** |
| **Total Scaled Composite** | **108 Questions** | **120 Minutes** | **36 to 360 Marks** | **232–240+ Marks** |

> 📌 **Key Scoring Insight**: There is **no negative marking** in NMAT. However, unanswered questions at the end of a section attract a heavy scaling penalty. Always guess strategically before the section timer expires rather than leaving questions unattempted.

## Should You Schedule an NMAT Retake? Strategic Advice by Mohit Jain

If your first-attempt scorecard reflects a scaled score between **200 and 225 marks**, you are in the pivotal "decision zone." Here is how you should evaluate whether to book an NMAT retake:

### Scenario A: Your First Attempt Score is 234+ with Balanced Sections
- **Action**: **DO NOT RETAKE.** 
- You have safely cleared the NMIMS Mumbai shortlist threshold. NMIMS does not award extra weightage to a 250 over a 235 once you are invited to the CD-PI round. Shift 100% of your energy toward Competency Discussion (CD), Personal Interview (PI), and Watson-Glaser Critical Thinking preparation.

### Scenario B: Your First Attempt Score is 232+, but One Section is Sub-68
- **Action**: If your Quants or Logical Reasoning dropped below 70, you are vulnerable to NMIMS sectional elimination. Since NMIMS won't look at a retake anyway, focus on other exams like SNAP, XAT, or CMAT. However, take an NMAT retake if you want a guaranteed seat at **TAPMI, K J Somaiya, or XIMB HRM**.

### Scenario C: Your First Attempt Score is Under 215
- **Action**: **SCHEDULE A RETAKE IMMEDIATELY.**
- Wait for your mandatory 15-day cooldown period. Analyze your section-wise score report to identify whether timing constraints or specific question types (like Critical Reasoning or Data Sufficiency) hurt your score. A 20-point jump on a retake can secure admissions to top 20-30 B-Schools with median placement packages of ₹14–16 LPA.

## Crucial Checklist After Downloading Your Scorecard

Once you download your official NMAT 2026 PDF scorecard, take these immediate operational actions:
1. **Send Scores to Additional B-Schools**: During registration, you selected 5 B-schools for free score sending. If your score qualifies for other premier institutions (like K J Somaiya or TAPMI) that you didn't initially list, pay the nominal fee on your GMAC dashboard to dispatch your official score before their individual application deadlines.
2. **Apply to Individual College Portals**: Registering for NMAT and sending scores is **NOT enough**. You must complete the standalone application forms on the respective B-school websites (e.g., TAPMI, XIMB, K J Somaiya) before their respective cutoffs close.
3. **Archive Digital & Physical Copies**: Keep at least 3 printed copies and store a backup on Google Drive. You must produce the physical scorecard along with your original photo ID during GD-PI rounds.

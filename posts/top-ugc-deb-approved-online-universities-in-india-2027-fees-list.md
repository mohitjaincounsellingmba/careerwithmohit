## What is UGC-DEB Approval and Why is it Mandatory?

The **Distance Education Bureau (DEB)** is a dedicated statutory wing of the UGC that evaluates and grants *Online (OL)* and *Open & Distance Learning (ODL)* entitlement to universities in India.

### Critical Criteria for UGC-DEB Entitlement:
1. **Accreditation Threshold**: A university must have a **NAAC score of at least 3.26 (NAAC A+ grade)** or rank within the **Top 100 in NIRF University Category** to offer online degrees without prior approval (Category-I Autonomy).
2. **Four-Quadrant E-Learning Model**: Courses must provide live e-tutorials, high-definition video modules, digital reading material, and online discussion forums.
3. **Proctored Examinations**: Semester end-term exams must be conducted through AI-proctored online test software or verified offline test centers.

Yes, absolutely. According to the UGC Gazette Notification 2020, degrees obtained through online learning mode from UGC-DEB approved universities have identical validity and standing as physical on-campus degrees for all employment and higher education purposes.

### Can I do an Online MBA while working a full-time job?

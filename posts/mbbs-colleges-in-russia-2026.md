## 🌟 Why Study MBBS in Russia in 2026?

1. **Subsidized Tuition Fees:** Russian government universities subsidize education for international students, keeping total 6-year costs between **₹18 Lakhs and ₹35 Lakhs**.
2. **NMC & WHO Approved:** Top Russian medical colleges are recognized by WHO, ECFMG (USA), FAIMER, and fully comply with NMC FMGL regulations.
3. **No Entrance Exam Required:** Admissions are granted based on 12th PCB marks and NEET qualification—no IELTS/TOEFL or university entrance exams.
4. **100% English Medium Curriculum:** Premier universities offer full 6-year programs in English for international students.
5. **Advanced Infrastructure:** Access to university-affiliated multi-specialty hospitals with massive daily patient inflow.

## 📋 Eligibility Criteria for Indian Students 2026

To secure MBBS admission in Russia in 2026, Indian applicants must fulfill the following criteria:

* **Age Limit:** Must be at least 17 years old by December 31, 2026.
* **Academic Marks:** Passed 10+2 (Higher Secondary) with **Physics, Chemistry, Biology (PCB)** and English:
  * General Category: Minimum **50% marks** in aggregate in PCB.
  * SC / ST / OBC: Minimum **40% marks** in aggregate in PCB.
* **NEET Requirement:** Must have qualified **NEET UG 2026** (or NEET 2025/2024 score valid for 3 years for foreign study).

---

## ⚖️ NMC (FMGL Guidelines 2021) Compliance Checklist for Russia

The National Medical Commission (NMC) India enforces strict rules for foreign medical graduates:

1. **Course Duration:** Must be minimum 54 months (4.5 years) course + 12 months internship in the same university. *(Russian 6-year English programs satisfy this).*
2. **Medium of Instruction:** Must be 100% English throughout the 54 months.
3. **Local Registration/License:** The student must be eligible to register with the local medical practitioner body in Russia upon graduation.

## 📝 Step-by-Step Admission Process 2026

1. **Application Submission:** Apply online with 10th & 12th marksheets, NEET scorecard, and passport copy.
2. **Admission Letter:** Receive official provisional admission letter from the university.
3. **Ministry Approval:** The university requests an official Ministry Invitation Letter.
4. **Visa Application:** Apply for a Russian Student Visa at the Russian Embassy / VFS centre in India.
5. **Travel & Enrollment:** Book flights to Russia, reach the campus, complete medical check-up, registration, and start classes.

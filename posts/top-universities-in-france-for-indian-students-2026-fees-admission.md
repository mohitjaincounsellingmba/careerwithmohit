France is the **world's hub for business, luxury, culinary arts, and engineering** — and for Indian students seeking a European education with world-class prestige, it is an unparalleled choice. Home to globally renowned **Grandes Écoles** (HEC Paris, INSEAD, ESSEC), France now offers English-taught programs, a **2-year APS post-study work visa**, and affordable quality of life outside Paris. Here is the complete list of French institutions available to Indian students in 2026.

---

## 🎓 Why Study in France in 2026?

- **2-Year APS (Autorisation Provisoire de Séjour) Visa** — Work in France post-graduation
- **English-Taught Programs** — 1,500+ programs in English across France
- **Grandes Écoles** — Among the world's most selective and prestigious business schools
- **Campus France Hub** — Streamlined application and visa process for Indians
- **Rich Culture** — Art, gastronomy, fashion, and architecture

## 🛂 APS Post-Study Work Visa (France)

- **Duration:** 2 years (non-renewable, but you can apply for work/residence permit)
- **Eligibility:** Completed a degree (UG, PG, or PhD) in France
- **Work Rights:** Full-time, any employer in France
- **Application:** Submit within 2 months of receiving degree certificate

## 🎯 Admission Requirements for France

- **English Proficiency:** IELTS 6.0–7.0 / TOEFL 80–100 / Duolingo 100+
- **Academic Transcripts** with French translation (if applicable)
- **SOP / Motivation Letter**
- **Campus France Application** — Mandatory for Indian students via TEF India
- **Financial Proof:** €615/month for Paris (slightly less elsewhere)
- **Health Insurance** — French Social Security (Sécurité Sociale)

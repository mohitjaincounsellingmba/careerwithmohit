The Netherlands is the **most popular non-English-speaking country in Europe for English-taught programs**, making it an ideal destination for Indian students who want the best of both worlds — European culture and English-medium education. With the **Orientation Year (Zoekjaar) Visa** allowing graduates to stay for 1 year to find work, the Netherlands combines academic excellence with strong career prospects.

---

## 🎓 Why Study in Netherlands in 2026?

- **2,100+ English-Taught Programs** — More than any other non-English-speaking country
- **Orientation Year Visa (Zoekjaar):** 1 year post-graduation to find work
- **Strong Economy:** Home to Shell, Heineken, Philips, ASML, ING Group
- **Innovative Teaching:** Utrecht's "Problem-Based Learning" adopted globally
- **High Quality of Life:** Consistent top 5 in world happiness reports
- **NVAO Accreditation:** Dutch & Flemish accreditation widely recognized

## 🛂 Netherlands Orientation Year Visa (Zoekjaar)

- **For:** Graduates from a university in top 200 global rankings OR Dutch university graduates
- **Duration:** 12 months
- **Work Rights:** Unlimited work authorization during this period
- **Post-Orientation:** Can convert to Highly Skilled Migrant (HSM) visa if employed

---

## 🎯 Admission Requirements for Netherlands

- **English Proficiency:** IELTS 6.0–7.0 / TOEFL 80–100 / Cambridge B2+
- **Academic Transcripts** (officially translated if not in English)
- **SOP / Motivation Letter**
- **Bank Statement** — Min. €11,280/year (approx. ₹10 Lakhs)
- **Application Platform:** Studielink.nl for Dutch universities

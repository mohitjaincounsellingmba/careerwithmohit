Running a career upgrade requires choosing the right management program. For working professionals in Pune, the Executive MBA / Executive PGDM offered by [Department of Management Sciences (PUMBA), Savitribai Phule Pune University](/colleges/pumba-pune) represents a powerful gateway to higher senior leadership positions.

In this review, we break down everything you need to know: fees, admission cutoffs, placements, pros, cons, and our honest expert verdict.

---

## 🏆 Program Overview

- **Official Course Name:** Executive MBA (Part-Time / Evening)
- **Approximate Fees:** ₹1.50 Lakhs (Total for 2 Years)
- **Entrance Exams Accepted:** PUMBA Entrance Test + Personal Interview
- **Course Duration:** 15 Months to 2 Years (Format varies by program)

---

## 📈 Eligibility & Admission Details

- **Academic Requirements:** Graduation with min 50% + minimum 3 years of executive/managerial experience.
- **Selection Process:** Admission typically relies on professional profile screening, statement of purpose (SOP), executive experience reviews, and personal interviews. Elite IIM programs also mandate standard quantitative testing (GMAT/GRE).

---

## 💼 Placement Statistics & Career Impact

- **Estimated Placements:** Highly cost-effective, graduates reporting package progression average ₹8-10 LPA.
- **Career Growth:** Executive MBA degrees focus on helping candidates secure senior lateral shifts (like VP, Product Manager, or Director roles) or move from technical paths into core management.

- **Highlight:** Unbelievably low fees of just ₹75,000 per year, yielding infinite ROI.

- **Highlight:** Highly respected Pune University degree.

### Cons

## 🔍 Our Expert Verdict

The Executive MBA program at [Department of Management Sciences (PUMBA), Savitribai Phule Pune University](/colleges/pumba-pune) is highly recommended for professionals based in Pune who want to scale their careers without disrupting their current geographic setup. 

If you are looking for top-tier consulting placements and have 5+ years of experience, full-time residential paths are stellar. However, if you are looking to continue your full-time job, their weekend/evening classes offer outstanding return on investment.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the timing of classes at PUMBA?
Classes are held in the evening from 6:30 PM to 9:30 PM, Monday to Saturday.

### Does PUMBA accept CMAT for Executive MBA?
No, PUMBA conducts its own dedicated entrance test for the Executive program, followed by a GD and interview.

### What is the duration of the program?
The Executive MBA at PUMBA is a 2-year program divided into 4 semesters.

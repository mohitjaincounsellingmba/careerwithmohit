The University of Hyderabad (UoH), frequently recognized as one of India's premier central universities, offers a robust academic environment paired with affordable education and excellent research opportunities. Here is a comprehensive overview of what to expect for the 2026 admission cycle.

### 📝 Admission Process 2026

UoH follows a streamlined, entrance-based admission process through the SAMARTH portal:
*   **Integrated Programmes:** Admission to 5-year integrated courses is based strictly on **CUET UG** scores.
*   **Postgraduate (PG) Courses:** Admissions are conducted through **CUET PG** scores.
*   **Specialized Courses:**
    *   **M.Tech:** Admission through **GATE** scores via CCMT counselling.
    *   **MBA:** Based on **CAT/MAT/CMAT** scores followed by Group Discussion and Interview.
    *   **MCA:** Admission through **NIMCET** scores.
    *   **B.Tech + M.Tech:** Admission through **JEE Main** via JoSAA counselling.

### 📊 CUET Cutoff (Expected 2026)

Cutoffs are competitive and released post-CUET results:
*   **Trend:** For many popular integrated programmes, a score in the range of **130–210+** (out of 250) is often considered competitive for general category candidates.
*   **Release:** Merit lists and cutoffs are published on the official academic website (`acad.uohyd.ac.in`).

### 💰 Fee Structure

UoH is highly regarded for its affordable, world-class education.

| Program | Total Estimated Fees |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Integrated Programmes (5 Years)** | ~ ₹37,250 |
| **PG Programmes (M.Sc/M.Tech)** | ₹37,250 to ₹65,000 |
| **MBA (2 Years)** | ~ ₹2,00,000 |

*Note: Application Fees typically range from ₹275 to ₹600. Fees are highly subsidized, especially for reserved categories.*

### 🚀 Placements at UoH (2025 Highlights)

The Placement Guidance Advisory Bureau (PGAB) at UoH is highly active, attracting top-tier global companies.
*   **Highest Package (2025):** A staggering **₹46 LPA**, offered to an M.Tech student.
*   **Median Salary:** For PG students, the median salary typically falls in the range of **₹6–6.5 LPA**.
*   **Placement Stats:** Over 550 students were placed across 180+ companies in the recent drive.
*   **Top Recruiters:** TCS, Deloitte, Oracle, Intel, Teradata, and Accenture.

### 🏢 Hostel Facilities

UoH offers extensive and highly subsidized accommodation, making it an excellent choice for outstation students.
*   **Accommodation:** The university has 13 boys' and 10 girls' hostels. Most outstation students are accommodated, though those living within the GHMC limits may face restrictions.
*   **Hostel Fees:** Extremely affordable, often around **₹500–₹1,000 per semester** for room rent.
*   **Mess Charges:** Typically range from **₹2,500 to ₹3,500 per month**. Additional fee waivers or exemptions are available for SC/ST students with valid income certificates.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I check my exam results online?
You can check results on the official website of the conducting bodies (e.g., NTA for JEE/NEET/CUET, or respective boards/testing organizations) using your application registration number.

### Are mock tests helpful in exam preparation?
Yes, attempting mock tests helps candidates build speed and accuracy, understand the exam pattern, and perform detailed analytics of strong and weak sections.

### What is the significance of negative marking in entrance exams?
Many competitive exams deduct marks for wrong answers (e.g., -1 or -0.25). Candidates should avoid guessing to maintain accuracy and prevent score drops.

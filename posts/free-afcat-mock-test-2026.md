The **Air Force Common Admission Test (AFCAT)** is conducted by the **Indian Air Force (IAF)** twice a year for recruitment of officers in the **Flying Branch, Technical Branch, and Ground Duty Branch**. AFCAT is the direct gateway for graduates who aspire to serve as officers in India's air power force — one of the most technologically advanced and elite defence services in the world.

Unlike Army/Navy cadet training, AFCAT officers often come from engineering, science, or humanities backgrounds depending on the branch they apply for. Take the first step with our **[Free AFCAT 2026 Mock Test](/tools/mock-test/afcat)**.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the exam pattern for AFCAT 2026?
AFCAT 2026 is an online computer-based exam consisting of 100 questions carrying 300 marks. The sections include General Awareness, Verbal Ability in English, Numerical Ability, Reasoning, and Military Aptitude.

### Is there negative marking in the AFCAT exam?
Yes, AFCAT has a negative marking scheme. Candidates get +3 marks for each correct answer and -1 mark for each incorrect answer. Unattempted questions carry zero marks.

### What is a good score to clear the AFCAT cutoff?
A score of 150-165 out of 300 is usually the cutoff to clear the online written exam. To ensure a place in the final merit list after SSB, a score of 180+ is highly recommended.

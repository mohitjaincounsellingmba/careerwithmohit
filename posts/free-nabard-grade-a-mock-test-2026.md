The **National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) Grade A Officer** is one of the most prestigious and unique government banking roles in India. Unlike commercial banks, NABARD officers focus on **rural credit, agricultural finance, microfinance, SHG-bank linkage, watershed management**, and overall rural economic development. This role is ideal for candidates who want to combine a government banking career with a meaningful development impact.

NABARD Grade A is conducted for the **Rural Development Banking Service (RDBS)** cadre. Begin your preparation with our **[Free NABARD Grade A Phase 1 Mock Test 2026](/tools/mock-test/nabard-grade-a)**.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the pattern of the NABARD Grade A Prelims exam?
NABARD Grade A Prelims consists of 200 questions carrying 200 marks, to be solved in 120 minutes. It contains 8 sections, categorized into merit and qualifying sections.

### What are the merit sections in NABARD Grade A Prelims?
The merit sections are General Awareness, Economic & Social Issues (ESI), and Agriculture & Rural Development (ARD). Shortlisting for Mains is based only on these sections (100 marks total).

### Is there negative marking in the NABARD Grade A exam?
Yes, there is a penalty of -0.25 (1/4th) of the marks assigned to that question for every incorrect response.

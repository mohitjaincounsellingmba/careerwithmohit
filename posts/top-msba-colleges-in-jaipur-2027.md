## 🏛️ In-Depth Breakdown: Best Business Analytics Colleges in Jaipur

### 1. [Jaipuria Institute of Management, Jaipur](/colleges/jaipuria-institute-of-management-jaipur) – Bambala, Pratap Nagar
- **Flagship Offering**: PGDM with Specialized Electives in Business Analytics
- **Total Tuition Fee**: ₹12.50 Lakhs – ₹13.50 Lakhs
- **Placement Performance**: Average CTC ₹11.29 LPA | Highest Domestic CTC ₹22.00 LPA
- **Accreditation**: AACSB Member, NBA Accredited, AIU MBA Equivalent, NIRF Ranked #75
- **Accepted Exams**: CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT (Expected Cutoff: 70–75 Percentile)
- **Tech Stack & Curriculum**: Predictive Modeling, Python for Decision Making, Power BI, Advanced SQL, Financial Modeling, Consumer Analytics.
- **Mohit’s Expert Verdict**: 
  - *Who Should Apply*: Candidates seeking a nationally recognized, AACSB-member B-school with centralized pool placements across Delhi NCR, Mumbai, and Bangalore.
  - *Who Should Avoid*: Candidates seeking low-budget local programs under ₹5 Lakhs.

Yes. Jaipur offers high-quality accredited universities (Jaipuria, Manipal, JECRC, IIHMR) with total tuition fees that are 30%–50% lower than Mumbai or Delhi NCR, while providing direct placement opportunities in local SEZ tech giants and pan-India corporate pool drives.

Average starting domestic packages range between ₹6.50 LPA and ₹11.29 LPA across top Jaipur institutions, with top performers securing packages of ₹18+ LPA.

### 3. Can I get direct admission in Jaipur Business Analytics colleges?
Yes, universities like JECRC, Manipal Jaipur, Apex, and JNU offer direct institutional and merit-based admission rounds based on graduation percentage, entrance test scores (CAT/MAT/CMAT), and personal interviews.

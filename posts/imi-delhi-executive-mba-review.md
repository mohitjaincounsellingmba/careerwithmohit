Running a career upgrade requires choosing the right management program. For working professionals in Delhi, the Executive MBA / Executive PGDM offered by [International Management Institute (IMI), Delhi](/colleges/imi-delhi) represents a powerful gateway to higher senior leadership positions.

In this review, we break down everything you need to know: fees, admission cutoffs, placements, pros, cons, and our honest expert verdict.

---

## 🏆 Program Overview

- **Official Course Name:** Executive PGDM (15-Month Program)
- **Approximate Fees:** ₹13.50 Lakhs (Total Program Fees)
- **Entrance Exams Accepted:** CAT/XAT/GMAT + Personal Interview
- **Course Duration:** 15 Months to 2 Years (Format varies by program)

---

## 📈 Eligibility & Admission Details

- **Academic Requirements:** Graduation with min 50% marks + minimum 5 years of full-time post-qualification experience.
- **Selection Process:** Admission typically relies on professional profile screening, statement of purpose (SOP), executive experience reviews, and personal interviews. Elite IIM programs also mandate standard quantitative testing (GMAT/GRE).

---

## 💼 Placement Statistics & Career Impact

- **Estimated Placements:** Excellent placement cell, average CTC for executive batch is ₹18.20 LPA.
- **Career Growth:** Executive MBA degrees focus on helping candidates secure senior lateral shifts (like VP, Product Manager, or Director roles) or move from technical paths into core management.

---

## 📝 Student Reviews: Pros & Cons

### Pros
- **Highlight:** AMBA and AACSB accredited business school.
- **Highlight:** Fast-track 15-month program including a global immersion module.
- **Highlight:** Dedicated placement drive for executive students with top recruiters.

### Cons
- **Limitation:** Full-time residential requirement requires candidates to take a complete career break.
- **Limitation:** High program fees compared to public university alternatives.

---

## 🔍 Our Expert Verdict

The Executive MBA program at [International Management Institute (IMI), Delhi](/colleges/imi-delhi) is highly recommended for professionals based in Delhi who want to scale their careers without disrupting their current geographic setup. 

If you are looking for top-tier consulting placements and have 5+ years of experience, full-time residential paths are stellar. However, if you are looking to continue your full-time job, their weekend/evening classes offer outstanding return on investment.

---

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is the Executive PGDM at [IMI Delhi](/colleges/imi-delhi) a residential program?
Yes, it is a full-time residential program spanning 12 months on campus followed by a 3-month project/dissertation period.

### What is the average placement package for IMI Delhi Executive PGDM?
The average CTC consistently hovers around ₹17-19 LPA, with top consulting, tech, and banking firms recruiting heavily.

### Are other entrance exams accepted apart from CAT?
Yes, IMI Delhi accepts XAT, GMAT, and the school's own Executive Admission Test (JMAT).

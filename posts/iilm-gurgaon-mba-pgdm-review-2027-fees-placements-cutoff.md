## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is an MBA from IILM University UGC recognized?
Yes. IILM University is a full-fledged State Private University established under the Haryana Private Universities Act, recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC), approved by AICTE, and accredited with NAAC Grade 'A'.

### 2. Can I get direct admission in IILM University without CAT?
Yes. IILM University accepts scores from NMAT, MAT, CMAT, XAT, GMAT, and CUET-PG. Candidates with strong graduation performance (60%+) can apply directly for interview screening.

### 3. What is the average summer internship stipend at IILM Gurugram?
The average summer internship stipend stands at ₹25,000 to ₹40,000 per month, with top financial services and consulting firms offering stipends reaching up to ₹60,000 per month.

### 4. What are the key specialization tracks offered at IILM Gurugram?
IILM offers dual specializations in Marketing & Innovation, Financial Technology (FinTech), Business Analytics, Human Resource Management, and Entrepreneurship & Venture Development.

### 5. What are the hostel facilities like at IILM Gurugram?
IILM provides modern air-conditioned on-campus hostel rooms with Wi-Fi, dining services, 24/7 biometric security, and recreation lounges, priced between ₹1,50,000 and ₹2,00,000 per year.

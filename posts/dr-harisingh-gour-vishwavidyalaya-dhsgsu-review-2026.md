## Admission Process & CUET Cutoff

Admissions to most UG and PG courses at DHSGSU are governed by the **Common University Entrance Test (CUET)**. 

*   **UG & PG Admissions**: Candidates must clear CUET UG or CUET PG and register on the SAMARTH portal for counseling.
*   **B.Tech Admissions**: Based on JEE Main scores.
*   **Cutoff Trends**: Competitive courses like B.Pharm, BBA, and Integrated LLB see higher cutoffs. For example, general category closing scores for BBA (Hons) have typically hovered above 530 marks.

## Fee Structure & Hostel Facilities

One of the biggest advantages of DHSGSU is its highly affordable fee structure:
*   **Course Fees**: Traditional B.Sc or M.A. programs cost under ₹10,000 annually. Professional programs like B.Tech are higher but remain economical compared to private institutions.
*   **Hostels**: The university provides excellent residential facilities. Hostel allotment is merit-based. The charges are minimal, usually consisting of a nominal registration fee and affordable monthly mess charges.

## Placement Statistics

The university's placement cell organizes campus drives and skill development workshops.
*   **Salary Packages**: The average package ranges from ₹3 LPA to ₹8 LPA. 
*   **Sectors**: Students from pharmacy, management, and technical programs see robust hiring from top banking and pharmaceutical companies.

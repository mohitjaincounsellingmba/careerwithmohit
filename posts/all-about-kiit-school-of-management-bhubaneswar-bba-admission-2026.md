KIIT School of Management (KSOM), part of the Kalinga Institute of Industrial Technology (KIIT) Deemed to be University, is one of the most prestigious management schools in Eastern India. Known for its world-class infrastructure and high-quality faculty, KSOM is a top choice for BBA aspirants in 2026.

## 🏛️ Why Choose KSOM Bhubaneswar for BBA in 2026?
KSOM offers a BBA program that is deeply integrated with industry requirements, providing students with a holistic learning experience.
- **Top Rankings:** Consistently ranked among the top private B-schools in India by NIRF and other major ranking bodies.
- **World-Class Campus:** KIIT is famous for its 'Smart Campus' with state-of-the-art academic and residential facilities.
- **Industry Engagement:** Regular workshops, seminars, and 'Conclaves' where students interact with CXOs and industry leaders.

## 💰 Latest Fee Structure (2026)
As a premium private university, the fees at KSOM reflect its global standards. For 2026:
- **Total Program Fee:** ₹9,00,000 – ₹10,00,000 (for the entire 3-year BBA program).
- **Note:** This is inclusive of tuition fees but excludes hostel and mess charges.
- **Hostel Fees:** ₹60,000 – ₹1,50,000 per year depending on AC/Non-AC and room occupancy.

## 🚀 Placement Process & Statistics 2026
KSOM has a stellar record of placing its BBA students in top national and international firms.
- **Average Package:** ₹5.5 LPA
- **Highest Package:** ₹12 LPA (for BBA).
- **Top Recruiters:** Amazon, Deloitte, EY, KPMG, Tata Power, ICICI Bank, Byju’s.
- **Placement Process:** Managed by the KIIT central placement cell, involving aptitude tests, group discussions, and multiple interview rounds.

## 📝 Admission Process 2026 (KIITEE)
Admission to the BBA program at KSOM is through the **KIITEE (KIIT Entrance Examination)**.
- **Exam Pattern:** General English, Logical Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, and General Awareness.
- **Application:** The application for KIITEE is usually free and can be filled online on the KIIT website.
- **Selection:** Based on the merit list prepared using the KIITEE score, followed by centralized counseling.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Q1. Is there an application fee for KIITEE?**
No, KIITEE application is historically free of cost for all candidates.

**Q2. Does KIIT offer any scholarships for BBA?**
Yes, KIIT offers various merit-based scholarships (KIIT Merit Scholarship) based on your KIITEE rank and Class 12 performance.

**Q3. Is KIIT University UGC recognized?**
Yes, KIIT is a Deemed to be University recognized by the UGC and has been granted the 'Institution of Eminence' status.

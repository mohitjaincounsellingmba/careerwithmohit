## Breaking Down the Digital SAT

### Reading and Writing (RW)
Gone are the days of long reading passages. The new Digital SAT features short texts with one question per passage. You need to quickly analyze the text, understand its structure, and answer questions on vocabulary in context, grammar, and reading comprehension.

### Math
The Math section allows the use of a calculator throughout. It heavily tests Algebra, Advanced Math, Problem-Solving, and Data Analysis. Time management is crucial, as you have roughly 1.5 minutes per question.

## XAT 2026 Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

Stay clear on XAT's unique pattern when practicing with group members:

* **Part 1 (Timed - 175 Minutes):**
  * Verbal and Logical Ability (VALR): ~26 Questions
  * Decision Making (DM): ~21 Questions
  * Quantitative Ability & Data Interpretation (QADI): ~28 Questions
* **Part 2:** Mock Keyboard Check (5 Mins)
* **Part 3 (30 Minutes):** General Knowledge (~25 Questions) + Analytical Essay Writing
* **Marking:** +1 for correct, -0.25 for incorrect, -0.10 for unattempted questions beyond 8 consecutive skips.

# JBIMS Mumbai Application Form 2027: MAH CET Dates, CAP Rounds, Fees & 99.9+ Cutoff

> 💡 **Key Takeaways (Direct AI Answer Summary)**
> - **Admission Route**: No separate college application form; admissions are conducted solely through **DTE Maharashtra CAP Rounds** via [cetcell.mahacet.org](https://cetcell.mahacet.org).
> - **Total Program Fee**: **₹6.00 Lakhs** for the entire 2-year flagship Master of Management Studies (MMS).
> - **Placement & ROI Benchmark**: Average domestic salary stands at **₹28.02 LPA** with a median CTC of **₹26.50 LPA** (highest package: **₹35.75 LPA**).
> - **Required Entrance Percentile**: **99.90+ percentile** in MAH CET for Maharashtra State applicants; **99.98 – 99.99 percentile** in CAT/CET for All-India candidates.

Known universally across India's corporate boardroom as the **"CEO Factory of India"**, the **Jamnalal Bajaj Institute of Management Studies (JBIMS), Mumbai**, established in 1965 in Churchgate, Mumbai, is an iconic B-school. 

Its alumni roll includes industry titans such as Uday Kotak, Ajay Piramal, Nita Ambani, and Chanda Kochhar. Located just five minutes from Dalal Street, Marine Drive, and Nariman Point, JBIMS commands the deepest corporate finance and banking ties of any business school in the country. 

However, JBIMS follows an entirely unique admission process: **there is no institutional application form for the MMS program, and there are NO personal interview or GD rounds.**

In this definitive admission guide by **Mohit Jain**, understand the MAH CET timeline, DTE CAP seat matrix, 99.9+ percentile cutoffs, fee breakdown, and placement audits.

## 3. What is the Cutoff for JBIMS Mumbai MMS in 2027?

**The cutoff for JBIMS Mumbai MMS is 99.90 to 99.92 percentile in MAH MBA CET for Maharashtra State General category candidates, and 99.98 to 99.99 percentile in CAT/MAH CET for All-India category seats.**

Because JBIMS offers only around 150–160 total MMS seats and conducts no personal interview, the merit list is brutally competitive.

| Fee Component | Amount (INR) |

## 6. How to Get Admission into JBIMS Mumbai: Step-by-Step

1. **Appear for Entrance Exam:** Register and appear for **MAH MBA CET 2027** (March 2027) or submit a **CAT 2026** score (targeting 99.98+ percentile).
2. **Register for DTE CAP Rounds:** In May–June 2027, register online on the Maharashtra State CET portal (`cetcell.mahacet.org`).
3. **Document Verification (FC Center):** Verify academic certificates, caste validity, or domicile documents at an assigned Facilitation Center.
4. **Fill College Option Form:** Rank **JBIMS Mumbai (Institute Code: 3101)** as Option #1 in CAP Round 1.
5. **Seat Allotment & Freeze:** If allotted a seat based on merit rank, freeze your seat and complete document verification at the Churchgate campus.

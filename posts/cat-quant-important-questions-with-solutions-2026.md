## 2. Module 1: Arithmetic (The Scoring Engine)

### **Q1. Time, Speed & Distance (Relative Speed & Escalators)**
*A passenger walking up a moving escalator from the ground floor to the first floor takes 30 seconds if he walks at a speed of 1 step per second. If he walks at a speed of 2 steps per second on the same escalator, he reaches the top in 20 seconds. How many total steps are there on the escalator when it is stationary?*

1. 50 steps  
2. 60 steps  
3. 70 steps  
4. 40 steps  

#### **Step-by-Step Solution:**
* Let the speed of the moving escalator be $e$ steps per second, and let the total number of steps on the stationary escalator be $N$.
* **Case 1 (Walking at 1 step/sec for 30 sec):**
  * Total steps covered by passenger + escalator = $(1 + e) \times 30 = N$
  * $\implies 30 + 30e = N \quad \text{--- (Equation 1)}$
* **Case 2 (Walking at 2 steps/sec for 20 sec):**
  * Total steps covered = $(2 + e) \times 20 = N$
  * $\implies 40 + 20e = N \quad \text{--- (Equation 2)}$
* **Equating Equation 1 and Equation 2:**
  $$30 + 30e = 40 + 20e$$
  $$10e = 10 \implies e = 1 \text{ step per second}.$$
* **Finding Total Steps ($N$):**
  $$N = 30 + 30(1) = \mathbf{60 \text{ steps}}.$$
* **Correct Option:** **(2) 60 steps**

### **Q2. Profit, Loss & Dishonest Shopkeeper**
*A dishonest oil merchant claims to sell olive oil at his cost price. However, he uses a faulty measuring liter bottle that reads 1000 ml but actually delivers only 800 ml. Moreover, he mixes 100 ml of cheap vegetable oil (which costs 50% less per liter than olive oil) into every 800 ml of mixture delivered to the customer. What is his net percentage profit on the transaction?*

1. 25.0%  
2. 33.33%  
3. 38.88%  
4. 42.85%  

#### **Step-by-Step Solution:**
* Let the cost price (CP) of pure olive oil be **₹1 per ml** ($\implies$ ₹1000 per liter).
* Then the CP of cheap vegetable oil = **₹0.5 per ml**.
* **Selling Price (SP) charged to customer:**
  * The merchant charges for a 1000 ml bottle at the claimed CP = **₹1000**.
* **Actual Cost Price (CP) incurred by merchant:**
  * He delivers a total volume of 800 ml.
  * In this 800 ml mixture, 100 ml is vegetable oil and 700 ml is olive oil.
  * Cost of 700 ml olive oil = $700 \times ₹1 = ₹700$.
  * Cost of 100 ml vegetable oil = $100 \times ₹0.5 = ₹50$.
  * Total Actual Cost Price = $700 + 50 = \mathbf{₹750}$.
* **Net Percentage Profit:**
  $$\text{Profit} = \text{SP} - \text{CP} = 1000 - 750 = ₹250.$$
  $$\text{Profit \%} = \frac{250}{750} \times 100 = \frac{1}{3} \times 100 = \mathbf{33.33\%}.$$
* **Correct Option:** **(2) 33.33%**

### **Q3. Time & Work (Efficiency Ratios)**
*Ankit is twice as efficient as Bharat, and Bharat is thrice as efficient as Chirag. If Ankit, Bharat, and Chirag work together, they can complete a job in 10 days. How many days will Bharat alone take to complete 50% of the same job?*

1. 30 days  
2. 25 days  
3. 20 days  
4. 15 days  

#### **Step-by-Step Solution:**
* Let the daily work efficiency of **Chirag (C) = 1 unit/day**.
* Therefore, daily efficiency of **Bharat (B) = $3 \times 1 = 3 \text{ units/day}$**.
* Daily efficiency of **Ankit (A) = $2 \times 3 = 6 \text{ units/day}$**.
* **Combined daily efficiency of A, B, and C:**
  $$\text{Total Efficiency} = 6 + 3 + 1 = 10 \text{ units/day}.$$
* **Total Work:**
  $$\text{Total Work} = \text{Efficiency} \times \text{Days} = 10 \text{ units/day} \times 10 \text{ days} = 100 \text{ units}.$$
* **Bharat completing 50% of the job:**
  * 50% of Total Work = $\frac{100}{2} = 50 \text{ units}$.
  * Time taken by Bharat alone = $\frac{50 \text{ units}}{3 \text{ units/day}} = \mathbf{16.66 \text{ days}}$.
  * *Wait, let us check the option values!* Let's see: If Bharat takes $\frac{50}{3} = 16.67$ days, let us verify if the question asked for Bharat or if Bharat + Chirag together take $\frac{50}{4} = 12.5$ days.
  * Let us make the option clean: If Ankit is twice as efficient as Bharat ($A=6, B=3, C=1$, sum = 10), time taken by Bharat alone to complete the **full job** = $\frac{100}{3} = 33.33$ days. Let us set the options as:
    1. 16.67 days  
    2. 20.00 days  
    3. 25.00 days  
    4. 33.33 days  
* **Correct Option:** **(1) 16.67 days**

## 3. Module 2: Algebra (Logarithms, Quadratics & AP/GP)

### **Q4. Logarithms & Exponential Equations**
*If $\log_2(x - 1) + \log_2(x + 1) = 3$, what is the positive real value of $x$?*

1. 3  
2. $\sqrt{10}$  
3. 4  
4. 5  

#### **Step-by-Step Solution:**
* Use the logarithmic identity $\log_a(m) + \log_a(n) = \log_a(mn)$:
  $$\log_2((x - 1)(x + 1)) = 3$$
* Simplify the algebraic product $(x - 1)(x + 1) = x^2 - 1$:
  $$\log_2(x^2 - 1) = 3$$
* Convert from logarithmic to exponential form:
  $$x^2 - 1 = 2^3 = 8$$
  $$x^2 = 9 \implies x = \pm 3.$$
* Since $x > 1$ (for $\log_2(x-1)$ to be defined in real numbers), we select $x = \mathbf{3}$.
* **Correct Option:** **(1) 3**

### **Q5. Arithmetic & Geometric Progressions**
*What is the sum of the infinite series:*
$$S = \frac{1}{3} + \frac{2}{3^2} + \frac{3}{3^3} + \frac{4}{3^4} + \cdots \infty?$$

1. $\frac{3}{4}$  
2. $\frac{1}{2}$  
3. $\frac{2}{3}$  
4. $1$  

#### **Step-by-Step Solution:**
* This is an **Arithmetico-Geometric Progression (AGP)**.
* Write the series:
  $$S = \frac{1}{3} + \frac{2}{9} + \frac{3}{27} + \frac{4}{81} + \cdots \infty \quad \text{--- (Equation 1)}$$
* Multiply both sides by the common ratio $r = \frac{1}{3}$:
  $$\frac{1}{3}S = \frac{1}{9} + \frac{2}{27} + \frac{3}{81} + \cdots \infty \quad \text{--- (Equation 2)}$$
* Subtract Equation 2 from Equation 1 (aligning terms of same denominator):
  $$S - \frac{1}{3}S = \frac{1}{3} + \left(\frac{2}{9} - \frac{1}{9}\right) + \left(\frac{3}{27} - \frac{2}{27}\right) + \cdots \infty$$
  $$\frac{2}{3}S = \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{3^2} + \frac{1}{3^3} + \cdots \infty$$
* The right-hand side is now a pure infinite Geometric Progression (GP) with first term $a = \frac{1}{3}$ and common ratio $r = \frac{1}{3}$:
  $$\text{Sum of infinite GP} = \frac{a}{1 - r} = \frac{\frac{1}{3}}{1 - \frac{1}{3}} = \frac{\frac{1}{3}}{\frac{2}{3}} = \frac{1}{2}.$$
* Substitute this back:
  $$\frac{2}{3}S = \frac{1}{2} \implies S = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{3}{2} = \mathbf{\frac{3}{4}}.$$
* **Correct Option:** **(1) 3/4**

## 4. Module 3: Geometry & Coordinate Geometry

### **Q6. Triangles (Inradius & Circumradius Relationship)**
*In a right-angled triangle, the lengths of the two perpendicular legs are 6 cm and 8 cm. What is the distance between the incenter and the circumcenter of this triangle?*

1. $\sqrt{5} \text{ cm}$  
2. $\sqrt{8} \text{ cm}$  
3. $\sqrt{10} \text{ cm}$  
4. $2\sqrt{2} \text{ cm}$  

#### **Step-by-Step Solution:**
1. **Find Hypotenuse ($c$):**
   $$c = \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{36 + 64} = 10 \text{ cm}.$$
2. **Find Inradius ($r$):**
   * For a right-angled triangle with legs $a, b$ and hypotenuse $c$:
     $$r = \frac{a + b - c}{2} = \frac{6 + 8 - 10}{2} = \frac{4}{2} = \mathbf{2 \text{ cm}}.$$
3. **Find Circumradius ($R$):**
   * In a right-angled triangle, the circumcenter lies at the midpoint of the hypotenuse:
     $$R = \frac{c}{2} = \frac{10}{2} = \mathbf{5 \text{ cm}}.$$
4. **Use Euler's Formula for Distance ($d$) between Incenter and Circumcenter:**
   $$d^2 = R^2 - 2Rr$$
   $$d^2 = 5^2 - 2(5)(2) = 25 - 20 = 5$$
   $$d = \mathbf{\sqrt{5} \text{ cm}}.$$
* **Correct Option:** **(1) $\sqrt{5} \text{ cm}$**

## 5. Module 4: Number System & Remainders

### **Q7. Remainders (Fermat's Little Theorem)**
*What is the remainder when $3^{102}$ is divided by 101?*

1. 1  
2. 3  
3. 9  
4. 27  

#### **Step-by-Step Solution:**
* Notice that **101 is a prime number**, and $\gcd(3, 101) = 1$.
* According to **Fermat's Little Theorem**, for any prime $p$ and integer $a$ not divisible by $p$:
  $$a^{p-1} \equiv 1 \pmod p$$
* Here, $p = 101 \implies p - 1 = 100$:
  $$3^{100} \equiv 1 \pmod{101}$$
* Now rewrite $3^{102}$:
  $$3^{102} = 3^{100} \times 3^2$$
  $$\text{Remainder} = (1 \times 9) \pmod{101} = \mathbf{9}.$$
* **Correct Option:** **(3) 9**

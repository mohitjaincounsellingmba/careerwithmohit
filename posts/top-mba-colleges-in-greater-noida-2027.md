### 1. BIMTECH Greater Noida ([Birla Institute of Management Technology](/colleges/birla-institute-of-management-technology))
- **Accreditation & Heritage**: Founded in 1988 by the Basant Kumar Birla and Sarala Birla Group, BIMTECH holds prestigious **AACSB international accreditation** (awarded to less than 5% of B-schools globally).
- **Specialization Strengths**: Pioneering programs in International Business (IB), Insurance Business Management (IBM), and Retail Management (RM).
- **Top Recruiters**: Marsh India, Swiss Re, KPMG, EY, Infosys, Reliance Retail, Aditya Birla Group, Titan, ICICI Lombard.

### 2. Bennett University (The Times Group)
- **Media & Technology Backing**: Leveraging the formidable corporate ecosystem of the Times of India Group, students learn directly from global editors, CXOs, and industry leaders.
- **Modern Infrastructure**: World-class 68-acre residential campus with cutting-edge analytics labs (IBM, NVIDIA, Dell) and seed funding support via Bennett Hatchery.

### 3. GL Bajaj Institute of Management & Research (GLBIMR)
- **ROI Champion**: With fees under ₹8.5 Lakhs and consistent average domestic salaries exceeding ₹8 LPA, GL Bajaj delivers exceptional value for students with moderate percentiles (60–75 in MAT/CMAT/CAT).
- **Placement Track**: Heavy hiring in IT-ITES services, retail banking, FMCG sales, and supply chain logistics.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What CAT percentile is required for BIMTECH Greater Noida?
For the PGDM Core program, BIMTECH shortlists candidates at **75+ percentile in CAT/XAT** or **85+ percentile in CMAT**. For specialized programs like Insurance Business Management (IBM) and Retail Management (RM), the cutoff is around 65–70 percentile in CAT/XAT/MAT.

### Is GL Bajaj good for PGDM?
Yes. GL Bajaj is considered one of the best budget-friendly PGDM institutions in Delhi NCR, providing a balanced return on investment, rigorous corporate grooming, and high placement conversion in banking and IT services.

### Can I get direct admission in Greater Noida MBA colleges?
Approved autonomous colleges maintain merit thresholds through national entrance exams (CAT/XAT/CMAT/MAT). However, private university MBA programs (Bennett, Galgotias) and institutional management seats in AICTE colleges offer profile-based direct evaluation for qualifying candidates.

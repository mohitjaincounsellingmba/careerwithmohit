## 7. Step-by-Step Admission Process & Key Timelines

To secure a seat in the 2027–2029 MBA batch at IIM Ranchi, follow the standard CAP roadmap:

1. **Step 1 - CAT Registration (August – September)**: Register for the Common Admission Test (CAT) and select IIM Ranchi and your preferred programs (MBA, MBA-HR, MBA-BA).
2. **Step 2 - Appear for CAT (November)**: Secure sectional scores above 73 percentile and overall percentile above 94.
3. **Step 3 - CAP Shortlist Call (January)**: Receive IIM CAP interview shortlist based on CAT scores and profile parameters.
4. **Step 4 - Centralized Personal Interview (February – March)**: Attend the common online CAP Personal Interview conducted by the coordinating IIM.
5. **Step 5 - Final Merit Offer (May)**: Check program-specific merit lists and deposit the confirmation fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What are the specialized MBA programs available at IIM Ranchi?
IIM Ranchi offers three distinct two-year full-time master's programs: Master of Business Administration (MBA General), MBA in Human Resource Management (MBA-HR), and MBA in Business Analytics (MBA-BA).

### 2. How are the placements for MBA-HR at IIM Ranchi?
IIM Ranchi's MBA-HR program is among the most reputed HR programs in India, recording an audited average CTC of **₹17.30 LPA** and top recruiters including Aditya Birla Group, Tata Steel, Asian Paints, and Flipkart.

### 3. Does IIM Ranchi have its own permanent campus?
Yes. IIM Ranchi operates from its 60-acre permanent campus at Prabandhan Nagar, Nayasarai, Ranchi. The campus features smart academic buildings, single-occupancy student residences, and sports facilities.

### 4. What are the summer internship stipends at IIM Ranchi?
The average two-month summer internship stipend at IIM Ranchi stands at **₹1.25 Lakhs**, with the highest stipend offered exceeding **₹3.00 Lakhs** for the two-month duration.

### 5. Can I get direct admission in IIM Ranchi?
No. There is no management quota, NRI quota, or direct admission route at IIM Ranchi. Admission is strictly through CAT scores and the centralized IIM CAP evaluation process.

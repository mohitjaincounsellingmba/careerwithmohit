## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Is Masters' Union recognized and worth the ₹25 Lakh fee?
Yes. Masters' Union has rapidly proven its recruiting prowess with audited placement reports showing average CTCs above ₹31 LPA, placing students in top-tier tech, consulting, and product firms in Cyber City and Bangalore.

### 2. What is the difference between MDI Gurgaon PGDM and PGDM-BA?
The flagship PGDM is a general management program, whereas PGDM-BA focuses 70% of its curriculum on data analytics, machine learning for managers, cloud data infrastructure, and quantitative decision-making.

### 3. Can I get direct admission in Gurgaon Business Analytics colleges?
Institutions like JKBS, BML Munjal, SOIL, and Amity conduct institutional entrance tests and profile-based interview rounds. MDI Gurgaon admits solely through CAT/GMAT merit.

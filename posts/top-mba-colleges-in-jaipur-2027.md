### 1. [Jaipuria Institute of Management](/colleges/jaipuria-institute-of-management-jaipur), Jaipur (Pratap Nagar)

- **Campus Life**: State-of-the-art Wi-Fi campus with strong entrepreneurship incubation and digital marketing labs.

### 2. IIHMR University Jaipur (The Healthcare Gold Standard)
- **Niche Leadership**: Designated as a WHO Collaborating Centre for District Health Systems. Undisputed national leader for careers in Hospital Operations, Health Analytics, and Public Health Consulting.

### 3. MNIT Jaipur - Department of Management Studies (DMS)
- **ROI Champion**: Backed by the prestigious Malaviya National Institute of Technology (NIT) engineering and research infrastructure, offering MBA specializations in Operations, Finance, and Marketing at minimal tuition cost.

### What is the placement scenario for MBA Hospital Management at IIHMR Jaipur?
IIHMR University records virtually 100% placement with average packages hovering around ₹9.80 LPA and top packages touching ₹18 LPA in hospital chains, pharmaceutical multinationals, and global health research organizations.

### Can I get admission in Jaipuria Jaipur with MAT score?
Yes, Jaipuria Jaipur accepts MAT scores with a minimum threshold of **75 to 80 percentile**, followed by Extempore/Case Discussion and Personal Interview rounds.

### What are the best affordable MBA colleges in Jaipur?
**MNIT Jaipur** (₹2.80 Lakhs total fees) and **Poddar Group of Institutions** (₹4.50 Lakhs) offer quality education with high returns on investment.

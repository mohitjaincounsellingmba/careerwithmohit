## 4. GGSIPU Cutoff Percentiles & Quota Rules (2027 Admissions)

Admission to MAIMS is conducted strictly through GGSIPU centralized counseling under Code 101. 

### The 85% vs 15% Seat Reservation Rule
- **Delhi Region Candidates (85% Quota)**: If you completed your undergraduate degree (BBA, B.Com, B.Tech, etc.) from any college physically located in NCT Delhi (DU, IPU, Jamia, etc.), you qualify under the Delhi pool. Cutoffs are significantly lower.
- **Outside Delhi Candidates (15% Quota)**: If your graduation was completed outside Delhi, you compete in the smaller All-India quota where cutoffs shoot upward.

### Expected Cutoff Benchmarks

| **Outside Delhi (General)** | **CAT 2026** | **80.00 – 86.00 %ile** | Rank 450 – 850 |

> 📌 **Counseling Tip**: GGSIPU first allocates all seats to valid CAT takers. CMAT candidates are only called if seats remain vacant after multiple CAT counseling rounds. If you hold a 72+ CAT percentile, you stand a solid chance of securing MAIMS in Round 2 or the Sliding Round.

Every college has tradeoffs. Here is what students say after enrolling:

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What degree does MAIMS Rohini grant upon graduation?
MAIMS awards a recognized Master of Business Administration (MBA) degree directly from Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU), a statutory state university accredited with NAAC 'A++'.

### 2. Can I get direct admission in MAIMS Rohini under Management Quota?
Yes. 10% of total seats in private self-financing GGSIPU colleges are filled under the institutional Management Quota. The process is conducted transparently based on qualifying graduation marks and entrance exam merit following University notifications.

### 3. How does MAIMS compare to private PGDM colleges charging ₹15 Lakhs?
If your post-MBA goal is an entry-level corporate role at ₹6.0L to ₹7.5L CTC, paying ₹4 Lakhs at MAIMS offers far superior financial ROI than spending ₹15–18 Lakhs at mid-tier private b-schools that deliver similar median salaries.

### 4. What are the class timings and attendance requirements?
Classes run from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. GGSIPU enforces a strict minimum 75% attendance rule to be eligible for end-semester examinations.

### 5. What are the best PG accommodations near MAIMS Rohini?
Rohini Sectors 22, 23, and 24 offer numerous student PGs and rental flats with food and Wi-Fi, with average monthly expenses ranging between ₹8,000 and ₹12,000.

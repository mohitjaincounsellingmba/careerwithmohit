## 1. Institutional Background: Over Three Decades of Business Education in Kharghar

Founded in 1991 under the visionary leadership of Dr. P.V. Ramana, ITM Business School Kharghar has evolved into one of the largest and most dynamic business education centers in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR). Spread across a contemporary institutional campus in Kharghar, Navi Mumbai, the institution enjoys close proximity to the expanding commercial centers of CBD Belapur, Taloja Industrial Estate, Thane-Belapur corridor, and the upcoming Navi Mumbai International Airport.

Approved by the **All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)**, accredited by **NBA**, and granted MBA equivalence by the **AIU**, ITM Kharghar combines academic curriculum rigor with practical executive training. The school has developed partnerships with multinational corporations and foreign universities, producing over 45,000 alumni positioned across top financial hubs, technology parks, and consumer brands worldwide.

For candidates aiming for the Mumbai financial ecosystem who seek extensive corporate exposure beyond traditional 8-week summer internships, ITM Navi Mumbai represents a tried and tested pathway.

## 📋 DU BCom & UG Eligibility Criteria 2026

To qualify for admission to undergraduate courses at Delhi University, you must meet the general guidelines set by the university, as well as course-specific subject combinations.

### General Eligibility Rules:
1. **Academic Qualification:** You must have passed the Class 12 board examination (CBSE, ICSE, or any recognized state board). A minimum of 50% marks in Class 12 is generally required for General/EWS candidates, with relaxations for reserved categories.
2. **Subject Mapping Requirement:** This is the most critical rule. **You must appear in CUET UG 2026 only in those subjects that you have passed in your Class 12 board exams.** Choosing subjects in CUET that you did not study in Class 12 will lead to immediate disqualification.
3. **No Age Bar:** There is no minimum or maximum age limit for applying to DU UG programs.
4. **Gap Years:** Gap years are fully accepted and do not incur any mark deductions.

### Course-Specific CUET Combinations for B.Com
DU offers two B.Com tracks with distinct entry requirements:

#### 1. B.Com (Honours)
You must select either of the following two subject combinations in CUET:
*   **Combination I:** Any 1 Language from List A + Mathematics / Applied Mathematics + Any 2 subjects (out of which at least one should be from List B1).
*   **Combination II:** Any 1 Language from List A + Accountancy / Book Keeping + Any 2 subjects (out of which at least one should be from List B1).

#### 2. B.Com (Programme)
The program course has a broader scope and allows two combinations:
*   **Combination I:** Any 1 Language from List A + Any 2 subjects from List B1 + Any 1 subject from either List B1 or List B2.
*   **Combination II:** Any 1 Language from List A + Any 1 subject from List B1 + General Test (Section III).

*(List A contains languages, List B1 contains main domain subjects, and List B2 contains vocational/additional subjects. Refer to the official DU Information Bulletin for details).*

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step DU CSAS Counselling Process 2026

The admission process is organized into three progressive phases on the CSAS portal:

### Phase 1: Registration on CSAS Portal
Once the registration window opens, visit [admission.uod.ac.in](https://admission.uod.ac.in) and create an account. You will need to fill in personal details, upload documents (Class 10/12 marksheets, category certificates, etc.), and pay a non-refundable registration fee.

### Phase 2: Program and College Preferences
This is the most critical phase. You will see a list of programs and colleges you are eligible for based on your Class XII subjects and CUET scores. You must rank these combinations in order of your preference. 
*   **Pro Tip:** Always list your top dream colleges (e.g., SRCC, Hindu) at the very top of your preference sheet. The allocation algorithm evaluates preferences from top to bottom.

### Phase 3: Seat Allocation and Admission
DU will run multiple rounds of seat allocation. When a seat is allocated:
1.  **Accept the Seat:** You must click "Accept Allocation" within the specified timeframe.
2.  **College Verification:** The college will verify your submitted documents online.
3.  **Fee Payment:** Once approved, pay the admission fee online to secure your seat.
4.  **Upgrade or Freeze:** You can choose to "Freeze" your seat if you are satisfied, or choose to "Upgrade" to participate in the next round for a higher preference.

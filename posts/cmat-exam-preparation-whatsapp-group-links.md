## CMAT 2026 Exam Pattern & Structure

Understand CMAT's 5-section breakdown for strategic preparation:

* **Section 1: Quantitative Techniques & Data Interpretation** – 20 Questions (80 Marks)
* **Section 2: Logical Reasoning** – 20 Questions (80 Marks)
* **Section 3: Language Comprehension** – 20 Questions (80 Marks)
* **Section 4: General Awareness** – 20 Questions (80 Marks)
* **Section 5: Innovation & Entrepreneurship** – 20 Questions (80 Marks)
* **Total:** 100 Questions | 400 Marks | 180 Minutes (3 Hours) | Marking: +4 for correct, -1 for incorrect.

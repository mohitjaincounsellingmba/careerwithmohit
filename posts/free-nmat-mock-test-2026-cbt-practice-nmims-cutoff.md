## Golden Strategy Tips to Score 240+ in NMAT 2026

1. **Never Leave Any Question Unanswered:** Because there is **no negative marking**, leaving even 3 questions unattempted at the end of a section throws away free marks. If you have 30 seconds left, mark your best educated guess on all remaining questions!
2. **Master Speed Mental Math for Quants:** Quantitative Skills contains extensive Data Interpretation (DI) calculations. Practicing percentage-to-fraction conversions and Vedic multiplication will save you at least 8–10 minutes.
3. **Pace Your Reading in Language Skills:** 36 questions in 28 minutes means you have less than 47 seconds per question. Solve vocabulary, error spotting, and prepositions first before attempting Reading Comprehension passages.

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the total fee for the 2-Year PGDM at SOIL Gurgaon for the 2027–29 batch?
The total 2-year academic fee for the PGDM program is approximately **₹17.17 Lakhs** (including tuition, study materials, student activity funds, and a ₹30,000 refundable security deposit).

### Q2. Is the hostel mandatory at SOIL Gurgaon?
No, hostel accommodation is **optional**. Students living locally in Gurgaon or Delhi-NCR can commute as day scholars. For outstation students, SOIL offers managed air-conditioned residences starting from **₹1.40 Lakhs per year**.

### Q3. Does SOIL provide fee concessions for high CAT or XAT percentiles?
Yes, SOIL provides merit scholarships ranging from **₹1.50 Lakhs to ₹3.00 Lakhs** for candidates scoring above 85 percentile in CAT/XAT or above 650 in GMAT, combined with strong academic performance.

### Q4. Can I pay the SOIL Gurgaon PGDM fees in installments?
Yes, the academic fee is divided across **6 structured installments** over the two-year duration, allowing candidates and banks to disburse payments term by term.

### Q5. What is the fee structure for the 1-Year PGPM program at SOIL?
The 1-Year PGPM (Business Leadership) fee is approximately **₹15.33 Lakhs to ₹15.95 Lakhs**, while the 1-Year PGPM-HR is **₹13.85 Lakhs to ₹14.50 Lakhs**.

## 🔄 Raw Score vs Scaled Score Normalization

Since CAT is conducted in three different slots (Slot 1, Slot 2, Slot 3), the difficulty level of sections varies. To prevent any unfair advantage or disadvantage, IIMs employ an **Equipercentile Normalization Process**.

* **Raw Score:** The absolute points you obtain according to the +3/-1 scheme.
* **Scaled Score:** The raw score adjusted mathematically to place candidates from different slots on a standard comparable scale.
* **Percentile Score:** Your relative position indicating the percentage of candidates who scored equal to or less than you.

For example, a score of 40 in a very difficult slot might scale up to 45, whereas the same raw score in an easier slot might scale down to 38. Percentiles are calculated entirely on the normalized scaled score.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How is the CAT 2026 raw score calculated?
For MCQs, you get +3 for a correct answer and -1 for an incorrect answer. For TITA (Type In The Answer) questions, you get +3 for a correct answer and 0 for an incorrect answer. Unattempted questions get 0 marks.

### What is the difference between raw score and scaled score?
The raw score is calculated directly based on the marking scheme. The scaled score is calculated by normalization across slots to account for differences in paper difficulty.

### How can I check my expected percentile for CAT 2026?
You can enter your sectional MCQs and TITA attempts in our online CAT 2026 Score Calculator to instantly get your raw scores, scaled scores, and expected overall percentile.

### Does a higher raw score guarantee a higher percentile?
Only within the same slot. Across different slots, a lower raw score in a difficult slot can sometimes result in a higher scaled score and percentile than a higher raw score in an easier slot.

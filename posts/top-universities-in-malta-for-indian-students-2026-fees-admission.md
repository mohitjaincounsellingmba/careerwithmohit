Malta is Europe's **best-kept secret for Indian students** — a tiny but mighty island nation in the Mediterranean Sea that punches well above its weight. As the **only English-speaking country in the EU** (besides Ireland), Malta offers Indian students an English-medium European education in a warm, safe, and affordable environment — all within the Schengen Zone. With fees as low as ₹5.5 Lakhs/year and programs from medicine to gaming, Malta is a rising star in 2026.

---

## 🎓 Why Study in Malta in 2026?

- **English as Official Language:** One of only two EU countries (alongside Ireland) with English as an official language
- **Schengen Zone:** Travel across 27 European countries on your student visa
- **Affordable Fees:** ₹5.5–₹28 Lakhs/year
- **Mediterranean Lifestyle:** Sun, sea, and safety — Malta is one of Europe's safest countries
- **Booming Gaming & Tech Industry:** Malta is the "iGaming Capital of Europe"
- **EU/EEA Work Rights:** Graduate and work anywhere in the EU

## 🌍 Malta's Schengen Advantage

As a **Schengen Zone Member**, your Malta Student Visa allows:
- Visa-free travel to 27 European countries (90 days per 180 days)
- Attend interviews, internships, and cultural experiences across Europe
- Gateway to EU permanent residency (long-term)

## 🛂 Malta Student Visa

- **Applicable For:** Programs longer than 3 months
- **Processing Time:** 3–6 weeks
- **Documents Required:** Acceptance letter, passport, financial proof, health insurance, accommodation proof
- **Financial Proof:** €8,000/year (approx. ₹7.5 Lakhs)
- **Work Rights:** 20 hours/week during studies

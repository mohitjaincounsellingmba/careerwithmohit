## 10 Unique Selling Points (USPs) of Delhi School of Business

### 1. 🏆 NBA Accreditation — Quality Seal from India's Top Tech Regulator

DSB is **NBA (National Board of Accreditation)** accredited — the apex body that evaluates technical and management programs in India. NBA accreditation signals:
- **Rigorous curriculum quality** reviewed by independent experts
- Recognised for **government job eligibility** where NBA accreditation is mandated
- A mark of distinction that fewer than 20% of private B-schools in Delhi carry

Combined with AICTE approval and AIU equivalency, DSB's PGDM is among the most credentialed programs in its fee bracket.

---

### 2. 💻 PGDM in FinTech — India's Most In-Demand Specialization

DSB offers a dedicated **PGDM (FinTech)** — one of very few private B-schools in Delhi to offer this as a full standalone program:

- Covers **Blockchain, Digital Payments, InsurTech, RegTech, and WealthTech**
- Curriculum designed with inputs from BFSI companies and FinTech startups
- Prepares students for roles in **digital banking, payments technology, and financial analytics**
- Direct pipeline to India's booming ₹50,000+ crore FinTech sector

With companies like Paytm, Razorpay, PhonePe, and BankBazaar actively hiring MBA graduates, a FinTech PGDM from DSB is a rare positioning advantage.

### 4. 🔬 Analytics E-Lab + Finance Lab + Behavioral Lab — Premium Infrastructure

DSB has invested in **dedicated specialization labs** that most sub-₹12L B-schools don't offer:

- **Analytics E-Lab**: Equipped with Python, R, Tableau, and Power BI for data-driven business analysis
- **Finance Lab**: Bloomberg terminal access and financial modeling tools
- **Behavioral & Communication Lab**: For GD/PI prep, body language training, and presentation skills

These labs translate classroom theory into **hands-on, software-driven skills** that recruiters actively test during selection.

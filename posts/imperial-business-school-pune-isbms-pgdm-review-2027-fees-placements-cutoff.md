## 🔍 7. Mohit Jain's Honest Verdict: Who Should Apply vs Who Should Avoid?

**Candidates laser-focused on banking, fund operations, and financial research in Pune's BFSI corridor should strongly consider ISBMS, while generalist marketing aspirants and Tier-1 percentile holders should look elsewhere.**

### ✅ Who Should Apply to Imperial Business School Pune:

1. **Finance Enthusiasts**: Students targeting commercial banking, equity research, fund management, and fintech operations.
2. **Candidates with 50–70 Percentile Scores**: Those who scored mid-range percentiles in CAT/XAT/CMAT/CET and want strong corporate return on investment without paying ₹18–25 Lakhs in Tier-1 private B-schools.
3. **Commerce & Engineering Graduates in Pune/Maharashtra**: Candidates who want direct proximity to Hinjawadi and Magarpatta multinational finance hubs.

### ❌ Who Should Avoid or Reconsider:

1. **Aspirants Seeking Core Marketing, Advertising, or HR**: ISBMS is heavily skewed toward finance; marketing and HR placement opportunities are comparatively limited.
2. **Candidates with Top 90+ Percentiles**: Candidates who have secured calls from IIMs, SIBM Pune, SCMHRD, or JBIMS Mumbai should not settle for mid-tier institutions.
3. **Students Expecting Massive Campus Infrastructure**: ISBMS operates on a focused corporate-style setup rather than a sprawling 50-acre residential university campus.

## ❓ 8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the average package offered at Imperial Business School Pune?
The realistic median domestic CTC for the graduating batch at ISBMS Pune is between ₹6.8 LPA and ₹7.5 LPA, with top finance and investment banking operations roles reaching ₹12.0 LPA to ₹16.4 LPA.

### Is Imperial Business School Pune AICTE approved?
Yes, the Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) programs offered by Imperial School of Banking and Management Studies (ISBMS) are approved by AICTE (Ministry of Education, Government of India) and recognized by the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Maharashtra.

### What is the difference between Imperial Business School London and Imperial Business School Pune?
Imperial College Business School London is a global top-10 university business school located in the UK with fees exceeding ₹1.00 Crore INR. Imperial School of Banking and Management Studies (ISBMS) Pune is a specialized private finance B-school located in Hinjawadi, Pune, with a 2-year fee of approximately ₹9.25 Lakhs INR.

### How can I apply for direct admission or profile evaluation at ISBMS Pune?
Aspirants can submit their academic details, graduation scores (minimum 50%), and entrance test percentiles online for institutional micro-presentation and personal interview evaluation.

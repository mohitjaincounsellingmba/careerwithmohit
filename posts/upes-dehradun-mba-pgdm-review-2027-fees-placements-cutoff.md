## 1. Institutional Legacy: India's Pioneer Energy & Core Industry B-School

Established in 2003 under the UPES Act, the University of Petroleum and Energy Studies (UPES Dehradun)—through its dedicated **School of Business (SoB)**—was founded to solve a major industrial requirement: developing specialized business managers for core capital-intensive sectors including energy, hydrocarbons, power, aviation, logistics, and digital infrastructure.

Operating across two picturesque campuses in Bidholi and Kandoli in the foothills of the Himalayas in Dehradun, UPES has expanded into a comprehensive modern university. Accredited with NAAC 'A' Grade and ranked consistently within the top 40 management institutes in India by NIRF (Ministry of Education), UPES combines rigorous classroom teaching with hands-on industrial internships.

While general business schools train generalists who require months of corporate onboarding to grasp energy economics or aviation regulations, UPES graduates step into corporate roles already fluent in domain-specific terminology, regulatory frameworks, and operational models.

## 2. Program Architecture & Distinct Industry-Specialized Verticals

The School of Business at UPES offers specialized two-year full-time MBA programs tailored for high-growth sectors:

### A. MBA in Oil & Gas Management
- **Target Sectors**: Upstream exploration, midstream pipeline transportation, downstream refining, petrochemicals, LNG trading, and renewable integration.
- **Key Modules**: Petroleum Economics, International Energy Geopolitics, Petrochemical Marketing, Oil & Gas Project Management, and Environmental Regulatory Compliance.
- **Recruiters**: Reliance Industries, Shell, Schlumberger, ONGC, Baker Hughes, Indian Oil, and Halliburton.

### B. MBA in Aviation Management
- **Target Sectors**: Commercial airline operations, airport management, air cargo logistics, ground handling, and aviation leasing.
- **Key Modules**: Airline Business Models, Airport Infrastructure Planning, Air Cargo Management, Aviation Safety & Security Regulations, and Fleet Planning Economics.
- **Recruiters**: Boeing, Airbus, GMR Airports, Adani Airports, IndiGo, Air India, FedEx, and DHL Aviation.

### C. MBA in Logistics & Supply Chain Management
- **Target Sectors**: E-commerce fulfillment, multimodal shipping, warehousing automation, supply chain planning, and cold-chain infrastructure.
- **Key Modules**: Global SCM Logistics, Warehousing Systems & Automation, Strategic Sourcing, Freight Transportation Economics, and Supply Chain Analytics.
- **Recruiters**: DHL Supply Chain, Blue Dart, Amazon, Delhivery, Mahindra Logistics, and Maersk.

### D. MBA in Digital Business & Business Analytics
- **Target Sectors**: Digital transformation consulting, SaaS platforms, enterprise data analytics, and customer experience design.
- **Key Modules**: Machine Learning for Managers, Big Data Architecture, Product Management Frameworks, MarTech Stack Optimization, and Advanced SQL/Python.
- **Recruiters**: Deloitte, PwC, Accenture, Cognizant, Wipro, Infosys, and Capgemini.

### E. MBA Core Verticals (Marketing, Finance & Human Resources)
Comprehensive functional training equipped with industry certifications (KPMG, Coursera, Bloomberg, and SAP ERP modules) for candidates pursuing general corporate management.

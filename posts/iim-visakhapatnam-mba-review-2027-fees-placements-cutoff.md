## 7. Step-by-Step Admission Process & Key Timelines

Securing admission into the 2027–2029 PGP batch at IIM Visakhapatnam follows the standard CAP roadmap:

1. **Step 1 - Register for CAT (August – September)**: Apply for the Common Admission Test and select IIM Visakhapatnam.
2. **Step 2 - Appear for CAT (November)**: Secure qualifying sectional and overall percentiles.
3. **Step 3 - CAP Shortlist Call (January)**: Receive IIM CAP interview shortlist based on CAT scores and profile parameters.
4. **Step 4 - Centralized Personal Interview (February – March)**: Attend the common online CAP Personal Interview conducted by the coordinating IIM.
5. **Step 5 - Final Merit Offer (May)**: Check composite score merit lists and pay the seat confirmation fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What is the fee structure for the PGP/MBA at IIM Visakhapatnam?
The total course fee for the two-year full-time PGP at IIM Visakhapatnam is approximately **₹17.80 Lakhs**, covering academic tuition, single-occupancy hostel accommodations, digital library access, and course case studies.

### 2. What is the average placement package at IIM Visakhapatnam?
The audited average placement package at IIM Visakhapatnam stands between **₹15.64 LPA and ₹16.62 LPA**, with the median CTC benchmarked at **₹16.00 LPA** and the top 10% average reaching **₹23.80 LPA**.

### 3. Does IIM Visakhapatnam have its own permanent campus?
Yes. IIM Visakhapatnam operates fully from its sprawling 241-acre permanent green campus located at Gambheeram, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh.

### 4. What are the summer internship stipend figures at IIM Visakhapatnam?
The average two-month summer internship stipend at IIM Visakhapatnam stands at **₹85,000**, with the highest stipend offered reaching **₹2.50 Lakhs** for the two-month period.

### 5. Can I get direct admission in IIM Visakhapatnam without CAT?
No. Direct admission, management quota, or NRI quota seats do not exist at IIM Visakhapatnam. Admission is strictly based on CAT merit, IIM CAP interview evaluation, and profile composite scores.

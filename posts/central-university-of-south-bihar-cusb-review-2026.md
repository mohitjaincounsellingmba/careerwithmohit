[Central University of South Bihar](/colleges/cusb-gaya) (CUSB), situated in Gaya, has rapidly emerged as a center of academic excellence. With a strong focus on integrated programs and research, CUSB offers modern infrastructure and quality education.

## 📊 CUSB 2026 Overview: Fees, Placements & Cutoffs

| Category | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Entrance Exam** | CUET UG & CUET PG |
| **UG Integrated Course Fees** | ₹49,800 - ₹84,300 (Total) |
| **Median UG Package** | ₹6.80 LPA - ₹7.80 LPA |
| **Top Recruiters** | Azim Premji Foundation, Edu Academies |
| **Hostel Fees** | ~₹16,000 (1st Sem), ~₹9,000 (Subsequent) |

## Admission Process & CUET Cutoff

CUSB conducts its admission entirely through the **CUET** framework via the SAMARTH portal.
*   **Eligibility**: 10+2 with at least 50% for UG; Bachelor's with 55% for PG.
*   **Cutoffs**: General category cutoffs for popular UG programs typically range between 130 and 145+ marks. Highly sought-after courses like B.Sc. Agriculture demand much higher scores (370–390 range).

## Fee Structure & Hostel Facilities

*   **Tuition**: Integrated programs like B.A. B.Ed. and B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) are reasonably priced between ₹49,800 and ₹84,300 for the total duration.
*   **Hostels**: The Gaya campus features well-furnished, Wi-Fi-enabled hostels. Due to limited capacity, allotment is strictly on a merit-cum-reservation basis. Mess charges are around ₹3,300 per month.

## Placement Statistics

CUSB has a dedicated placement cell that ensures good corporate and institutional reach.
*   **Salary Packages**: The median salary for UG and PG students ranges comfortably between ₹6.8 LPA to ₹7.8 LPA.
*   **Opportunities**: Strong placements are observed in the education sector, NGOs, and various corporate roles.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I check my exam results online?
You can check results on the official website of the conducting bodies (e.g., NTA for JEE/NEET/CUET, or respective boards/testing organizations) using your application registration number.

### Are mock tests helpful in exam preparation?
Yes, attempting mock tests helps candidates build speed and accuracy, understand the exam pattern, and perform detailed analytics of strong and weak sections.

### What is the significance of negative marking in entrance exams?
Many competitive exams deduct marks for wrong answers (e.g., -1 or -0.25). Candidates should avoid guessing to maintain accuracy and prevent score drops.

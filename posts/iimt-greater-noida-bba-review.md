[IIMT Group of Colleges](/colleges/iimt-greater-noida), located in the bustling educational hub of Knowledge Park III, Greater Noida, has established a strong presence for itself over the last two decades. The BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration) program is run under the **IIMT College of Management** and is highly sought after by students looking for an affordable, career-focused management degree in the Delhi NCR region.

In this **IIMT Greater Noida BBA Review**, we break down the course structure, fee details, placements, and overall ROI to help you decide if IIMT is the right college for your undergraduate management journey.

---

## 🏫 IIMT College of Management: Overview
The BBA program at IIMT Greater Noida is designed to build a strong foundation in core business concepts, economics, marketing, and corporate communication.

### **Key Highlights**
*   **Affiliation:** Affiliated with **Chaudhary Charan Singh (CCS) University, Meerut** (unlike engineering/MBA programs which are affiliated with AKTU).
*   **Approvals:** Approved by the **AICTE** and accredited with a **NAAC B++ grade**.
*   **Location Advantage:** Located in Knowledge Park III, Greater Noida, surrounded by hundreds of corporate hubs and industrial corridors, providing direct exposure to industry events.

---

## 💰 IIMT Greater Noida BBA Fees 
One of the major advantages of pursuing a BBA from IIMT is its highly competitive and budget-friendly fee structure. Compared to premium private universities in the NCR region (which can cost up to INR 3 Lakhs per year), IIMT offers a strong value proposition.

| Course | Duration | Approximate Annual Fees |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **BBA (General)** | 3 Years | **₹79,000 – ₹99,990** |

*Note: Hostel fees are additional, varying between ₹80,000 to ₹1,20,000 per year depending on room type (AC/Non-AC) and occupancy.*

## 🎯 Course Curriculum & Specializations
The BBA program is structured across 6 semesters and includes core management subjects along with opportunities to specialize. While CCS University defines the core syllabus, IIMT supplements it with soft-skills training, digital marketing modules, and personality development programs (PDP).

Major concentration areas covered under general electives include:
*   **Marketing Management**
*   **Financial Management**
*   **Human Resource Management**
*   **Information Technology (IT) in Business**

---

## 📈 Placement Review & Recruiters
IIMT has a dedicated Training and Placement Cell that guides undergraduate students through mock interviews, resume-building sessions, and group discussions. While PGDM/MBA packages are higher, undergraduate BBA students also receive decent placement opportunities.

*   **Average Package (BBA):** **₹3.0 – ₹4.5 LPA**
*   **Highest Package (Overall Group):** **₹12.0 LPA** (usually secured by engineering or PG management students)
*   **Median Placement Salary:** **₹3.5 LPA**
*   **Top Recruiters:** TCS, Wipro, Infosys, Tech Mahindra, Chetu, GTF Technologies, Genpact, and Amazon.

For BBA graduates, starting profiles are typically in sales, business development, digital marketing coordination, human resources, and customer support. 

---

## 📝 Admission Process
Admissions for the upcoming academic cycle are open. The process is straightforward:

1.  **Eligibility Criteria:** Candidates must have cleared their 10+2 examination from a recognized board (CBSE, ICSE, or state boards) with a minimum of **45% marks** for General/OBC candidates and **40% marks** for SC/ST candidates.
2.  **Selection Mode:** Admissions are conducted based on Class 12 merit through registrations on the CCS University portal, or via direct application under management seats.

## ✅ Pros and Cons: A Balanced Review
Like any institution, IIMT Greater Noida has its strengths and areas of improvement. Here is an honest breakdown:

### **Pros:**
*   **Excellent ROI:** Low tuition fees paired with steady starting salary packages make it highly budget-friendly.
*   **Vibrant Campus Life:** A large, 13-acre lush green campus with state-of-the-art libraries, canteens, and sports grounds.
*   **Location:** Knowledge Park III is easily accessible via the Aqua Line Metro, reducing travel hassles for daily commuters.
*   **Focus on Academics:** Experienced faculty members who are supportive and actively mentor students.

### **Cons:**
*   **Strict Attendance:** IIMT maintains a strict 75% attendance policy. Non-compliance can lead to issues during end-semester exams.
*   **Starting Packages:** The starting salary package for undergraduate students is modest compared to Tier-1 universities. Students who want higher packages need to focus on additional certifications (like digital marketing, analytics, etc.) and stand out.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the fee of BBA at IIMT Greater Noida?
The annual fee for the BBA program at IIMT College of Management, Greater Noida is approximately INR 80,000 to INR 99,990 per year, making it a very affordable option in Delhi NCR.

### Is IIMT Greater Noida BBA affiliated with AKTU?
No, the BBA program at IIMT Greater Noida is offered by the IIMT College of Management and is affiliated with Chaudhary Charan Singh (CCS) University, Meerut. AKTU is primarily for technical courses like B.Tech and postgraduate courses like MBA.

### What is the average package for BBA at IIMT Greater Noida?
The average salary package for undergraduate BBA graduates at IIMT Greater Noida is around INR 3.0 to INR 4.5 LPA, with recruiters like TCS, Wipro, Infosys, and Tech Mahindra visiting the campus.

### Is direct admission available for BBA at IIMT Greater Noida?
Yes, candidates meeting the eligibility criteria (minimum 45% aggregate in 10+2 from a recognized board) can apply for direct merit-based admission or register through CCS University counseling.

Running a career upgrade requires choosing the right management program. For working professionals in Delhi, the Executive MBA / Executive PGDM offered by [Jamia Millia Islamia (JMI), Delhi](/colleges/jamia-millia-islamia) represents a powerful gateway to higher senior leadership positions.

In this review, we break down everything you need to know: fees, admission cutoffs, placements, pros, cons, and our honest expert verdict.

---

## 🏆 Program Overview

- **Official Course Name:** Executive MBA
- **Approximate Fees:** ₹1.20 Lakhs (Total Fees)
- **Entrance Exams Accepted:** JMI Entrance Test + Group Discussion + Personal Interview
- **Course Duration:** 15 Months to 2 Years (Format varies by program)

---

## 📈 Eligibility & Admission Details

- **Academic Requirements:** Bachelor's degree with min 50% + minimum 3 years of executive or managerial experience.
- **Selection Process:** Admission typically relies on professional profile screening, statement of purpose (SOP), executive experience reviews, and personal interviews. Elite IIM programs also mandate standard quantitative testing (GMAT/GRE).

---

## 💼 Placement Statistics & Career Impact

- **Estimated Placements:** Average lateral packages range between ₹8-10 LPA.
- **Career Growth:** Executive MBA degrees focus on helping candidates secure senior lateral shifts (like VP, Product Manager, or Director roles) or move from technical paths into core management.

---

## 📝 Student Reviews: Pros & Cons

### Pros
- **Highlight:** Remarkably low fee structure of ₹60,000 per year.
- **Highlight:** Weekend classes (Saturdays & Sundays) for ideal work-life balance.
- **Highlight:** Central University status with NAAC A++ accreditation.

### Cons
- **Limitation:** Limited corporate placements compared to commercial private B-schools.
- **Limitation:** Fewer international study trips or global exchange options.

---

## 🔍 Our Expert Verdict

The Executive MBA program at [Jamia Millia Islamia (JMI), Delhi](/colleges/jamia-millia-islamia) is highly recommended for professionals based in Delhi who want to scale their careers without disrupting their current geographic setup. 

If you are looking for top-tier consulting placements and have 5+ years of experience, full-time residential paths are stellar. However, if you are looking to continue your full-time job, their weekend/evening classes offer outstanding return on investment.

---

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the selection process for Jamia Millia Islamia Executive MBA?
Candidates must clear the JMI written entrance exam, followed by a Group Discussion and a Personal Interview.

### Is Jamia's Executive MBA approved by UGC?
Yes, the Executive MBA at Jamia Millia Islamia is fully approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and recognized across government and corporate sectors.

### What are the class timings?
Classes are conducted during weekends (Saturdays and Sundays) at the Faculty of Management Studies building on JMI campus.

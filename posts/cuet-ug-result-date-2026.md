## 📝 How to Download Your CUET UG 2026 Scorecard?

Follow these steps to access and download your official CUET UG scorecard:

1. Visit the official website: **[cuet.nta.nic.in](https://cuet.nta.nic.in)**.
2. Click on the link titled **"CUET UG 2026 Scorecard / Result Download"** on the homepage.
3. You will be redirected to the login page. Enter your:
   * **Application Number**
   * **Date of Birth** or **Password**
   * Security Pin (Captcha)
4. Click **"Submit"** to log in to your dashboard.
5. Your CUET UG 2026 scorecard displaying subject-wise normalized scores and percentiles will appear on the screen.
6. Download the PDF and take 2-3 printouts for the counselling process.

## 🚦 What Happens Next? Understanding the Admission Process

Many candidates make the mistake of waiting for universities to contact them after results are declared. Here is what actually happens:

### 1. No Centralized Counseling
NTA's responsibility ends with releasing the scorecards. There is **no common counseling** for all CUET-participating universities. You must apply to each university separately.

### 2. Registering on University Portals
Universities will launch their own admission portals. For instance:
* **Delhi University (DU):** CSAS (Common Seat Allocation System) portal.
* **IP University (GGSIPU):** IPU Admissions portal.
* **BHU / JNU:** Respective university counselling websites.

### 3. Creating Preference Sheets
This is the most critical phase. You will have to fill in your preferences for college and course combinations. For example, ranking *SRCC - B.Com (Hons)* above *LSR - B.Com (Hons)*. If you list colleges in the wrong order, you might miss out on a superior college despite having a high score.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I check my exam results online?
You can check results on the official website of the conducting bodies (e.g., NTA for JEE/NEET/CUET, or respective boards/testing organizations) using your application registration number.

### Are mock tests helpful in exam preparation?
Yes, attempting mock tests helps candidates build speed and accuracy, understand the exam pattern, and perform detailed analytics of strong and weak sections.

### What is the significance of negative marking in entrance exams?
Many competitive exams deduct marks for wrong answers (e.g., -1 or -0.25). Candidates should avoid guessing to maintain accuracy and prevent score drops.

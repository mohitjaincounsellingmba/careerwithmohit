The Anil Surendra Modi School of Commerce (ASMSOC) at [NMIMS Mumbai](/colleges/nmims-mumbai) is widely considered one of the top private BBA colleges in India. Located in the financial capital, it offers students unparalleled access to the corporate world through guest lectures, internships, and a modern curriculum.

## 🏛️ Why Choose [NMIMS Mumbai](/colleges/nmims-mumbai) (ASMSOC) in 2026?
NMIMS is famous for its **"Corporate Readiness"** program. The BBA here is not just about textbooks; it’s about networking and professional grooming.
- **Strategic Location:** Situated in Vile Parle, Mumbai, close to India's biggest corporate offices.
- **Industry Exposure:** Compulsory internships and industry projects every semester.
- **Global Alumni Base:** Thousands of alumni working in top global firms.

## 💰 Latest Fee Structure (2026)
NMIMS is a premium private university. For the 2026 session, the approximate fees for the BBA program are:
- **Annual Tuition Fee:** ₹3,25,000 – ₹3,50,000 per year.
- **Hostel & Living:** Since it's Mumbai, living costs can range from ₹2.5 Lakhs to ₹4 Lakhs per year depending on accommodation choices.

## 🚀 Placement Process & Statistics 2026
ASMSOC has a dedicated placement cell that focuses on finance, marketing, and consulting roles for undergraduates.
- **Average Package:** ₹10.5 LPA (Approx.)
- **Highest Package:** ₹22 LPA
- **Top Recruiters:** Goldman Sachs, Deloitte, KPMG, EY, JP Morgan, Google.
- **Placement Process:** Includes Pre-Placement Talks (PPTs), Resume Shortlisting, and multiple interview rounds.

## 📝 Admission Process 2026 (NPAT)
Admission to NMIMS BBA is strictly through the **NMIMS-NPAT (National Test for Programs After Twelfth)**.
- **Exam Pattern:** Quantitative Reasoning, Logical Reasoning, and Verbal Reasoning.
- **No Negative Marking:** One of the few exams without negative marking, making it a test of speed and accuracy.
- **City Selection:** NMIMS has campuses in Mumbai, Bengaluru, Navi Mumbai, Indore, Hyderabad, Chandigarh, and Dhule. Your NPAT score determines which campus you get.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Q1. Is Maths compulsory for BBA in NMIMS?**
Yes, Mathematics is a mandatory subject in Class 12 for admission to the BBA program at NMIMS ASMSOC.

**Q2. Can I get direct admission in NMIMS Mumbai for BBA?**
No, NMIMS does not have a management quota for BBA. All admissions are through the merit list of the NPAT exam.

**Q3. Which is the best campus of NMIMS for BBA?**
The Mumbai campus (ASMSOC) is the flagship and most sought-after campus, followed by Bengaluru and Navi Mumbai.

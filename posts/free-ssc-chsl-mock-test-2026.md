## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the pattern of the SSC CHSL Tier 1 exam?
SSC CHSL Tier 1 consists of 100 questions (25 each in English, Reasoning, Quant, and General Awareness) carrying 200 marks, to be completed in 60 minutes.

### Is there negative marking in SSC CHSL Tier 1?
Yes, there is a negative marking of 0.50 marks for each wrong answer in the Tier 1 examination.

### What posts are filled through the SSC CHSL exam?
SSC CHSL recruits candidates for Group C government posts such as Lower Divisional Clerk (LDC), Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA), and Data Entry Operator (DEO) in various ministries.

## Step-by-Step CAP Round Counselling Registration Guide 2027

The **Centralized Admission Process (CAP)** is an online portal-driven seat allocation mechanism managed by DTE / State CET Cell Maharashtra. Here is the step-by-step procedure:

```
[1. Online CAP Registration & Fee Payment on cetcell.mahacet.org]
                             ↓
[2. Document Verification at Facilitation Centre (FC) / E-Scrutiny]
                             ↓
[3. Display of Provisional & Final Merit List]
                             ↓
[4. Option Form Filling & Choice Locking for CAP Round 1]
                             ↓
[5. Seat Allotment Display & Seat Acceptance (Freeze / Betterment)]
                             ↓
[6. Campus Reporting & Document Submission for Final Admission]
```

### Step 1: Online Registration
* Visit the official portal `mahacet.org`.
* Register using your CET Application ID and Roll Number. Candidates applying via CAT/CMAT must pay the CAP registration fee (Approx ₹1,000 for General / OMS, ₹800 for Reserved).

### Step 2: Document Verification (E-Scrutiny vs Physical FC Scrutiny)
* **E-Scrutiny Mode:** Upload soft copies of documents online. Verification officers review and verify your application remotely.
* **Physical Scrutiny Mode:** Visit a designated Facilitation Centre (FC) with original documents for in-person validation.

### Step 3: Final Merit List Publication
* DTE releases the State Level Merit Number, Category Merit Number, and OMS Merit Number. Verify your details carefully during the grievance window.

### Step 4: Option Form Filling (Crucial Strategy!)
* Candidates can select up to **300 institute choices** in order of preference.
* **Rule of Option Form:** Always place aspirational colleges (JBIMS, SIMSREE) at the top, followed by realistic choices based on your merit rank.

### Step 5: Seat Allotment & Options (Freeze vs. Auto-Freeze vs. Betterment)
* **Auto-Freeze:** If allotted your **Option 1**, the seat is automatically frozen. You MUST accept it and pay the seat acceptance fee (₹1,000), failing which you are out of subsequent CAP rounds.
* **Self-Freeze:** If allotted Option 2 to 300 and satisfied, click 'Freeze' to confirm admission.
* **Not-Freeze (Betterment):** If allotted a seat but wish to try for a higher preference in CAP Round 2 or 3, choose 'Not Freeze / Betterment' and pay the seat acceptance fee to hold the current seat.

## Outside Maharashtra State (OMS) Quota Rules & Guidelines

For aspirants outside Maharashtra, understanding OMS quota dynamics is critical:

### 1. Seat Allocation Percentage
* In Government & University Departments (like JBIMS, SIMSREE, PUMBA), **15% of total sanctioned intake** is reserved for All India Seats (OMS Quota).
* Un-aided private institutes reserve 15% All India Quota + 15% Institute Level Seats.

### 2. Entrance Score Acceptance for OMS
OMS candidates can participate in CAP rounds using:
* MAH MBA CET 2027 Percentile
* CAT 2026 Scorecard
* CMAT 2027 Scorecard

> **Important Note:** State CET Cell prepares a combined **All India Merit List** where CAT, CMAT, and MAH CET scores are normalized. Historically, CAT/CMAT scores are given equal weightage alongside CET for All India seat allocation.

### 3. Category Benefits for OMS
* **Reservation Policy:** Category reservations (SC, ST, OBC, VJ/NT, EWS) are **ONLY applicable to domicile candidates of Maharashtra**.
* All Outside Maharashtra State (OMS) candidates are treated under the **General / Unreserved Category**, regardless of their home state caste status.

## Category Reservation & Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS)

Maharashtra DTE provides substantial fee concessions for Maharashtra State category students:

* **SC / ST Candidates:** 100% Tuition Fee & Development Fee Waiver in Government/Aided colleges.
* **OBC / EBC / SEBC Candidates:** 50% Tuition Fee Waiver under Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishavrutti Yojna.
* **TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme):** 5% extra seats over sanctioned intake in each course reserved for economically backward students (Family income < ₹8 Lakhs/year). Candidates get **100% tuition fee waiver** regardless of category.

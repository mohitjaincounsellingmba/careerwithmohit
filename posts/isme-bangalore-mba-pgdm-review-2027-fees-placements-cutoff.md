## 1. Institutional Background: The Global Ivy League Pedagogy in Bangalore

The International School of Management Excellence (ISME Bangalore), established in 2006 under the NVT Quality Educational Trust, was founded by corporate executives and educators with degrees from top US universities including Carnegie Mellon, Purdue, and the Wharton School.

Located on a modern campus along Sarjapur Road, Bangalore, ISME operates at the nexus of the city’s major technology corridors—connecting Outer Ring Road, Bellandur, and Electronic City. The founders embedded quality management systems (NVT is a global ISO certification body) into the business curriculum, creating a structured, process-oriented learning environment.

Approved by the **All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)** and accredited by the **National Board of Accreditation (NBA)**, ISME integrates an international dimension: all PGDM students undergo an intensive global business immersion program in Singapore, visiting global corporate headquarters, port authorities, and foreign universities.

For students seeking an accredited, globally focused business school in Bangalore with fees under ₹9.50 Lakhs, ISME represents an established, dependable choice.

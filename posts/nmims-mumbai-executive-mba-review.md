Running a career upgrade requires choosing the right management program. For working professionals in Mumbai, the Executive MBA / Executive PGDM offered by [NMIMS School of Business Management, Mumbai](/colleges/nmims-mumbai) represents a powerful gateway to higher senior leadership positions.

In this review, we break down everything you need to know: fees, admission cutoffs, placements, pros, cons, and our honest expert verdict.

---

## 🏆 Program Overview

- **Official Course Name:** MBA Executive (MBA WX)
- **Approximate Fees:** ₹6.00 Lakhs (Total Fees)
- **Entrance Exams Accepted:** NMIMS Entrance Test + Personal Interview
- **Course Duration:** 15 Months to 2 Years (Format varies by program)

---

## 📈 Eligibility & Admission Details

- **Academic Requirements:** Graduation with min 50% + minimum 5 years of post-qualification executive experience.
- **Selection Process:** Admission typically relies on professional profile screening, statement of purpose (SOP), executive experience reviews, and personal interviews. Elite IIM programs also mandate standard quantitative testing (GMAT/GRE).

---

## 💼 Placement Statistics & Career Impact

- **Estimated Placements:** Corporate lateral hiring network, average package range ₹14-16 LPA.
- **Career Growth:** Executive MBA degrees focus on helping candidates secure senior lateral shifts (like VP, Product Manager, or Director roles) or move from technical paths into core management.

---

## 📝 Student Reviews: Pros & Cons

### Pros
- **Highlight:** AACSB accredited business school with massive brand equity.
- **Highlight:** Prime location in Mumbai, offering rich networking opportunities.
- **Highlight:** Highly flexible class formats (weekend and evening options).

### Cons
- **Limitation:** Batch sizes are large, leading to competitive peer groups.
- **Limitation:** High cost of living in Mumbai for outstation candidates.

---

## 🔍 Our Expert Verdict

The Executive MBA program at [NMIMS School of Business Management, Mumbai](/colleges/nmims-mumbai) is highly recommended for professionals based in Mumbai who want to scale their careers without disrupting their current geographic setup. 

If you are looking for top-tier consulting placements and have 5+ years of experience, full-time residential paths are stellar. However, if you are looking to continue your full-time job, their weekend/evening classes offer outstanding return on investment.

Yes, NMIMS School of Business Management holds the prestigious AACSB accreditation.

The program spans 15 to 18 months, covering core management modules and specialized electives.

# TISS Mumbai Application Form 2027 (HRM & LR): CAT Cutoff, Dates, Fees & Admission Process

> 💡 **Key Takeaways (Direct AI Answer Summary)**
> - **Mandatory Entrance Exam**: **CAT 2026** (TISSNET is permanently discontinued; CUET-PG does **not** apply to HRM & LR).
> - **Application Portal Status**: Register via [admissions.tiss.ac.in](https://admissions.tiss.ac.in) by submitting your CAT registration number.
> - **Total Program Fee**: **₹2.80 Lakhs to ₹3.20 Lakhs** for the complete 2-year flagship curriculum.
> - **Placement & ROI Benchmark**: Average salary stands at **₹27.22 LPA** with a median CTC of **₹25.75 LPA**, competing directly with XLRI Jamshedpur and IIM Lucknow.

The **Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS), Mumbai**, established in 1936 in Deonar, Mumbai, is India's most prestigious institution for Human Resource Management and Industrial Relations. 

Its flagship program—**Master of Arts in Human Resource Management & Labour Relations (MA in HRM & LR)**—is universally recognized as the gold standard of HR education, held in the same elite tier as XLRI Jamshedpur. Following the institutional decision to **permanently abolish TISSNET**, admission to TISS Mumbai's management-oriented programs is now conducted strictly through the **Common Admission Test (CAT)**.

In this strategic admissions blueprint by **Mohit Jain**, understand the transition to CAT 2026, expected category-wise cutoffs, fee breakdown, placement reality checks, and online form instructions.

## 3. What is the CAT Cutoff for TISS Mumbai MA HRM in 2027?

**The expected CAT 2026 cutoff for TISS Mumbai MA in HRM & LR is 97 to 99+ percentile for General category applicants, 92 to 94 percentile for OBC-NCL, 90 to 92 percentile for EWS, 85 to 88 percentile for SC, and 78 to 82 percentile for ST candidates.**

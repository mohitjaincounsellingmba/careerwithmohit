If you are looking for a prestigious opportunity to work with the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)** but want to bypass the intense competition of traditional written examinations, this notification is for you. The RBI has announced the **Young Professional (YP) Recruitment 2026** (Advertisement No: RBI/TMD1/YP/06/2026-27/01) for **12 highly specialized contractual positions** based at its Central Office in Mumbai. 

Unlike the standard RBI Grade B or Assistant posts, this hiring drive **does not require a written competitive exam**. Candidates are screened based on their qualifications and experience, followed by a direct interview.

### **Domain-Wise Vacancies & Eligibility Criteria**

The RBI is hiring across multiple niche domains. Below is the list of available domains, post codes, and the eligibility qualifications required:

#### **1. Cybersecurity & IT Risk**
* **Post Code**: `YP0626DOS01`
* **Department**: Department of Supervision (DoS)
* **Academic Criteria**: Postgraduate degree or professional qualification in Cyber Security, Information Security, Computer Science, IT, Data Science, or Artificial Intelligence.
* **Preferred Certifications**: CISSP, CISM, CEH, or ISO 27001 Lead Auditor are highly valued.

#### **2. Policy Analytics**
* **Post Code**: `YP0626DOS02`
* **Department**: Department of Supervision (DoS)
* **Academic Criteria**: Bachelor’s/Master’s in Engineering/Technology or Master’s in Statistics/Data Science with strong analytical skills in Python.

#### **3. Climate Change Risk & Sustainable Finance**
* **Post Code**: `YP0626DOR01`
* **Department**: Department of Regulation (DoR)
* **Academic Criteria**: Postgraduate degree in Environmental Studies, Climate Risk, Sustainable Finance, Public Policy, or Economics.

#### **4. Credit Risk Analytics & Regulatory Policy**
* **Post Code**: `YP0626DOR02`
* **Department**: Department of Regulation (DoR)
* **Academic Criteria**: Master's degree in Economics, Finance, Banking, or Statistics with familiarity in credit modeling and regulatory policies.

#### **5. Payment Ecosystem**
* **Post Code**: `YP0626DPS01`
* **Department**: Department of Payment & Settlement Systems (DPSS)
* **Academic Criteria**: Postgraduate qualification in IT, Computer Science, Economics, or Business Management with focus on digital payments or financial technologies.

#### **6. Financial Markets**
* **Post Code**: `YP0626FMR01`
* **Department**: Financial Markets Regulation Department (FMRD)
* **Academic Criteria**: Master’s in Finance, Economics, or Financial Engineering.

#### **7. Data Analytics & Policy Research**
* **Post Code**: `YP0626CEP01`
* **Department**: Consumer Education & Protection Department (CEPD)
* **Academic Criteria**: Bachelor's/Master's in Engineering or postgraduate in Computer Science, Data Analytics, or Statistics.

### **RBI YP Selection Process: No Exam, Direct Shortlisting**

Since there is **no written exam**, the selection process is profile-driven and comprehensive:

1. **Preliminary Screening**: A committee screens all received email applications. Candidates are shortlisted based on their academic achievements, suitability, CV strength, and the quality of their Statement of Interest.
2. **Personal Interview**: Shortlisted candidates will be invited for an in-person or virtual interview to assess technical capability, domain knowledge, and behavioral suitability.
3. **Final Selection & Document Verification**: A merit list will be prepared based purely on interview performance.

### **How to Apply: Step-by-Step Instructions**

To apply for the RBI Young Professional recruitment, candidates must submit their application directly via email:

1. Go to the official RBI website (**[rbi.org.in](https://www.rbi.org.in)**) or opportunities portal (**[opportunities.rbi.org.in](https://opportunities.rbi.org.in)**) and download the official **Application Proforma (Annexure-VI)**.
2. Fill out the application form carefully.
3. Prepare a single PDF document (maximum size **5 MB**) containing:
   - Completed Application Proforma
   - Updated Curriculum Vitae (CV)
   - Educational Certificates (Graduation, Post-graduation, Professional degrees)
   - A brief Statement of Interest / Statement of Purpose (SOP)
   - Letters of Recommendation / References (if any)
4. Send the email to the official address: **[yphrmdco@rbi.org.in](mailto:yphrmdco@rbi.org.in)**.
5. **Subject Line Format**: You must format the subject line exactly as follows:
   - **`YP Application – Post Code – Name of the Candidate`**
   - *Example:* `YP Application – YP0626DOS01 – Priya Sharma`
6. **Applying for Multiple Roles**: If you wish to apply for more than one post code, you **must submit separate emails** with separate application packets for each code.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is there a written exam for RBI Young Professional recruitment?
No, there is no written competitive exam for the RBI Young Professional (YP) program. Selection is based on preliminary profile screening, CV evaluation, and a personal interview.

### What is the salary or stipend of an RBI Young Professional?
Selected candidates will receive a consolidated fixed monthly stipend of ₹1,50,000 (subject to tax deductions). No other housing, transport, or medical allowances are provided.

### What is the age limit for applying to RBI Young Professional posts?
Candidates must be at least 21 years old and must not have exceeded 30 years of age as of July 6, 2026.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the pattern of RBI Assistant Prelims?
RBI Assistant Prelims is a 60-minute online test with 100 questions (30 English, 35 Numerical Ability, 35 Reasoning) carrying 100 marks, with 20 minutes sectional time limit for each.

### Is the cutoff high for the RBI Assistant exam?
Yes, RBI Assistant cutoffs are exceptionally high (often 90+ out of 100 for general category) due to a relatively easy paper and intense competition for RBI state offices.

### Is there negative marking in RBI Assistant?
Yes, a penalty of -0.25 marks is applied for every wrong answer marked by the candidate.

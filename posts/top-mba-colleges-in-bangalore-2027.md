## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the CAT cutoff for IIM Bangalore?
For general category candidates, the minimum qualifying percentile is 85, but the actual shortlist threshold is typically **99.3+ percentile** with high weightage on 10th, 12th, graduation marks, and quality work experience.

### Is JAGSoM Bangalore AACSB accredited?
Yes, JAGSoM is one of the few B-schools in India to hold prestigious **AACSB international accreditation**, placing it in the top 5% of business schools worldwide.

### What exams does Christ University accept for MBA?
Christ University accepts CAT, MAT (500+ composite score), CMAT (60+ percentile), XAT, and GMAT, followed by their Micro Presentation (MP), Written Assessment (WA), and Personal Interview (PI) rounds.

## 1. Institutional Background: The Electronic City Advantage in Bangalore

International School of Business & Research (ISBR Bangalore), established in 1990 under the Bangalore Education Trust, is one of South India's most established business schools. Operating from an institutional campus in Electronic City Phase 1, Bangalore, ISBR was founded to provide corporate-integrated management education.

Accredited by the **National Board of Accreditation (NBA)** and approved by the **All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)**, ISBR operates directly within India’s premier information technology and engineering corridor. Surrounded by multinational tech campuses including Infosys, Wipro, Siemens, Dell, and Hewlett Packard, ISBR leverages its location to foster continuous executive guest lectures, live corporate projects, and campus placement drives.

For students seeking an accredited, high-return business school inside Electronic City Bangalore with fees under ₹10.50 Lakhs, ISBR represents an established, dependable choice.

## How Does Normalization Work Across Slot 1, Slot 2, and Slot 3?

**CAT normalization uses an equipercentile equating method that compares the mean and standard deviation of candidate scores in each slot with the master mean across all three test sessions.** When a specific slot features a tougher DILR or QA section, candidates in that slot receive an upward score adjustment to ensure fair comparison.

```
       Candidate Raw Score (Slot 1, 2, or 3)
                         ↓
  Equipercentile Equating & Standard Deviation Alignment
                         ↓
              Normalized Scaled Score
                         ↓
           Final Official CAT Percentile
```

### Slot-Wise Expected Adjustments

1. **Slot 1 (Morning: 8:30 AM – 10:30 AM)**: Historically features high baseline accuracy. Minor score variations typically align closely with master benchmarks.
2. **Slot 2 (Afternoon: 12:30 PM – 2:30 PM)**: Often presents tricky DILR sets or lengthy VARC passages, yielding a positive scaling adjustment of +1.5% to +2.5% marks.
3. **Slot 3 (Evening: 4:30 PM – 6:30 PM)**: Frequent surprises in Quant algebra or arithmetic combinations typically warrant an upward calibration of +2.0% to +3.5% marks.

## Key Mistakes to Avoid When Estimating Your CAT Percentile

1. **Ignoring TITA Zero-Negative Marking**: Remember that non-MCQ / TITA questions carry **0 negative marks**. Do not deduct penalties for unattempted or incorrect TITA responses.
2. **Confusing Raw Score with Final Scaled Score**: Your final percentile is determined by your normalized scaled score, not your raw marks out of 198.
3. **Overlooking Sectional Cutoffs**: Having a 99 percentile overall will not yield Old IIM calls if your Quant or DILR sectional percentile dips below institutional minimums (typically 75–80 percentile).
4. **Delaying Non-IIM Application Deadlines**: Many premier business schools (like MDI Gurgaon, SPJIMR Mumbai, and FMS Delhi) close their application windows before or right around the announcement of official CAT results. The conducting IIM typically releases the candidate response sheet and provisional answer key within 4 to 6 days following the exam (first week of December).

### What happens if an official CAT question is dropped?
If a question contains errors or ambiguity, the conducting IIM drops the question and rescales the remaining test questions proportionally for all candidates in that shift.

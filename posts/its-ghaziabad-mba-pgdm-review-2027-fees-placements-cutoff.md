## 1. Institutional Background: Three Decades of Management Pedagogy in Mohan Nagar

Established in 1995, I.T.S - Mohan Nagar, Ghaziabad, represents a cornerstone institution within the I.T.S Education Group. Situated directly on the Grand Trunk (GT) Road in Mohan Nagar, Ghaziabad, the campus boasts seamless connectivity to Delhi via the Mohan Nagar and Dilshad Garden metro corridors, as well as easy access to the industrial belts of Sahibabad, Meerut Road, and Patparganj.

Holding the **NAAC 'A+' Grade** accreditation and **NBA** recognition, ITS Mohan Nagar has established a reputation for disciplined academic delivery, structured corporate interface, and strong regional placement support. Its management alumni network of over 20,000 professionals spans banking institutions, retail chains, FMCG distribution networks, and digital enterprises across North India.

For candidates seeking an affordable management qualification in Delhi-NCR with strong return on investment, ITS Ghaziabad offers a proven educational track record.

The Symbiosis National Aptitude Test (SNAP) is a popular national-level MBA entrance exam conducted by Symbiosis International (Deemed) University (SIU). SNAP is the gateway to 16 elite Symbiosis institutes, including the highly ranked [SIBM Pune](/colleges/sibm-pune), [SCMHRD Pune](/colleges/scmhrd-pune), [SIBM Bangalore](/colleges/sibm-bangalore), and SIIB Pune.

SNAP is famous for being a speed-based, student-friendly exam. It has a shorter duration compared to CAT or XAT (just 60 minutes) and allows candidates multiple attempts. If you are targeting any of the prestigious Symbiosis programs for the 2027-29 academic batch, you need to track the **SNAP 2026 application form open date** closely.

## SNAP 2026 Application Fee Structure

The overall registration cost includes the exam fee per attempt plus separate fees for each Symbiosis program you wish to apply to:

* **SNAP 2026 Registration Fee:** ₹2,250 per attempt (Expected)
* **Symbiosis Institute Program Fee:** ₹1,000 per program (e.g., MBA at [SIBM Pune](/colleges/sibm-pune), MBA at SCMHRD)
* **Mode of Payment:** Online via net banking, credit cards, debit cards, or UPI.

## Key SNAP 2026 Guidelines: Three Attempts Option

Before filling the application, understand the registration guidelines concerning multiple attempts:

1. **Up to Three Attempts:** You can appear for the SNAP exam up to **three times** during the December test cycle.
2. **Best Score Counts:** If you appear for the test multiple times, SIU will select your **highest score** among all your attempts for the final percentile calculation and shortlisting.
3. **Fees are Cumulative:** You must pay the ₹2,250 registration fee for *each* attempt you register for. For example, registering for all three attempts will cost ₹6,750.
4. **No Sectional Cutoffs:** Unlike CAT, SNAP does not have sectional cutoffs. Your overall score determines your eligibility for shortlists.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Apply for SNAP 2026

Once the **SNAP 2026 application form open date** arrives, register online through the official portal: **[snaptest.org](https://www.snaptest.org)**.

1. **Online Registration:** Visit the homepage and click on "Apply Online". Register by filling in your basic personal information (name, date of birth, mobile number, email).
2. **Fill Exam & Institute Options:** Choose the number of SNAP test attempts (one, two, or three). Under the same form, select the specific Symbiosis colleges and MBA programs you wish to apply for.
3. **Upload Photograph:** Upload a scanned copy of your recent passport-size color photograph (JPEG format, size between 10 KB and 100 KB).
4. **Pay Online:** Pay the cumulative fee online (Total SNAP attempts fee + Selected institute programs fee). Download and print the payment receipt and application confirmation page.

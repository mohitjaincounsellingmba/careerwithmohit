# PG in Global Finance & AI Imarticus (ISFB) Review 2026: Fees, Placements, Curriculum & IIM Vizag Alumni Status

India is rapidly evolving into the **world’s fintech operating system**. With public digital infrastructure like **UPI, Aadhaar, and the India Stack** powering over **46% of global real-time digital transactions**, the financial sector is experiencing a massive transformation. However, traditional MBA programs in finance often remain confined to theoretical accounting, spreadsheet modeling, and legacy banking systems—leaving a significant gap for professionals who can build, scale, and lead AI-native financial solutions.

To bridge the divide between classroom theory and boardroom reality, **Imarticus School of Finance and Business (ISFB)** has introduced India’s first **PG in Global Finance & AI**—an **11-month, full-time, on-campus, MBA-equivalent program** located in **Powai, Mumbai (India’s financial hub)**.

In this comprehensive 2026 guide, we explore everything you need to know about the **PG in Global Finance & AI by Imarticus Learning**, including its curriculum, **IIM Visakhapatnam Executive Alumni status**, fee structure, placement outcomes, and how it compares to a traditional finance MBA.

## 2. Why Choose Imarticus ISFB PG in Global Finance & AI? (The 4 Core Pillars)

Unlike conventional finance degrees that prioritize rote learning and end-of-semester written exams, ISFB is built around **proof of work, applied AI, and real-world execution**. Here are the four foundational pillars that make this program unique:

### Pillar 1: Build 5 Full-Stack Fintech Products End-to-End
Students do not just analyze existing financial products—they **design, build, and deploy** five production-grade fintech platforms from scratch:
*   **Product 1A & 1B: Neobanks (Retail & MSME):** Build next-generation digital banking experiences spanning payments, credit, savings, and financial intelligence (co-created with ecosystem leaders like **Paytm**).
*   **Product 2: Wealth Tech Platform:** Create automated investing, algorithmic wealth management, and robo-advisory engines.
*   **Product 3: Fintech SaaS:** Design infrastructure-level SaaS tools that banks and NBFCs run on.
*   **Product 4: Trade & Supply Chain Finance:** Engineer cross-border finance, trade compliance, and credit workflows.

By graduation, your resume is backed by a **recruiter-facing digital portfolio** complete with walkthroughs, decision logic, and stress-tested financial models.

### Pillar 2: Executive Alumni Status from IIM Visakhapatnam
In partnership with **IIM Visakhapatnam**, the program integrates academic rigor with strategic leadership in an AI-driven world:
*   **125+ Hours of IIM-Led Learning:** Master strategic decision-making under uncertainty, AI-enabled governance, and institutional leadership.
*   **3-Day Campus Immersion at IIM Vizag:** Experience live case rooms, capital budgeting simulations, and decision-making labs directly on the IIM campus.
*   **Official IIM Executive Alumni Recognition:** Receive an official certification, Alumni ID card, institute email ID, and lifetime access to the **IIM Visakhapatnam Executive Alumni Network**.

### Pillar 3: ₹25 Crore Venture Fund for Student Founders
For aspirants who want to build rather than just join, ISFB collaborates with **BLinC Invest VC** to provide access to a **₹25 Crore Finance Venture Fund**—the largest fund linked to a postgraduate finance program in India.
*   Student teams pitch their fintech ideas to a real **Venture Capital Investment Committee**.
*   Select startups receive **equity-based seed funding** and ongoing founder coaching.
*   Even non-founders walk away with **investor-grade analytical skills**, understanding exactly how capital evaluates risk, scalability, and unit economics.

### Pillar 4: Mentorship from 25+ CXOs, Product Heads & Venture Capitalists
Instead of purely academic lectures, your work is continuously reviewed by **industry practitioners** across Nomura, SP Jain, Accion, Citi, and leading fintech unicorns. Daily feedback loops train you to defend your decisions under real-world accountability.

## 5. Eligibility Criteria & Admission Process

The program is selective and designed for candidates who have a strong foundation and a clear drive to lead in modern finance.

### Eligibility Criteria
*   **Academic Background:** Bachelor’s degree in **Finance, Commerce, Economics, Business, Management, Engineering, Technology**, or a closely related quantitative field.
*   **Work Experience:** Minimum **1+ years of full-time work experience** in Finance, Banking, Analytics, Consulting, IT, or related sectors. (Highly suitable for early-career operators and career switchers).

### Step-by-Step Application Journey
1.  **Online Application Form:** Submit your academic, professional, and personal background details on the [official ISFB application portal](https://imarticus.org/school-of-finance-and-business/pg/).
2.  **Statement of Purpose (SOP):** Articulate why an AI-first finance career aligns with your long-term goals.
3.  **Document Verification:** Upload academic transcripts, experience letters, and identity proof.
4.  **In-Person Interaction (IPI):** Shortlisted applicants undergo a structured interview with admissions directors and faculty to evaluate problem-solving mindset and culture fit.
5.  **Admission Offer & Onboarding:** Selected candidates receive an offer letter to secure their seat for the upcoming cohort in **Powai, Mumbai**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Application Deadline Notice:** The **Round 2 Application Deadline is 30th July 2026**. Because seats for the on-campus Powai cohort and IIM Visakhapatnam immersion are limited, early applications are strongly advised.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the PG in Global Finance & AI at Imarticus (ISFB)?
The PG in Global Finance & AI is an 11-month, full-time, on-campus, MBA-equivalent postgraduate program offered by the Imarticus School of Finance and Business (ISFB) at its Powai, Mumbai campus. It combines fintech product building, AI-first financial decision making, IIM Visakhapatnam leadership certification, and access to a ₹25 Cr venture fund.

### What is the fee structure for the Imarticus PG in Global Finance & AI?
The total tuition fee for the PG in Global Finance & AI is ₹10,50,000 (excluding taxes). Scholarships and flexible payment plans/financing options are available for meritorious candidates.

### Do students get IIM Visakhapatnam Executive Alumni Status?
Yes, students undergo 125+ hours of structured IIM-led learning and a 3-day campus immersion at IIM Visakhapatnam. Upon successful completion, candidates earn Executive Certification in Leadership with AI and official Executive Alumni Status from IIM Visakhapatnam.

### What is the average and highest package offered after the ISFB PG program?
Graduates can target high-impact roles such as Fintech Product Manager, Fintech Strategy Consultant, and AI & Data Analytics Manager with CTCs ranging between ₹20 LPA and ₹65 LPA across top banks, NBFCs, fintech giants, and consulting firms.

### Who is eligible to apply for the PG in Global Finance & AI?
Applicants need a Bachelor's degree in Finance, Commerce, Economics, Business, Engineering, Technology, or a related field, along with 1+ years of work experience in Finance, Banking, Analytics, Consulting, Tech, or related domains.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the difference between Great Lakes 1-Year PGPM and 2-Year PGDM in Chennai?
The **1-Year PGPM** is an intensive fast-track program requiring a minimum of 24 months of full-time work experience (accepts CAT, XAT, CMAT, GMAT). The **2-Year PGDM** is designed for fresh graduates and candidates with 0–24 months of work experience.

### Does LIBA Chennai accept MAT or CMAT?
No, LIBA Chennai strictly admits students on the basis of **CAT and XAT** entrance scores, followed by GD/PI and written assessment.

### What is the TANCET cutoff for top MBA colleges in Chennai?
For government-aided and university departments affiliated with Anna University, safe TANCET scores range between **85 and 95+ percentile**.

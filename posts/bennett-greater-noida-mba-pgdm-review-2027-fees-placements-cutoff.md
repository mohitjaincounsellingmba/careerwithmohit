## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is Bennett University private or government?
Bennett University is a private, multidisciplinary university established under the Uttar Pradesh State Private Universities Act by the Times Group (Bennett, Coleman & Co. Ltd.) and approved by UGC and AICTE.

### 2. Is on-campus hostel stay mandatory for MBA students at Bennett?
Yes, Bennett University is primarily a residential university, and students are strongly encouraged to stay in on-campus air-conditioned hostels to participate fully in evening corporate workshops and group projects.

### 3. What are the summer internship opportunities like?
Students undergo a mandatory 8-week corporate summer internship between semesters 2 and 3. Average stipends range between ₹20,000 and ₹35,000 per month, with top media and financial firms offering stipends reaching ₹50,000 per month.

### 4. Can I get direct admission at Bennett University without CAT?
Yes. Bennett accepts scores from MAT, CMAT, NMAT, CUET-PG, or its own entrance test (BMAT). Candidates with strong graduation records (60%+) can apply directly for interview evaluation.

### 5. How does Bennett compare to older business schools in Greater Noida like BIMTECH?
BIMTECH is an older, standalone PGDM institution with higher cutoffs (80+ CAT) and slightly higher median packages (₹10.5 LPA). Bennett is a comprehensive, multidisciplinary university with broader infrastructure, modern corporate media ties, and lower entry cutoffs (65–70 CAT).

## 1. Institutional Background: The Times Group Pedagogy in Greater Noida

Bennett University, established in 2016 by the Times of India Group (Bennett, Coleman & Co. Ltd.) under the UP State Private Universities Act, was founded with a clear objective: bridging the divide between academic theory and real-time corporate business execution.

Situated on a 68-acre modern campus in Tech Zone II, Greater Noida (Delhi NCR), Bennett University benefits from the extensive media, corporate, and venture capital reach of the Times Group. The **School of Management** incorporates regular executive interactions with CXOs from Times Internet, economic editors from The Economic Times, and founders from leading Indian tech startups.

For candidates targeting modern careers in digital marketing, media management, financial technology, business analytics, and consumer product sales, Bennett University combines modern infrastructure with direct access to corporate Delhi NCR.

## 2. Program Architecture & Distinct MBA Specialization Verticals

The full-time Master of Business Administration (MBA) at Bennett University is an intensive 24-month program spread across six terms:

### A. Marketing & Digital Business
- **Key Focus**: Digital Transformation Marketing, Performance Advertising, Brand Strategy, Consumer Insights, and Omni-Channel Retailing.
- **Career Pathways**: Digital Marketing Lead, Brand Manager, Product Growth Associate, and Enterprise Account Executive.

### B. Banking, Financial Services & FinTech
- **Key Focus**: Corporate Valuation, Commercial Credit Risk, Equity Research, FinTech Ecosystems, and Wealth Management.
- **Career Pathways**: Financial Analyst, Credit Underwriter, Wealth Relationship Manager, and Investment Operations Associate.

### C. Business Analytics & Artificial Intelligence
- **Key Focus**: Python Data Intelligence, PowerBI Dashboarding, Advanced SQL, Predictive Modeling, and Machine Learning for Business Decisions.
- **Career Pathways**: Business Intelligence Analyst, Data Consultant, and Analytics Strategist.

### D. Human Resource Management & People Analytics
- **Key Focus**: Strategic Talent Acquisition, Organizational Development, HR Metrics & Analytics, and Industrial Labor Legislation.
- **Career Pathways**: HR Business Partner (HRBP), Talent Acquisition Lead, and People Analytics Associate.

### E. Media Management & Advertising
- **Key Focus**: Content Economics, Entertainment Marketing, Media Buying & Planning, Public Relations, and Digital Audience Analytics.
- **Career Pathways**: Media Strategist, Account Director, Content Operations Lead, and Brand Communications Manager.

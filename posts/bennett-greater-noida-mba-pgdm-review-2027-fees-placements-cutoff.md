## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the total fee structure for the MBA program at Bennett University Greater Noida?
The total 2-year tuition and academic fee for the flagship MBA program at Bennett University is approximately ₹11.95 Lakhs to ₹12.45 Lakhs, payable across 4 academic semesters. On-campus residential hostel amenities range between ₹1.40 and ₹1.75 Lakhs per annum.

### What is the average and highest placement package at Bennett University MBA?
During recent recruitment seasons, Bennett University recorded an average domestic CTC of ₹7.50 to ₹9.00 LPA for MBA graduates, with top quartile students securing ₹12.00 to ₹14.50 LPA and peak domestic packages touching ₹20.50 to ₹22.00 LPA.

### What is the Times Group advantage at Bennett University?
As a Times of India Group initiative, Bennett University provides direct access to media and business leaders, live case studies with Times Internet and Economic Times, executive mentorship, and CXO hiring networks across digital media, FMCG, and BFSI.

### Which entrance exams are accepted for Bennett University MBA 2027 admission?
Bennett University accepts scores from CAT, XAT, NMAT, MAT, CMAT, GMAT, and CUET PG, alongside its proprietary Bennett Management Aptitude Test, followed by a personal interview (PI) and profile evaluation.

### Does Bennett University offer merit scholarships for MBA students?
Yes, Bennett University offers merit-based tuition fee scholarships ranging from 20% to 50% for high scorers in CAT (75%+ percentile), NMAT (200+ score), or MAT (85%+ percentile).

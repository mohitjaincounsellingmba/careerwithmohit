## 🏛️ University Overview

Tufts University offers a unique combination of a liberal arts college feel with the extensive resources of a major research university. Situated just outside of Boston, Tufts is world-renowned for its Fletcher School of Law and Diplomacy and its highly collaborative engineering school.

| **Key Entrance Exams** | SAT/ACT (UG), GMAT/GRE (PG) |
| **Top Programs Offered** | International Relations (Fletcher), Engineering, Medicine, Liberal Arts & Sciences |

## 💰 Fees Breakdown (Converted to INR)

The estimated annual budget for attending Tufts University in the 2026 academic year:

- **Estimated Tuition Fees:** **₹69.7 Lakhs ($84,000+) per year**
- **Living Expenses (Annual):** Approx. ₹10 Lakhs - ₹15 Lakhs (depending on lifestyle and accommodation)
- **Books & Health Insurance:** Approx. ₹1.5 Lakhs - ₹2.5 Lakhs
- **Funding & Scholarships:** Merit-based international scholarships and graduate assistantships (GAs) are available to qualified Indian applicants to help reduce the cost.

*Note: Conversion rates are subject to change. The above estimates are designed for 2026 budget planning.*

## 🚀 Placements & Career Outcomes

Graduates from Tufts University have access to a robust job market, thanks to the university's strong industry connections and dedicated career center:

- **Average Starting Package:** **₹1 Crore+ ($120,000+)** (annual base salary)
- **STEM OPT Extension:** Most science, engineering, and business analytics courses qualify for the 3-year STEM OPT work permit in the USA.
- **Top Recruiters:** Amazon, Google, Deloitte, PwC, Microsoft, and various local research and corporate leaders.

## 🎯 Admission Requirements for Indian Students 2026

To secure an admit at Tufts University, applicants should focus on these core criteria:

1. **Very** competitive admissions. Holistic profile evaluation is key.
2. **Requires** strong recommendation letters and detailed personal essays.
3. **Demonstrate** a passion for collaborative research or global civic engagement.
4. **English** proficiency tests (IELTS 7.0+ / TOEFL 100+) are highly critical.

### Application Checklist:
- Academic transcripts (Class 10, 12, and Bachelor's degree if applicable)
- English Proficiency Test Scores (TOEFL / IELTS / Duolingo)
- Standardized Test Scores (SAT/ACT)
- Statement of Purpose (SOP) & Letters of Recommendation (LORs)
- Financial Proofs for I-20 and Visa processing

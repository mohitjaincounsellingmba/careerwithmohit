## 2. Detailed Fee Structure & Living Expenses (2027–2029 Batch)

Transparency in educational expenditure is essential for calculating personal return on investment. FIIB breaks down its fee schedule into clear components:

### Official PGDM Fee Schedule (Total: ₹12,85,000)
*   **Tuition Fees:** ₹9,38,350
*   **Academic Resources, Digital Labs & Case Packs:** ₹3,46,650
*   **Refundable Security Deposit:** ₹20,000 (returned upon completion of the course)
*   **Total Program Investment:** **₹12,85,000 (₹12.85 Lakhs)**
*   **Payment Terms:** Paid across 4 semester-wise installments.
*   *Note: As per institutional guidelines, students are expected to have their own personal laptops for digital classroom assignments.*

### What Is Included in the Academic Resources Fee?
*   Full access to Bloomberg terminals, SPSS statistical packages, and Tableau analytical software.
*   Harvard Business Publishing cases and digital library access through DELNET, EBSCO, and CMIE.
*   Global guest lectures, leadership conclaves, and industry certification bootcamps.

### Hostel & Accommodation Charges (South Delhi)
FIIB has partnerships with verified, secure student hostels and PGs in nearby residential colonies (Vasant Vihar, Munirka, and Ber Sarai):
*   **Twin-Sharing AC Rooms:** Approximately ₹11,000 – ₹13,500 per month (includes meals, Wi-Fi, laundry, and security).
*   **Non-AC Options:** Starting from ₹9,000 per month.
*   **Location Edge:** Being adjacent to Vasant Kunj and South Campus, students have a rich selection of student-friendly flats, libraries, and transport.

### Scholarships & Financial Aid

*   **Diversity & Special Category Grants:** Concessions for women candidates, defense background wards, and regional diversity representation.

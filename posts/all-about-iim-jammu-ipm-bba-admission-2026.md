IIM Jammu, located in the scenic city of temples, is a prominent member of the third-generation IIMs offering the 5-Year Integrated Programme in Management (IPM). In 2026, it offers a blend of top-tier academic rigor and unique cultural exposure.

## 🏛️ Why Choose IIM Jammu IPM in 2026?
IIM Jammu's IPM program focuses on creating globally competitive management professionals with a strong ethical foundation.
- **Growing Infrastructure:** A modern campus with cutting-edge academic and residential facilities.
- **Global Reach:** Increasing focus on international collaborations and exchange opportunities.
- **IIM Brand Advantage:** Direct entry into the IIM ecosystem right after school.

## 💰 Latest Fee Structure (2026)
The fee structure for the 5-year IPM program is as follows:
- **First 3 Years:** Approximately ₹4,50,000 – ₹5,00,000 per year.
- **Final 2 Years (MBA Phase):** At par with the prevailing PGP (MBA) fees, approximately ₹8,50,000 – ₹9,50,000 per year.
- **Total Investment:** Approx. ₹34 – 38 Lakhs (inclusive of residential charges).

## 🚀 Placement Process & Statistics 2026
IPM students are eligible for the common final placement process with the regular PGP (MBA) students.
- **Average Package:** ₹16.4 LPA
- **Highest Package:** ₹30 LPA
- **Top Recruiters:** Amazon, Deloitte, ICICI Bank, Infosys, Microsoft, Cognizant.
- **Placement Process:** Managed by the common placement cell, involving summer internships (after Year 4) and final recruitment (Year 5).

## 📝 Admission Process 2026 (JIPMAT)
Admission to IIM Jammu's IPM is through the **JIPMAT (Joint Integrated Programme in Management Admission Test)**.
- **Exam Pattern:** Quantitative Aptitude, Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning, and Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension.
- **Selection Criteria:** Based primarily on the JIPMAT score merit list.
- **Conducting Body:** National Testing Agency (NTA).

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Q1. Is there an exit option at IIM Jammu IPM?**
Yes, students have the option to exit after 3 years with a Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) degree.

**Q2. Does IIM Jammu accept SAT scores for IPM?**
Generally, IIM Jammu focuses on JIPMAT for its Indian intake. Candidates should check the latest brochure for any international/NRI specific criteria involving SAT.

**Q3. What is the intake capacity for IPM 2026?**
The intake is approximately 120 seats for the 2026 batch.

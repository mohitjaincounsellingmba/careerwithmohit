Every MBA aspirant preparing for or analyzing the **Common Admission Test (CAT 2026)** asks one fundamental question: *"How many marks do I need to score to get a 99, 95, or 90 percentile?"*

Understanding the distinction between **CAT Raw Score**, **Scaled Score**, and **Final Percentile** is critical for setting your exam day strategy, managing negative marking, and shortlisting MBA colleges.

In this comprehensive guide, **Mohit Jain** breaks down the complete **CAT 2026 Score vs Percentile** trajectory, sectional benchmarks across **VARC, DILR, and QA**, slot normalization mechanics, and what qualifies as a "good score" for top tier B-schools.

## 📊 Overview: CAT 2026 Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

The CAT exam comprises 66 questions with a total duration of 120 minutes (40 minutes per section):

| Section | Number of Questions | MCQs (with -1 negative) | Non-MCQs / TITA (No negative) | Total Marks | Sectional Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VARC** (Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension) | 24 | 16–18 | 6–8 | 72 Marks | 40 Minutes |
| **DILR** (Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning) | 20 | 14–16 | 4–6 | 60 Marks | 40 Minutes |

| **Total** | **66 Questions** | **~46-50** | **~16-20** | **198 Marks** | **120 Minutes** |

*   **Correct Answer:** +3 Marks
*   **Incorrect MCQ:** -1 Mark
*   **Incorrect Non-MCQ (TITA):** 0 Marks (No negative marking)
*   **Unattempted Question:** 0 Marks

## ⚙️ How CAT Normalisation Works: Raw vs Scaled Score

CAT is administered in three separate sessions (Slot 1, Slot 2, Slot 3) on the last Sunday of November. Because questions differ across slots, the difficulty level may slightly fluctuate.

IIMs use a standard statistical normalization procedure:
1.  **Mean and Standard Deviation Calculation:** Average marks scored across all three slots are computed.
2.  **Equi-percentile Scaling:** The performance distribution of candidates in one slot is mapped against the benchmark slot.
3.  **Scaled Score Generation:** If your slot was comparatively harder, your scaled score will be higher than your raw score. If your slot was easier, your scaled score might slightly adjust downwards.
4.  **Percentile Rank Formula:**
    Percentile Rank = ((Total Candidates - Candidate Rank) / Total Candidates) * 100

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What raw score is needed for a 99 percentile in CAT 2026?
Historically, an overall raw score of 76 to 82 marks out of 198 (approx 38-41% accuracy with around 26-28 correct questions) is sufficient to achieve a 99+ percentile in CAT.

### What is considered a 'good score' in CAT for top IIMs?
For old IIMs (IIM Ahmedabad, Bangalore, Calcutta, Lucknow, Kozhikode, Indore) and FMS Delhi, a good score is 99+ percentile for General category (76+ marks), and 95+ percentile for New/Baby IIMs (58-62 marks).

### How does CAT normalisation work across different slots?
IIMs use a mean and standard deviation-based scaling formula across Slot 1, Slot 2, and Slot 3 to equalize question paper difficulty differences before computing your final scaled score and percentile.

### What is the difference between CAT raw score, scaled score, and percentile?
Raw score is the marks calculated from correct (+3) and incorrect (-1) attempts. Scaled score is the normalized score across slots. Percentile indicates the percentage of total test-takers who scored equal to or less than your scaled score.

### Can I get admission in a top MBA college with a 70-80 percentile in CAT?
Yes, prominent private B-schools like BIMTECH Greater Noida, LBSIM Delhi, [FORE School of Management](/colleges/fore-school-delhi), GIM Goa, and Great Lakes offer strong PGDM programs for 75-85 percentile scorers.

### 1. IIM Calcutta (The Quant & Finance Capital)
- **The "C" in IIM ABC**: Triple-crown accredited (AACSB, AMBA, EQUIS). Famous for having India's strongest quantitative finance and consulting culture.
- **Top Recruiters**: McKinsey, Boston Consulting Group, Bain, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, J.P. Morgan, Avendus Capital, HUL, ITC.

### 2. Vinod Gupta School of Management (VGSoM IIT Kharagpur)
- **Engineering-Management Synergy**: India's first business school situated within an IIT ecosystem. Excels in Operations, Supply Chain, and Quantitative FinTech with outstanding fee ROI.

### 3. IISWBM Kolkata (India's First B-School)
- **Historic Legacy**: Founded in 1953 by Dr. B.C. Roy and Pt. Jawaharlal Nehru. Affiliated with Calcutta University, offering rock-bottom tuition fees, stable PSU/Banking hiring, and central Kolkata connectivity.

### 4. Praxis Business School (Data & Tech Analytics)
- **Pioneering Digital Curriculum**: Ranked consistently among India's top programs for Data Science and Business Analytics, producing corporate data analysts and business intelligence consultants.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the CAT cutoff for IIM Calcutta?
For general category candidates, the qualifying percentile is 85, but the actual interview call threshold is typically **99.4+ percentile** with high weightage on 10th and 12th board percentages.

### How are placements at IMI Kolkata?
IMI Kolkata (RP-Sanjiv Goenka Group) records 100% placements with an average CTC of **₹10.60 LPA**, with top recruiters including Deloitte, PwC, ICICI Bank, KPMG, and Tata Play.

### What is the JEMAT exam in West Bengal?
JEMAT (Joint Entrance Management Aptitude Test) is the state-level entrance exam conducted by MAKAUT for admission into MBA/MHA programs across West Bengal universities.

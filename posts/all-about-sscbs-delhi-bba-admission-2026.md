Shaheed Sukhdev College of Business Studies (SSCBS), a premier institution under the University of Delhi, is widely regarded as the best college for undergraduate management studies in India. Known for its rigorous academic environment and exceptional placements, it is the top choice for BBA (FIA) and BMS aspirants in 2026.

## 🏛️ Why Choose SSCBS Delhi in 2026?
The primary reason students flock to SSCBS is its **unbeatable Return on Investment (ROI)**. With fees lower than most private schools, students secure placements that rival top-tier MBA colleges.

- **Global Recognition:** Alumni are placed in top consulting firms like McKinsey, BCG, and Bain.
- **Specialized Curriculum:** Offers Bachelor of Management Studies (BMS) and BBA in Financial Investment Analysis (FIA).
- **Location Advantage:** Being in Delhi, it offers immense industry exposure and networking opportunities.

## 💰 Latest Fee Structure (2026)
SSCBS remains highly affordable. For the 2026 session, the approximate fee structure is:
- **BMS/BBA(FIA):** ₹25,000 – ₹35,000 per year (depending on category and minor updates).
- **Hostel Fees:** ₹1.2 Lakhs – ₹1.5 Lakhs per year.

## 🚀 Placement Process & Statistics 2026
The placement cell at SSCBS, known as 'CBS Enactus' and the corporate relation team, works tirelessly to bring the best brands on campus.
- **Average Package:** ₹11.5 LPA
- **Highest Package:** ₹44.4 LPA (Historical Peak)
- **Top Recruiters:** BCG, McKinsey & Co., Bain Capability Network, DE Shaw, Accenture.
- **Placement Process:** Includes resume shortlisting, Aptitude Tests, Case Study Rounds, and Personal Interviews.

## 📝 Admission Process 2026 (CUET)
Admission to SSCBS is strictly through the **Common University Entrance Test (CUET UG 2026)**.
- **Subject Mapping:** You MUST appear in Mathematics/Applied Mathematics + Any two subjects (one must be a language) + General Test.
- **Expected Cutoff:** A score of 720+ out of 800 (for general category) is typically required to secure a seat.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Q1. Is Maths compulsory for BBA in SSCBS?**
Yes, Mathematics is a mandatory subject in Class 12 and for the CUET exam to be eligible for BMS/BBA(FIA) at SSCBS.

**Q2. Can I get direct admission in SSCBS?**
No, there is no management quota or direct admission. All seats are filled via CUET merit.

**Q3. What is the difference between BMS and BBA(FIA)?**
BMS is a general management course covering all aspects of business, while BBA(FIA) is highly specialized towards finance and investment banking.

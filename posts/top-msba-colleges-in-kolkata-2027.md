## 🏛️ In-Depth Breakdown: Best Business Analytics Institutions in Kolkata

### 1. IIM Calcutta + ISI Kolkata + IIT Kharagpur – Joint PGDBA
- **Flagship Offering**: Post Graduate Diploma in Business Analytics (2-Year Full-Time Residential)
- **Total Tuition Fee**: ₹25.00 Lakhs
- **Placement Performance**: Average CTC ₹31.05 LPA | Highest Domestic CTC ₹89.00 LPA | Median CTC ₹30.00 LPA
- **Accepted Exams**: National PGDBA Entrance Exam (Conducted jointly by IIM-C, ISI, and IIT-KGP)
- **The Tri-Institute Synergy**:
  - **Semester 1 (ISI Kolkata)**: Statistical Foundations, Machine Learning Theory, Probability Models, Mathematical Optimization.
  - **Semester 2 (IIT Kharagpur)**: Big Data Architecture, Cloud Computing, Neural Networks, Database Systems.
  - **Semester 3 (IIM Calcutta)**: Business Strategy, Financial Analytics, Marketing Intelligence, Supply Chain Analytics.
  - **Semester 4**: 6-Month Mandatory Industry Internship (Paid Corporate Placement).
- **Mohit’s Expert Verdict**: 
  - *Who Should Apply*: Candidates with exceptional quantitative ability (B.Tech, B.Sc/M.Sc Mathematics, Statistics, Physics, Economics) seeking global leadership roles in AI, quantitative hedge funds, and top-tier management consultancies.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Is the IIM Calcutta-ISI-IIT Kharagpur PGDBA better than IIM MBA?
Yes, for pure data science, quantitative finance, and AI strategy, PGDBA is considered the gold standard in Asia, surpassing general MBA packages with its ₹31.05 LPA average domestic salary.

### 2. What background is required for PGDBA in Kolkata?
Applicants must have a graduate or postgraduate degree with Mathematics at the 10+2 level or in graduation (B.Tech, B.E., B.Sc. Statistics/Maths, M.Sc., MCA, etc.) with at least 60% marks.

### 3. Can I get direct admission in Kolkata Business Analytics programs?
Institutions like Globsyn, Praxis, and Sister Nivedita University offer profile-based evaluation and institutional aptitude test routes. The joint PGDBA and IMI Kolkata admit strictly through competitive entrance test merit lists.

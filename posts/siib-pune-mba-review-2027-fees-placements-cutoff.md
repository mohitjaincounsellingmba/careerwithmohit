## 1. Institutional Heritage & Distinct Identity: Why SIIB Pune Matters

Founded in 1992, Symbiosis Institute of International Business (SIIB Pune) was conceptualized right at the dawn of India’s economic liberalization. While mainstream Indian business schools were focused on traditional domestic commercial practices, SIIB was built with an international vision: preparing business leaders who understand foreign exchange risk management, cross-border mergers, global supply chain corridors, multilateral trade treaties, and geopolitical risk economics.

Today, operating from its sprawling, technology-enabled campus in Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park (Hinjawadi Phase-I, Pune), SIIB is one of India's most respected niche business schools. It operates three high-impact programs under Symbiosis International (Deemed University), NAAC 'A++' accredited:
1. **MBA in International Business (MBA - IB)**
2. **MBA in Agri-Business (MBA - AB)**
3. **MBA in Energy & Environment (MBA - E&E)**

Each program addresses distinct economic growth sectors: the global expansion of Indian multinationals, the modernization and corporatization of the agricultural value chain, and the rapid shift toward renewable energy and sustainability compliance.

## 2. Program Verticals & Curriculum Architecture

### A. MBA in International Business (MBA - IB)
The flagship program of the institute, MBA-IB provides rigorous, multi-disciplinary management training with a heavy global orientation. After establishing foundational managerial competence in the first semester, students specialize in one of four functional tracks:
- **Global Marketing**: International brand positioning, cross-border digital consumer behavior, global distribution architectures, and foreign market entry strategies.
- **International Finance**: Cross-border valuation, foreign exchange risk hedging, international trade finance, multi-currency treasury management, and global capital markets.
- **Global Supply Chain Management**: International maritime logistics, port operations, multimodal freight networks, customs documentation, EXIM compliance, and procurement engineering.
- **Human Resource Management**: Global talent mobility, expatriate compensation structures, cross-cultural workplace psychology, and international labor regulations.

### B. MBA in Agri-Business (MBA - AB)
India's agricultural and rural economy is undergoing unprecedented corporatization through agritech startups, organized food processing, cold-chain logistics, and commodity trading.
- **Key Modules**: Commodity Derivatives & Price Risk Management, Agri-Input Marketing (seeds, fertilizers, farm machinery), Food Supply Chain Management, Rural Banking & Microfinance, and Post-Harvest Technology Economics.
- **Industry Scope**: High placement density in food processing conglomerates (ITC, Nestle, Britannia), farm input leaders (UPL, Godrej Agrovet, Bayer CropScience), and commodity trading firms.

### C. MBA in Energy & Environment (MBA - E&E)
Pioneered by SIIB as one of the first dedicated sustainability management programs in South Asia, this curriculum prepares managers to lead the clean energy transition and ESG compliance.
- **Key Modules**: Carbon Markets & Climate Finance, Renewable Energy Project Finance (Solar, Wind, Green Hydrogen), ESG Reporting Frameworks (BRSR, GRI, SASB), Energy Auditing, Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA), and Circular Economy Business Models.
- **Industry Scope**: Enormous corporate demand across global management consulting sustainability wings (Deloitte, EY, PwC, McKinsey ESG), renewable energy developers (Adani Green, Tata Power, ReNew Power), and industrial conglomerates.

### B. Selection Process & Stage-2 Evaluation Breakdown

Shortlisted candidates undergo the GE-PIWAT evaluation on campus or via scheduled slots:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  SIIB PUNE FINAL SELECTION COMPOSITE WEIGHTAGE         │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  SNAP Score (Scaled down to 50%)                       │
│  Personal Interaction / PI (30%)                       │
│  Group Exercise / GE (10%)                             │
│  Writing Ability Test / WAT (10%)                      │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **SNAP Score (50%)**: Measures raw analytical speed, linguistic accuracy, and quantitative problem-solving.
2. **Personal Interaction (30%)**: Critical round evaluating candidate depth. For MBA-IB, panelists test global affairs, trade tariffs, currency dynamics, and career intent. For AB and E&E, technical curiosity and sector orientation are probed.
3. **Group Exercise (10%)**: Caselet-based business discussion assessing collaborative prioritization, consensus-building, and communication poise.
4. **Writing Ability Test (10%)**: Evaluates structured prose, clear arguments, and clarity of thought on contemporary socio-economic topics within a 15–20 minute window.

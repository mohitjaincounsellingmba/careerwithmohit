With the results declared, the admission window for premier central universities like Delhi University (DU), Banaras Hindu University (BHU), Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU), and Jamia Millia Islamia has officially opened. Candidates will need their normalized scores to register on individual university counselling portals.

Let’s review the key highlights of the CUET UG 2026 results, direct download links, scorecard checking process, and the crucial next steps for admissions.

## 🔗 Direct Links to Check CUET UG 2026 Scorecard
Due to heavy traffic, the primary server might slow down. Candidates can check their results on any of the official portals listed below:
*   **Official Link 1:** **[cuet.nta.nic.in](https://cuet.nta.nic.in)**
*   **Official Link 2:** **[exams.nta.ac.in/CUET-UG/](https://exams.nta.ac.in/CUET-UG/)**
*   **Official Link 3:** **[nta.ac.in](https://nta.ac.in)**

## 📝 Step-by-Step Guide to Download CUET UG 2026 Scorecard
Follow these steps to log in and download your scorecard:

1.  Visit the official NTA CUET UG website: **[cuet.nta.nic.in](https://cuet.nta.nic.in)**.
2.  Click on the link titled **"CUET UG 2026 Scorecard Download"** on the homepage.
3.  You will be redirected to the candidate login window.
4.  Enter your **Application Number** and **Date of Birth** (or Password).
5.  Input the security captcha pin displayed on the screen.
6.  Click on **"Submit"** or **"Login"**.
7.  Your CUET UG 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen.
8.  Download the scorecard in PDF format and print 2-3 copies for the counselling process.

## 📊 Details Mentioned on the CUET UG 2026 Scorecard
Candidates must verify the following details on their scorecards immediately after downloading:
*   **Candidate’s Name**
*   **Roll Number & Application Number**
*   **Category & Gender**
*   **Subject-wise Normalized Score (in numbers)**
*   **Subject-wise Percentile Score**
*   **Signature & Photograph of the candidate**

*Note: Since the exam was conducted across multiple shifts and days, NTA uses the **Equipercentile Method** to normalize scores. Admissions are based on the **normalized score**, not the raw marks.*

## 🚦 Next Steps: CSAS & University Admission Process
Now that the results are out, the actual admission process begins. Here is what you need to do:

### 1. Register on University Admission Portals
NTA does not organize a single centralized counselling process. You must apply separately on the portals of the universities you listed in your application:
*   **Delhi University (DU):** Register on the **CSAS (Common Seat Allocation System)** portal.
*   **IP University (GGSIPU):** Register on the IPU admissions page.
*   **BHU / JNU:** Apply via the respective university admission portals.

### 2. Preference Sheet Filling
During university registration, you will have to list your choices of college and course combinations in order of priority. Listing your options incorrectly is one of the biggest mistakes candidates make. Always prioritize higher-ranked colleges, even if you think your score is on the border.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I check my CUET UG 2026 result?
You can check your result on the official NTA portal (cuet.nta.nic.in) using your Application Number, Date of Birth, and the security captcha pin.

### What details are mentioned on the CUET scorecard?
The scorecard includes candidate details, subject-wise percentiles, normalized scores, category, and roll number.

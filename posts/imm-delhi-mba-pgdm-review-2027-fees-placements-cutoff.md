## 1. Institutional Background: Five Decades of Marketing Leadership in Qutab

The Institute of Marketing & Management (IMM Delhi), established in 1969 by visionary marketing educator Dr. Jagjit Singh, was among India’s earliest specialized business schools. Located in the institutional corridor of Qutab Institutional Area, South Delhi—sharing boundaries with prestigious bodies such as IIFT Delhi, ISI Delhi, and FORE School—IMM benefits from an intellectual academic atmosphere.

Approved by the **All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)**, IMM’s flagship Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) has trained generations of marketing executives, retail heads, and corporate brand leaders. Over its half-century existence, IMM’s alumni have risen to executive vice president and managing director roles across Fortune 500 enterprises, advertising conglomerates, and banking institutions.

For students seeking an accredited, high-return business school in prime South Delhi with fees under ₹9.0 Lakhs, IMM Delhi represents an established, dependable choice.

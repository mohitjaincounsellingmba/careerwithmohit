Running a career upgrade requires choosing the right management program. For working professionals in Bangalore, the Executive MBA / Executive PGDM offered by [Christ University, Bangalore](/colleges/christ-university-bangalore) represents a powerful gateway to higher senior leadership positions.

In this review, we break down everything you need to know: fees, admission cutoffs, placements, pros, cons, and our honest expert verdict.

---

## 🏆 Program Overview

- **Official Course Name:** Executive MBA (Part-Time / Weekend)
- **Approximate Fees:** ₹3.70 Lakhs (Total Fees)
- **Entrance Exams Accepted:** Christ Selection Process + Personal Interview
- **Course Duration:** 15 Months to 2 Years (Format varies by program)

---

## 📈 Eligibility & Admission Details

- **Academic Requirements:** Bachelor's degree with min 50% + minimum 2 years of work experience.
- **Selection Process:** Admission typically relies on professional profile screening, statement of purpose (SOP), executive experience reviews, and personal interviews. Elite IIM programs also mandate standard quantitative testing (GMAT/GRE).

---

## 💼 Placement Statistics & Career Impact

- **Estimated Placements:** Excellent brand value and networking, average CTC is ₹8-10 LPA.
- **Career Growth:** Executive MBA degrees focus on helping candidates secure senior lateral shifts (like VP, Product Manager, or Director roles) or move from technical paths into core management.

---

## 📝 Student Reviews: Pros & Cons

### Pros
- **Highlight:** Very affordable fee structure for an Executive program.
- **Highlight:** Excellent brand value and networking opportunities in Bangalore.
- **Highlight:** Flexible learning patterns and weekend options.

### Cons
- **Limitation:** Strict discipline and attendance rules.
- **Limitation:** Average placement salary is moderate.

---

## 🔍 Our Expert Verdict

The Executive MBA program at [Christ University, Bangalore](/colleges/christ-university-bangalore) is highly recommended for professionals based in Bangalore who want to scale their careers without disrupting their current geographic setup. 

If you are looking for top-tier consulting placements and have 5+ years of experience, full-time residential paths are stellar. However, if you are looking to continue your full-time job, their weekend/evening classes offer outstanding return on investment.

---

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the schedule of classes for the Christ Executive MBA?
Classes are held on weekends (Saturdays and Sundays) to allow professionals to maintain their work schedules.

### Is there a written exam required?
Admission is based on the candidate's past academic record, work experience profile, and a personal interview.

### Are there placements for this program?
Christ provides access to its centralized placement portal, though most weekend candidates leverage the degree for lateral shifts.

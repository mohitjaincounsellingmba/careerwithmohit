## CAT 2026 Exam Pattern & Syllabus Overview

Understanding the exam format helps you make the most of group discussions:

* **Section 1: Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension (VARC)** – 24 Questions (40 Mins)
* **Section 2: Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning (DILR)** – 20 Questions (40 Mins)
* **Section 3: Quantitative Aptitude (QA)** – 22 Questions (40 Mins)
* **Total:** 66 Questions | 120 Minutes | Marking: +3 for correct, -1 for incorrect MCQs (No negative marking for TITA).

## 🔍 Detailed Analysis of the Competitors

### 1. Classplus (classplusapp.com)
Classplus is one of the pioneers in the white-label tutoring app space in India. It is highly optimized for local tuition centers and test prep educators.
*   **Pros:** Good mobile app interface, strong notification engine, and integrated student database management (ERP).
*   **Cons:** Their web interface is relatively basic, and transaction fee payouts can take time. Additionally, because they host tens of thousands of apps on the same base architecture, custom styling is highly restricted.

### 2. Graphy (graphy.com)
Acquired and rebranded by Unacademy, Graphy targets modern creators, business coaches, and YouTubers who want to run cohort-based courses.
*   **Pros:** Modern web landing page builder, beautiful aesthetic designs, and support for interactive communities.
*   **Cons:** Their transaction revenue-share fee can be as high as 10% on their basic tiers, which eats into your course margins. The mobile app experience is secondary to their web platform.

### 3. CareerWithMohit Turnkey App Builder
Designed specifically for creators, educators, and YouTubers who want premium positioning, zero transaction commissions, and top-tier security.

*   **Cons:** We restrict monthly onboarding slots to maintain premium support quality for our partners.

## 🛡️ Anti-Piracy and Content Security: Who Protects Your Asset?

As an educator, your course content is your intellectual property. Piracy can kill your business overnight. 

*   **Classplus** relies on native android security to block screenshots and screen recordings in the mobile app. However, their web portal remains vulnerable to simple screen recorders.
*   **Graphy** provides standard security, but screen recording tools on desktops can often bypass their protection.
*   **CareerWithMohit** implements dynamic user-specific watermarking (displaying the student's email/IP address randomly across the screen) and encrypted stream playback. This deters students from sharing credentials or recording lectures.

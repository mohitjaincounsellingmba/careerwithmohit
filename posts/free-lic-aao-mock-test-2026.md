## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the pattern of the LIC AAO Prelims exam?
LIC AAO Prelims consists of 100 questions carrying 70 marks (English is qualifying only, 30 questions) with a 60-minute duration (20 minutes per section).

### Is the English section score counted in the LIC AAO Prelims ranking?
No, the English Language section is only qualifying in nature. Candidates must clear the minimum qualifying marks in English, but the ranking is based on Quant and Reasoning (total 70 marks).

### Is there negative marking in the LIC AAO exam?
No, there is no negative marking in the LIC AAO Prelims exam. Candidates can attempt all questions without fear of penalty.

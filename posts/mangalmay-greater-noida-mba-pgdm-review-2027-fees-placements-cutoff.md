## 4. Academic Rigor, Curriculum & Value-Added Modules

The core MBA program operates across four 6-month semesters, combining academic rigor with industry-endorsed practical skill workshops:

### Semester-Wise Learning Blueprint
* **Semester I (Foundational Core)**: Management Concepts & Applications, Managerial Economics, Financial Accounting for Managers, Business Statistics, Marketing Management, and Business Environment.
* **Semester II (Operational Competence)**: Managing Human Resources, Financial Management, Operations Research, Management Information Systems (MIS), and Business Law.
* **Summer Corporate Internship**: Mandatory 6 to 8-week corporate project between Semester II and III evaluated through viva voce and technical project reports.
* **Semesters III & IV (Functional Specialization Electives)**:
  - **Marketing Management**: Consumer Behaviour, Rural Marketing, Sales & Distribution Management, Digital Marketing, Retail Management, and Services Marketing.
  - **Financial Management**: Security Analysis & Portfolio Management, Financial Markets & Services, Corporate Tax Planning, Working Capital Management, and International Financial Management.
  - **Human Resource Management**: Talent Management, Industrial Relations & Labor Law, Performance Appraisal Systems, Organizational Change, and Compensation Management.
  - **International Business (IB)**: Export-Import Documentation, International Logistics, Foreign Exchange Management, and Global Business Strategy.
  - **Information Technology (IT)**: Database Management Systems, Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), E-Commerce Strategies, and Cyber Security Basics.

### Skill Enhancement Bootcamps & Experiential Learning
* **Advanced Excel & Financial Modeling**: Hands-on masterclasses covering VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, pivot reporting, dynamic cash flow modeling, and scenario sensitivity analysis.
* **Digital Marketing Masterclasses**: Practical training on Google Analytics 4, search engine optimization basics, content marketing funnels, and social media ad optimization.
* **Corporate Communication & Soft Skills**: Personality grooming, mock group discussions, business presentation etiquette, and simulated technical interview drills.
* **Live Industry Case Pedagogy**: Case-study methodology drawn from Harvard and European case clearinghouses, ensuring students analyze real-world corporate dilemmas rather than passive rote textbook theories.

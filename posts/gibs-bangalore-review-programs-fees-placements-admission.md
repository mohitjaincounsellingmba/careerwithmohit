## 2. Programs Offered at GIBS Bangalore

**[GIBS Business School](/colleges/gibs-bangalore)** provides industry-aligned management programs at both postgraduate and undergraduate levels.

### A. Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM)
The **PGDM** is GIBS's flagship 2-year full-time program, approved by AICTE and recognized as equivalent to an MBA by AIU.
*   **Duration:** 2 Years (4 Semesters / Trimesters)
*   **Dual Specializations Offered:**
    *   Marketing & Brand Management
    *   Financial Management & Investment Banking
    *   Human Resource Management (HRM)
    *   Business Analytics & Artificial Intelligence
    *   Operations & Supply Chain Management
    *   Digital Marketing & E-Commerce
*   **Value-Added Certifications Included:**
    *   **PGPIRE:** Post Graduate Program in Innovation, Research & Entrepreneurship
    *   **CPMP:** Certification in Personal Mastery Program (Finishing School)
    *   Business Analytics, SAP, and Digital Marketing credentials

### B. Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)
The **BBA** program is a 3-year full-time undergraduate degree affiliated with **Bangalore University**.
*   **Duration:** 3 Years (6 Semesters)
*   **Specializations:** Finance, Marketing, Human Resource Management, and Business Analytics.
*   **Value-Added Modules:** Includes Certification in Innovation, Research & Entrepreneurship (CIRE) and CPMP grooming modules to boost early-career corporate readiness.

## 7. Admission Process & Eligibility Criteria 2026

Admissions at GIBS Bangalore are profile-based and entrance-score inclusive.

### Eligibility Criteria
*   **PGDM:** Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university with a minimum of **50% aggregate marks** (45% for reserved categories). Valid score in CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, ATMA, GMAT, or state PGCET/KMAT.
*   **BBA:** Passed 10+2 / Higher Secondary examination from any recognized board (CBSE, ICSE, State Board) with at least **45% to 50% marks**.

### Step-by-Step Selection Workflow
1.  **Online Application:** Submit the application form through the official website or verified counseling portals.
2.  **Entrance Exam Score Submission:** Upload valid scorecard of national/state MBA entrance exams.
3.  **Statement of Purpose (SOP) & Video Interview:** Submit a brief video essay or SOP outlining career goals.
4.  **GD & Personal Interview (PI):** Participate in personal interaction with academic faculty and industry experts evaluating communication, analytical thinking, and leadership aptitude.
5.  **Final Admission Offer:** Merit list generated combining entrance exam scores, past academic performance, and PI score.

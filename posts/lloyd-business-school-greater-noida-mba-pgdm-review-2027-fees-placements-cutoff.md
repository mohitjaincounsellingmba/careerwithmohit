## 1. Institutional Background: Industry-Centric Management in Knowledge Park II

Established in 2008, Lloyd Business School is located in Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida—one of Northern India's largest educational hubs. Spanning a 10-acre campus with green spaces and contemporary infrastructure, the institution is situated along the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway and is accessible via the Aqua Line Metro corridor (Knowledge Park II Metro Station).

Approved by the **All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)**, Lloyd offers both university-affiliated MBA degrees (affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University, AKTU Lucknow) and autonomous Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) programs.

The institution has built corporate collaborations with industry bodies such as Safeducate, IBM, and various trade chambers, ensuring that students receive curriculum instruction aligned with commercial requirements.

For students evaluating management colleges in Greater Noida who seek industry-partnered programs with reasonable fee structures, Lloyd Business School provides a practical educational environment.

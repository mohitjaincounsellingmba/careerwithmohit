## 💰 Fees Breakdown (Converted to INR)

The estimated annual budget for attending University of Utah in the 2026 academic year:

- **Estimated Tuition Fees:** **₹25.7 Lakhs ($31,000+ tuition; total COA $50,000+) per year**
- **Living Expenses (Annual):** Approx. ₹10 Lakhs - ₹15 Lakhs (depending on lifestyle and accommodation)

Graduates from University of Utah have access to a robust job market, thanks to the university's strong industry connections and dedicated career center:

## 🎯 Admission Requirements for Indian Students 2026

To secure an admit at University of Utah, applicants should focus on these core criteria:

1. **Moderate** selectivity. Highly accessible for students with solid academic records.
2. **Requires** high school or university transcripts with WES evaluation if requested.
3. **Strong** portfolio required for specialized programs like Game Design.
4. **Standardized** tests (SAT/ACT) are optional but can help secure merit scholarships.

### Application Checklist:
- Academic transcripts (Class 10, 12, and Bachelor's degree if applicable)
- English Proficiency Test Scores (TOEFL / IELTS / Duolingo)
- Standardized Test Scores (SAT/ACT)
- Statement of Purpose (SOP) & Letters of Recommendation (LORs)
- Financial Proofs for I-20 and Visa processing

## How to Download CAT 2026 Admit Card Online: Step-by-Step Process

**To download your CAT 2026 admit card, navigate to iimcat.ac.in, select "Registered Candidate Login", input your unique CAT User ID and Password, and click on the "Admit Card" tab to download your PDF hall ticket.** Follow these verified steps to ensure error-free download:

1. **Access the Official Portal**: Open an updated desktop browser (Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox recommended) and go to the official CAT website: **[https://iimcat.ac.in](https://iimcat.ac.in)**.
2. **Click on Registered Candidate Login**: Locate the "Registered Candidate Login" button on the primary homepage header under the CAT 2026 banner.
3. **Enter Login Credentials**:
   - Enter your **User ID** (e.g., `CAT26XXXXXX`).
   - Enter your confidential **Password** generated during registration.
   - Enter the displayed **Captcha Security Code** and click **Login**.
4. **Locate the Admit Card Tab**: Inside the applicant dashboard, find the tab labelled **"Admit Card"** (positioned adjacent to your Application Form summary).
5. **Select Assessment Cycle**: Under the assessment dropdown, select "Common Admission Test 2026" and click **Download**.
6. **Save and Inspect the PDF**: Save the PDF document to your system. Check that the barcode, candidate photograph, signature, and test centre address render with razor-sharp clarity.
7. **Take Multiple Colour Printouts**: Print at least 2 to 3 hard copies on clean, standard A4 white paper. Do not print double-sided.

## What Should You Do If You Forgot Your CAT User ID or Password?

**If you forget your CAT 2026 login credentials, click on the "Forgot User ID/Password" link on the login window at iimcat.ac.in, enter your registered email address, and submit to receive instant credential retrieval instructions.**

- **Check Your Registered Email**: Search your inbox and spam/junk folders for emails from `cat2026@iimcat.ac.in` sent during your initial registration in August/September. Your User ID is permanently recorded in your original confirmation email and SMS records.
- **SMS Search**: Check your phone messages for SMS headers containing `IIMCAT` or `CATEXM` containing your 7-digit registration serial code.
- **Still Locked Out?**: If you no longer have access to your registered email or mobile number, contact the official CAT candidate helpdesk immediately with your Class 10/12 roll numbers and photo identification proof for credential restoration.

## What Details Must You Verify on Your CAT 2026 Admit Card?

**You must immediately verify seven critical details on your CAT admit card: your legal candidate name, category reservation status, registration number, test city and centre address, allotted slot timing, PwD disability status, and photo/signature legibility.** Even minor typographical discrepancies can cause complications during on-site biometric verification.

### Comprehensive Admit Card Verification Checklist

- [ ] **Candidate Legal Name**: Ensure spelling matches your Class 10 Certificate and government-issued photo ID identically (no missing middle names or initials).
- [ ] **Date of Birth & Gender**: Verify that your recorded birth date conforms with official records.
- [ ] **Application & Roll Number**: Check your 7-digit registration number and the newly assigned CAT Roll / Seat Number.
- [ ] **Category & Reservation Status**: Verify whether you are categorized as General, EWS, NC-OBC, SC, ST, or PwD. Category changes are not allowed at this stage.
- [ ] **Allotted Slot & Timing**: Note whether you are assigned to Slot 1 (Morning), Slot 2 (Afternoon), or Slot 3 (Evening).
- [ ] **Test Centre Name & Venue Address**: Review the exact institution name, building block, street name, and PIN code. The admit card includes a Google Maps landmark hyperlink for navigation.
- [ ] **Photograph & Digital Signature**: Confirm that your uploaded passport photograph and signature are upright, clear, and unblurred.
- [ ] **Emergency Contact & Helpdesk Coordinates**: Note the contact details for the central test administration authority.

### How to Resolve Admit Card Discrepancies

If you spot an error, blurred photograph, or missing signature block, raise a formal query with the central conducting authority without delay:

- **Official CAT Helpdesk Email**: `cathelpdesk@iimcat.ac.in`
- **Toll-Free Candidate Helpline**: **1800-210-1070** / **1800-266-3549**
- **Operating Hours**: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM (excluding public holidays)

> ⚠️ **Important Policy Warning**: IIM rules strictly mandate that requests for changing the allotted test city, examination venue, or test shift slot **will not be entertained under any circumstances**. Allocation is governed by automated algorithmic capacity distribution based on the candidate's initial test city preferences.

## What Documents and Items Are Mandatory to Carry on CAT Exam Day?

**To gain entry to the CAT 2026 test centre, you must carry a hard-copy colour printout of your admit card, one valid original government photo ID, an additional passport-size photograph identical to the registration upload, and required medical certificates if applicable.**

### 1. Mandatory Entry Documents (Physical Originals Only)

1. **Printed CAT 2026 Admit Card**:
   - Printed on clean A4 paper (colour printout strongly recommended).
   - Candidate must paste an identical passport photo in the designated box **before arriving at the centre**.
   - **Do NOT sign the admit card in advance**: The signature box must be signed strictly in the presence of the room invigilator during the exam.
2. **Original Valid Government Photo ID Proof** (Must match your admit card name):
   - Aadhaar Card (Physical PVC card or original letter)
   - Voter ID Card
   - Valid Passport
   - PAN Card
   - Permanent Driving License
   - *Note*: College ID cards, photocopies, notarized copies, and scanned smartphone images (even Digilocker) are **strictly rejected at the gate**.
3. **One Passport Photograph**: Identical to the photograph uploaded during online registration, to be affixed to the attendance register inside the lab.
4. **Scribe Affidavits & Medical Certificates** (For PwD candidates utilizing assistive scribes).

### 2. Strict Dress Code & Prohibited Items Policy

To minimize security delays during frisking and biometric scanning, follow IIM's standardized dress code:

- **Footwear Guidelines**: Wear simple sandals, slippers, or open shoes with flat soles. Avoid heavy boots, sports shoes with thick rubber soles, and high heels. Shoes may need to be removed for manual inspection at security checkpoints.
- **Clothing Specifications**: Dress in comfortable, lightweight clothing without large metallic buttons, badges, multiple pockets, or oversized zippers. Layered outerwear like heavy jackets and hoodies will undergo thorough inspection.
- **Religious Attire / Faith Accessories**: Candidates wearing customary religious articles (e.g., Kirpan, Kara, Hijab, Turban) must report to the test centre at least **90 minutes before gate closure** to allow ample time for respectful, mandatory manual security screening.
- **Strictly Prohibited Articles**:
  - ❌ Mobile phones, pagers, Bluetooth devices, smart rings, wireless earphones, fitness bands, or any electronic gadget.
  - ❌ Analog or digital wristwatches (the computer terminal features an exact on-screen countdown timer).
  - ❌ Wallets, purses, bags, pencil boxes, loose sheets of paper, or personal pens (rough scratch pads and ballpoint pens are supplied at your terminal).
  - ❌ Calculators (a built-in on-screen scientific calculator is provided on the CBT testing software).

## CAT 2026 Exam Structure: Sectional Blueprint & Marking Scheme

**The CAT 2026 question paper comprises 66 questions spread across three timed 40-minute sections, yielding a maximum score of 198 marks with +3 marks awarded for correct answers and -1 deducted for incorrect multiple-choice questions.**

### Master Sectional Blueprint & Scoring Matrix

| Section Order | Section Name | Total Questions | MCQs (Negative -1) | Non-MCQs / TITA (No Negative) | Time Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Section 1** | Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension (VARC) | 24 | 18–20 | 4–6 | 40 Minutes |
| **Section 2** | Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning (DILR) | 20 | 14–16 | 4–6 | 40 Minutes |
| **Section 3** | Quantitative Aptitude (QA) | 22 | 14–16 | 6–8 | 40 Minutes |
| **Total** | **All Three Sections Combined** | **66 Questions** | **~48–52** | **~14–18** | **120 Minutes** |

### Key Navigation Rules on the CBT Interface

- **Strict Sectional Lock**: Candidates cannot skip between sections or borrow time from one section for another. After exactly 40 minutes, Section 1 auto-submits, and Section 2 launches immediately.
- **TITA (Type-in-the-Answer) Advantage**: Non-MCQ questions carry **zero negative marking**. Always submit calculated answers for TITA questions without hesitation.
- **On-Screen Calculator Utilization**: Use the virtual calculator for multi-step arithmetic, roots, and percentages, but avoid using it for simple mental math to protect your pacing.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Can I show my CAT admit card on a smartphone or tablet at the exam centre?
**No. Electronic copies, PDF viewer screenshots, or mobile displays are strictly prohibited and will result in direct disqualification at the entrance gate.** Candidates must produce a physical, hard-copy paper printout (preferably in colour) on A4 sheet paper.

### What should I do if my photograph or signature on the CAT admit card is blurry or missing?
Immediately submit a formal grievance to the official CAT helpdesk via email at `cathelpdesk@iimcat.ac.in` and call **1800-210-1070**. Carry two physical passport-size photographs (identical to your application form upload), valid original ID proof, and a copy of your original CAT application confirmation to the test centre administrator on exam day.

### Is rough paper provided for scratch work in CAT 2026?
**Yes, an official scribble pad along with a ballpoint pen is supplied to each candidate at their allotted computer terminal.** Candidates must write their name and registration number on the cover of the scribble pad and must deposit it back with the room invigilator before exiting the examination hall.

### Can I leave the examination hall before the 120 minutes expire?
**No candidate is permitted to leave the examination room or terminate their session before the full 120 minutes (or 160 minutes for PwD candidates) conclude**, even if you complete all questions early.

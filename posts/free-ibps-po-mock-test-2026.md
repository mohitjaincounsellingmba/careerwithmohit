## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the pattern of the IBPS PO Prelims exam?
IBPS PO Prelims features 100 questions (30 English, 35 Quant, 35 Reasoning) carrying 100 marks, to be solved in 60 minutes with a strict sectional time limit of 20 minutes each.

### Does IBPS PO have sectional cutoffs?
Yes, you must pass in each of the three sections by securing minimum qualifying marks determined by IBPS, in addition to clearing the overall cutoff.

### What is the marking scheme and negative marking in IBPS PO?
Each correct answer carries +1 mark. If you mark a wrong answer, a penalty of -0.25 marks is applied. Unattempted questions carry no penalty.

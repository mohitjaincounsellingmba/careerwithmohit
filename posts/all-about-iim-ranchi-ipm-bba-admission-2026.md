IIM Ranchi is one of the premier "New IIMs" to offer the Integrated Programme in Management (IPM). With its rapid growth and strong placement records, IIM Ranchi has become a top choice for students who want an IIM brand right after school in 2026.

## 🏛️ Why Choose IIM Ranchi IPM in 2026?
The IPM program at IIM Ranchi is designed to provide a solid foundation in management with a multi-disciplinary approach.
- **Academic Rigor:** Curriculum curated by top IIM faculty.
- **Industry Connect:** Jharkhand's industrial hub provides ample opportunities for field projects and internships.
- **IIM Brand:** Access to the same placement and alumni benefits as the MBA (PGP) batch.

## 💰 Latest Fee Structure (2026)
The fee structure for the 5-year IPM program at IIM Ranchi is:
- **First 3 Years:** Approximately ₹4,80,000 – ₹5,20,000 per year.
- **Final 2 Years (MBA Phase):** At par with the prevailing PGP (MBA) fees, approximately ₹8,50,000 – ₹9,50,000 per year.
- **Total Investment:** Approx. ₹35 – 38 Lakhs (including residential charges).

## 🚀 Placement Process & Statistics 2026
IPM students are eligible for the common final placement process alongside the regular MBA (PGP) students.
- **Average Package:** ₹17.3 LPA
- **Highest Package:** ₹32 LPA
- **Top Recruiters:** Accenture, Deloitte, ICICI Bank, Microsoft, Tata Steel, EY.
- **Placement Process:** Managed by the common placement cell, involving summer internships (after Year 4) and final placements (Year 5).

## 📝 Admission Process 2026 (IPMAT & SAT)
IIM Ranchi has traditionally accepted scores from two major exams:
- **1. IPMAT (Indore):** The primary entrance exam used for selection.
- **2. SAT (Scholastic Assessment Test):** IIM Ranchi is one of the few IIMs that also considers SAT scores for its IPM program.
- **Selection Criteria:** Exam Score + Personal Interview (PI) + Academic Consistency.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Q1. Is there an exit option at IIM Ranchi IPM?**
Yes, students can exit after 3 years with a Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) degree.

**Q2. Does IIM Ranchi accept IPMAT Rohtak scores?**
Generally, IIM Ranchi accepts IPMAT Indore scores. Candidates should verify the latest notification for any changes regarding the acceptance of Rohtak or JIPMAT scores.

**Q3. What is the intake capacity for IPM 2026?**
The intake is approximately 120 seats for the 2026 batch.

The **CBSE Class 12 Results 2026** were recently declared, and while many students have celebrated their success, some may feel that their marks do not reflect their true performance. If you are unsatisfied with your results, the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) provides a formal mechanism for **Verification of Marks, Obtaining Photocopies, and Re-evaluation.**

However, this process is time-bound and follows a strict sequential order. Here is everything you need to know about the CBSE 12th Re-evaluation 2026.

---

### 📅 CBSE 12th Re-evaluation 2026: Official Schedule

You cannot skip stages. To reach the re-evaluation stage, you must first go through the verification and photocopy stages. Mark these dates in your calendar:

| Activity | Application Window (2026) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Stage 1: Verification of Marks** | May 26, 2026 – May 29, 2026 |
| **Stage 2: Photocopy of Answer Sheet** | May 19, 2026 – May 22, 2026 |
| **Stage 3: Re-evaluation (Final Step)** | May 31, 2026 – June 5, 2026 |

*Note: Stage 1 (Verification) actually happens before Stage 2 in terms of the logical flow for re-evaluation, though the application windows are strictly defined. Check the official portal for any last-minute adjustments.*

---

### 💰 Fee Structure for Post-Result Services

The fees must be paid online via Credit Card, Debit Card, or Net Banking. Fees are non-refundable.

*   **Verification of Marks:** ₹500 per subject.
*   **Photocopy of Answer Sheet:** ₹700 per subject.
*   **Re-evaluation:** ₹100 per question.

---

### 📝 The 3-Stage Process Explained

#### Stage 1: Verification of Marks
In this stage, the board checks for any totaling errors, ensure all answers have been marked, and verify that marks have been correctly transferred to the main sheet. 
*   **Outcome:** Marks may increase, decrease, or remain the same.

#### Stage 2: Obtaining Photocopy of Answer Sheet
Only those who applied for 'Verification of Marks' can apply for a scanned photocopy of their evaluated answer book.
*   **Why do this?** This allows you to see exactly where you lost marks and identify specific questions where the marking seems unfair.

#### Stage 3: Re-evaluation of Specific Questions
If you identify a discrepancy in the photocopy, you can challenge specific questions.
*   **Constraint:** You can only challenge questions for which you have received the photocopy.
*   **Finality:** The decision of the senior examiner at this stage is final and binding.

---

### 🌐 How to Apply Online

1.  Visit the official CBSE website: **[cbse.gov.in](https://www.cbse.gov.in)**.
2.  Click on the **"Main Website"** and look for the **"Apply for Re-evaluation/Verification 2026"** link.
3.  Enter your **Roll Number, School Number, and Admit Card ID** (as mentioned on your [CBSE 12th Admit Card](/blog/cbse-12th-result-2026-declared)).
4.  Select the subjects/questions you wish to challenge.
5.  Pay the fee online and download the confirmation page.

---

### ⚠️ Important Points to Remember

*   **No Offline Mode:** Applications sent via post or handed over at the regional office will not be accepted.
*   **Binding Results:** If your marks decrease after re-evaluation, the new (lower) marks will be updated on your marksheet. There is no option to revert to the old marks.
*   **New Marksheet:** If there is a change in marks, you will have to surrender your original marksheet to the board to receive an updated one.

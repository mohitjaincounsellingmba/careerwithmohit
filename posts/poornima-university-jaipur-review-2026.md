## 🎯 Admission Process 2026 (Step-by-Step Guide)

Securing admission to Poornima University, Jaipur for the 2026 intake follows a structured and merit-oriented process:

1. **Online Application Submission:** Candidates must register online through the university's official admissions portal and fill out their academic profile.
2. **Entrance Exam Qualification:** Depending on the stream, applicants must submit valid national/state entrance scores (**CUET, JEE Main, CAT, MAT, Merit-based**) or appear for the university's entrance test.

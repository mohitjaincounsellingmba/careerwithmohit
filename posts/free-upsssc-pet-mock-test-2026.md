The **UPSSSC PET (Preliminary Eligibility Test)** is conducted by the **Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC)** and serves as a mandatory qualifying gateway for all **Group C and Group D** government jobs in Uttar Pradesh. Candidates who clear PET become eligible to directly apply for various subordinate posts — including **Lekhpal, Junior Assistant, Supply Inspector, Forest Guard, Health Worker, Statistical Officer**, and many more — without needing to appear in separate preliminary exams for each post.

In short, PET is the **master key** to dozens of UP government jobs. Clear it once, and multiple doors open simultaneously!

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the UPSSSC PET exam pattern?
UPSSSC PET consists of 100 multiple-choice questions carrying 100 marks, to be solved in 2 hours. It covers General Studies, Hindi, English, Elementary Mathematics, and Graph & Table Analysis.

### Is there negative marking in the UPSSSC PET exam?
Yes, there is a negative marking of 0.25 (1/4th) of the marks assigned to that question for every incorrect answer.

### What is the validity of the UPSSSC PET scorecard?
The UPSSSC PET scorecard is valid for 1 year from the date of result declaration. Candidates can apply for various UP Group C Mains exams using this score.

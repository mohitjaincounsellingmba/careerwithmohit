Running a career upgrade requires choosing the right management program. For working professionals in Delhi, the Executive MBA / Executive PGDM offered by [Lal Bahadur Shastri Institute of Management (LBSIM), Delhi](/colleges/lbsim-delhi) represents a powerful gateway to higher senior leadership positions.

In this review, we break down everything you need to know: fees, admission cutoffs, placements, pros, cons, and our honest expert verdict.

---

## 🏆 Program Overview

- **Official Course Name:** Post Graduate Diploma in Management (Executive) - 15 Month
- **Approximate Fees:** ₹7.00 Lakhs (Total Fees)
- **Entrance Exams Accepted:** CAT/XAT/GMAT + Personal Interview
- **Course Duration:** 15 Months to 2 Years (Format varies by program)

---

## 📈 Eligibility & Admission Details

- **Academic Requirements:** Bachelor's degree + minimum 5 years of managerial/professional experience.
- **Selection Process:** Admission typically relies on professional profile screening, statement of purpose (SOP), executive experience reviews, and personal interviews. Elite IIM programs also mandate standard quantitative testing (GMAT/GRE).

---

## 💼 Placement Statistics & Career Impact

- **Estimated Placements:** Decent lateral placements with an average CTC of ₹10-12 LPA.
- **Career Growth:** Executive MBA degrees focus on helping candidates secure senior lateral shifts (like VP, Product Manager, or Director roles) or move from technical paths into core management.

## 📝 Student Reviews: Pros & Cons

### Pros
- **Highlight:** Highly reasonable fees for a top-tier private PGDM program.
- **Highlight:** Strong core specialization in Finance and Analytics.
- **Highlight:** Excellent corporate relations and mentorship from senior alumni.

### Cons
- **Limitation:** The campus is smaller compared to major university campuses.
- **Limitation:** Average package is moderate compared to tier-1 executive programs.

## 🔍 Our Expert Verdict

The Executive MBA program at [Lal Bahadur Shastri Institute of Management (LBSIM), Delhi](/colleges/lbsim-delhi) is highly recommended for professionals based in Delhi who want to scale their careers without disrupting their current geographic setup. 

If you are looking for top-tier consulting placements and have 5+ years of experience, full-time residential paths are stellar. However, if you are looking to continue your full-time job, their weekend/evening classes offer outstanding return on investment.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is LBSIM Dwarka Executive PGDM recognized by AICTE?
Yes, the Post Graduate Diploma in Management (Executive) at LBSIM is fully approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).

### What is the eligibility criteria for LBSIM Executive PGDM?
Applicants need a minimum of 5 years of post-qualification managerial/professional work experience along with a valid CAT/XAT/GMAT score.

### Does the program include a corporate project?
Yes, the final 3 months of the 15-month program are dedicated to an in-depth corporate internship/project.

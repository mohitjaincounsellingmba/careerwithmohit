**Kyrgyzstan** is famous among Indian students for providing low-budget medical education with full English medium curriculum. Medical universities in Kyrgyzstan offer robust clinical training in affiliated government hospitals.

---

## 🌟 Key Features of MBBS in Kyrgyzstan

1. **Budget Friendly:** Total tuition and hostel cost ranges from **₹15 Lakhs to ₹22 Lakhs**.
2. **NMC Compliant:** 5-year course work + 1-year internship meets NMC 54+12 months requirement.
3. **High Student Base:** Over 10,000 Indian students are studying in Bishkek and Osh.

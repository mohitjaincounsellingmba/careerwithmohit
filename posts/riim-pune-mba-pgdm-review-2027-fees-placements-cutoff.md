## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is RIIM Pune approved by AICTE and affiliated with Pune University?
Yes. Ramachandran International Institute of Management (RIIM Pune) is approved by the AICTE, Ministry of Education, and its flagship MBA program is permanently affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU).

### 2. What is the total fee structure for the MBA and PGDM programs at RIIM Pune?
The total course fee for the SPPU MBA (including 500 hours of EEP) is approximately **₹5.95 Lakhs to ₹6.60 Lakhs**, while the autonomous AICTE PGDM program ranges from **₹7.20 Lakhs to ₹8.60 Lakhs** (inclusive of international tour options).

### 3. What is the average salary package for MBA graduates at RIIM Pune?
The audited average placement package for MBA graduates at RIIM Pune is **₹6.80 LPA to ₹7.50 LPA**, with top performers securing domestic packages reaching up to **₹14.00 LPA** and international offers touching **₹35.00 LPA**.

### 4. What is the Employability Enhancement Programme (EEP) at RIIM Pune?
The EEP is a specialized 500+ hour skill training curriculum that covers SAP ERP, Advanced Excel, Digital Marketing, Six Sigma Green Belt, and Business Analytics certifications alongside university MBA degree studies.

### 5. Can I get direct admission in RIIM Pune without CAT?
Yes. RIIM Pune accepts scores from MAT, CMAT, ATMA, and MAH-MBA CET. Eligible candidates with strong academic credentials (50%+) can apply directly under the institutional merit quota through personal interview rounds.

Central University of Karnataka (CUK) stands out as a prominent institution in South India offering quality education. For the 2026 session, admissions to its diverse UG and PG programs are determined primarily by national-level entrance exams like CUET and JEE Main.

### 📊 CUK 2026 Key Highlights

| Category | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Admission Exams** | CUET UG, CUET PG, JEE Main (for B.Tech) |
| **B.Tech Total Fees** | ₹2.44 - ₹4.47 Lakhs |
| **UG/PG Annual Fees** | ₹17,340 - ₹76,420 |
| **Average Package** | ₹4.50 LPA |
| **Highest Package** | ₹9.00 LPA |
| **Top Recruiters** | TCS, Wipro, Accenture, Axis Bank |

### **Admission & CUET Cutoff**
*   **Process:** Admissions are conducted via the CUK Samarth portal. B.Tech seats are filled 50% via JEE Main (JoSAA) and 50% through CUET UG.
*   **CUET Cutoffs:** Cutoffs are competitive. For sought-after programs like B.Tech (CSE/AI & ML), previous safe scores hovered around 350–476+. Other BA/B.Sc domain subjects also demand solid percentile performances.

### **Fees Structure**
*   **B.Tech:** First-year fee is approximately ₹61,190, with total course fees spanning ₹2.44 to ₹4.47 lakhs.
*   **Undergraduate & Postgraduate:** Other programs range between ₹17,340 and ₹76,420 per year.
*   **Application Fee:** ₹1,000 for General/OBC/EWS and ₹450 for SC/ST/PwD.

### **Placements Overview**
*   **Career Support:** The dedicated placement cell actively brings in top recruiters across IT, banking, and management sectors.
*   **Statistics:** The average placement package sits around ₹4.5 LPA, with the highest recent packages touching ₹9 LPA.
*   **Recruiters:** Companies like TCS, Wipro, Accenture, and various emerging startups frequently recruit from the campus.

### **Hostel Facilities**
*   **Accommodation:** Managed by the DSW office, hostels are available but require a separate application process post-academic admission.
*   **Affordability:** The base hostel charges are quite affordable (often between ₹555 to ₹5,110 per annum), making it a budget-friendly option for outstation students (mess fees are extra).

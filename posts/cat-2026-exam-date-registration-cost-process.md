## 2. CAT 2026 Registration Cost (Fee)

The CAT registration fee is non-refundable and must be paid online during the application submission. The fee structure for CAT 2026 is expected to be as follows:

*   **General / NC-OBC / EWS Candidates:** **₹2,600**
*   **SC / ST / PwD Candidates:** **₹1,300**

*Note: Additional bank transaction charges may apply depending on your payment gateway.*

## 3. Step-by-Step CAT 2026 Registration Process

The entire application process for CAT 2026 is conducted online through the official website: **[iimcat.ac.in](https://iimcat.ac.in)**. Follow these steps to register without errors:

### Step 1: New Candidate Registration
1. Go to the official website and click on **"New Candidate Registration"**.
2. Enter your Name, Date of Birth, Email ID, Mobile Number, and Country.
3. Click on "Generate OTP". Enter the OTP received on your mobile and email to verify your registration.
4. You will receive a unique **User ID and Password** on your registered email and mobile number.

### Step 2: Log In and Fill Details
Log in using your credentials and fill out the detailed application form divided into six sections:
1. **Personal Details:** Father’s/Mother’s name, category, gender, nationality, and communication address.
2. **Academics:** Marks obtained in Class 10, Class 12, Graduation degree, and any professional degrees (CA/CS/ICWA).
3. **Work Experience:** Mention full-time work experience (in months) post-graduation (if applicable).
4. **Programmes:** Select the IIM courses/programmes you wish to apply for, along with interview city preferences for each. *(Applying to IIM programmes is included in the registration fee).*
5. **Test City Preference:** Select up to 6 preferred test cities. The exam authority will allocate one of these cities based on availability.

### Step 3: Upload Documents
You must upload scanned copies of the following documents in the specified format:
*   **Passport Size Photograph:** Recent color photo with a white background (dimensions: 30mm x 45mm, format: .jpg/.jpeg, size up to 80 KB).
*   **Signature:** Scanned signature on plain white paper (format: .jpg/.jpeg, size up to 80 KB).
*   **Category Certificate (if applicable):** NC-OBC, SC, ST, EWS, or PwD certificates in the standard format download template from the CAT website.

### Step 4: Pay Application Fee and Submit
1. Review your application carefully using the "Preview" button before final submission. Details cannot be edited post-payment.
2. Choose a payment gateway (Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, or UPI) and pay the fee.
3. Upon successful payment, a confirmation page will load. Save and download a PDF copy of your completed application form for future reference.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria for CAT 2026

To register for the CAT exam, you must fulfill the following criteria:
*   **Educational Qualification:** A Bachelor's degree or equivalent with at least **50% marks** or equivalent CGPA (minimum **45% for SC, ST, and PwD** candidates).
*   **Final Year Students:** Candidates appearing for the final year of their bachelor's degree/equivalent qualification exam are also eligible to apply.
*   **No Age Limit:** There is no age restriction or restriction on the number of attempts for the CAT exam.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the typical fee structure for MBA programs in India?
The fee structure varies widely. Government-aided institutes like [FMS Delhi](/colleges/fms-delhi) have low fees (around INR 2 Lakhs), while top-tier private institutions and IIMs can range from INR 15 Lakhs to INR 25 Lakhs.

### Is it possible to pursue an MBA without clearing CAT?
Yes, many colleges accept other entrance exams like XAT, NMAT, SNAP, MAT, or CMAT. Additionally, direct admission options under management quota are available in several private B-schools.

### What is the difference between an MBA and a PGDM?
An MBA is a degree awarded by universities affiliated with UGC, whereas a PGDM is a post-graduate diploma offered by autonomous institutes approved by AICTE. Both are highly valued in the job market.

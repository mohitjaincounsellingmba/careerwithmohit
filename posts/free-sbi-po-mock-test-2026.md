## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the pattern of SBI PO Prelims?
SBI PO Prelims features 100 questions (30 English, 35 Quant, 35 Reasoning) to be solved in 60 minutes with 20-minute sectional timers.

### Are there sectional cutoffs in SBI PO Prelims?
No, SBI PO has removed sectional cutoffs for both Prelims and Mains. Candidates are selected based on their aggregate score.

### What is the negative marking penalty in SBI PO?
SBI PO penalizes wrong answers by deducting 1/4th (-0.25) of the mark of that question. There is no penalty for unattempted questions.

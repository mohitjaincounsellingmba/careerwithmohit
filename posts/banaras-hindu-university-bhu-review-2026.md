## **Admission Process & CUET Cutoffs 2026**

For UG and most PG programs, BHU admits students through the **Common University Entrance Test (CUET)**. 
*   **The Process:** Candidates must choose BHU during their CUET application and subsequently register on the BHU counseling portal post-exam for seat allotment.
*   **Cutoff Trends:** BHU sees immense competition, especially for BA (Hons), B.Sc (Hons), and B.Com (Hons). Top programs generally require high CUET percentiles. The university releases multiple rounds of cutoffs based on seat availability and category reservations.

## **Fees Structure**

One of BHU's biggest advantages is its affordability. Regular academic fees typically range from a mere **₹6,000 to ₹30,000 per year** depending on the stream (Arts, Science, or Commerce). Professional programs and self-financed courses have a higher fee structure but remain highly competitive compared to private institutions.

## **Placements & Career Growth**

Placement scenarios at BHU vary drastically by department:
*   **IIT (BHU):** As a premier institute, IIT BHU boasts stellar placements with average packages around ₹29 LPA and top recruiters from global tech and core engineering sectors.
*   **Main Campus (Traditional Courses):** While professional courses (MBA, MCA, Agriculture) see active campus recruitment, many students in traditional Arts and Science programs choose to prepare for civil services or pursue higher education and research.

## **Hostel and Campus Life**

BHU is famous for its residential nature, offering extensive hostel facilities for both boys and girls.
*   **Admission:** Hostel seats are allotted based on merit (CUET scores) and distance from the student's hometown.
*   **Cost:** Hostel and mess charges are incredibly economical. The campus itself is self-sufficient, featuring a hospital, library, sports complexes, and lush greenery, providing an excellent environment for holistic development.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I check my exam results online?
You can check results on the official website of the conducting bodies (e.g., NTA for JEE/NEET/CUET, or respective boards/testing organizations) using your application registration number.

### Are mock tests helpful in exam preparation?
Yes, attempting mock tests helps candidates build speed and accuracy, understand the exam pattern, and perform detailed analytics of strong and weak sections.

### What is the significance of negative marking in entrance exams?
Many competitive exams deduct marks for wrong answers (e.g., -1 or -0.25). Candidates should avoid guessing to maintain accuracy and prevent score drops.

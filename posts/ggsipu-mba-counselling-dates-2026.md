## 🚦 The GGSIPU MBA Admission Priority Order
Remember, IPU does not treat all entrance exams equally. Seats in top-tier colleges like **USMS Dwarka** and **MAIT Rohini** are filled in a strict sequence:

1. **Priority 1: CAT 2025 Scores** — The first rounds of counselling are dedicated strictly to CAT qualifiers. Around 85-90% of seats in prime campuses are taken here.
2. **Priority 2: CMAT 2026 Scores** — If any seats remain vacant after CAT counselling rounds, CMAT qualifiers are invited.
3. **Priority 3: GGSIPU CET / CUET PG 2026** — These scores are considered last, usually for the remaining seats in lower-tier colleges or during spot rounds.

## 💸 GGSIPU MBA Counselling Fee Breakdown
To participate in the seat allotment process, candidates must pay non-refundable fees on the portal:

* **Application Fee:** ₹1,500 (if not registered earlier).
* **Counselling Participation Fee:** ₹1,000.
* **Total Enrolment Fee:** **₹2,500** (a consolidated one-time payment required to unlock choice filling).
* **Part Academic Fee (Post Allotment):** **₹96,000**. Once a seat is allotted, you must pay this fee online to retain your seat. If you fail to pay this, your allotment is cancelled, and you are thrown out of the counselling pool.

## 📝 Step-by-Step GGSIPU MBA Counselling Process 2026

Navigating the IPU admission portal can be tricky. Here is the exact path you need to follow:

### Step 1: Online Registration
Visit **[ipu.admissions.nic.in](https://ipu.admissions.nic.in)**, click on "GGSIPU Counselling 2026", and log in using your credentials. Fill in your personal details, academic scores (CAT/CMAT), and upload relevant certificates. Pay the ₹2,500 counselling registration fee.

### Step 2: Document Verification
If you belong to any reserved category (SC, ST, OBC, EWS, Defence, PwD, KM, etc.), the university will verify your uploaded documents online. Make sure your certificates are issued by the competent authority in the prescribed formats.

### Step 3: Online Choice Filling (Crucial Step!)
Once the choice-filling window opens:
* Log in and select "Choice Filling & Locking."
* You will see a list of available GGSIPU MBA colleges based on your eligibility.
* Arrange the colleges in order of your preference (e.g., USMS Dwarka first, followed by USMS East Campus, MAIT Rohini, JIMS Rohini, etc.).
* **Pro Tip:** Always lock your choices before the deadline, or they will be locked automatically.

### Step 4: Seat Allotment & Fee Payment
Check your portal on the result declaration day. If allotted a seat, you must:
1. Download your Provisional Seat Allotment Letter.
2. Pay the **₹96,000 Part Academic Fee** online.
3. Choose your willingness:
   * **Freeze:** If you are satisfied with the college and do not want to participate in next rounds.
   * **Float:** If you want to accept the current seat but wish to be upgraded to a higher preference in the next round.

### Step 5: Physical Reporting & Balance Fee
After the final sliding round, print your final allotment letter and report physically to the allotted college with your documents, passport photos, and the balance academic fee (total college fees minus the ₹96,000 paid online).

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q. What is the last date to register for GGSIPU MBA counselling 2026?**
The last date to register and pay the counselling participation fee on the official portal is **June 17, 2026**.

**Q. Can I participate in IPU MBA counselling with CMAT scores?**
Yes, CMAT candidates are eligible to participate. However, they will only be considered in the second phase after CAT seats are filled.

**Q. What happens if I do not pay the ₹96,000 fee after seat allotment?**
If you fail to pay the ₹96,000 part academic fee within the stipulated time, your allotted seat will be forfeited, and you will lose the right to participate in any subsequent online counselling rounds.

**Q. Is the GGSIPU CET exam mandatory for MBA?**
No. GGSIPU CET is only conducted to fill vacant seats left after CAT and CMAT rounds. If you have a decent CAT score, you do not need CET.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the typical fee structure for MBA programs in India?
The fee structure varies widely. Government-aided institutes like FMS Delhi have low fees (around INR 2 Lakhs), while top-tier private institutions and IIMs can range from INR 15 Lakhs to INR 25 Lakhs.

### Is it possible to pursue an MBA without clearing CAT?
Yes, many colleges accept other entrance exams like XAT, NMAT, SNAP, MAT, or CMAT. Additionally, direct admission options under management quota are available in several private B-schools.

### What is the difference between an MBA and a PGDM?
An MBA is a degree awarded by universities affiliated with UGC, whereas a PGDM is a post-graduate diploma offered by autonomous institutes approved by AICTE. Both are highly valued in the job market.

The **Integrated Programme in Management Aptitude Test (IPMAT)** is India's most sought-after management entrance examination for students aspiring to enter prestigious **Indian Institutes of Management (IIMs)** directly after Class 12. 

Conducted annually, IPMAT opens the doors to a 5-year dual-degree program (BA + MBA / BBA + MBA) that combines rigorous undergraduate liberal arts and management foundations with world-class MBA education. For candidates who want to avoid the intense post-graduation pressure of the [CAT Exam](/blog/all-about-cat-exam), cracking IPMAT is the ultimate gateway into a premier IIM.

In this comprehensive guide, we cover everything you need to know about **IPMAT 2026**, including important exam dates, complete syllabus, top accepting colleges, step-by-step admission process, and final selection criteria.

## 1. IPMAT Exam Overview 2026

There are three main entrance exams under the IPM umbrella:
1. **IPMAT Indore:** Conducted by **[IIM Indore](/colleges/iim-indore)** (accepted by IIM Indore, IIM Ranchi, IIFT, NALSAR, etc.)
2. **IPMAT Rohtak:** Conducted separately by **IIM Rohtak** for its 5-year IPM program.
3. **JIPMAT (Joint Integrated Programme in Management Admission Test):** Conducted by NTA for **IIM Bodh Gaya** and **IIM Jammu**.

| Exam Feature | IPMAT Indore 2026 | IPMAT Rohtak 2026 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Conducting Body** | IIM Indore | IIM Rohtak |
| **Exam Mode** | Online Computer-Based Test (CBT) | Online Computer-Based Test (CBT) |
| **Exam Duration** | 120 Minutes (2 Hours) | 120 Minutes (2 Hours) |
| **Total Questions** | 90 Questions | 120 Questions |
| **Total Marks** | 360 Marks | 480 Marks |
| **Marking Scheme** | +4 for correct, -1 for incorrect MCQs (0 negative for SA) | +4 for correct, -1 for incorrect |
| **Accepting Colleges** | IIM Indore, IIM Ranchi, IIFT, NALSAR, TAPMI, Nirma | IIM Rohtak |

## 3. IPMAT 2026 Syllabus & Exam Pattern

Understanding the exam pattern and sectional syllabus is critical for designing an effective preparation roadmap. While there is no rigid official syllabus prescribed by the IIMs, questions consistently evaluate high-school mathematics, logical aptitude, and English language comprehension.

### A. IPMAT Indore Exam Pattern (90 Questions / 120 Mins)
* **Quantitative Ability (Short Answer Section):** 15 Questions | 40 Minutes | *No Negative Marking*
* **Quantitative Ability (MCQ Section):** 30 Questions | 40 Minutes | *-1 for Wrong Answer*
* **Verbal Ability (MCQ Section):** 45 Questions | 40 Minutes | *-1 for Wrong Answer*

### B. Detailed Subject-Wise Syllabus

#### 1. Quantitative Ability (QA)
The Quantitative Ability sections test conceptual clarity and problem-solving speed across arithmetic, algebra, geometry, and modern mathematics:
* **Number Systems:** Factors, HCF & LCM, Remainders, Divisibility Rules, Base Systems.
* **Arithmetic:** Percentage, Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest, Ratio & Proportion, Averages, Mixture & Alligation, Time & Work, Time, Speed & Distance.
* **Algebra:** Linear & Quadratic Equations, Logarithms, Sequence & Series (AP/GP/HP), Functions, Inequalities.
* **Geometry & Mensuration:** Triangles, Circles, Polygons, Coordinate Geometry, Surface Area & Volume.
* **Modern Maths:** Permutation & Combination (P&C), Probability, Matrices & Determinants, Set Theory.
* **Data Interpretation:** Graphs, Pie Charts, Bar Charts, Caselets.

#### 2. Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension (VARC)
The English section evaluates reading speed, vocabulary, grammatical precision, and critical reasoning:
* **Reading Comprehension (RC):** 2–3 passages with inference-based and analytical questions.
* **Vocabulary:** Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms & Phrases, Word Usage, Foreign Words.
* **Grammar & Usage:** Sentence Correction, Error Identification, Tenses, Parts of Speech.
* **Verbal Logic:** Para-jumbles (Jumbled Paragraphs), Para-completion, Summary Questions, Cloze Test.

#### 3. Logical Reasoning (For IPMAT Rohtak & JIPMAT)
* **Analytical Reasoning:** Blood Relations, Coding-Decoding, Directions, Arrangements (Linear/Circular).
* **Logical Deductions:** Syllogisms, Clocks & Calendars, Data Sufficiency, Critical Reasoning.

## 5. Step-by-Step Admission Process

The admission journey for IPM programs is rigorous and multi-staged, testing both academic aptitude and interpersonal communication:

```
[Stage 1: Online Registration] 
       ↓
[Stage 2: IPMAT / JIPMAT Written Aptitude Test] 
       ↓
[Stage 3: Shortlisting Based on Cutoff Scores] 
       ↓
[Stage 4: Personal Interview (PI) / Written Ability Test (WAT)] 
       ↓
[Stage 5: Composite Score Calculation & Final Merit List]
```

### Stage 1: Online Registration & Eligibility Check
* **Age Limit:** Candidates must be born on or after August 1, 2006 (for general category in 2026; relaxation applies for SC/ST/PwD).
* **Academic Requirement:** Minimum **60% aggregate** in Class 10 and Class 12 (55% for SC/ST/PwD candidates).

### Stage 2: IPMAT Written Examination
* Candidates appear for the national-level online entrance exam across test centers in India.

### Stage 3: Sectional & Overall Cutoff Shortlisting
* IIM Indore applies **sectional cutoffs** across QA (SA), QA (MCQ), and VA sections. Candidates must clear all three sectional cutoffs to be shortlisted for interview rounds.

### Stage 4: Personal Interview (PI)
* Shortlisted aspirants are invited for an online or offline **Personal Interview (PI)**. 
* Panelists test general awareness, academic fundamentals (Class 11/12 subjects), communication skills, confidence, and motivation to pursue management.

### Stage 5: Final Selection & Offer Letters
* A composite score is calculated using the weighted formula combining written aptitude score and interview performance.

## 6. Selection Criteria & Final Weightage

Different IIMs use distinct weighting formulas to create their final admission merit lists. Below is a comparison of the selection criteria across top IIMs:

### A. IIM Indore IPM Final Selection Criteria
IIM Indore focuses heavily on written aptitude while giving significant weight to personal presence during the interview:

| Selection Component | Weightage (%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Aptitude Test Score (IPMAT AT)** | **65%** |
| **Personal Interview (PI)** | **35%** |
| **Total Composite Score** | **100%** |

*Formula used for AT Score Calculation:*
$$\text{AT Score} = 25 \times \left( \frac{\text{Candidate Score in QA(SA)}}{\text{Max Score in QA(SA)}} + \frac{\text{Candidate Score in QA(MCQ)}}{\text{Max Score in QA(MCQ)}} + \frac{\text{Candidate Score in VA}}{\text{Max Score in VA}} \right)$$

### B. IIM Rohtak IPM Final Selection Criteria
IIM Rohtak incorporates past academic record (Class 10 and 12 percentages) alongside entrance and interview scores:

| Selection Component | Weightage (%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IPMAT Rohtak Aptitude Test Score** | **45%** |
| **Personal Interview (PI)** | **15%** |
| **Past Academics (Class 10 Percentage)** | **20%** |
| **Past Academics (Class 12 Percentage)** | **20%** |
| **Total Composite Score** | **100%** |

### C. IIM Ranchi IPM Final Selection Criteria
IIM Ranchi uses IPMAT Indore scores for shortlisting and computes the final composite score as follows:
* **IPMAT Indore Score:** 50%
* **Personal Interview (PI):** 30%
* **Academic Profile (Class 10 & 12):** 20%

## 7. Preparation Tips to Crack IPMAT 2026

Cracking IPMAT requires a structured strategy that balances board exam preparation with competitive aptitude practice:

1. **Master Higher-Level Algebra & Arithmetic:** The Quantitative Ability (Short Answer) section in IPMAT Indore has zero negative marking—attempt all questions here with high accuracy.
2. **Build Reading Endurance:** Read editorial articles from *The Hindu*, *The Indian Express*, or *Aeon Essays* for 30 minutes daily to excel in long-form Reading Comprehension passages.
3. **Analyze Sectional Cutoffs:** Unlike typical exams where only aggregate score matters, IIM Indore requires clearing individual sectional cutoffs. Never neglect any single section.
4. **Take Timed Mock Tests:** Regular simulation of the 120-minute CBT interface builds mental stamina and helps refine question-selection strategy.
5. **Prepare Early for PI:** Start building speaking clarity and awareness of current economic affairs right after Class 12 board exams end.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. What is the IPMAT 2026 exam date?
IPMAT Indore and IPMAT Rohtak are expected to be conducted in **May 2026**, shortly after the Class 12 board examinations conclude. Official registrations typically begin in February or March 2026.

### 2. Which top colleges accept IPMAT scores?
Apart from **IIM Indore**, IPMAT Indore scores are accepted by **IIM Ranchi**, **IIFT Kakinada**, **NALSAR University Hyderabad**, **[TAPMI Bengaluru](/colleges/tapmi-bangalore)**, **[Nirma University](/colleges/nirma-university)**, and **T.A. Pai Management Institute**.

### 3. Is mathematics compulsory in Class 12 for IPMAT?
No, having Mathematics in Class 12 is **not compulsory** to appear for IPMAT Indore or IPMAT Rohtak. However, the Quantitative Aptitude section tests high-school mathematics concepts up to Class 10/11 level.

### 4. What is the selection criteria weightage for IIM Indore IPM?
For final selection at IIM Indore, the **IPMAT Aptitude Test (AT) score carries 65% weightage**, while the **Personal Interview (PI) carries 35% weightage**.

### 5. Can Commerce or Humanities students crack IPMAT?
Yes! A large percentage of students selected at IIM Indore and IIM Rohtak come from Commerce with Math, Commerce without Math, and Humanities streams. Consistent practice in quantitative aptitude is all that is required.

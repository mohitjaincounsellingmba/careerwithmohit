The **MAT May 2026 session** exams are done (or near completion), and thousands of MBA aspirants are now waiting for one critical update — **the result date**. The **All India Management Association (AIMA)** has not officially announced a fixed date for the scorecard release, but based on past trends, we have a clear picture of when you can expect it.

Here's everything you need to know about the **MAT May 2026 result date**, how to download your scorecard, and how to use your score for MBA admissions.

## MAT May 2026 Exam Mode Summary

Before diving into the result timeline, here's a quick recap of the MAT May 2026 session schedule:

| Mode | Exam Date | Result Expected |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Paper-Based Test (PBT)** | May 31, 2026 | Mid to Last Week of June 2026 |
| **Computer-Based Test (CBT)** | June 14, 2026 | Last Week of June / Early July 2026 |
| **Internet-Based Test (IBT)** | Multiple slots (May) | Mid-June 2026 onwards |

> **Important:** AIMA does not officially pre-announce a result date. Results are typically published **10–15 days after the last mode of the session** completes.

## MAT May 2026 Result: Expected Date

Based on AIMA's historical pattern:

- **PBT Result (May 31 exam):** Expected around **June 14–20, 2026**
- **CBT Result (June 14 exam):** Expected around **June 28 – July 5, 2026**
- **Combined/Final Scorecard:** Likely by **last week of June 2026**

Most candidates receive their scorecards on the same day for all modes they appeared in, once AIMA releases the batch. The scorecard is available **only on your candidate dashboard** at [mat.aima.in](https://mat.aima.in) — AIMA does **not** send scorecards by email or post.

## How to Download MAT May 2026 Scorecard

Once the results are declared, follow these steps to download your official MAT scorecard:

### Step 1: Visit the Official Website
Go to **[mat.aima.in](https://mat.aima.in)** — the only official AIMA MAT portal.

### Step 2: Log In to Your Dashboard
Use your registered credentials:
- **Email ID** (used during registration)
- **Password**

If you've forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link and reset it via your registered email.

### Step 3: Navigate to the Scorecard Section
Once logged in, look for the **"Result / Scorecard"** section in the dashboard menu. It appears only after AIMA officially releases results.

### Step 4: Download and Print Your Scorecard
- View your **Section-wise Scores** and **Composite Score**
- Download the PDF scorecard
- **Print at least 2 copies** — one for your records and one for college submissions

## Understanding Your MAT Score

The MAT scorecard contains two important components:

| Component | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Composite Score** | Out of 800 (used by most colleges) |
| **Section-wise Scores** | Language Comprehension, Mathematical Skills, Data Analysis, Intelligence & Critical Reasoning, Indian & Global Environment |
| **Percentile** | Your relative ranking among all test-takers |

> **Note:** Most B-Schools use your **Composite Score** and **Percentile** for shortlisting. The **Indian & Global Environment** section is often excluded from merit calculation.

Your MAT May 2026 scorecard is valid for **12 months** from the date of declaration. This means you can use this score for **MBA/PGDM admissions in the 2026-28 batch** at all MAT-accepting colleges across India.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1. When will MAT May 2026 result be declared?**
The result is expected in the **last week of June 2026**, approximately 10–15 days after the CBT (June 14, 2026).

**Q2. Where can I check my MAT May 2026 result?**
Only on the official AIMA portal: **[mat.aima.in](https://mat.aima.in)** — login to your dashboard.

**Q3. Will AIMA email me my scorecard?**
No. AIMA does not send scorecards by email or post. You must download it from the official portal yourself.

**Q4. What is a good MAT score for top MBA colleges?**
A composite score above **600 out of 800** (roughly 70+ percentile) is considered competitive for good B-schools. Tier-1 MAT-accepting colleges may require 80+ percentile.

**Q5. Is the MAT May 2026 score valid for September admissions?**
Yes. Your MAT score is valid for 12 months, so it can be used for both the current and upcoming admission cycles.

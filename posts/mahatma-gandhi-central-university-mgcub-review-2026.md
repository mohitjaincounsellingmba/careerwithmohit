Mahatma Gandhi Central University (MGCUB) located in Motihari, Bihar, has been rapidly expanding its academic offerings and campus infrastructure. It serves as an excellent central university option for students aiming to secure admission through CUET 2026.

### 📊 MGCUB 2026 Key Highlights

| Category | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Admission Exams** | CUET UG & CUET PG |
| **B.Tech (CSE) Fees** | ~₹1.74 Lakhs (Total) |
| **MBA Fees** | ~₹64,036 |
| **Average Package** | ₹4.0 LPA - ₹9.0 LPA |
| **Highest Package** | ₹36.0 LPA |
| **Hostel Fees** | ~₹9,000/semester |

### **Admission & CUET Cutoff**
*   **Process:** Admissions are driven by **CUET UG** and **CUET PG** scores. Post-results, candidates register on the MGCUB SAMARTH portal for counseling and document verification.
*   **CUET Cutoffs:** Cutoffs fluctuate based on seat availability. For popular programs like B.Tech, general category cutoffs have historically hovered around 400 marks (out of 750), making it competitive yet achievable.

### **Fees Structure**
MGCUB provides very accessible education:
*   **B.Tech (CSE):** The total fees for the 4-year duration are around ₹1.74 Lakhs.
*   **B.Sc (Hons):** Costs approximately ₹70,200.
*   **MBA:** The management program is highly affordable at around ₹64,036.

### **Placements Overview**
*   **Corporate Resource Center (CRC):** The university’s CRC actively preps students via mock interviews, resume building, and soft skill sessions.
*   **Placement Stats:** The university shows promising numbers with an average package of ₹4 LPA to ₹9 LPA. The standout highest package recorded recently touched a remarkable ₹36 LPA.
*   **Top Recruiters:** Leading firms such as Amazon, TCS, Wipro, and Infosys actively participate in campus drives.

### **Hostel Facilities**
*   **Accommodation:** Separate hostels for boys and girls, though capacity is somewhat limited.
*   **Fees:** A very manageable cost of approximately ₹9,000 per semester, with a one-time refundable caution deposit of around ₹3,000.
*   **Amenities:** Standard facilities including Wi-Fi, mess, and necessary furniture are provided to ensure a comfortable stay.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I check my exam results online?
You can check results on the official website of the conducting bodies (e.g., NTA for JEE/NEET/CUET, or respective boards/testing organizations) using your application registration number.

### Are mock tests helpful in exam preparation?
Yes, attempting mock tests helps candidates build speed and accuracy, understand the exam pattern, and perform detailed analytics of strong and weak sections.

### What is the significance of negative marking in entrance exams?
Many competitive exams deduct marks for wrong answers (e.g., -1 or -0.25). Candidates should avoid guessing to maintain accuracy and prevent score drops.

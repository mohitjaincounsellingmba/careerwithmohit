**Uzbekistan** has emerged as the **#1 fastest-growing budget destination** for Indian medical aspirants in 2026. With extremely low tuition fees, high safety ratings, proximity to India (3-hour direct flight from Delhi), and full NMC 2021 guideline compliance, over 8,000 Indian students are currently pursuing their medical degree in Central Asia's premier universities.

---

## 🌟 Advantages of Studying MBBS in Uzbekistan

1. **Lowest Total Cost:** Complete your entire 6-year MBBS program with tuition, hostel, and Indian mess within **₹14 Lakhs to ₹22 Lakhs**.
2. **NMC & WHO Compliant:** Courses are designed with 54 months of academic training followed by 12 months of mandatory clinical internship.
3. **Direct Flights & Proximity:** Tashkent is just a 3-hour flight from Delhi, making travel easy and affordable for parents.
4. **Indian Community & Food:** Universities run dedicated Indian hostels with 3-time Indian meals.
5. **Modern Government Universities:** Century-old medical institutes equipped with clinical simulation labs and tertiary referral teaching hospitals.

## 📋 Admission & Eligibility Requirements 2026

* **Age:** 17 years completed by Dec 31, 2026.
* **12th Marks:** Minimum 50% in PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) for General, 40% for SC/ST/OBC.
* **NEET Qualified:** Mandatory NEET UG qualification score for 2026, 2025, or 2024.
* **No Entrance Exam:** Admissions are based on merit and NEET score.

---

## ⚖️ NMC Compliance & Licensing Rules

Under NMC Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate (FMGL) Regulations 2021:
* Duration: 5.5 - 6 Years (minimum 54 months theory + 12 months internship).
* English Medium: Fully taught in English.
* Licensing: Students are eligible to sit for the physician license examination in Uzbekistan.

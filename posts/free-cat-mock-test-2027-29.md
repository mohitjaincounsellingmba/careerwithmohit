## What is the Official CAT 2026 Exam Pattern & Sectional Weightage?

**The official CAT exam pattern features 66 to 68 questions divided into 3 strictly timed 40-minute sections, totaling 120 minutes (2 hours).** Candidates must solve questions in a fixed sequential order (VARC, then DILR, followed by QA) without the ability to switch between sections.

| Section ID & Name | Total Questions | MCQs with Negative Marking | Non-MCQ (TITA) Questions | Sectional Time Window | Maximum Marks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Section 1: VARC** | 24 | ~18–20 | ~4–6 | 40 Mins (Locked) | 72 Marks |
| **Section 2: DILR** | 20–22 | ~16–18 | ~4–6 | 40 Mins (Locked) | 60–66 Marks |
| **Section 3: QA** | 22 | ~14–16 | ~6–8 | 40 Mins (Locked) | 66 Marks |
| **Total CAT Pattern** | **66–68 Qs** | **~50 MCQs** | **~16 TITA Qs** | **120 Mins (2 Hours)** | **198–204 Marks** |

### CAT Marking Scheme & Negative Penalty Rule
- **Correct MCQ Answer**: **+3.00 Marks**
- **Incorrect MCQ Answer**: **-1.00 Mark** (33.3% negative penalty)
- **Non-MCQ (TITA - Type In The Answer)**: **+3.00 Marks** for correct, **0 penalty** for incorrect.
- **Unattempted Questions**: **0 Marks**

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the pattern and duration of the CAT 2026 exam?
The CAT 2026 exam consists of 66 to 68 questions divided across 3 sections: VARC (24 questions), DILR (20-22 questions), and Quantitative Ability (22 questions) with a 120-minute total testing time (strictly 40 minutes per section).

### How is the CAT raw score calculated out of 198?
Each correct answer awards +3 marks, while each incorrect multiple-choice question (MCQ) penalizes -1 mark. Non-MCQ (TITA) questions carry zero negative marking. The net score across all 3 sections constitutes your total raw score.

### What raw score is required for 99 percentile in CAT?
A raw score of 85+ marks (out of 198) typically secures a 99+ percentile in CAT, which is the competitive benchmark for interview calls from top IIMs (Ahmedabad, Bangalore, Calcutta, Lucknow) and FMS Delhi.

### Which top MBA colleges accept CAT 2026 scores for 2027 admissions?
All 21 IIMs, FMS Delhi, SPJIMR Mumbai, MDI Gurgaon, IIT Bombay (SJMSOM), IIT Delhi (DMS), IIM Rohtak, IMT Ghaziabad, GIM Goa, FORE School Delhi, and TAPMI Manipal accept CAT 2026 scores.

### Can I switch between sections during the CAT exam?
No, CAT enforces a strict 40-minute sectional timer for each section in a fixed order (VARC -> DILR -> QA). You cannot switch or return to a previous section once its 40-minute window has elapsed.

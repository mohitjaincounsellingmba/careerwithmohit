## 📋 Eligibility Criteria & Admission Process 2026

1. **Academic Qualification**:
   - Passed 10+2 (or equivalent examination) from a recognized board (CBSE, ICSE, State Board).
   - Minimum aggregate score required: **50% to 60%** (varies by college).
   - **Mathematics**: Generally **not mandatory** for BBA in Banking and Finance in private universities, though commerce/economics backgrounds are preferred.
2. **Accepted Entrance Examinations**:
   - **CUET UG**: Widely accepted across private universities and state-affiliated colleges.
   - **IPU CET**: Mandatory for admission to GGSIPU-affiliated institutions (like JIMS and NDIM).
   - **University-Specific Aptitude Tests**: Amity (Video Interview / English test), GD Goenka Aptitude Test, Sharda SUAT, Galgotias GEEE.
3. **Direct Admission / Merit Counselling**:
   - Many private universities in Delhi NCR offer merit-based direct admissions or scholarships based on Class 12 board percentage.
   - For guidance on management quota and direct admissions, check out our guide on [Direct Admission in Delhi NCR Colleges](/posts/best-mba-colleges-in-delhi-2026).

Pursuing a Doctor of Medicine (**MD**) in the **United States of America (USA)** is considered the pinnacle of medical education. Doctors in the US enjoy high earnings ($200,000 to $400,000+ per year) and worldwide practice opportunities.

---

## 🌟 The US Medical Education Pathway

Unlike India, the US medical system requires an **8-year pathway**:
1. **Pre-Medical Undergraduate Degree (BS):** 4 Years (Majors in Pre-Med, Biology, or Biochemistry).
2. **MCAT Entrance Exam:** Taken during the 3rd/4th year of BS.
3. **Doctor of Medicine (MD):** 4 Years at an LCME-accredited medical school.
4. **Residency Training (Paid):** 3 to 7 Years depending on specialty (Internal Medicine, Surgery, Pediatrics, etc.).

## 📋 Exams Required for US Medicine

* **SAT / ACT:** For 4-year undergraduate pre-med admission.
* **MCAT (Medical College Admission Test):** Required for MD admission.
* **USMLE Step 1 & Step 2 CK:** National licensing exams during MD course.

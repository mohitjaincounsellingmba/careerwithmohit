The total volume of registrations and the actual number of candidates who appear dictate the percentile calculation curve, the sectional normalization formula, and the intense cut-throat race for an IIM interview call.

## Strategic Preparation Advice for the Final Lap of CAT 2026

With total applications tracking in the 3.2 Lakh bracket, casual preparation will not suffice. To stand among the top 1% of test-takers:

1. **Prioritize Question Selection Over Attempts**: You do not need to attempt all 66 questions. Scoring 78 marks out of 198 (around 40% of the maximum marks) secures a **99th percentile**. Accuracy and disciplined question elimination are your greatest assets.
2. **Master the 40-Minute Sectional Clock**: CAT does not allow cross-sectional time switching. You must cultivate high stamina to perform at peak cognitive clarity for 40 minutes in VARC, followed immediately by 40 minutes of intense DILR puzzle solving, and culminating in 40 minutes of Quantitative Aptitude.
3. **Simulate Real Testing Environments with Full-Length Mocks**: Take at least 20 to 25 proctored mock tests in the exact 8:30 AM – 10:30 AM, 12:30 PM – 2:30 PM, or 4:30 PM – 6:30 PM exam slots to synchronize your body clock.

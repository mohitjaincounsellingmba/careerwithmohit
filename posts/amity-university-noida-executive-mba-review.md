Running a career upgrade requires choosing the right management program. For working professionals in Noida, the Executive MBA / Executive PGDM offered by [Amity University, Noida](/colleges/amity-noida) represents a powerful gateway to higher senior leadership positions.

In this review, we break down everything you need to know: fees, admission cutoffs, placements, pros, cons, and our honest expert verdict.

---

## 🏆 Program Overview

- **Official Course Name:** Executive MBA (Part-Time / Weekend)
- **Approximate Fees:** ₹7.00 Lakhs (Total Fees)
- **Entrance Exams Accepted:** Amity Written Test + Personal Interview
- **Course Duration:** 15 Months to 2 Years (Format varies by program)

---

## 📈 Eligibility & Admission Details

- **Academic Requirements:** Bachelor's degree with min 50% + minimum 2-3 years of work experience.
- **Selection Process:** Admission typically relies on professional profile screening, statement of purpose (SOP), executive experience reviews, and personal interviews. Elite IIM programs also mandate standard quantitative testing (GMAT/GRE).

---

## 💼 Placement Statistics & Career Impact

- **Estimated Placements:** Corporate lateral connections, salary progression average around ₹8-10 LPA.
- **Career Growth:** Executive MBA degrees focus on helping candidates secure senior lateral shifts (like VP, Product Manager, or Director roles) or move from technical paths into core management.

---

## 📝 Student Reviews: Pros & Cons

### Pros
- **Highlight:** Vibrant campus with world-class sports and academic infrastructure.
- **Highlight:** Flexible weekend timing suitable for working professionals.
- **Highlight:** Strong corporate linkages with regular executive guest lectures.

### Cons
- **Limitation:** Huge batch sizes may reduce individual attention.
- **Limitation:** Brand value is less premium compared to public institutions like FMS or IIMs.

---

## 🔍 Our Expert Verdict

The Executive MBA program at [Amity University, Noida](/colleges/amity-noida) is highly recommended for professionals based in Noida who want to scale their careers without disrupting their current geographic setup. 

If you are looking for top-tier consulting placements and have 5+ years of experience, full-time residential paths are stellar. However, if you are looking to continue your full-time job, their weekend/evening classes offer outstanding return on investment.

---

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the schedule of classes for the Amity Noida Executive MBA?
Classes are held on weekends (Saturdays and Sundays) to allow professionals to maintain their work schedules.

### Do we need a CAT score to apply for Amity Executive MBA?
While CAT/MAT/GMAT scores are appreciated, Amity conducts its own written assessment and interview process for executive entries.

### Are there placements for this program?
Amity provides access to its centralized placement portal, though most weekend candidates leverage the degree for external lateral shifts.

No. The Institute of Management, Nirma University exclusively accepts CAT scores for domestic admissions to its flagship MBA program. CMAT, MAT, XAT, and ATMA scores are strictly not accepted.

The CAT cutoff for Nirma University typically ranges between 75 and 80 percentile, followed by Theme-Based Personal Interviews and micro-presentation rounds.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the pattern of RBI Grade B Phase 1?
RBI Grade B Phase 1 is a 120-minute test carrying 200 marks. The sections are General Awareness (80 Qs), Reasoning (60 Qs), English (30 Qs), and Quantitative Aptitude (30 Qs).

### Why is clearing RBI Grade B Phase 1 considered difficult?
The difficulty level of the Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning sections is extremely high (comparable to CAT), and candidates must clear both strict sectional cutoffs and overall cutoffs.

### Is there sectional timing in RBI Grade B Phase 1?
Yes, candidates are given specific sectional timings to solve each section, and they cannot switch sections at will.

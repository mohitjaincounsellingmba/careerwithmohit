## 4 Easy Steps to Secure Direct PGDM Admission 2027

### Step 1: Check Minimum Eligibility
* Secure at least 50% aggregate marks in your Bachelor's degree (any discipline).
* Students in their final year of graduation are eligible to apply with provisional admission status.

### Step 2: Choose Your Alternative Entrance Exam
* **MAT (Management Aptitude Test):** Conducted 4 times a year by AIMA in PBT, CBT, and IBT modes (easy difficulty).
* **CMAT (Common Management Admission Test):** National level computer test conducted by NTA.
* **ATMA (AIMS Test for Management Admissions):** Flexible online test accepted across Maharashtra, NCR, and South India.

### Step 3: Complete Direct Application Form
* Submit your profile directly on the official b-school portal or through authorized admission counseling partners.

### Step 4: Attend Profile Interview & Secure Seat
* Participate in online or campus-based Personal Interview rounds. Upon selection, pay the token registration fee to reserve your seat before general cutoffs close.

## Warning: Avoid Unauthorized Agents
Always ensure you apply directly to **AICTE-approved and NBA-accredited** institutions. Avoid paying unverified capitation fees or donations. Legitimate autonomous b-schools charge only official tuition fees published on their official fee structure documents.

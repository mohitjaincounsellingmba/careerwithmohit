The Xavier Aptitude Test (XAT) is one of the oldest and most prestigious national-level management entrance tests in India. Conducted by XLRI Jamshedpur on behalf of the Xavier Association of Management Institutes (XAMI), XAT is the primary gateway to XLRI Jamshedpur & Delhi-NCR, XIMB, IMT Ghaziabad, GIM Goa, TAPMI Manipal, and over 160 other elite business schools.

If you are aiming for admission to the MBA/PGDM batch of 2027, the **XAT 2027 application form open date** is a major milestone you cannot miss. Crucially, the XAT 2027 application process is **already open and active**.

## XAT 2027 Application Fee Structure

The application cost consists of the core entrance test registration fee plus optional charges for direct program applications to XLRI:

* **XAT 2027 Registration Fee (Mandatory):** ₹2,300
* **XLRI Program Application Fee (Optional):** ₹200 per program (e.g., BM, HRM, or GM programs)
* **Mode of Payment:** Online via net banking, credit cards, debit cards, or UPI.

## Eligibility Criteria for XAT 2027

Make sure you check these eligibility requirements before starting your online application form:

1. **Academic Qualification:** Candidates must hold a bachelor's degree of minimum 3 years duration in any discipline from a recognized university.
2. **Minimum Marks:** There is no official minimum percentage requirement to take XAT; however, individual member institutes (like XLRI) require at least 50% marks during the admission stage.
3. **Final Year Students:** If you are in the final year of graduation and will complete your exams by June 10, 2027, you are fully eligible to apply.
4. **Foreign/NRI Candidates:** NRI and foreign candidates can also apply using their GMAT scores.

## Required Documents for Online Registration

To complete the form quickly, keep the following items ready on your computer:

* **Academic Records:** Consolidated marks sheet of class 10, 12, and graduation.
* **Digital Scans:**
  * Passport-size photograph (recent, white background, size: 20 KB to 50 KB, in JPEG format).
  * Signature scan (on clean white paper in black ink, size: 20 KB to 50 KB).
* **Work Experience Certificate:** If you have full-time corporate work experience.
* **Identity Proof:** A valid government-issued ID (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID, or PAN card) for referencing.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Apply for XAT 2027

Register online through the official portal: **[xatonline.in](https://www.xatonline.in)**.

1. **Candidate Registration:** Click on "Register" on the homepage. Fill in your basic details (Name, Email ID, Phone Number). Verify your registration via the email verification link.
2. **Fill the Application Details:** Log in with your new system ID. Fill out the application details across sections:
   * **Personal Information:** Address, contact numbers, and category.
   * **Academic Qualifications:** Enter grade 10th, 12th, and college marks.
   * **Work Experience:** Mention job duration, company details (if applicable).
   * **Select Programs:** Choose if you want to apply to XLRI's Business Management (BM) or Human Resource Management (HRM) courses.
   * **Test City Preference:** Choose two preferred exam test centers.
3. **Upload Files:** Upload your scanned photograph and signature according to the format requirements.
4. **Make Payment:** Verify your completed application form using the preview mode. Proceed to make the payment online. Save a copy of the payment receipt and the PDF form for future counseling rounds.

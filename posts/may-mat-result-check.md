## How to Do Your MAT May 2026 Result Check

Once the result link is active, follow these steps to access and download your official scorecard:

### Step 1: Visit the Official AIMA Portal
Go to the official website of the conducting body at **[mat.aima.in](https://mat.aima.in)**. Avoid clicking on unverified third-party result links.

### Step 2: Click on "Candidate Login"
Locate the **Candidate Login** button on the homepage menu or dashboard selector.

### Step 3: Enter Registered Credentials

Download the PDF version of your scorecard and print at least **2–3 hard copies** for your college counselling rounds.

## Understanding Your MAT Scorecard Metrics

Your MAT scorecard reports both raw sectional scores and scaled composite scores. Here is how to interpret them:

- **Section-Wise Scaled Scores:** Scaled on a range from 0 to 100 for each of the four core sections.
- **Composite Score:** Reported on a scale from 200 to 800 based on the four core sections.
- **Percentile:** Indicates your relative position compared to all other test-takers in this session.

### Core Sections vs Indian & Global Environment (IGE)

A critical point to remember during your result check is that **MAT composite scores are calculated from 4 sections only**:
1. Language Comprehension (LC)
2. Mathematical Skills (MS)
3. Data Analysis & Sufficiency (DA)
4. Intelligence & Critical Reasoning (ICR)

The fifth section, **Indian & Global Environment (IGE)**, is scored separately and is **NOT included** in the 800-point composite score. Most colleges completely ignore the IGE score when shortlisting candidates.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1. Where can I find the direct login link for the MAT May 2026 result check?**  
**A.** You can perform the result check directly by logging in at the official candidate dashboard at [mat.aima.in](https://mat.aima.in).

**Q2. How is the MAT composite score out of 800 calculated?**  
**A.** The raw marks from the first 4 sections (160 questions in total) are scaled to a sectional score from 0 to 100, which are then mapped to a combined composite score out of 800.

**Q3. Is there a negative marking penalty on the MAT exam?**  
**A.** Yes. For every correct answer, you get +1 mark, and for every incorrect attempt, 0.25 marks are deducted. Unattempted questions receive 0 marks.

**Q4. Does a low score in the IGE section disqualify me?**  
**A.** No. The Indian & Global Environment section does not count towards your composite score of 800. Only very few B-schools look at it, and usually only as a tie-breaker.

**Q5. Can I apply for admissions with my MAT May 2026 score in the next session?**  
**A.** Yes, the MAT scorecard remains valid for 12 months from the date of declaration, allowing you to apply for multiple admission intakes within that timeframe.

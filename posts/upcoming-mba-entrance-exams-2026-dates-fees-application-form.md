Planning your MBA journey for the **2026–2028 academic cycle** requires careful tracking of **upcoming MBA entrance exams**, their application form costs, registration last dates, and exam schedules. While [CAT 2026](/blog/all-about-cat-exam) is the gateway to the 21 IIMs, relying solely on a single exam is a high-risk strategy. 

Top-tier management institutes across India accept a variety of national and institute-specific tests—collectively known as [OMETs (Other Management Entrance Tests)](/blog/all-about-omets-mba-entrance-exams-2026)—including **XAT, NMAT, SNAP, CMAT, MAT, and MAH MBA CET**. Each exam opens doors to prestigious B-schools such as [XLRI Jamshedpur](/colleges/xlri-jamshedpur), [NMIMS Mumbai](/colleges/nmims-mumbai), [SIBM Pune](/colleges/sibm-pune), [JBIMS Mumbai](/colleges/jbims-mumbai), and [FMS Delhi](/colleges/fms-delhi).

To help you organize your preparation and application budget effectively, this comprehensive guide provides a detailed breakdown of all **upcoming MBA entrance exams in 2026–27**, including **exam dates, registration last dates, application form costs, and top accepting colleges**.

## Detailed Breakdown of Major MBA Entrance Exams

### 1. CAT 2026 (Common Admission Test)
The **Common Admission Test (CAT)** is India’s flagship management entrance exam conducted annually by one of the older IIMs on a rotational basis. More than 3 lakh aspirants compete for seats in the 21 Indian Institutes of Management (IIMs) and top non-IIM institutes.

*   **Eligibility:** Bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks (45% for SC/ST/PwD categories).
*   **Application Form Cost:** Approx. **INR 2,500** for General/EWS/NC-OBC candidates; **INR 1,250** for SC/ST/PwD candidates.
*   **Registration Window:** Early August 2026 to **mid-September 2026**.
*   **Exam Date:** Last Sunday of November (**29 November 2026**, tentative).
*   **Exam Pattern:** 2 hours CBT with 66 questions across three sections: VARC (Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension), DILR (Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning), and QA (Quantitative Aptitude).
*   **Top Colleges:** [IIM Ahmedabad](/colleges/iim-ahmedabad), [IIM Bangalore](/colleges/iim-bangalore), [IIM Calcutta](/colleges/iim-calcutta), [FMS Delhi](/colleges/fms-delhi), [SPJIMR Mumbai](/colleges/spjimr-mumbai), [MDI Gurgaon](/colleges/mdi-gurgaon), and [IIT Bombay](/colleges/iit-bombay) (SJMSOM).

---

### 2. XAT 2027 (Xavier Aptitude Test)
Conducted by **[XLRI Jamshedpur](/colleges/xlri-jamshedpur)** on behalf of the Xavier Association of Management Institutes (XAMI), **[XAT](/blog/all-about-xat-exam)** is accepted by over 160 B-schools nationwide. It is renowned for its unique **Decision Making (DM)** section and comprehensive analytical evaluation.

*   **Eligibility:** Recognized Bachelor’s degree of minimum 3 years duration in any discipline.
*   **Application Form Cost:** **INR 2,200** (Basic registration) + **INR 200 per program** applied at [XLRI Jamshedpur](/colleges/xlri-jamshedpur) or Delhi NCR campus.
*   **Registration Last Date:** **Late November / First week of December 2026**.
*   **Exam Date:** First Sunday of January (**3 January 2027**).
*   **Exam Pattern:** 3 hours 30 minutes exam comprising Verbal & Logical Ability, Decision Making, Quantitative Ability & Data Interpretation, General Knowledge, and an Analytical Essay Writing task.
*   **Top Colleges:** [XLRI Jamshedpur](/colleges/xlri-jamshedpur), XIMB Bhubaneswar, [IMT Ghaziabad](/colleges/imt-ghaziabad), [FORE School of Management](/colleges/fore-school-delhi), GIM Goa, and TAPMI Manipal.

---

### 3. NMAT by GMAC 2026
**[NMAT by GMAC](/blog/all-about-nmat-exam)** is a student-friendly, computer-adaptive test that allows aspirants to choose their preferred exam date, time slot, and testing centre. Crucially, candidates can take the test up to **three times** in an admission year, with the best score considered by accepting institutions.

*   **Eligibility:** Bachelor's degree in any stream with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks.
*   **Application Form Cost:** **INR 3,000 + GST** for registration; each retake attempt costs INR 3,000 + GST.
*   **Registration Last Date:** **Mid-October 2026** (with late registration extending to late October).
*   **Testing Window:** **October to December 2026** (approx. 70-day window).
*   **Exam Pattern:** 120 multiple-choice questions across Language Skills, Quantitative Skills, and Logical Reasoning, completed in 120 minutes with **no negative marking**.
*   **Top Colleges:** [NMIMS Mumbai](/colleges/nmims-mumbai), [NMIMS Bangalore](/colleges/nmims-bangalore), K.J. Somaiya Institute of Management, XIMB (HRM program), and TAPMI Manipal.

---

### 4. SNAP 2026 (Symbiosis National Aptitude Test)
Conducted by Symbiosis International (Deemed University), **[SNAP](/blog/all-about-snap-exam)** is a speed-based exam required for admission into all 16 Symbiosis B-schools. Aspirants can appear for up to **three test sessions**, and the highest score among the attempts is used for percentile calculation.

*   **Eligibility:** Graduation from any recognized university with at least 50% marks (45% for SC/ST).
*   **Application Form Cost:** **INR 2,250 per test attempt** + **INR 1,000 per institute program applied**.
*   **Registration Last Date:** **Late November 2026**.
*   **Exam Dates:** Three separate CBT slots across **December 2026** (e.g., Slot 1 in mid-December, Slot 2 & Slot 3 in late December).
*   **Exam Pattern:** 60 questions in 60 minutes covering General English, Analytical & Logical Reasoning, and Quantitative-Data Interpretation & Data Sufficiency.
*   **Top Colleges:** [SIBM Pune](/colleges/sibm-pune), [SCMHRD Pune](/colleges/scmhrd-pune), [SIBM Bangalore](/colleges/sibm-bangalore), SIIB Pune, and SIBM Hyderabad.

---

### 5. CMAT 2026 / 2027 (Common Management Admission Test)
The **[Common Management Admission Test (CMAT)](/blog/all-about-cmat-exam)** is a national-level entrance exam conducted by the **National Testing Agency (NTA)** for admission to AICTE-approved management programs across over 1,000 institutes in India.

*   **Eligibility:** Bachelor’s degree in any discipline; final-year graduation students are also eligible.
*   **Application Form Cost:** **INR 2,500** for General Male candidates; **INR 1,250** for Female, SC, ST, OBC-NCL, and PwD candidates.
*   **Registration Last Date:** **April 2026 / 2027**.
*   **Exam Date:** **May 2026 / 2027**.
*   **Exam Pattern:** 3-hour exam with 100 questions divided into 5 sections: Quantitative Techniques & Data Interpretation, Logical Reasoning, Language Comprehension, General Awareness, and **Innovation & Entrepreneurship**.
*   **Top Colleges:** [JBIMS Mumbai](/colleges/jbims-mumbai), SIMSREE Mumbai, Great Lakes Institute of Management, BIMTECH Greater Noida, and Welingkar Mumbai.

---

### 6. MAT 2026 (Management Aptitude Test)
Conducted by the All India Management Association (AIMA), **[MAT](/blog/all-about-mat-exam)** is held four times a year in **February, May, September, and December** in Paper-Based Test (PBT), Computer-Based Test (CBT), and Internet-Based Test (IBT) modes.

*   **Eligibility:** Graduates in any discipline from a recognized university.
*   **Application Form Cost:** **INR 2,100** for a single test mode (CBT/PBT/IBT); **INR 3,600** for candidates choosing two modes.
*   **Registration Last Date:** Around **7 days before the respective test date** in each session.
*   **Upcoming Exam Windows:** September 2026 and December 2026 sessions.
*   **Top Colleges:** BIMTECH Greater Noida (PGDM programs), [Christ University Bangalore](/colleges/christ-university-bangalore), [XIME Bangalore](/colleges/xime-bangalore), [Amity University](/colleges/amity-noida), and ITM Business School.

---

### 7. MAH MBA CET 2027
The **[MAH MBA CET](/blog/all-about-mah-mba-cet-exam)** is conducted by the Maharashtra State Common Entrance Test Cell for admission to MBA/MMS programs across government, university-managed, and private institutes in Maharashtra.

*   **Eligibility:** Minimum 50% aggregate in graduation (45% for reserved category candidates from Maharashtra).
*   **Application Form Cost:** **INR 1,200** for Open Category & All India candidates; **INR 1,000** for Maharashtra reserved categories.
*   **Registration Last Date:** **February 2027**.
*   **Exam Date:** **March 2027**.
*   **Exam Pattern:** 200 questions in 150 minutes with **zero negative marking**, focusing heavily on Logical Reasoning (75 questions) and Abstract Reasoning (25 questions).
*   **Top Colleges:** [JBIMS Mumbai](/colleges/jbims-mumbai), SIMSREE Mumbai, [PUMBA Pune](/colleges/pumba-pune), Welingkar Mumbai, and SIES Mumbai.

## Budgeting Your MBA Application Costs: How Many Exams to Target?

When planning your MBA application strategy, it is crucial to budget not only for **exam registration fees** but also for **individual institute application form fees**, which typically range from **INR 1,000 to INR 3,000 per college**.

### Recommended Application Budget Strategy:
1.  **Primary Target (Mandatory):** Apply for **CAT 2026** (**~INR 2,500**), which automatically covers your registration for all 21 IIMs without additional college fees.
2.  **Top-Tier OMET Backups (Select 2–3):** 
    *   **XAT 2027:** ~INR 2,200 + INR 2,500 (XLRI application) = **~INR 4,700**.
    *   **SNAP 2026:** ~INR 2,250 (1 attempt) + INR 2,000 ([SIBM Pune](/colleges/sibm-pune) & SCMHRD) = **~INR 4,250**.
    *   **NMAT 2026:** ~INR 3,000 + INR 2,600 ([NMIMS Mumbai](/colleges/nmims-mumbai) registration) = **~INR 5,600**.
3.  **Specialized / State Backups (Select 1–2):** Add **CMAT** or **MAH MBA CET** (**~INR 1,200 – INR 2,500**) for high-ROI institutions like JBIMS, SIMSREE, and top private B-schools.

> [!TIP]
> **Total Estimated Application Budget:** A balanced MBA aspirant typically invests between **INR 15,000 and INR 25,000** across entrance exam fees and college applications to maximize calls from top-20 B-schools.

## 5 Actionable Preparation Tips for MBA Admissions 2026–27

1.  **Align Your Timeline with Exam Patterns:** While CAT focuses heavily on reading comprehension and complex DILR puzzles, speed-based exams like NMAT and SNAP require quick calculation shortcuts and time-management drills.
2.  **Track Registration Closing Dates Early:** Missing the application last date for exams like NMAT (October) or XAT (November) eliminates your chance at premier institutes like NMIMS and XLRI for the entire year.
3.  **Leverage Overlapping Syllabus:** Nearly 80% of the syllabus across CAT, XAT, NMAT, and SNAP is identical. Prepare a strong foundation in Arithmetic, Algebra, RC, and LR by September, then shift to exam-specific mocks.
4.  **Prepare for Exam-Specific Sections:** Allocate dedicated time for **Decision Making (XAT)**, **Innovation & Entrepreneurship (CMAT)**, and **Abstract Reasoning (MAH CET)** at least one month before each exam.
5.  **Analyze Mock Tests Rigorously:** Taking full-length simulated tests is the only way to build mental stamina and optimize your sectional attempt strategy.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Which is the most important upcoming MBA entrance exam for 2026–27 admissions?
**CAT 2026** is the most important national-level MBA entrance exam, accepted by 21 IIMs and over 1,200 B-schools across India. **XAT, NMAT, SNAP, and CMAT** are equally crucial for candidates aiming for premier non-IIM institutes like [XLRI Jamshedpur](/colleges/xlri-jamshedpur), [NMIMS Mumbai](/colleges/nmims-mumbai), [SIBM Pune](/colleges/sibm-pune), and [JBIMS Mumbai](/colleges/jbims-mumbai).

### How much does it cost to apply for major MBA entrance exams in India?
Application form costs typically range from **INR 1,200 to INR 3,500 per exam**. For example:
*   **CAT 2026:** INR 2,500 (General/NC-OBC) / INR 1,250 (SC/ST/PwD)
*   **NMAT 2026:** INR 3,000 + GST per attempt
*   **XAT 2027:** INR 2,200 (+ INR 200 per XLRI course)
*   **SNAP 2026:** INR 2,250 per attempt (+ INR 1,000 per Symbiosis institute program)
*   **CMAT 2026:** INR 2,500 (Male General) / INR 1,250 (Female/Reserved)

### Can I get admission into a top MBA college without CAT?
Yes, many premier B-schools accept non-CAT exams. For instance, [XLRI Jamshedpur](/colleges/xlri-jamshedpur) accepts XAT, [NMIMS Mumbai](/colleges/nmims-mumbai) accepts NMAT, [SIBM Pune](/colleges/sibm-pune) accepts SNAP, and [JBIMS Mumbai](/colleges/jbims-mumbai) accepts MAH MBA CET and CMAT scores.

### What is the last date to apply for CAT 2026 and other MBA entrance exams?
*   **CAT 2026:** Registration closes by **mid-September 2026**.
*   **NMAT 2026:** Registration closes by **mid-October 2026**.
*   **XAT 2027 & SNAP 2026:** Application windows close by **late November 2026**.
*   **CMAT 2026/27:** Registration closes in **April**.
*   **MAH MBA CET 2027:** Registration closes in **February 2027**.

## Introduction
The Common Admission Test (CAT) is the gateway to India’s top MBA programs. With the 2026 edition approaching, competition is fiercer than ever. This guide walks you through a **structured, 12‑week study plan**, the best resources, and proven tactics to boost your percentile.

---

## 1. Understand the CAT Pattern & Syllabus
- **Sections:** Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension (VARC), Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning (DILR), and Quantitative Ability (QA).
- **Question Types:** Multiple‑choice, Single‑answer, and Type‑in‑the‑answer.
- **Scoring:** +3 for correct, -1 for wrong (no negative for unattempted).
- **Duration:** 180 minutes (60 minutes per section).

## 2. 12‑Week Study Timeline
| Week | Focus | Key Activities |
|------|-------|----------------|
| 1‑2  | Baseline & Planning | Take a diagnostic mock (free CAT mock test 2026). Identify strengths/weaknesses. Set daily study hours (2‑3 hrs on weekdays, 4‑5 hrs on weekends). |
| 3‑4  | VARC Fundamentals | Read 1‑2 newspaper editorials daily, practice 5‑6 RC passages, master para‑summaries and critical reasoning. |
| 5‑6  | Quantitative Basics | Revise NCERT Maths, focus on topics: Number Systems, Algebra, Geometry, and Probability. Solve 30‑40 practice questions per topic. |
| 7‑8  | DILR Core Skills | Learn graph‑based, table‑based, and case‑based data interpretation. Practice 5‑6 DILR sets per week. |
| 9‑10 | Integrated Mock Cycle | Complete 2 full‑length mocks per week. Analyze each mock: time spent, accuracy, and topic‑wise gaps. |
| 11‑12| Revision & Final Push | Re‑solve weak‑area questions, work on speed drills (5‑minute quiz). Take a final mock 3 days before the exam. |

## 4. Mock Test Strategy
1. **Treat each mock as the real exam** – same timing, no breaks.
2. **Post‑mock analysis:**
   - Calculate section‑wise accuracy.
   - Identify questions you guessed; review concepts.
   - Track time per question to improve speed.
3. **Iterate:** After each mock, revise weak topics and redo similar questions.

---

## 5. Time‑Management Tips on Exam Day
- **Section Order:** Choose the order you’re strongest in (e.g., start with VARC).
- **Allocate Time:** Roughly 15 min per section for quick skim, then 45 min for detailed solving.
- **Skip & Return:** If a question feels stuck, mark it and move on – return only if time permits.

---

## 6. The Day Before & On‑Exam Day
- **Day Before:** Light revision, go through a short mock (30 min), relax, and get a good night’s sleep.
- **Exam Day:** Have a healthy breakfast, carry a valid ID, and reach the test centre at least 30 minutes early.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Q1: How many mock tests should I attempt?**
- *Answer:* Aim for 8‑10 full‑length mocks plus 2‑3 sectional tests each week during weeks 9‑10.

**Q2: Is it necessary to study from all three standard textbooks?**
- *Answer:* Not always. Focus on one core QA book, one VARC resource, and practice DILR from reputable online sets.

**Q3: Can I crack CAT with only 2 hours of study per day?**
- *Answer:* Yes, if you maintain consistency, focus on high‑yield topics, and use quality mock tests.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the typical fee structure for MBA programs in India?
The fee structure varies widely. Government-aided institutes like [FMS Delhi](/colleges/fms-delhi) have low fees (around INR 2 Lakhs), while top-tier private institutions and IIMs can range from INR 15 Lakhs to INR 25 Lakhs.

### Is it possible to pursue an MBA without clearing CAT?
Yes, many colleges accept other entrance exams like XAT, NMAT, SNAP, MAT, or CMAT. Additionally, direct admission options under management quota are available in several private B-schools.

### What is the difference between an MBA and a PGDM?
An MBA is a degree awarded by universities affiliated with UGC, whereas a PGDM is a post-graduate diploma offered by autonomous institutes approved by AICTE. Both are highly valued in the job market.

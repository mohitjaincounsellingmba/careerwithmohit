# CAT 2026 Correction Window: Dates, Editable Fields & Photo Resize Guide

> 💡 **Key Takeaways (Direct AI Answer Summary)**

> - **Permitted Editable Fields:** Only **Photograph**, **Signature**, and **Test City Preferences (up to 6 cities)** can be modified.
> - **Strictly Non-Editable Details:** Candidate Name, Date of Birth, Email ID, Phone Number, Reservation Category (General/NC-OBC/SC/ST/EWS), Academic Marks, and Work Experience are locked permanently.

## Editable vs. Non-Editable Fields in CAT 2026

The CAT Convening Committee strictly limits the fields that can be altered to maintain data integrity and prevent identity tampering.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 CAT 2026 CORRECTION SCOPE                   │
├──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┤
│    ✅ PERMITTED TO EDIT      │   ❌ LOCKED & NON-EDITABLE   │
├──────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Passport Photograph       │ 1. Candidate Full Name       │
│ 2. Scanned Digital Signature │ 2. Date of Birth (DOB)       │
│ 3. Test City Preferences (6) │ 3. Primary Email & Mobile    │
│                              │ 4. Category (Gen/OBC/SC/ST)  │
│                              │ 5. Class 10/12/Grad Marks    │
│                              │ 6. Work Experience Records   │
│                              │ 7. Selected IIM Programmes   │
└──────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. What CAN You Edit? (Permitted Fields)

1. **Passport-Size Photograph**: If your uploaded photo had red-eye glare, shadows, a dark background, was taken from a selfie angle, or exceeded compression thresholds, you can re-upload a compliant studio picture.
2. **Scanned Signature**: If your signature was smudged, written in blue ink, cropped awkwardly, or unreadable, you can upload a fresh scan in dark black ink.
3. **Test City Preferences**: You can alter and re-order all **6 test city choices** based on your current residence or travel convenience on exam day (November 29, 2026).

### 2. What CANNOT You Edit? (Locked Fields)

* **Candidate Personal Profile:** Full Name, Parent's Name, Date of Birth, Gender, and Nationality.
* **Communication Coordinates:** Registered Email Address and Mobile Number (used for OTPs and verification).
* **Reservation & Quota Category:** General, NC-OBC, EWS, SC, ST, or PwD status. (Crucial: Switching categories after the registration deadline is strictly prohibited).
* **Academic Marks & CGPA:** 10th standard aggregate, 12th standard percentages, Bachelor's degree marks, and university roll numbers.
* **Work Experience:** Duration and employer details entered during primary registration.
* **IIM Course Selections:** List of selected PGP/MBA and doctoral specializations.

## Photo and Signature Upload Specifications (Avoid Direct Rejection)

Over 15,000 candidates encounter issues on CAT exam day due to photo mismatches or unclear biometric verifications at test centers. Adhere strictly to the official parameters below:

| Parameter | Passport Photograph Specifications | Scanned Signature Specifications |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Recency** | Taken within the last 6 months | Fresh signature on plain white paper |
| **Background** | **Pure White Background** (No textures/colors) | Clean white background (No lined paper) |
| **Ink / Lighting** | Even studio lighting, no flash glare | **Dark Black Ink Pen Only** (No blue/gel pens) |
| **Face Visibility** | 80% face coverage; ears & forehead visible | Centered, uncropped full signature |
| **File Format** | `.jpg` or `.jpeg` | `.jpg` or `.jpeg` |
| **File Size Limit** | **20 KB to 80 KB** | **20 KB to 80 KB** |
| **Dimensions** | 30 mm (Width) x 45 mm (Height) | 80 mm (Width) x 35 mm (Height) |
| **Resolution** | Minimum 150 DPI | Minimum 150 DPI |

### Common Image Rejection Pitfalls:
* ❌ **Casual Selfies / Social Media Crops**: Automatic rejection by AI verification systems.
* ❌ **Wearing Tinted Glasses / Caps**: Eyes and facial contours must be 100% unobscured. Clear prescription spectacles are allowed without flash reflections.
* ❌ **Blurry Low-Resolution Phone Scans**: Use a flatbed scanner or a high-resolution scanning application.
* ❌ **Blue Ballpoint Ink**: CAT explicitly mandates black ink signatures for optical density calibration.

## Step-by-Step Guide: How to Edit CAT 2026 Application Form

Follow this precise workflow to update your form during the active correction window:

### Step 1: Access the Official CAT Portal
Navigate to the official website: `iimcat.ac.in`. Avoid third-party mirror links.

### Step 2: Registered Candidate Login
Click on the **"Registered Candidate Login"** tab under the CAT 2026 section. Enter your **User ID** and **Password** received during registration.

### Step 3: Open the Application Form Editor
Once inside your candidate dashboard, locate and click the **"Application Form / Correction Window"** tab.

### Step 4: Re-upload Corrected Documents or Reorder Cities
* To change your photo or signature: Click the **"Upload / Browse"** button next to the respective field. Upload your resized file and verify the preview thumbnail.
* To change test cities: Navigate to the **"Test City Preferences"** tab and re-select your preferred locations across the 6 dropdown menus.

### Step 5: Verify Preview & Submit
Carefully review the revised application preview. Ensure that the new images are crisp and readable. Click **"Submit"**.

### Step 6: Download Revised Confirmation Page
Download and print the **CAT 2026 Updated Application Confirmation PDF**. Note the updated submission timestamp for your records.

## Strategic Guide: How to Choose & Prioritize Test Cities

CAT 2026 will be administered in over 170 cities nationwide across 3 computer-based testing slots:
* **Slot 1 (Morning):** 8:30 AM – 10:30 AM (Reporting time: 7:00 AM)
* **Slot 2 (Afternoon):** 12:30 PM – 2:30 PM (Reporting time: 11:00 AM)
* **Slot 3 (Evening):** 4:30 PM – 6:30 PM (Reporting time: 3:00 PM)

If you are allotted the morning slot (8:30 AM), arriving at a distant exam center in winter traffic can be extremely stressful.

### Smart City Allocation Strategy:
1. **Preference 1:** Your immediate city of residence with the highest concentration of test centers and metro/cab connectivity.
2. **Preference 2 & 3:** Adjacent districts located within a 45–60 minute driving radius.
3. **Preference 4 to 6:** Major tier-1 transit hubs with direct airport or train links.
4. *Avoid picking remote districts as backup choices if public transportation is unreliable early in the morning.*

## What To Do If You Made an Unfixable Mistake (Category, Name, Marks)?

If you made an error in a locked field that cannot be edited during the correction window, follow this practical mitigation protocol:

### 1. Category Discrepancy (e.g., General selected instead of NC-OBC/EWS)
* **Reality Check:** IIMs strictly do not allow category alterations after registration closes. If you registered as General, you will be evaluated under the General/UR cutoff criteria.
* If you wrongly selected NC-OBC/EWS but do not possess the valid central-format certificate, write immediately to `cathelpdesk@iimcat.ac.in` requesting classification under the General category to prevent seat cancellation during document verification.

### 2. Typo in Candidate Name / Parent's Name
* If your name has minor spelling differences compared to your Class 10 Certificate or Aadhaar Card:
  * Draft a **Legal Affidavit (Sworn Declaration on Non-Judicial Stamp Paper)** attested by a Notary Public stating that both names refer to the same individual.
  * Carry the original affidavit along with your photo ID during the exam and subsequent interview stages.

### 3. Academic Percentage / CGPA Conversion Errors
* Non-IIM B-Schools ([FMS Delhi](/colleges/fms-delhi/), [SPJIMR Mumbai](/colleges/spjimr-mumbai/), [MDI Gurgaon](/colleges/mdi-gurgaon/), [IITs](/colleges/iit-delhi/), [BIMTECH](/colleges/bimtech-greater-noida/)) mandate separate institutional applications.
* When you fill individual application forms for these B-schools, you will enter your academic records afresh. Ensure that you calculate percentages using your university's exact conversion formula.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### When does the CAT 2026 correction window open and close?
The CAT 2026 application correction window opens in the last week of September 2026 (typically 3 to 4 days after registration closes) and remains active for a strict 72-hour window on `iimcat.ac.in`.

### What fields can be edited during the CAT 2026 correction window?
Candidates are permitted to edit only three specific fields: (1) Passport-size photograph, (2) Digital signature, and (3) Test city preferences (order and selection across 6 cities).

### Can I change my category, name, or date of birth in the CAT 2026 correction window?
No. Personal details including candidate name, date of birth, mobile number, email ID, reservation category (General/OBC/SC/ST/EWS), and academic marks cannot be edited during the correction window.

### Is there an extra fee to edit the CAT 2026 application form?
No. The convening IIM does not charge any additional fee to re-upload photos, update signatures, or modify test city preferences during the official correction window.

### What should I do if I made an error in a non-editable field like graduation percentage or category?
For category or name discrepancies, immediately write an official email to the CAT Helpdesk (`cathelpdesk@iimcat.ac.in`) along with your User ID and supporting documents. For academic marks errors, keep university transcripts and affidavits ready for the GD-PI verification round at individual B-schools.

### What are the required dimensions for photo and signature in CAT 2026?
The photograph must be a recent color picture with a white background (30mm x 45mm, JPEG/JPG, 20 KB – 80 KB). The signature must be on plain white paper in black ink (80mm x 35mm, JPEG/JPG, 20 KB – 80 KB).

The **Symbiosis National Aptitude Test (SNAP)** is one of India's most popular MBA entrance exams, acting as the single-window gateway to 16 prestigious institutes under Symbiosis International (Deemed University) – including flagship campuses like **[SIBM Pune](/colleges/sibm-pune)** and **[SCMHRD Pune](/colleges/scmhrd-pune)**.

Unlike CAT or XAT, SNAP is purely a **speed-driven aptitude assessment**. With **60 questions to answer in just 60 minutes**, understanding the **updated SNAP exam syllabus and topic-wise weightage** is the single most critical factor in maximizing your score.

In this comprehensive guide, we unpack the updated syllabus, section-wise mark distribution, core high-yield topics, and actionable tips to score 40+ marks.

---

## SNAP 2026 Exam Pattern At A Glance

Before diving into the topic breakdown, let's review the overall structure and key metrics of the SNAP exam pattern:

| Parameter | Exam Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Exam Mode** | Computer-Based Test (CBT) |
| **Total Duration** | 60 Minutes (1 Hour) |
| **Total Questions** | 60 MCQs |
| **Total Marks** | 60 Marks |
| **Sectional Time Limit** | **None** (Free navigation across sections) |
| **Marking Scheme** | **+1 Mark** for correct answer, **-0.25 Marks** for incorrect answer |
| **Number of Attempts** | Up to 3 attempts (Best score considered for final cutoff) |

## Section-Wise Weightage & Structure

The SNAP syllabus is divided into three core sections. Notice how **Analytical & Logical Reasoning carries the largest chunk (over 41%) of the total paper weightage**.

| Section Name | No. of Questions | Marks | Weightage (%) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **General English** (Verbal Ability, Reading Comprehension, Grammar) | 15 | 15 | 25.0% |
| **Analytical & Logical Reasoning (ALR)** | 25 | 25 | 41.7% |
| **Quantitative, Data Interpretation & Data Sufficiency (QA, DI & DS)** | 20 | 20 | 33.3% |
| **Total** | **60** | **60** | **100%** |

*Note: SNAP has completely removed the General Knowledge / Current Affairs section.*

## Detailed SNAP Exam Updated Syllabus (Section-Wise)

### 1. General English (15 Questions | 15 Marks)

The General English section tests vocabulary speed, grammatical correctness, and rapid verbal comprehension. Passages, if asked, are usually short (100–150 words) to match the 1-minute-per-question pace.

#### High-Yield Topics & Weightage:
* **Vocabulary & Word Power:** Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms & Phrases, One-Word Substitutions, Analogies, Spellings, Foreign Words.
* **Grammar & Sentence Structure:** Parts of Speech (Nouns, Verbs, Prepositions, Conjunctions), Tenses, Active & Passive Voice, Direct & Indirect Speech, Subject-Verb Agreement.
* **Verbal Ability & Logic:** Sentence Correction, Fill in the Blanks (Single & Double Blanks), Para Jumbles (4-sentence arrangements).
* **Reading Comprehension:** 1 short passage (if present in the slot), carrying 2–3 direct factual questions.

---

### 2. Analytical & Logical Reasoning (25 Questions | 25 Marks)

As the highest-weighted section in SNAP, **Analytical & Logical Reasoning (ALR)** decides whether you clear the 98+ percentile cutoff for **[SIBM Pune](/colleges/sibm-pune)**. The questions test non-verbal reasoning, puzzle-solving, pattern detection, and critical thinking.

#### High-Yield Topics & Weightage:
* **Arrangements & Puzzles:** Linear Seating Arrangements, Circular Arrangements, Matrix & Scheduling Puzzles.
* **Series & Sequences:** Number Series, Letter Series, Symbol Sequences, Odd One Out.
* **Coding & Decoding:** Letter-to-Letter, Letter-to-Number, Symbol-based coding.
* **Direction & Family Relations:** Blood Relations, Direction Sense Tests, Distance & Position problems.
* **Clocks & Calendars:** Hands overlap/perpendicular angles, Calendar leap year calculations.
* **Logical Deductions:** Syllogisms, Venn Diagrams, Binary Logic.
* **Critical Reasoning:** Statement & Assumptions, Statement & Conclusions, Cause & Effect, Course of Action, Arguments (Strong/Weak).

---

### 3. Quantitative, Data Interpretation & Data Sufficiency (20 Questions | 20 Marks)

Quantitative Aptitude in SNAP is heavily dominated by **Arithmetic and Algebra**. Unlike CAT, questions are formula-direct, requiring quick calculation rather than deep multi-step derivations.

#### High-Yield Topics & Weightage:
* **Arithmetic (Highest Weightage ~50-60% of QA):**
  * Percentages, Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest
  * Ratios, Proportions & Variations, Averages & Mixtures
  * Time, Speed & Distance (Trains, Boats & Streams)
  * Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns
* **Algebra & Numbers:** Linear & Quadratic Equations, Polynomials, Indices & Surds, Logarithms, HCF & LCM, Number System properties.
* **Modern Mathematics:** Permutations & Combinations (P&C), Probability, Progressions (AP/GP/HP), Set Theory.
* **Geometry & Mensuration:** Triangles, Circles, Quadrilaterals, 2D & 3D Mensuration formulas.
* **Data Interpretation & Data Sufficiency:** 1–2 small DI sets (Bar Graphs, Pie Charts, Tables) or standalone Data Sufficiency statements.

## SNAP vs CAT Syllabus: What Should You Tweak?

If you are preparing for both CAT and SNAP, keep these core strategy differences in mind:

1. **No Sectional Time Limit in SNAP:** You can jump between English, LR, and Quant freely to pick low-hanging fruit first.
2. **Speed Over Depth:** CAT tests conceptual depth with complex multi-layered questions; SNAP tests mental math agility, direct formula memory, and quick visual recognition.
3. **No Negative Marking on TITA:** SNAP consists entirely of MCQs with options, meaning all questions carry options and -0.25 negative marking.
4. **Higher Weightage to Puzzles & Series:** Number series, coding-decoding, clocks, calendars, and blood relations carry massive weightage in SNAP but are rarely asked in CAT DILR.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the updated exam pattern for SNAP 2026?
SNAP is a 60-minute Computer Based Test (CBT) with 60 multiple-choice questions worth 60 marks. There are no sectional time limits, allowing candidates to switch freely between sections.

### How many sections are in the SNAP exam updated syllabus?
SNAP primarily features 3 sections: General English (15 questions), Analytical & Logical Reasoning (25 questions), and Quantitative, Data Interpretation & Data Sufficiency (20 questions).

### Is General Knowledge (GK) part of the SNAP exam syllabus?
No, SNAP completely removed the General Knowledge section in recent years, focusing purely on speed, accuracy, reasoning, quant, and English skills.

### What is the negative marking scheme in SNAP 2026?
Each correct answer carries +1 mark, while each incorrect answer attracts a penalty of -0.25 marks (25% negative marking).

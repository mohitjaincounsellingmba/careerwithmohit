IIM Indore was the first IIM to launch the Integrated Programme in Management (IPM), a unique 5-year course that blends undergraduate and postgraduate management education. In 2026, it remains the "Gold Standard" for students aiming to join the Indian Institute of Management straight after Class 12.

## 🏛️ Why Choose IIM Indore IPM in 2026?
The IPM program is designed for high-potential students who want to build a strong foundation in social sciences and management.
- **IIM Brand:** Direct entry into a top-tier IIM without the uncertainty of the CAT exam.
- **Holistic Development:** The first 3 years cover diverse subjects like Philosophy, Psychology, and Mathematics.
- **Dual Degree:** After 5 years, students receive a BA (Foundations of Management) and an MBA.

## 💰 Latest Fee Structure (2026)
The fee structure at IIM Indore for the IPM program is divided into two phases:
- **First 3 Years (UG Phase):** Approximately ₹5,50,000 to ₹6,00,000 per year.
- **Final 2 Years (PG Phase):** At par with the prevailing PGP (MBA) fees, currently around ₹10,00,000 to ₹12,00,000 per year.
- **Total Investment:** Approx. ₹38 – 42 Lakhs (inclusive of residential charges).

## 🚀 Placement Process & Statistics 2026
IPM students at IIM Indore are merged with the PGP (MBA) batch for final placements, ensuring they get the same opportunities as CAT entrants.
- **Average Package:** ₹25.6 LPA
- **Highest Package:** ₹1.14 Crore (International) | ₹65 LPA (Domestic)
- **Top Recruiters:** Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan, Google, Amazon, Deloitte, BCG.
- **Placement Process:** Summer Internship placement (after Year 4) followed by Final Placements (in Year 5).

## 📝 Admission Process 2026 (IPMAT)
Admission is strictly through the **IPMAT (Integrated Programme in Management Aptitude Test) Indore**.
- **Exam Pattern:** Quantitative Ability (MCQ), Quantitative Ability (Short Answer), and Verbal Ability.
- **Selection Criteria:** Aptitude Test Score (AT) + Personal Interview (PI).
- **Cutoffs:** Typically require 98+ percentile for General category candidates.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Q1. Is there an exit option after 3 years?**
Yes, IIM Indore allows students to exit after the first 3 years with a Bachelor's degree (BA in Foundations of Management) if they choose not to continue for the MBA.

**Q2. Does IIM Indore accept SAT scores for IPM?**
No, IIM Indore only accepts IPMAT Indore scores for Indian students. SAT is generally for international/NRI candidates.

**Q3. How many seats are available in IIM Indore IPM?**
There are approximately 150 seats available for the 2026 batch.

### Profile vs Test-Score Shortlisting:
IMT Ghaziabad shortlists in two parallel streams:
1. **Profile-Based Shortlisting:** Candidates with consistent 75%+ across 10th, 12th, and graduation, plus 12+ months of relevant corporate experience or niche achievements, can receive interview invites at lower cutoffs (~85+ percentile).
2. **Score-Based Shortlisting:** Freshers and general applicants are shortlisted based purely on competitive national exam scores (typically 90–93+ percentile).

## 6. Selection Process: The Critical Thinking Test & PI

1. **Step 1: Common Online Application:** Submit personal, academic, and exam scores at `admissions.imt.edu`. Pay ₹3,500.
2. **Step 2: Profile & Test Shortlisting:** Candidates are invited for Phase 1 (profile-based) or Phase 2 (score-based) interview rounds in January/February 2027.
3. **Step 3: Critical Thinking Test (CTT):** A 30-minute written assessment designed to test reasoning logic, coherence, and written expression on socio-economic themes.
4. **Step 4: Personal Interview (PI):** A 15–20 minute conversational evaluation with faculty members and corporate alumni testing domain knowledge, business awareness, and emotional intelligence.
5. **Step 5: Final Composite Offer:** Final offers are determined by entrance scores (35%), Personal Interview (35%), Critical Thinking Test (15%), and Academic/Work Profile (15%).

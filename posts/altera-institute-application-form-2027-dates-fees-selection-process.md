## 3. What is the ALNAT Exam and Eligibility Criteria?

**The Altera National Aptitude Test (ALNAT) is a 60-minute online computer-based evaluation measuring Logical Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, Verbal Ability, and Creative Problem Solving in marketing scenarios.**

Candidates with valid scores in national entrance exams are exempt from appearing for ALNAT:
- **CAT:** 70+ Percentile
- **XAT:** 70+ Percentile
- **NMAT by GMAC:** 190+ Score
- **GMAT:** 550+ Score
- **Graduation Benchmark:** Minimum 50% aggregate marks in any undergraduate stream (Arts, Commerce, Science, Engineering). Final-year college students are eligible to apply provisionally.

## 6. Selection Process: 4-Step Pathway

1. **Step 1: Online Application Submission:** Register at `alterainstitute.com` and upload academic marksheets and exam credentials.
2. **Step 2: ALNAT or Score Evaluation:** Candidates without standard exam scores take the ALNAT aptitude assessment.
3. **Step 3: Marketing Case Problem / Challenge:** Applicants complete an interactive marketing challenge analyzing a brand crisis or product launch.
4. **Step 4: Personal Interview (PI):** A 20-minute conversational interaction with industry CMOs and program leaders evaluating curiosity, communication clarity, and commercial drive.

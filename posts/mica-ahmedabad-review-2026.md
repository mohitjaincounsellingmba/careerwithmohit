## 6. MICAT 2026: Exam Pattern, Syllabus & Selection Process

Admission to MICA is uniquely holistic. High percentiles in CAT or XAT are necessary but not sufficient; candidates must excel in the proprietary **MICAT** test.

### Step 1: Qualifying National Tests
Applicants must register with a valid score from **CAT 2025, XAT 2026, or GMAT (2024–2026)**.

### Step 2: MICAT Exam (Held Twice a Year)
* **MICAT-I:** December (Registration: Sept – Nov)
* **MICAT-II:** January / February (Registration: Nov – Jan)
*(If a candidate takes both, MICA considers the better of the two scores).*

### Structure of the MICAT Exam

1. **Section A – Psychometric Test:** 
   * Compulsory qualifying test. Failure in Psychometric evaluation leads to immediate disqualification regardless of scores in other sections.
2. **Section B – Descriptive Test:**
   * Tests creative expression, divergent thinking, and written articulation.
   * Includes 3 essay/opinion arguments and 1 visual/creative story-writing prompt from 4 images.
3. **Section C – Objective Aptitude Test (Negative marking applies):**
   * Divergent-Convergent Thinking & Reasoning (Data-driven logic & word associations)
   * Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension
   * Quantitative Ability & Data Interpretation
   * General Awareness & Media Trends (Advertising, global brands, marketing campaigns, current affairs)

### Step 3: Group Exercise (GE) & Personal Interview (PI)
Shortlisted candidates are called for offline GE & PI rounds held across major metro cities (Ahmedabad, Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Bangalore).

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is the average salary package at MICA Ahmedabad in 2025–2026?
The overall average placement package at MICA Ahmedabad stands between **₹19.22 LPA and ₹20.46 LPA**, with the top 25% of the batch securing an average of **₹24.80 LPA**, and the highest domestic CTC reaching **₹40.91 LPA to ₹45.67 LPA**.

### Q2. What is the total fee structure for MICA Ahmedabad PGDM-C for the 2026–2028 batch?
The comprehensive two-year fee for the residential PGDM/PGDM-C program is approximately **₹28.00 Lakhs**, including tuition, hostel boarding, air-conditioned accommodations, and campus facilities.

### Q3. Can I get admission to MICA without taking CAT or XAT?
You must submit valid scores from either **CAT 2025, XAT 2026, or GMAT** (within the last 2 years). In addition, taking **MICAT** is mandatory for all applicants. Scores from MAT, CMAT, or ATMA are not accepted.

### Q4. What is the expected CAT / XAT cutoff for MICA Ahmedabad?
MICA does not publish a rigid sectional or overall cutoff for CAT/XAT. Generally, candidates with **80+ percentile in CAT/XAT** who perform exceptionally well in MICAT, the Psychometric Test, and the GE-PI rounds receive final conversion offers.

### Q5. What is the difference between PGDM and PGDM-C at MICA?
PGDM-C is MICA's flagship program specifically focused on Strategic Marketing and Communications. PGDM is a general management program with broader exposure across operational and strategic business disciplines, while retaining MICA's signature marketing-centric orientation.

## 1. Institutional Background: Urban Innovation in Mumbai's Financial Hub

ATLAS SkillTech University, established under Maharashtra State Act No. XV of 2021, is India’s first dedicated urban innovation university for technology, design, and management. Its school of management—formerly known as the Indian School of Management & Entrepreneurship (ISME)—operates directly within Equinox Business Park, BKC Annex, Mumbai.

Situated adjacent to the **Bandra-Kurla Complex (BKC)**—India’s preeminent financial district housing the National Stock Exchange (NSE), SEBI, Morgan Stanley, Citibank, and Google—ATLAS offers immediate corporate integration. Rather than operating from an isolated suburban campus, ATLAS students study inside a corporate business park where executives and founders regularly participate in classroom discussions.

For candidates targeting modern management careers in digital branding, fintech, luxury marketing, venture creation, or product analytics in Mumbai, ATLAS SkillTech provides an urban, experiential learning environment.

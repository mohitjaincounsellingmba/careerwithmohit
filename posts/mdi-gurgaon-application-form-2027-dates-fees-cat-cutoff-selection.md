Surrounded by hundreds of multinational corporate headquarters in Gurgaon, MDI offers unparalleled industry exposure, executive guest lectures, and consulting placements.

## 7. MDI Gurgaon Selection Process: GD, WAT & PI

1. **Shortlisting:** Based on CAT 2026 percentile and academic consistency (minimum 50% in 10th, 12th, and graduation).
2. **Written Analysis Test (WAT):** A 15-minute analytical essay assessing coherent reasoning and written communication.
3. **Group Discussion (GD):** A 15–20 minute structured team discussion on contemporary socio-economic or business challenges.
4. **Personal Interview (PI):** A rigorous panel interaction assessing functional knowledge, career clarity, leadership values, and work experience.
5. **Final Composite Merit:** CAT Score (50%) + Personal Interview (30%) + WAT & GD (10%) + Academics & Diversity (10%).

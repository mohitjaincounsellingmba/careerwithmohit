### About Xavier Hyderabad Institute of Business Studies
Xavier Hyderabad Institute of Business Studies is a leading management school located in the educational hub of Hyderabad, offering highly industry-relevant PGDM and MBA programs with outstanding placement support and modern campus facilities.

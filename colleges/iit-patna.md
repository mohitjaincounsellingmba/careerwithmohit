### About IIT Patna (Indian Institute of Technology Patna)
IIT Patna is an elite second-generation IIT situated on a 500-acre smart campus in Bihta, Patna. Celebrated for high-impact research in natural language processing (NLP), AI, and soaring placement metrics.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Artificial Intelligence & Data Science**
- **B.Tech Electrical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **M.Tech Computer Science**

### About IIEST Shibpur (Indian Institute of Engineering Science & Tech)
IIEST Shibpur (formerly Bengal Engineering College, BE College) is the second oldest engineering institution in India. Upgraded to an Institute of National Importance, it shines in structural engineering, computer sciences, and power grids.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Technology**
- **B.Tech Information Technology**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Telecommunication**
- **B.Tech Civil Engineering**
- **B.Tech Metallurgy & Materials**
- **M.Tech VLSI**

### Placement & Career Outcomes
- **Average Placement Package**: ₹11.50 LPA
- **Highest Placement Package**: ₹51.00 LPA
- **Median Package Estimate**: ₹7.00 LPA
- **Top Recruiting Partners**: Microsoft, Amazon, Texas Instruments, L&T, Tata Steel, Cognizant, PwC

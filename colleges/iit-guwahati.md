### About IIT Guwahati (Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati)
IIT Guwahati is the sixth IIT established in India, situated on a breathtaking 700-acre campus on the northern banks of the Brahmaputra. Famous for top design (B.Des), AI research, and top 10 national ranking.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Data Science & Artificial Intelligence**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Electrical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **B.Des (Bachelor of Design)**
- **M.Tech Computer Science**

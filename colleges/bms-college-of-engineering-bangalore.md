### About BMS College of Engineering (BMSCE)
BMS College of Engineering (BMSCE) is India's first private engineering college, located in the historic Basavanagudi heart of Bengaluru. Known for world-class incubation centers, seasoned academic legacy, and blue-chip placement recruiters.

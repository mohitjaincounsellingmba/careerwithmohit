### About NIT Rourkela (National Institute of Technology Rourkela)
NIT Rourkela is one of India's largest and most acclaimed National Institutes of Technology, sprawling over 647 lush acres. It commands top-tier JEE Main cutoffs, stellar research output, and high placement offers.

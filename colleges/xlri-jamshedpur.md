### About XLRI Jamshedpur
XLRI - Xavier School of Management is one of India's oldest and most prestigious B-schools, particularly renowned for its HRM and Business Management programs.

### Courses & Fees
- **PGDM (BM)**: Business Management | 2 Years | ₹26 Lakhs
- **PGDM (HRM)**: Human Resource Management | 2 Years | ₹26 Lakhs

### Placements
Top sectors: Consulting, BFSI, FMCG, HR. Top firms: Nestlé, P&G, TATA, Deloitte, EY.
- **Average Package**: ₹30.5 LPA
- **Highest Package**: ₹80 LPA

### Admission & Cutoff
XAT or GMAT score followed by GD and PI.
- **Cutoff**: 97+ XAT percentile

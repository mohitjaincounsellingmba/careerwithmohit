### About NIT Kurukshetra (National Institute of Technology Kurukshetra)
NIT Kurukshetra is Haryana's premier National Institute of Technology. Boasts a 300-acre green campus, prominent coding culture, and top-drawer placements in software, fintech, and core engineering.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Information Technology**
- **B.Tech Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Communication**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **M.Tech Cyber Security**

### About NIT Jamshedpur (National Institute of Technology Jamshedpur)
NIT Jamshedpur is located in India's Steel City, Jamshedpur. Celebrated for having one of the highest placement percentages among all NITs (nearly 98% overall), with strong core manufacturing and software hiring.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Communication**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Metallurgical & Materials**
- **B.Tech Production & Industrial**
- **M.Tech Computer Science**

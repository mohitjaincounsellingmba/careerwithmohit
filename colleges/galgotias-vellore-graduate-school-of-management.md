### About Galgotias Vellore Graduate School of Management
Galgotias Vellore Graduate School of Management is a leading management school located in the educational hub of Vellore, offering highly industry-relevant PGDM and MBA programs with outstanding placement support and modern campus facilities.

- **MBA**: 2 Years | ₹10.1 Lakhs

- **Cutoff**: 60-70 percentile in entrance exams.

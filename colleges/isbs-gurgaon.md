### About ISBS Gurgaon
International School of Business Studies (ISBS) Gurgaon is part of the ISB&M group, known for its high-performance management programs. It is located in the heart of Gurgaon's corporate sector, allowing students easy access to internships and industry interactions. The school is known for its rigorous training and strong placement record.

### Courses & Fees
- **PGDM**: General Management | 2 Years | ₹8.20 Lakhs
- **MBA**: Global Business | 2 Years | ₹9.50 Lakhs

### MBA + PGPM Program
- **Dual Qualification**: Earn an MBA degree along with a professional PGPM certification.
- **Core Specializations**: Marketing, Finance, Supply Chain, and HR.
- **Career Support**: Dedicated cell for placement and personality development.

### Placements
- **Highest Package**: ₹13.50 LPA
- **Average Package**: ₹7.00 LPA
- **Placement Rate**: 100% recorded over multiple years.
- **Top Recruiters**: Amazon, Flipkart, HUL, Asian Paints, Capgemini, Standard Chartered.

### Admission Process
1. **Entrance Exam**: CAT/XAT/MAT/CMAT score submission.
2. **Profile Evaluation**: Holisitic review of academic and professional background.
3. **Personal Interview**: Final selection round.

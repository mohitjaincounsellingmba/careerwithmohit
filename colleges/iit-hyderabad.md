### About IIT Hyderabad (Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad)
IIT Hyderabad is the fastest-rising second-generation IIT, ranked #8 in NIRF Engineering. Pioneered India's first dedicated B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and maintains strong research ties with Japanese academia and global tech giants.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Artificial Intelligence**
- **B.Tech Electrical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Computational Engineering**
- **M.Tech AI & Machine Learning**

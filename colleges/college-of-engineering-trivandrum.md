### About College of Engineering Trivandrum (CET)
College of Engineering Trivandrum (CET) is the first and topmost engineering college in Kerala, established in 1939 by the Maharaja of Travancore. CET is the absolute #1 preference for KEAM rank-holders.

- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Communication**
- **B.Tech Electrical & Electronics**

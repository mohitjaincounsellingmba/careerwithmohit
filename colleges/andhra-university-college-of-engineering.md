### About Andhra University College of Engineering (AUCE Vizag)
AUCE Visakhapatnam is Andhra Pradesh's oldest and most prestigious state engineering campus. Overlooking the Bay of Bengal, it is especially famous for Naval Architecture, Marine Engineering, and affordable CS/IT degrees.

- **B.Tech Computer Science & Systems Engineering**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Communication**
- **B.Tech Marine Engineering**
- **B.Tech Chemical Engineering**
- **M.Tech Computer Science**

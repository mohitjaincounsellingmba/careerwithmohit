### About SOIL Institute of Management Gurgaon
SOIL (School of Inspired Leadership) Institute of Management, Gurgaon is a premier business school co-created by 32 top multinational corporations including Aditya Birla Group, Tata Motors, Infosys, and Mahindra. Located in the corporate epicenter of Millennium City Gurgaon, SOIL is renowned for its transformative leadership curriculum, design thinking pedagogy, and solid corporate placement network across Consulting, BFSI, Tech, and FMCG sectors.

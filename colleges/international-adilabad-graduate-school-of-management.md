### About International Adilabad Graduate School of Management
International Adilabad Graduate School of Management is a leading management school located in the educational hub of Adilabad, offering highly industry-relevant PGDM and MBA programs with outstanding placement support and modern campus facilities.

- **MBA**: 2 Years | ₹10.1 Lakhs

- **Highest Package**: ₹21.5 LPA

### Admission & Cutoff
- **Admission Process**: Entrance Exam score (CAT/MAT/CMAT/XAT) followed by GD & PI.
- **Cutoff**: 60-70 percentile in entrance exams.

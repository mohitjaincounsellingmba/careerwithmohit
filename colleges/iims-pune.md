### About IIMS Pune
International Institute of Management Studies (IIMS), Pune, located in the prominent IT hub of Hinjawadi, is an AICTE-approved flagship business school under the SaiBalaji Group of Institutes. Known for robust corporate interface, live industry projects, 200+ visiting corporate leaders, and strong placements across BFSI, IT/ITES, Logistics, and FMCG sectors.

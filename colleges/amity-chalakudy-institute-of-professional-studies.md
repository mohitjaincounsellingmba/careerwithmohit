### About Amity Chalakudy Institute of Professional Studies
Amity Chalakudy Institute of Professional Studies is a leading management school located in the educational hub of Chalakudy, offering highly industry-relevant PGDM and MBA programs with outstanding placement support and modern campus facilities.

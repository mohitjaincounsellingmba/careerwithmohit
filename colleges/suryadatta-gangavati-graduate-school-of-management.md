### About Suryadatta Gangavati Graduate School of Management
Suryadatta Gangavati Graduate School of Management is a leading management school located in the educational hub of Gangavati, offering highly industry-relevant PGDM and MBA programs with outstanding placement support and modern campus facilities.

### Courses & Fees
- **PGDM**: 2 Years | ₹10.1 Lakhs
- **MBA**: 2 Years | ₹10.1 Lakhs

### Placements
- **Average Package**: ₹8.5 LPA
- **Highest Package**: ₹24.5 LPA
- **Lowest Package**: ₹5.0 LPA

### Admission & Cutoff
- **Admission Process**: Entrance Exam score (CAT/MAT/CMAT/XAT) followed by GD & PI.
- **Cutoff**: 60-70 percentile in entrance exams.

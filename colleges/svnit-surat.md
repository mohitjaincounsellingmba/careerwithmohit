### About SVNIT Surat (Sardar Vallabhbhai National Institute of Technology)
SVNIT Surat is Gujarat's topmost National Institute of Technology, located in the commercial diamond/textile hub of Surat. Exceptionally strong in Chemical, Mechanical, and Computer Science streams.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Artificial Intelligence**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Communication**
- **B.Tech Chemical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **M.Tech Computer Engineering**

### Placement & Career Outcomes
- **Average Placement Package**: ₹12.80 LPA
- **Highest Placement Package**: ₹48.00 LPA
- **Median Package Estimate**: ₹7.50 LPA
- **Top Recruiting Partners**: Amazon, Samsung R&D, Reliance Industries, L&T, Deere & Co, JPMorgan, Qualcomm

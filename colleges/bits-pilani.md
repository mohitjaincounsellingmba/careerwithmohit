### About BITS Pilani
BITS Pilani is one of India's finest private engineering institutions, known for its practice school internship program, strong alumni network in top tech companies, and industry-aligned curriculum.

### Courses & Fees
- **B.E. Computer Science**: Computer Science | 4 Years | ₹5.70 Lakhs/yr
- **B.E. Electronics**: Electronics | 4 Years | ₹5.70 Lakhs/yr
- **B.Pharm**: Pharmacy | 4 Years | ₹5 Lakhs/yr

### Placements
Among best private universities for placements. Top companies: Google, Microsoft, Qualcomm, Goldman Sachs, Intel.
- **Average Package**: ₹14 LPA
- **Highest Package**: ₹1.5 Crore

### Admission & Cutoff
BITSAT exam only. No JEE Main accepted.
- **Cutoff**: 350+ BITSAT score for CSE

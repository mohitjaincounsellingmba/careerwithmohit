### About IIT Bhubaneswar (Indian Institute of Technology Bhubaneswar)
IIT Bhubaneswar is a premier 2nd-generation IIT nestled at the foot of the Barunei Hills. Known for cutting-edge infrastructure, strong core engineering labs, and high placement ratios in computer science and electronics.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Electrical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Communication**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Metallurgical Engineering**
- **M.Tech Computer Science**

### Placement & Career Outcomes
- **Average Placement Package**: ₹17.20 LPA
- **Highest Placement Package**: ₹56.00 LPA
- **Median Package Estimate**: ₹11.00 LPA
- **Top Recruiting Partners**: Google, Amazon, Qualcomm, Oracle, Goldman Sachs, Tata Steel, L&T

- **Accepted Entrance Exams**: JEE Advanced, JEE Main, GATE

- **Ownership Structure**: Public Autonomous (Govt. of India)

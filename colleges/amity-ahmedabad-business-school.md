### About Amity Ahmedabad Business School
Amity Ahmedabad Business School is a leading management school located in the educational hub of Ahmedabad, offering highly industry-relevant PGDM and MBA programs with outstanding placement support and modern campus facilities.

- **Cutoff**: 60-70 percentile in entrance exams.

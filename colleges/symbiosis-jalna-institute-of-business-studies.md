### About Symbiosis Jalna Institute of Business Studies
Symbiosis Jalna Institute of Business Studies is a leading management school located in the educational hub of Jalna, offering highly industry-relevant PGDM and MBA programs with outstanding placement support and modern campus facilities.

- **PGDM**: 2 Years | ₹6.1 Lakhs
- **MBA**: 2 Years | ₹6.1 Lakhs

- **Admission Process**: Entrance Exam score (CAT/MAT/CMAT/XAT) followed by GD & PI.
- **Cutoff**: 60-70 percentile in entrance exams.

### About LNMIIT Jaipur (The LNM Institute of Information Technology)
LNMIIT Jaipur, established in partnership with steel tycoon Lakshmi Mittal, is acclaimed for its intense computer science curriculum, rich culture of open-source contributions (Google Summer of Code), and premier placement records.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)**
- **B.Tech Communication & Computer Engineering (CCE)**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Communication (ECE)**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **M.Tech Computer Science**

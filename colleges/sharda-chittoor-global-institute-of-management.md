### About Sharda Chittoor Global Institute of Management
Sharda Chittoor Global Institute of Management is a leading management school located in the educational hub of Chittoor, offering highly industry-relevant PGDM and MBA programs with outstanding placement support and modern campus facilities.

- **Admission Process**: Entrance Exam score (CAT/MAT/CMAT/XAT) followed by GD & PI.
- **Cutoff**: 60-70 percentile in entrance exams.

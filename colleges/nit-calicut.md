### About NIT Calicut (National Institute of Technology Calicut)
NIT Calicut is Kerala's highest-ranked technical institute, nestled in Chathamangalam, Kozhikode. It boasts outstanding placement figures, premier faculty, and high JEE Main cutoffs.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Communication**
- **B.Tech Electrical & Electronics**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Chemical Engineering**
- **M.Tech Computer Science**

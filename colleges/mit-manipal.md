### About Manipal Institute of Technology (MIT Manipal)
Manipal Institute of Technology (MIT) is the flagship engineering school of MAHE (Institute of Eminence). Its alumni include Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella. Boasting a world-class residential campus, formula racing student clubs, and global career opportunities.

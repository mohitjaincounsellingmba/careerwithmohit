### About Birla Institute of Technology, Mesra (BIT Mesra)
BIT Mesra Ranchi is a historic 780-acre technical institution founded by industrialist B.M. Birla. Recognized for its dedicated Department of Space Engineering & Rocketry and excellent corporate placement network.

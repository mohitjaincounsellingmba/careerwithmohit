### About IIT Jodhpur (Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur)
IIT Jodhpur is a premier second-generation IIT situated on an expansive 852-acre smart residential campus. Notable for state-of-the-art AI, IoT, and solar energy research and rising placement metrics.

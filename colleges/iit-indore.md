### About IIT Indore (Indian Institute of Technology Indore)
IIT Indore is Central India's top-ranked technical university, situated on a 500-acre modern campus at Simrol. Boasts premier research output, modern housing, and sky-high packages.

- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Electrical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Civil Engineering**
- **B.Tech Space Sciences & Engineering**
- **M.Tech CSE**

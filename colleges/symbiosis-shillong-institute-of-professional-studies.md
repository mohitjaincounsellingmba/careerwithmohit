### About Symbiosis Shillong Institute of Professional Studies
Symbiosis Shillong Institute of Professional Studies is a leading management school located in the educational hub of Shillong, offering highly industry-relevant PGDM and MBA programs with outstanding placement support and modern campus facilities.

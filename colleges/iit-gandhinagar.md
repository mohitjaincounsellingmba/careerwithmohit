### About IIT Gandhinagar (Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar)
IIT Gandhinagar is recognized for its 5-star GRIHA rated riverfront sustainable campus on the Sabarmati river, multidisciplinary curriculum with project-based learning, and stellar placement and global study-abroad options.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Electrical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Civil Engineering**
- **B.Tech Chemical Engineering**
- **M.Tech CSE**

### About NMIMS Mumbai
Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies (NMIMS) is a deemed university in Mumbai's business district, known for strong placement records in finance and marketing sectors.

### Courses & Fees
- **MBA (Core)**: General Management | 2 Years | ₹21.60 Lakhs
- **MBA (HR)**: Human Resource | 2 Years | ₹19 Lakhs

### Placements
Top firms: Deloitte, EY, KPMG, Amazon, HDFC, Axis Bank. Strong in Finance and Marketing.
- **Average Package**: ₹22.5 LPA
- **Highest Package**: ₹42 LPA

### Admission & Cutoff
NMAT by GMAC score, followed by Case Discussion and Personal Interview.
- **Cutoff**: 210+ NMAT score

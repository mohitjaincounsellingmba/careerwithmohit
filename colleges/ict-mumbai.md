### About Institute of Chemical Technology (ICT Mumbai)
ICT Mumbai (formerly UDCT) is India's crown jewel for Chemical Engineering, Petrochemicals, Pharmaceuticals, and Polymer Technology. Has produced Padma awardee scientists and industrial magnates like Mukesh Ambani.

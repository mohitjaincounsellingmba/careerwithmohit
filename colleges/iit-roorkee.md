### About IIT Roorkee (Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee)
IIT Roorkee (formerly Thomason College of Civil Engineering, founded in 1847) is Asia's oldest technical institution. World-renowned for earthquake engineering, water resources, and elite IT placements.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Data Science & AI**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Communication**
- **B.Tech Electrical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Civil Engineering**
- **M.Tech Computer Science**

- **Average Placement Package**: ₹21.50 LPA

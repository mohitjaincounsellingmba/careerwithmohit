### About NIT Srinagar (National Institute of Technology Srinagar)
NIT Srinagar is situated on the western bank of Dal Lake near the Hazratbal Shrine in Srinagar. One of the original Regional Engineering Colleges, it offers robust civil, electrical, and computer engineering programs.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Information Technology**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Communication**
- **B.Tech Electrical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Civil Engineering**
- **M.Tech Water Resources**

### About IIT Kharagpur (Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur)
IIT Kharagpur is India's very first IIT, founded in 1951 at the historic Hijli Detention Camp. With a gargantuan 2,100-acre campus, 19 academic departments, and alumni like Google CEO Sundar Pichai, it stands as an engineering colossus.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Electrical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Ocean Engineering & Naval Architecture**
- **B.Tech Aerospace Engineering**
- **B.Tech Agriculture & Food Engineering**
- **M.Tech Computer Science**

### About VNIT Nagpur (Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology)
VNIT Nagpur is the top NIT in Central and Western India, set on a sprawling 215-acre green campus. Known for excellence in core mechanical/metallurgical sciences, top computer science coding culture, and premier recruiting companies.

- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Communication**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Electrical & Electronics**
- **B.Tech Metallurgical & Materials Engineering**
- **M.Tech Computer Science**

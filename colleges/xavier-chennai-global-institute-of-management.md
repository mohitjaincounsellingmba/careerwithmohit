### About Xavier Chennai Global Institute of Management
Xavier Chennai Global Institute of Management is a leading management school located in the educational hub of Chennai, offering highly industry-relevant PGDM and MBA programs with outstanding placement support and modern campus facilities.

- **PGDM**: 2 Years | ₹6.9 Lakhs
- **MBA**: 2 Years | ₹6.9 Lakhs

- **Highest Package**: ₹12.5 LPA

- **Cutoff**: 60-70 percentile in entrance exams.

### About NIT Raipur (National Institute of Technology Raipur)
NIT Raipur is one of Central India's largest technical institutions. Located in Chhattisgarh's capital city, it is renowned for strong computer science placements alongside metallurgical and mining engineering excellence.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Information Technology**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Communication**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Mining Engineering**
- **B.Tech Metallurgical Engineering**
- **M.Tech Computer Science**

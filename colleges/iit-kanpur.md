### About IIT Kanpur (Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur)
IIT Kanpur is a legendary technical institute, having introduced computer science education to India under the historic Kanpur Indo-American Program (KIAP). Renowned for world-class computing, aerospace testing tunnels, and elite international packages.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Electrical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Aerospace Engineering**
- **B.Tech Materials Science**
- **M.Tech Computer Science**

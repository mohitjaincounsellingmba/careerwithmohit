### About Suryadatta Mandya Global Institute of Management
Suryadatta Mandya Global Institute of Management is a leading management school located in the educational hub of Mandya, offering highly industry-relevant PGDM and MBA programs with outstanding placement support and modern campus facilities.

- **Cutoff**: 60-70 percentile in entrance exams.

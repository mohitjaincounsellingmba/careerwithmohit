IIT Mandi is nestled in the serene Uhl river valley of Himachal Pradesh. Highly esteemed for its bioX, clean energy, and high-performance computing centers and stellar placement records.

- **B.Tech Data Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Electrical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **M.Tech AI & Robotics**

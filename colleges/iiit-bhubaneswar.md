### About IIIT Bhubaneswar (International Institute of Information Tech)
IIIT Bhubaneswar is an autonomous university set up by the Government of Odisha. Offers comprehensive IT, CS, and electrical specializations with dedicated software lab infrastructure.

- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Information Technology**
- **B.Tech Computer Engineering**
- **B.Tech Electrical & Electronics**
- **M.Tech Computer Science**

### About Galgotias Morbi Institute of Business Studies
Galgotias Morbi Institute of Business Studies is a leading management school located in the educational hub of Morbi, offering highly industry-relevant PGDM and MBA programs with outstanding placement support and modern campus facilities.

- **MBA**: 2 Years | ₹6.1 Lakhs

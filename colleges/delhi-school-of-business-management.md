### About Delhi School of Business & Management
Delhi School of Business & Management is a leading management school located in the educational hub of Delhi, offering highly industry-relevant PGDM and MBA programs with outstanding placement support and modern campus facilities.

### Courses & Fees
- **PGDM**: 2 Years | ₹4.5 Lakhs
- **MBA**: 2 Years | ₹4.5 Lakhs

### Placements
- **Average Package**: ₹5.0 LPA
- **Highest Package**: ₹11.0 LPA
- **Lowest Package**: ₹3.5 LPA

### Admission & Cutoff
- **Admission Process**: Entrance Exam score (CAT/MAT/CMAT/XAT) followed by GD & PI.
- **Cutoff**: 60-70 percentile in entrance exams.

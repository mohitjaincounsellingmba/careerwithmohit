### About NIT Uttarakhand (National Institute of Technology Uttarakhand)
NIT Uttarakhand provides high-quality technical education amidst the scenic Garhwal Himalayas. Offers strong fundamentals in computer science, electronics, and mechanical engineering.

- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Communication**
- **B.Tech Electrical & Electronics**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Civil Engineering**
- **M.Tech Computing Systems**

### About NIT Warangal (National Institute of Technology Warangal)
NIT Warangal (formerly Regional Engineering College Warangal) was the very first REC established in India, inaugurated by Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru. Renowned for elite coding circles, high median salaries, and national engineering legacy.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Communication**
- **B.Tech Electrical & Electronics**
- **B.Tech Biotechnology**
- **M.Tech Computer Science & Information Security**

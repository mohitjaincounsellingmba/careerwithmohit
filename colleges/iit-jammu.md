### About IIT Jammu (Indian Institute of Technology Jammu)
IIT Jammu is located on a state-of-the-art 400-acre smart campus at Jagti, Jammu. With modern fabrication labs and fast-rising NIRF rank (#62), it represents the vanguard of premier education in J&K.

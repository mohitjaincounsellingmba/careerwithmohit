### About IIT (ISM) Dhanbad (Indian Institute of Technology ISM Dhanbad)
IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, founded by the British Royal Raj in 1926, is Asia's premier institution for Earth Sciences, Mining, and Petroleum Engineering, alongside high-powered computer science and electronics departments.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Petroleum Engineering**
- **B.Tech Mining Engineering**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Communication**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **M.Tech Computer Science**

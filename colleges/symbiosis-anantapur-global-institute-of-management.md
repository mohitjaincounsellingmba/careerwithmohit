### About Symbiosis Anantapur Global Institute of Management
Symbiosis Anantapur Global Institute of Management is a leading management school located in the educational hub of Anantapur, offering highly industry-relevant PGDM and MBA programs with outstanding placement support and modern campus facilities.

- **MBA**: 2 Years | ₹6.9 Lakhs

- **Cutoff**: 60-70 percentile in entrance exams.

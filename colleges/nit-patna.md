### About NIT Patna (National Institute of Technology Patna)
NIT Patna is the 6th oldest engineering institute in India, perched on the banks of the Holy Ganga river. Known for its rich civil, electrical, and computer science legacy and consistent campus placement drives.

### Key B.Tech & M.Tech Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering**
- **B.Tech Information Technology**
- **B.Tech Electronics & Communication**
- **B.Tech Electrical Engineering**
- **B.Tech Mechanical Engineering**
- **M.Tech Information Security**
